## Goal

You bench press with a stable upper back, controlled bar touch, and safe rack setup that protects the shoulders and chest.

## Preconditions

- A bench press station or rack with safeties, a barbell, plates, and collars.
- A spotter for challenging sets, or safety arms set just below chest height.
- You can perform controlled push-up regressions from `fitness/strength/progress-to-your-first-push-up`.
- Warm up shoulders, upper back, wrists, and several lighter pressing sets.

## Steps

1. **Set the rack and safeties.** Position hooks so you can unrack with nearly straight arms and safeties just below your arched chest position. → *Expect:* the bar can be unracked without shrugging and safeties can catch a failed rep.
2. **Build your bench position.** Lie with eyes under the bar, shoulder blades pulled back and down, feet planted, and a small natural arch. → *Expect:* upper back feels locked to the bench and feet can drive into the floor.
3. **Grip evenly.** Wrap thumbs around the bar, set wrists mostly stacked over elbows, and squeeze the bar hard. → *Expect:* grip marks are even and wrists are not bent far backward.
4. **Unrack and settle.** Lift the bar out, bring it over the shoulder joints, and pause until it stops wobbling. → *Expect:* elbows are locked over shoulders and the bar is steady.
5. **Lower under control.** Bend elbows about 45-75 degrees from the torso and lower to the lower chest or sternum. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if you feel sharp shoulder pain or cannot control the descent. → *Expect:* forearms are near vertical at the bottom and shoulder blades stay retracted.
6. **Press back up and slightly back.** Drive feet into the floor, press the bar up toward the rack line, and keep the chest high. → *Expect:* bar returns over the shoulders without bouncing off the chest.
7. **Rack with control.** Finish the rep, guide the bar back to the hooks, and confirm both sides are seated before releasing. → *Expect:* the bar rests evenly in both hooks.

## Decision points

- Shoulders pinch at the bottom → narrow the grip slightly, reduce depth, or switch to dumbbell pressing.
- Bar path drifts toward the stomach or face → lighten the load and practice consistent touch point.
- Feet lift or body slides → reset foot position and use lighter weight.
- You are near failure → use a spotter or safeties; do not attempt a risky final rep.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bouncing the bar:** detect the bar rebounding off the chest → pause lightly on the chest and reduce load.
- **F2 Flared elbows:** detect elbows straight out from shoulders → tuck slightly and keep forearms vertical.
- **F3 Loose upper back:** detect shoulders rolling forward → reset shoulder blades before continuing.
- **F4 Bent wrists:** detect knuckles falling back toward the forearm → grip lower in the palm and stack wrists.
- **F5 Unsafe failed rep:** detect the bar stalling on the chest → lower to safeties or have the spotter assist evenly.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 5 bench press reps with even grip, planted feet, stable shoulder blades, controlled chest touch, no bounce, and a safe rack or spotter setup.

## Variations

- `easier`: use dumbbell bench press, push-ups, or an empty bar.
- `harder`: add small weight jumps, paused reps, or close-grip bench press after technique is consistent.
- `equipment`: use a power rack with safeties if no spotter is available.
- `at-home`: train push-ups, floor press with dumbbells, or resistance-band presses.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because a loaded bar can fall onto the chest, neck, or face. Use safeties or a reliable spotter, keep thumbs wrapped, and stop for sharp shoulder, elbow, wrist, or chest pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a medical professional if you have relevant conditions or symptoms.
