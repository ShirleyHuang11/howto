## Goal

You perform a goblet squat with a held weight, stable torso, full-foot pressure, and controlled knee tracking.

## Preconditions

- A dumbbell or kettlebell light enough to hold close to the chest.
- Clear floor space and stable shoes.
- You can perform `fitness/strength/do-a-proper-bodyweight-squat` with good control.
- Warm up with bodyweight squats and hip mobility.

## Steps

1. **Hold the weight close.** Cup a dumbbell head or kettlebell horns at chest height with elbows pointing down. → *Expect:* the weight touches or stays close to the sternum without pulling shoulders forward.
2. **Set your stance.** Stand shoulder width with toes slightly out and full-foot pressure. → *Expect:* heels, big toes, and little toes stay grounded.
3. **Brace and descend.** Inhale, tighten the abdomen, and squat down between the feet. → *Expect:* elbows travel inside or near the knees while the chest stays tall.
4. **Reach stable depth.** Lower until thighs are at least near parallel or your deepest controlled position. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop before your back rounds, heels lift, or knees hurt sharply. → *Expect:* knees track with toes and the weight remains close.
5. **Stand with control.** Push through the whole foot and keep elbows under the weight as you rise. → *Expect:* hips and chest rise together without tipping forward.
6. **Reset at the top.** Stand tall, breathe, and rebrace before the next rep. → *Expect:* each rep begins from a balanced, stacked posture.

## Decision points

- Weight pulls you forward → choose a lighter weight or hold it closer.
- Elbows hit knees early → slightly widen stance or turn toes out a bit more.
- Heels lift → reduce depth, work ankle mobility, or use a small heel wedge temporarily.
- Sets of 12 are easy → increase load or progress to barbell squats.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Forward collapse:** detect chest dropping and weight drifting away → lighten the load and keep elbows down.
- **F2 Knee cave:** detect knees moving inward → slow down and guide knees over toes.
- **F3 Heel lift:** detect pressure on toes at the bottom → shorten range or adjust stance.
- **F4 Relaxed bottom:** detect bouncing or hanging on joints → keep tension and pause with control.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 10 goblet squats with the weight close to the chest, heels down, knees tracking over toes, neutral spine, and controlled depth.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a lighter object or squat to a box.
- `harder`: use a heavier kettlebell, tempo reps, or pauses at the bottom.
- `equipment`: hold a dumbbell vertically, kettlebell by the horns, or medicine ball at the chest.
- `at-home`: use a loaded backpack hugged to the chest.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk with manageable load and good footing. Stop for sharp knee, hip, back, or wrist pain, and do not hold a weight you cannot control close to the body. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional before training if you have relevant conditions.
