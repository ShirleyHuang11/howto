## Goal

You perform a controlled bodyweight squat with stable feet, knees tracking over toes, and an upright, braced torso.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space where you can stand with arms forward without hitting anything.
- Flat shoes or bare feet with enough grip to keep the whole foot planted.
- Warm up first with 3-5 minutes of easy walking, leg swings, and shallow squats.

## Steps

1. **Set your stance.** Stand with feet about shoulder width, toes turned slightly out, and weight balanced across heel, big toe, and little toe. → *Expect:* both feet feel rooted and your arches stay active.
2. **Brace before you descend.** Inhale lightly, tighten your abdomen as if preparing for a cough, and keep your ribs stacked over your pelvis. → *Expect:* your torso feels firm without holding your breath hard.
3. **Start by bending hips and knees together.** Sit down between your feet, letting knees travel in the same direction as your toes. → *Expect:* knees stay aligned over the middle toes and heels remain down.
4. **Reach a controlled bottom position.** Squat as low as you can while keeping your spine neutral and feet planted. [BRANCH: limited mobility, stop above parallel with control | good mobility, descend until thighs reach at least parallel] → *Expect:* hips are back and down, chest stays proud, and there is no pinching pain.
5. **Stand by driving the floor away.** Push through the whole foot, squeeze the glutes, and keep knees tracking out as you rise. → *Expect:* hips and shoulders rise together instead of the hips shooting up first.
6. **Finish tall without overextending.** Stand fully upright with glutes lightly squeezed and ribs down. → *Expect:* hips and knees are straight, but your lower back is not arched hard.

## Decision points

- Heels lift or arches collapse → widen the stance slightly, reduce depth, and slow down.
- Knees cave inward → use a shallower squat and actively guide knees over the second and third toes.
- Sharp knee, hip, or back pain → stop the set and choose a pain-free regression.
- Squat feels too easy for 3 sets of 15 → progress to `fitness/strength/do-a-goblet-squat`.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Heel lift:** detect weight shifting onto the toes at the bottom → reduce depth, try a slightly wider stance, and practice ankle mobility.
- **F2 Knee cave:** detect knees moving inside the big toes → slow the descent and think "knees follow toes."
- **F3 Butt wink with pain:** detect the pelvis tucking under and lower back discomfort → stop just above that depth and rebuild control.
- **F4 Forward collapse:** detect the chest dropping toward the thighs → brace harder and reach arms forward as a counterbalance.

## Verification

Complete 10 controlled bodyweight squats to your available depth with heels down, knees tracking over toes, a neutral spine, and no sharp joint pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: squat to a chair or box and stand back up without rocking.
- `harder`: add a 3-second descent, a pause at the bottom, or progress to a goblet squat.
- `equipment`: hold a light counterweight in front of the chest if it helps balance.
- `at-home`: use a sturdy chair behind you as a depth target.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when done unloaded and under control. Stop for sharp knee, hip, or back pain, dizziness, or loss of balance. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant injury, medical condition, or have been told to avoid squatting.
