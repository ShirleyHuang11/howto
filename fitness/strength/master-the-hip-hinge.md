## Goal

You learn to move from the hips while keeping a neutral spine, which supports deadlifts, rows, swings, and safe lifting from the floor.

## Preconditions

- Clear space to stand and bend forward.
- A dowel, broomstick, or wall for feedback if available.
- Warm up with easy walking, glute bridges, and light hamstring movement.

## Steps

1. **Stand tall with soft knees.** Place feet hip width, unlock knees slightly, and brace the abdomen. → *Expect:* weight is balanced over the midfoot, not the toes.
2. **Use a three-point dowel check.** Hold a dowel along the back touching the head, upper back, and tailbone. [BRANCH: no dowel, place hands on ribs and hips for feedback] → *Expect:* the three contact points stay connected.
3. **Push hips back.** Send your hips toward the wall behind you while keeping shins nearly vertical. → *Expect:* hamstrings load and torso tips forward without the back rounding.
4. **Stop at your controlled range.** Hinge until hamstrings limit you or torso is roughly 45-70 degrees forward. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop before your lower back rounds or pain appears. → *Expect:* spine stays neutral and knees remain softly bent.
5. **Return by squeezing glutes.** Drive hips forward to stand tall without leaning back. → *Expect:* hips finish under ribs and glutes do the work.
6. **Practice with wall feedback.** Stand a short step from a wall and hinge until the hips tap it, then stand. → *Expect:* the wall touch comes from hips moving back, not knees bending forward.

## Decision points

- You feel only lower back → reduce range and brace harder.
- Knees bend like a squat → move hips back toward the wall and keep shins more vertical.
- Hamstrings cramp or pull sharply → stop and use a smaller range.
- You can perform 15 clean hinges → progress to light Romanian deadlifts or deadlifts.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Squat pattern:** detect knees traveling far forward → practice wall taps and keep hips reaching back.
- **F2 Spinal rounding:** detect dowel losing head or tailbone contact → reduce depth and brace.
- **F3 Neck extension:** detect looking forward hard → keep gaze on the floor several feet ahead.
- **F4 Overextended finish:** detect leaning back at the top → stop when ribs, hips, and ankles stack.

## Verification

Complete 15 hip hinges with soft knees, hips moving back, neutral spine, hamstring tension, and a glute-driven return to standing.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a wall tap from a shorter range.
- `harder`: hold a light kettlebell or dumbbells once the pattern is consistent.
- `equipment`: use a dowel, broomstick, or PVC pipe for spine feedback.
- `at-home`: practice near a wall or closed door with no equipment.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when unloaded and controlled. Stop for sharp back, hip, or hamstring pain, and do not force range by rounding the spine. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if you have a back injury or movement restrictions.
