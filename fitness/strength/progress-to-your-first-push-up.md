## Goal

You build from easier push-up variations to a strict floor push-up with a straight body line and controlled shoulder motion.

## Preconditions

- A wall, countertop, bench, or floor space with non-slip hand contact.
- Wrists and shoulders tolerate pressing without sharp pain.
- You can hold `fitness/strength/hold-a-proper-plank` for at least 15 seconds.
- Warm up wrists, shoulders, chest, and upper back.

## Steps

1. **Choose the right incline.** [BRANCH: very new, use a wall | building strength, use a countertop or bench | near full push-up, use the floor from knees or toes] → *Expect:* you can complete 5-8 smooth reps at that angle.
2. **Set hands and body line.** Place hands just outside shoulder width, fingers spread, and make a straight line from head to heels or knees. → *Expect:* wrists are under shoulders and hips do not sag.
3. **Brace before lowering.** Squeeze glutes lightly, tighten the abdomen, and keep neck neutral. → *Expect:* ribs stay down and the body moves as one unit.
4. **Lower with elbows angled.** Bend elbows about 30-60 degrees from the torso and lower chest toward the surface. → *Expect:* shoulders stay away from ears and elbows do not flare straight out.
5. **Press the surface away.** Push through the full hand until elbows straighten without locking aggressively. → *Expect:* chest and hips rise together.
6. **Progress gradually.** Lower the incline only after you can complete clean sets. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if wrists, shoulders, or low back hurt sharply. → *Expect:* each new level still keeps full-body tension and control.

## Decision points

- Hips sag → raise the incline and rebuild plank tension.
- Elbows flare wide → turn elbow creases slightly forward and keep elbows closer to ribs.
- Wrists hurt → use handles, dumbbells as grips, or a higher incline.
- You can do 3 sets of 10 at one level → move to a lower incline or harder variation.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Sagging hips:** detect low back arching during reps → squeeze glutes and use an easier incline.
- **F2 Short range:** detect only small elbow bend → use a higher surface and touch chest close to it.
- **F3 Shoulder shrug:** detect shoulders rising toward ears → push the floor away and keep neck long.
- **F4 Head reach:** detect chin touching before chest → keep the neck neutral and lower the chest.
- **F5 Uneven press:** detect one side rising first → slow down and check hand placement.

## Verification

Complete 1 strict floor push-up from toes with hands just outside shoulders, chest lowering near the floor, elbows controlled, body line straight, and hips rising with the chest.

## Variations

- `easier`: wall push-up, countertop push-up, bench push-up, or knee push-up.
- `harder`: slower tempo, pause at the bottom, decline push-up, or weighted push-up after strong form.
- `equipment`: use push-up handles if wrist extension is uncomfortable.
- `at-home`: use stairs or a sturdy countertop to choose a precise incline.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when progressed gradually. Stop for sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, chest, or back pain, and avoid collapsing at the bottom when fatigued. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if pressing causes persistent pain or you have relevant medical restrictions.
