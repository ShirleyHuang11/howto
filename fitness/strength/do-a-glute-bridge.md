## Goal

You perform a glute bridge that uses hip extension from the glutes rather than the lower back or hamstrings.

## Preconditions

- Floor space and a mat or comfortable surface.
- Knees and hips can bend while lying on your back.
- Warm up with easy walking, hip circles, and a few bodyweight hinges.

## Steps

1. **Lie down and set the feet.** Bend knees, place feet hip width, and keep heels close enough that fingertips can graze them. → *Expect:* shins are roughly vertical at the top of the bridge.
2. **Stack ribs and pelvis.** Lightly tighten the abdomen and keep ribs down before lifting. → *Expect:* lower back gently contacts or stays close to the floor.
3. **Drive through the whole foot.** Push feet into the floor and lift hips by squeezing glutes. → *Expect:* hips rise without the lower back arching first.
4. **Pause at the top.** Stop when shoulders, hips, and knees form a straight line. → *Expect:* glutes feel active and hamstrings do not cramp.
5. **Lower with control.** Bring hips back down slowly and keep knees pointing forward. → *Expect:* pelvis returns to the floor without dropping suddenly.
6. **Adjust if needed.** [BRANCH: hamstring cramp, move feet slightly farther away | low-back arch, reduce height and brace harder] → *Expect:* the next rep feels more glute-dominant.

## Decision points

- Hamstrings cramp → move feet slightly farther from hips and push through midfoot and heel.
- Lower back feels compressed → brace ribs down and stop before overextending.
- Knees fall outward or inward → keep knees aligned over ankles.
- Bodyweight is easy for 3 sets of 15 → progress to single-leg or loaded bridges.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Back extension:** detect ribs flaring and low back arching → brace abs and lift only to neutral hip extension.
- **F2 Feet too far away:** detect hamstrings doing all the work → move heels slightly closer unless cramping worsens.
- **F3 Feet too close:** detect pressure mostly on toes → move feet farther until shins are near vertical at the top.
- **F4 Knee drift:** detect knees collapsing inward → press knees gently over the middle toes.

## Verification

Complete 15 glute bridges with hips reaching a straight shoulder-hip-knee line, ribs down, knees aligned, and glutes working without low-back pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a smaller range or hold the top for shorter pauses.
- `harder`: single-leg glute bridge, banded bridge, or hip thrust with load.
- `equipment`: add a mini-band above the knees to cue knee alignment.
- `at-home`: perform on a mat with a folded towel under the head if needed.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when controlled. Stop for sharp back, hip, knee, or hamstring pain, and avoid forcing height by arching the lower back. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if symptoms persist or you have relevant restrictions.
