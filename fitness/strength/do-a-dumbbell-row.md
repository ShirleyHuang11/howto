## Goal

You perform a one-arm dumbbell row that trains the upper back and lats without twisting the torso or shrugging the shoulder.

## Preconditions

- One dumbbell and a bench, box, or sturdy support.
- Clear space beside the support.
- You can hinge or brace without back pain.
- Warm up with light rows, shoulder circles, and scapular squeezes.

## Steps

1. **Set your support.** Place one hand and same-side knee on a bench, or use a staggered stance with one hand on a sturdy surface. → *Expect:* torso is stable and roughly parallel to the floor or slightly above it.
2. **Brace and square the hips.** Keep ribs down, spine neutral, and hips facing the floor. → *Expect:* the body does not rotate before the pull.
3. **Start with the arm long.** Let the dumbbell hang under the shoulder without relaxing the trunk. → *Expect:* shoulder blade can move, but the shoulder does not yank downward painfully.
4. **Row toward the hip.** Pull the elbow back and slightly toward the ribs, keeping the forearm mostly vertical. → *Expect:* the dumbbell reaches the side of the torso without shoulder shrugging.
5. **Pause and lower slowly.** Briefly squeeze the back, then lower until the arm is long again. → *Expect:* the dumbbell does not swing or crash down.
6. **Keep the torso quiet.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop or lighten the load if you must twist hard to finish reps. → *Expect:* shoulders and hips remain mostly square through the set.

## Decision points

- You feel mostly biceps → pull the elbow toward the hip and pause with the shoulder blade back.
- Torso rotates → lighten the dumbbell and brace harder.
- Neck tightens → keep shoulder away from the ear and gaze down.
- Easy for 3 sets of 12 each side → add load or slower lowering.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Torso twist:** detect chest opening toward the working side → reduce load and keep both hip bones pointed down.
- **F2 Shrugged pull:** detect shoulder rising to the ear → pull elbow to hip and lengthen the neck.
- **F3 Swinging weight:** detect momentum at the bottom → pause fully before each pull.
- **F4 Rounded support position:** detect upper or lower back slumping → reset the brace and bench position.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 10 dumbbell rows per side with a neutral spine, square hips, elbow pulling toward the hip, controlled lowering, and no torso twisting.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a lighter dumbbell or chest-supported incline row.
- `harder`: pause at the top, use slower eccentrics, or progress to heavier rows.
- `equipment`: use a kettlebell, cable handle, or resistance band.
- `at-home`: row a loaded bag while supporting the other hand on a couch or table that will not move.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when the support is stable and the load is controlled. Stop for sharp back, shoulder, elbow, or wrist pain, and avoid twisting to force heavy reps. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional for persistent pain or relevant conditions.
