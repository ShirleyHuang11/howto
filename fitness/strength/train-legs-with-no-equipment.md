## Goal

You train quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves, and trunk using bodyweight leg exercises that can be progressed safely.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space and a stable wall, chair, or step if using support.
- Shoes or non-slip floor contact.
- No sharp knee, hip, ankle, or back pain during unloaded squats or lunges.
- Warmup with easy squats, hip hinges, and ankle movement.

## Steps

1. **Start with squat pattern.** Perform bodyweight squats with feet planted, knees tracking over toes, and torso balanced. → *Expect:* depth is repeatable and heels stay down.
2. **Add single-leg support work.** Use reverse lunges or split squats, lightly holding a wall if needed. → *Expect:* front knee tracks over toes without collapsing inward.
3. **Train hip extension.** Do glute bridges or single-leg bridges with ribs down and hips fully extended. → *Expect:* glutes contract without low-back arching.
4. **Train hamstrings carefully.** Use walkouts from a bridge or slow hip hinges. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if hamstrings cramp sharply or the lower back takes over. → *Expect:* hamstrings work under control.
5. **Add calves and ankles.** Do calf raises through full range, pausing at top and bottom. → *Expect:* ankles move smoothly without bouncing.
6. **Use tempo for difficulty.** Lower for 3 seconds, pause, and stand with control. → *Expect:* muscles fatigue even without external load.
7. **Progress one variable.** Add reps, sets, range, tempo, or unilateral difficulty gradually. → *Expect:* form remains stable as effort increases.

## Decision points

- Balance limits split squats → use fingertip support and slow the movement.
- Squats are too easy → use pauses, 1.5 reps, or single-leg variations.
- Knees feel irritated → reduce depth, try reverse lunges, and keep knees tracking.
- Hamstrings cramp → shorten range and add more glute bridge practice.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Knees cave inward:** detect knees moving inside big toes → slow down and press knees in line with toes.
- **F2 Heel lift:** detect weight shifting to toes in squats → reduce depth and sit hips between feet.
- **F3 Low-back arch in bridges:** detect ribs flaring upward → brace ribs down and squeeze glutes.
- **F4 Rushing reps:** detect bouncing out of the bottom → add a pause and reduce rep target.

## Verification

You complete a no-equipment leg session with squats, lunges or split squats, bridges, hamstring work, and calf raises while maintaining knee tracking, heel contact, controlled tempo, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- Easier: use chair squats, supported split squats, and two-leg bridges.
- Harder: use pistol-squat progressions, Bulgarian split squats, single-leg bridges, and slow eccentrics.
- Equipment: a backpack can add load once bodyweight reps are clean.
- At-home: use stairs for calf raises and a wall or chair for balance support.

## Safety & privacy

General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Stop for sharp knee, hip, ankle, or back pain, avoid jumping progressions until landing mechanics are sound, and consult a clinician for persistent symptoms.
