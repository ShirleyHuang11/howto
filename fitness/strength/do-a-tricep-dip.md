## Goal

You perform a dip variation that trains the triceps and chest while keeping shoulders controlled and pain-free.

## Preconditions

- Parallel bars, dip station, assisted dip machine, or a sturdy bench for a regression.
- Shoulders tolerate extension behind the torso without sharp pain.
- You can perform controlled push-up work from `fitness/strength/progress-to-your-first-push-up`.
- Warm up shoulders, elbows, wrists, and light pressing movements.

## Steps

1. **Choose an appropriate variation.** [BRANCH: new, use assisted dip machine or bench dip with knees bent | stronger, use parallel bars] → *Expect:* you can control the full descent without shoulder pain.
2. **Set the shoulders.** Support yourself with elbows straight, shoulders down away from ears, and chest open. → *Expect:* body feels tall and stable at the top.
3. **Brace and lower.** Bend elbows and lower until upper arms are about parallel to the floor or your pain-free range. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop before sharp shoulder pain or deep anterior shoulder strain. → *Expect:* elbows track back, not wildly flared.
4. **Press back up.** Push the bars or bench away until elbows straighten under control. → *Expect:* shoulders stay down and the body does not swing.
5. **Keep range honest.** Use the deepest range you can control without shoulder discomfort. → *Expect:* reps are smooth and repeatable, not bounced.
6. **End the set cleanly.** Step down or unload the assistance with control. → *Expect:* no dropping, shoulder shrugging, or elbow lock slam.

## Decision points

- Shoulder pinches at the bottom → reduce depth or switch to close-grip push-ups.
- Elbows flare wide → keep elbows tracking mostly backward.
- Body swings → use assistance, slow down, and brace legs.
- Bench dips bother shoulders → choose assisted parallel-bar dips or triceps pressdowns instead.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Too-deep shoulder extension:** detect front-shoulder stretch turning sharp → limit depth immediately.
- **F2 Shrugged top:** detect shoulders near ears → depress shoulder blades before each rep.
- **F3 Bouncing bottom:** detect rebound out of the deepest position → pause above the painful or loose range.
- **F4 Wrist discomfort:** detect pressure or bend pain → adjust hand angle or use parallel handles.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 6 controlled dips in a pain-free range with shoulders down, elbows tracking back, no bouncing, and a stable top support.

## Variations

- `easier`: assisted dip machine, band-assisted dips, bench dips with bent knees, or close-grip incline push-ups.
- `harder`: full bodyweight dips, slow eccentrics, pauses, or weighted dips after strong pain-free reps.
- `equipment`: use parallel bars or rings only after stable bar dips are easy.
- `at-home`: use close-grip push-ups if no safe dip station is available.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for many trainees, but dips can irritate shoulders if performed too deep or too heavy. Stop for sharp shoulder, elbow, wrist, chest, or neck pain, and do not force a range your shoulders do not tolerate. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional for persistent symptoms or prior shoulder injury.
