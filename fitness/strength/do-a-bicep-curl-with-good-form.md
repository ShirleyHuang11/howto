## Goal

You perform a bicep curl that moves at the elbow without swinging the torso, shrugging the shoulders, or bending the wrists.

## Preconditions

- Dumbbells, barbell, cable handle, or resistance band with manageable resistance.
- Enough space to stand or sit without hitting objects.
- Elbows and wrists tolerate curling without sharp pain.
- Warm up with light curls and gentle elbow and wrist movement.

## Steps

1. **Stand or sit tall.** Set feet hip width, ribs down, shoulders relaxed, and arms hanging by your sides. → *Expect:* posture is stable before the weight moves.
2. **Grip with neutral wrists.** Hold the weight with palms up or slightly turned in, keeping knuckles aligned with forearms. → *Expect:* wrists are not bent back or curled inward.
3. **Pin the upper arms.** Keep elbows near the ribs without squeezing them painfully. → *Expect:* upper arms stay mostly still during the rep.
4. **Curl smoothly.** Bend the elbows and lift the weight toward the shoulders without leaning back. → *Expect:* biceps tighten and the torso remains quiet.
5. **Squeeze without shrugging.** Pause near the top while keeping shoulders down. → *Expect:* elbows remain under or slightly in front of shoulders, not drifting far forward.
6. **Lower fully under control.** Straighten elbows almost all the way without snapping them locked. → *Expect:* the weight descends slowly and the biceps stay loaded.
7. **Stop momentum reps.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop or lighten the load if you need hip swing, back lean, or wrist pain to finish. → *Expect:* every rep uses the same tempo and range.

## Decision points

- You swing the weight → reduce load and pause at the bottom.
- Wrists hurt → use lighter dumbbells, neutral-grip hammer curls, or cable handles.
- Elbows drift far forward → keep upper arms closer to the sides.
- Easy for 3 sets of 15 → increase weight slightly or slow the lowering.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hip swing:** detect using the whole body to start the curl → brace and choose a lighter load.
- **F2 Back lean:** detect ribs flaring and spine arching at the top → stand against a wall or lower weight.
- **F3 Wrist break:** detect wrists bending under the weight → keep knuckles in line with forearms.
- **F4 Shoulder shrug:** detect traps taking over → relax shoulders and curl only through the elbows.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 12 curls with elbows mostly still, neutral wrists, controlled lowering, full comfortable range, and no torso swing.

## Variations

- `easier`: use lighter dumbbells, bands, or seated curls.
- `harder`: slow the eccentric, use incline dumbbell curls, or add load while keeping strict form.
- `equipment`: use dumbbells, EZ-bar, cable, barbell, or resistance band.
- `at-home`: curl a loaded backpack or band anchored under the feet.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when the load is reasonable. Stop for sharp elbow, wrist, shoulder, or forearm pain, and avoid jerking heavy weights with the lower back. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if symptoms persist.
