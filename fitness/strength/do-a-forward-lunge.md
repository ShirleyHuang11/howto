## Goal

You perform a forward lunge with balance, knee control, and a smooth push back to standing.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space at least one long step in front of you.
- Shoes or floor surface with enough traction to prevent slipping.
- You can do `fitness/strength/do-a-proper-bodyweight-squat` without knee or hip pain.
- Warm up with easy squats, step-backs, and hip mobility.

## Steps

1. **Start tall and braced.** Stand with feet hip width, ribs stacked over pelvis, and hands at hips or out for balance. → *Expect:* posture is upright and weight is even on both feet.
2. **Step forward deliberately.** Take a long enough step that both knees can bend without the front heel lifting. → *Expect:* front foot lands flat and quiet.
3. **Lower straight down.** Bend both knees until the back knee approaches the floor, keeping the front knee tracking over toes. → *Expect:* front shin angles naturally, torso stays tall, and the front heel remains down.
4. **Push back through the front foot.** Drive through the whole front foot to return to the start position without twisting. → *Expect:* you arrive back at hip-width stance with control.
5. **Alternate or repeat one side.** [BRANCH: balance practice, alternate sides | strength focus, complete all reps on one side before switching] → *Expect:* rep quality stays the same on both legs.
6. **Stop before form breaks.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if the knee caves inward, pain becomes sharp, or you cannot control the landing. → *Expect:* every rep remains quiet, stable, and pain-free.

## Decision points

- You wobble or step across your midline → shorten the stride and step on train tracks, not a tightrope.
- Front knee caves inward → slow down and guide the knee over the second and third toes.
- Knee discomfort appears → try reverse lunges or split squats instead.
- Bodyweight is easy for 3 sets of 12 each side → add dumbbells or a slower tempo.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Narrow landing:** detect feet crossing toward one line → step slightly wider for a stable base.
- **F2 Heel lift:** detect front heel rising at the bottom → lengthen the stride slightly and keep pressure through the heel and forefoot.
- **F3 Knee cave:** detect front knee collapsing inward → reduce depth and use a mirror for alignment.
- **F4 Push-off bounce:** detect springing off the back foot → drive mainly through the front leg.

## Verification

Complete 8 controlled forward lunges per leg with front heel down, knee tracking over toes, upright torso, quiet landing, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a split squat, reverse lunge, or hold a wall for balance.
- `harder`: hold dumbbells, add a pause near the bottom, or use walking lunges.
- `equipment`: use a suspension trainer or rail for balance support.
- `at-home`: lunge in a hallway with a wall nearby for light fingertip support.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when performed slowly on a non-slip surface. Stop for sharp knee, hip, ankle, or back pain, and avoid adding load until balance and alignment are consistent. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a clinician if lunges aggravate an injury or condition.
