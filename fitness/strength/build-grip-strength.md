## Goal

You strengthen support grip, crushing grip, and wrist control without irritating elbows or overworking hands.

## Preconditions

- Pull-up bar, dumbbells, kettlebells, farmer handles, grippers, towels, or similar tools.
- No current hand, wrist, or elbow pain during light gripping.
- Chalk or dry hands if grip slipping is an issue.
- Space to carry weights without obstacles.

## Steps

1. **Pick the grip quality.** Choose hangs for support, carries for whole-hand strength, grippers for crushing, or wrist work for forearms. → *Expect:* the exercise matches the task you want to improve.
2. **Warm up hands and elbows.** Open and close the hands, rotate wrists, and do light holds. → *Expect:* forearms feel warm without tingling.
3. **Use timed holds.** Hold a bar, dumbbells, or kettlebells for 10-30 seconds with shoulders down and wrists neutral. → *Expect:* grip fatigues before posture fails.
4. **Add carries.** Walk with weights at your sides, ribs stacked, and steps controlled. → *Expect:* weights stay quiet and do not bang your legs.
5. **Train both opening and closing.** Add finger extensions with a band or slow hand opening after hard gripping. → *Expect:* forearms feel balanced rather than cramped.
6. **Progress slowly.** Add 5-10 seconds, a little distance, or small load increases. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if you feel sharp elbow pain, numbness, or finger tingling. → *Expect:* hands tire but recover by the next session.
7. **Limit frequency.** Train grip 2-4 times per week depending on other pulling work. → *Expect:* grip improves without making rows, deadlifts, or daily tasks worse.

## Decision points

- Grip fails during back exercises → train support holds after main pulling work.
- Elbows ache → reduce gripper volume and add rest days.
- Skin tears or calluses pinch → file calluses and use chalk, not slippery gloves.
- One hand lags → start with the weaker side and match time or load.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrist bending:** detect loaded wrists collapsing into flexion or extension → reduce load and stack knuckles over forearm.
- **F2 Shoulder shrugging on hangs:** detect ears rising to shoulders → set shoulder blades down and use shorter hangs.
- **F3 Too much crushing work:** detect elbow tendon soreness → cut gripper work and emphasize carries.
- **F4 Dropping weights unpredictably:** detect grip opening suddenly → carry in a clear area and stop before failure.

## Verification

You complete 2-4 grip sessions in a week with timed holds or carries that improve by time, distance, or load while wrists stay neutral and elbows remain pain-free.

## Variations

- Easier: use lighter carries, assisted hangs, or shorter hold intervals.
- Harder: use towel hangs, thicker handles, heavier farmer carries, or plate pinches.
- Equipment: dumbbells, kettlebells, loaded bags, towels, and grippers all train different grip qualities.
- At-home: use grocery bags, a towel over a sturdy rail, or a backpack handle if the setup is secure.

## Safety & privacy

General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Avoid sudden max grip efforts with painful elbows or wrists, stop for numbness or tingling, and consult a clinician for persistent hand, wrist, or nerve symptoms.
