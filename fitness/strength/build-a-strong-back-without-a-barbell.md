## Goal

You train lats, upper back, rear shoulders, and spinal-support muscles without needing barbell rows or deadlifts.

## Preconditions

- Dumbbells, bands, cable machine, pull-up bar, suspension trainer, or sturdy row setup.
- A surface for chest-supported or one-arm rows if needed.
- No sharp shoulder, elbow, or back pain during pulling motions.
- Warmup for shoulders, thoracic spine, and hips.

## Steps

1. **Select a vertical pull.** Use pull-ups, assisted pull-ups, pulldowns, or band pulldowns with ribs down. → *Expect:* elbows drive toward ribs and shoulders do not shrug into ears.
2. **Select a horizontal row.** Use dumbbell rows, cable rows, band rows, or inverted rows. → *Expect:* shoulder blades move back and around the rib cage without torso twisting.
3. **Add rear-shoulder work.** Use reverse flyes, face pulls, or band pull-aparts with light tension. → *Expect:* rear shoulders and mid-back work without neck strain.
4. **Train hip-hinge support.** Use dumbbell Romanian deadlifts, back extensions, bird dogs, or hip hinges. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if lower-back rounding or sharp pain appears. → *Expect:* glutes and hamstrings share the work with a stable spine.
5. **Control the lowering.** Let shoulder blades protract or elevate naturally, then pull from the back rather than jerking. → *Expect:* each rep has stretch, pull, and controlled return.
6. **Balance volume.** Do 2-4 sets of each pattern, 6-15 reps depending on the exercise. → *Expect:* back muscles fatigue before grip or neck dominates.
7. **Progress with reps or angle.** Add reps, load, band tension, or a harder body angle only when range stays full. → *Expect:* the log improves without shortening pulls.

## Decision points

- Grip limits every set → add straps for back work and train grip separately.
- Neck tightens during rows → lower shoulders and reduce load.
- No pull-up bar → use bands, cables, dumbbells, or suspension rows.
- Lower back fatigues during rows → use chest-supported rows.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Torso rotation:** detect twisting on one-arm rows → widen stance, brace, and reduce load.
- **F2 Shrugging pulls:** detect traps taking over → start each rep by setting shoulder blades down.
- **F3 Half reps:** detect handles stopping far from the body or no stretch → lower load and use full range.
- **F4 Back pain in hinges:** detect spinal rounding or pinching → switch to bird dogs or supported back extensions.

## Verification

You complete one back session with one vertical pull, one horizontal row, one rear-shoulder exercise, and one hinge-support exercise while keeping shoulder control, full range, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- Easier: use band pulldowns, chest-supported rows, and light reverse flyes.
- Harder: add weighted pull-ups, slow eccentrics, single-arm cable rows, or loaded carries.
- Equipment: cables, dumbbells, bands, machines, and suspension trainers are all valid.
- At-home: use a doorway pull-up bar, bands, backpack rows, or inverted rows under a sturdy table.

## Safety & privacy

General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Secure any home pulling setup before loading it, stop for sharp shoulder, elbow, or back pain, and consult a professional for persistent symptoms.
