## Goal

You perform a lat pulldown with controlled shoulder motion, a strong upper-back position, and no behind-the-neck pulling.

## Preconditions

- A lat pulldown machine with adjustable thigh pad and appropriate handle.
- Weight selected so you can control the full range.
- Shoulders tolerate overhead reaching without sharp pain.
- Warm up with light pulldowns, shoulder circles, and scapular pulls.

## Steps

1. **Adjust the machine.** Set the thigh pad snug enough to keep you seated without pinning painfully. → *Expect:* thighs stay down when the weight pulls upward.
2. **Choose a grip.** Use a shoulder-width to moderately wide grip with hands even on the bar. [BRANCH: shoulder discomfort, use a neutral-grip handle] → *Expect:* wrists are comfortable and shoulders can reach overhead.
3. **Set the torso.** Sit tall, lean back slightly, brace, and let arms reach overhead without shrugging hard. → *Expect:* ribs stay down and the cable is directly above or slightly in front.
4. **Start with the shoulder blades.** Pull shoulder blades down, then drive elbows toward the sides of the ribs. → *Expect:* the bar moves toward the upper chest, not behind the neck.
5. **Finish at the upper chest.** Stop when the bar reaches the collarbone or upper chest area without craning the neck. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not pull behind the neck or through sharp shoulder pain. → *Expect:* chest stays lifted and shoulders stay away from ears.
6. **Control the return.** Let arms extend overhead while keeping the torso still. → *Expect:* the weight stack does not slam and shoulders remain controlled.

## Decision points

- You lean far back to move the weight → reduce load and keep the torso quiet.
- Bar cannot reach upper chest without pain → shorten range slightly or switch handles.
- Biceps dominate → initiate by pulling shoulder blades down and elbows toward ribs.
- Easy for 3 sets of 12 → increase load slightly or slow the return.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Behind-neck pull:** detect bar traveling behind the head → pull to the upper chest only.
- **F2 Momentum lean:** detect torso swinging backward → lower the weight and pause each rep.
- **F3 Shrugging:** detect shoulders rising to ears → depress shoulder blades before bending elbows.
- **F4 Neck reach:** detect chin jutting to meet the bar → keep head neutral and bring the bar to the chest.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 10 lat pulldowns with thigh pad secure, bar to upper chest, elbows driving down, controlled return, and no torso swinging or shoulder pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use lighter weight or a neutral-grip handle.
- `harder`: use slower eccentrics, pauses at the bottom, or progress toward pull-up training.
- `equipment`: use cable handles, a straight bar, or assisted pull-up machine.
- `at-home`: use a resistance band anchored securely overhead and kneel or sit below it.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk with controlled weight and a front-of-body pull. Stop for sharp shoulder, elbow, wrist, neck, or back pain, and never force behind-the-neck pulldowns. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if overhead pulling aggravates symptoms.
