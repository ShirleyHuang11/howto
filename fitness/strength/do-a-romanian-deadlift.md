## Goal

You perform a Romanian deadlift with a controlled hip hinge, loaded hamstrings, neutral spine, and close weight path.

## Preconditions

- Barbell, dumbbells, or kettlebells light enough to control.
- Flat shoes and clear floor space.
- You can demonstrate `fitness/strength/master-the-hip-hinge` without rounding.
- Warm up with hip hinges, glute bridges, and light RDL sets.

## Steps

1. **Start tall with the weight in hand.** Hold the bar or dumbbells against the thighs, feet hip width, knees softly bent. → *Expect:* weight is balanced over midfoot and the trunk is braced.
2. **Lock in the brace and lats.** Tighten the abdomen and pull shoulders down and back enough to keep the weight close. → *Expect:* the load stays near the body before moving.
3. **Hinge the hips back.** Push hips behind you while the weight slides down the thighs. → *Expect:* shins stay nearly vertical and hamstrings begin to stretch.
4. **Stop at hamstring limit.** Lower until you feel a strong hamstring stretch or the weights reach just below knees to mid-shin. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop before the back rounds or the weight pulls away. → *Expect:* spine remains neutral and the weight is close to the legs.
5. **Stand by driving hips forward.** Squeeze glutes and bring hips back under ribs without leaning back. → *Expect:* hamstrings and glutes lift the weight, not the lower back.
6. **Keep reps smooth.** Do not bounce at the bottom; pause briefly and reverse with control. → *Expect:* each rep has the same depth and tempo.

## Decision points

- You feel low back more than hamstrings → reduce range and rehearse the hinge.
- Knees lock straight → keep a small knee bend throughout.
- Weight drifts forward → engage lats and keep the load against the legs.
- You can do 3 sets of 10 easily → add load gradually or slow the eccentric.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rounded back:** detect loss of neutral spine near the bottom → stop higher and reduce load.
- **F2 Squat-like descent:** detect knees bending forward a lot → push hips back and keep shins quieter.
- **F3 Overstretching:** detect chasing the floor despite form loss → use your hamstring limit as the bottom.
- **F4 Lean-back finish:** detect arching at lockout → finish tall with glutes, ribs down.
- **F5 Loose bar path:** detect weight several inches from legs → pull shoulders into the back pockets.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 8 Romanian deadlifts with a neutral spine, soft knees, close weight path, hamstring stretch at the bottom, and glute-driven lockout.

## Variations

- `easier`: use bodyweight hinges, a dowel, or light dumbbells.
- `harder`: add load gradually, use a 3-second lower, or single-leg RDLs.
- `equipment`: use dumbbells if a barbell forces an awkward path.
- `at-home`: use a loaded backpack or household weights held close to the thighs.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because loaded hinging can strain the back or hamstrings if range or load exceeds control. Stop for sharp back, hamstring, hip, or nerve-like pain, and avoid bouncing from the bottom. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if you have back or hamstring injury history.
