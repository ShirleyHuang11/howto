## Goal

You arrive ready for a group fitness class by preparing joints, breathing, and movement patterns before the instructor increases intensity.

## Preconditions

- Class type known: cycling, HIIT, strength, dance, bootcamp, yoga, or mixed format.
- Comfortable clothing, water, and any required equipment.
- Enough space to move without bumping others.

## Steps

1. **Arrive early and scan the room.** Set up equipment, check floor traction, and choose a spot with enough movement clearance. → *Expect:* you are not rushing during the first drill.
2. **Start with easy pulse-raising movement.** Walk, march, pedal lightly, or step side to side for 3-5 minutes. → *Expect:* breathing warms up but you can still speak easily.
3. **Mobilize the main joints.** Move ankles, hips, thoracic spine, shoulders, and wrists through comfortable ranges based on the class demands. → *Expect:* joints feel smoother without forcing end ranges.
4. **Rehearse class patterns slowly.** Practice bodyweight squats, hinges, lunges, planks, light rows, or easy pedals that match the session. → *Expect:* the first working set or interval feels familiar.
5. **Check technique anchors.** Keep ribs stacked over pelvis, knees tracking over toes, shoulders away from ears, and spine neutral under load. → *Expect:* warmup reps look like easier versions of class reps.
6. **Set your intensity boundary.** Decide the regression you will use if the class moves too fast. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop or modify if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or joint instability. → *Expect:* you know how to scale before fatigue hits.

## Decision points

- Class starts with jumping → warm up calves, ankles, and landing mechanics first.
- Shoulders feel tight → use lighter overhead work or keep arms below pain-free range.
- Instructor pace exceeds form control → reduce speed, load, or range of motion.
- You arrive late → spend the first round at warmup intensity.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Cold start:** detect breathlessness in the first minutes → lower intensity and build gradually.
- **F2 Knee collapse:** detect knees diving inward on squats or jumps → slow down and track knees over toes.
- **F3 Shoulder shrugging:** detect neck tension during pressing → lower load and set shoulder blades.
- **F4 Social overpacing:** detect copying advanced participants despite form loss → choose the regression.

## Verification

Before the class becomes hard, you complete 10 minutes of easy movement, joint mobility, and slow pattern rehearsal with stable knees, neutral spine, controlled breathing, and a chosen regression.

## Variations

- `easier`: extend low-intensity movement and skip jumps during the first block.
- `harder`: add specific activation drills for hips, shoulders, or core if you know your weak points.
- `equipment`: use very light weights or bands only for rehearsal.
- `at-home`: follow the same warmup before a live-stream or recorded class.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Group energy can push intensity too fast; scale movements early and stop for sharp pain, chest pain, faintness, or severe breathlessness. Tell the instructor about relevant limitations if needed. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
