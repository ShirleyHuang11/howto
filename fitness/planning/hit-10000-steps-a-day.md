## Goal

You build a practical walking plan that reaches 10000 steps per day without sudden overuse or schedule failure.

## Preconditions

- Phone, watch, pedometer, or fitness tracker.
- Comfortable walking shoes and safe walking routes.
- Current average daily step count from at least 3 typical days if possible.

## Steps

1. **Measure your baseline.** Track normal steps for 3-7 days before changing behavior. → *Expect:* a realistic average starting point.
2. **Increase gradually.** Add 1000-2000 steps per day each week until 10000 is comfortable. → *Expect:* feet, calves, and knees adapt without sharp soreness.
3. **Break steps into blocks.** Use short walks after meals, commute segments, calls, errands, or a dedicated walk. → *Expect:* no single walk has to carry the whole target.
4. **Use posture and stride cues.** Walk tall, ribs stacked over pelvis, arms swinging naturally, and steps quiet rather than stomping. → *Expect:* effort feels smooth and repeatable.
5. **Protect recovery.** Rotate shoes if needed, vary surfaces, and keep one lower-step day if soreness builds. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp foot, shin, knee, hip, chest pain, or dizziness. → *Expect:* walking increases health without becoming an injury driver.
6. **Review weekly averages.** Aim for a weekly average near 10000 rather than forcing every single day. → *Expect:* busy days are balanced by easier opportunities.

## Decision points

- Baseline is below 4000 → build toward 6000 first, then 8000, then 10000.
- Shin or foot pain appears → reduce steps, avoid hills, check footwear, and resume gradually.
- Schedule blocks walking → use 5-10 minute walks after meals or during calls.
- 10000 is easy → add pace, hills, or zone 2 cardio rather than endless extra steps.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Weekend compensation:** detect very low weekdays and huge painful weekend walks → distribute steps more evenly.
- **F2 Shoe irritation:** detect blisters or arch pain → adjust socks, fit, lacing, or footwear.
- **F3 Tracker mismatch:** detect phone left on a desk → use the same device consistently.
- **F4 Too much too soon:** detect shin splints or aching joints → cut volume by 30-50% for a week and rebuild.

## Verification

You average at least 10000 steps per day across 7 days with no sharp pain, no worsening joint soreness, and at least three days reaching the target through planned walking blocks.

## Variations

- `easier`: start with 6000 or 8000 steps as the daily target.
- `harder`: add brisk 10-minute intervals while maintaining relaxed mechanics.
- `equipment`: a watch is convenient, but a phone pedometer is enough if carried consistently.
- `at-home`: use hallway laps, treadmill walking, step breaks, or indoor walking videos.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for most people, but increase gradually and stop for chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath, or sharp lower-body pain. Location and step data can reveal routines, so review sharing settings. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
