## Goal

You create a consistency system that keeps exercise happening through normal schedule friction, low motivation, and imperfect weeks.

## Preconditions

- A realistic view of your weekly schedule.
- One or two exercise options you can do with available equipment.
- Calendar, reminder app, or paper planner.

## Steps

1. **Define the minimum session.** Choose a version so small it is hard to skip, such as 10 minutes walking or 2 sets of three exercises. → *Expect:* you have a fallback that still counts.
2. **Schedule exercise like a fixed appointment.** Put sessions at specific times and locations, not vague intentions. → *Expect:* the calendar shows when training will happen.
3. **Prepare the environment.** Set out clothes, shoes, water, equipment, or gym bag before the session window. → *Expect:* starting requires fewer decisions.
4. **Use a simple tracking streak.** Mark completed full sessions and minimum sessions without judging them the same as failures. → *Expect:* consistency is visible.
5. **Plan for obstacles.** Write a rule for travel, late work, bad weather, or low energy. → *Expect:* common disruptions have a pre-decided response.
6. **Keep intensity appropriate.** Most sessions should end with controlled form and enough energy to recover. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not use consistency goals to train through illness, sharp pain, or dizziness. → *Expect:* the habit survives because recovery is respected.
7. **Review weekly.** Move session times that repeatedly fail and keep times that work. → *Expect:* the system adapts to real life.

## Decision points

- You miss the same day repeatedly → reschedule that day instead of blaming discipline.
- Motivation is low → do the minimum session.
- Fatigue is high → reduce intensity, not necessarily attendance.
- Pain or illness appears → rest or seek care rather than protecting a streak.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 All-or-nothing thinking:** detect skipped weeks after one missed workout → restart with the next minimum session.
- **F2 Overpacked schedule:** detect sessions always squeezed between conflicts → shorten or move them.
- **F3 Too much soreness:** detect dread from previous hard sessions → reduce volume and leave reps in reserve.
- **F4 Vague goals:** detect "exercise more" without a plan → set exact days, times, and session types.

## Verification

For 4 weeks, you complete at least 80% of planned sessions or minimum-session fallbacks, with no training through sharp pain or illness and at least one weekly schedule review.

## Variations

- `easier`: commit to 10 minutes, 3 days per week.
- `harder`: add performance targets after the attendance habit is stable.
- `equipment`: choose exercises that use equipment you can access reliably.
- `at-home`: keep a visible mat, bands, or dumbbells where sessions happen.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Consistency should support health, not override warning signs. Stop exercise for sharp pain, chest pain, faintness, or unusual breathlessness. Calendar and health logs are private information. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
