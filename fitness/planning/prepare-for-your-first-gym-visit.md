## Goal

You complete a first gym visit calmly, safely, and productively by planning the basics before you enter the facility.

## Preconditions

- Gym membership, day pass, or appointment.
- Comfortable clothes, closed-toe training shoes, water, towel, and lock if needed.
- A simple workout plan with exercises you can perform safely.

## Steps

1. **Review gym logistics.** Check hours, parking or transit, locker rules, check-in process, and whether an orientation is available. → *Expect:* arrival has no avoidable surprises.
2. **Pack only what you need.** Bring water, towel, headphones if desired, lock, ID, and written workout. → *Expect:* your bag supports the session without clutter.
3. **Choose a simple first workout.** Plan 4-6 machine or bodyweight exercises plus easy cardio, avoiding heavy free-weight testing on day one. → *Expect:* the goal is learning the space and moving well.
4. **Walk the floor before training.** Locate bathrooms, exits, water, cleaning stations, cardio area, machines, mats, and staff desk. → *Expect:* you know where to go before you need anything.
5. **Warm up and test settings.** Use 5-8 minutes of easy cardio, then adjust seats, pads, and handles before loading machines. → *Expect:* joints feel ready and machines fit your body.
6. **Use conservative loads.** Start lighter than you think and move through full, controlled range without bouncing or joint pain. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop any set that causes sharp pain, pinching, dizziness, or loss of control. → *Expect:* reps feel stable and repeatable.
7. **Clean up and log the session.** Wipe equipment, re-rack weights, note exercises, settings, loads, and reps. → *Expect:* you can repeat or progress the next visit.

## Decision points

- A machine is confusing → ask staff or choose a simpler alternative.
- The gym is crowded → use available machines that train the same movement pattern.
- You feel anxious → do the warmup and two exercises, then leave with the habit intact.
- Load feels easy → still keep the first visit conservative and progress next time.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 No plan:** detect wandering and decision fatigue → use the written 4-6 exercise list.
- **F2 Machine misfit:** detect awkward joint angles → adjust the seat or switch machines.
- **F3 Starting too heavy:** detect shaking, shortened reps, or breath-holding → reduce load immediately.
- **F4 Poor gym etiquette:** detect weights left out or equipment occupied too long → re-rack, share politely, and clean surfaces.

## Verification

You finish a first gym visit with a warmup, 4-6 controlled exercises or equivalent alternatives, no sharp pain, equipment cleaned and returned, and session details logged for next time.

## Variations

- `easier`: take a tour only, then do 10 minutes of cardio and two machines.
- `harder`: book an orientation and learn rack, cable, and free-weight setup with staff guidance.
- `equipment`: machines are useful for a first visit because setup is stable and loads are adjustable.
- `at-home`: rehearse the planned movements before going so the patterns feel familiar.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when loads are conservative. Avoid maximal lifts, unfamiliar heavy equipment, and training through sharp pain. Keep valuables locked and avoid filming others without consent. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
