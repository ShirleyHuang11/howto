## Goal

You use a sturdy chair for a low-barrier workout that trains legs, pressing, posture, and core control.

## Preconditions

- A stable chair without wheels.
- Clear space around the chair and a non-slip floor.
- Ability to stand from the chair safely, or a nearby support for balance.

## Steps

1. **Secure the chair.** Place it against a wall if needed and test that it does not slide. → *Expect:* the chair stays still when you press down and sit.
2. **Warm up seated and standing.** Do seated marches, shoulder rolls, ankle circles, and 5 easy sit-to-stands. → *Expect:* hips, knees, and shoulders feel ready.
3. **Do sit-to-stands.** Cross arms or reach forward, stand by pushing through the whole foot, then sit with control for 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and you do not drop into the chair.
4. **Do chair incline push-ups.** Put hands on the seat or back only if stable, keep body straight, and bend elbows under control. → *Expect:* chest moves toward the chair while shoulders stay away from ears.
5. **Do supported reverse lunges or step-backs.** Hold the chair lightly and step one foot back, bending both knees comfortably. → *Expect:* the chair helps balance without carrying your full weight.
6. **Do seated knee lifts.** Sit tall, brace lightly, and lift one knee at a time without leaning back. → *Expect:* lower abs and hip flexors work while posture stays tall.
7. **Finish with calf raises.** Hold the chair back, rise onto the balls of the feet, pause, and lower slowly. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not use a rolling or folding chair for loaded movements. → *Expect:* ankles move straight and balance stays steady.

## Decision points

- Standing from the chair is difficult → use hands on thighs or a higher seat.
- Chair moves → reposition against a wall or choose floor-only exercises.
- Shoulder discomfort during push-ups → raise hand position to a wall.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Dropping into the seat:** detect uncontrolled sitting → slow the descent and use a higher chair.
- **F2 Pulling on the chair:** detect chair tipping during lunges → hold a wall instead.
- **F3 Rounded seated posture:** detect slumping during knee lifts → sit closer to the front edge and reset ribs over hips.
- **F4 Knee cave:** detect knees moving inward when standing → press knees gently toward toes.

## Verification

Complete 2-3 rounds of sit-to-stands, chair incline push-ups, supported step-backs, seated knee lifts, and calf raises with a stable chair, controlled tempo, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: reduce range of motion and use hands for assistance.
- `harder`: add slow lowering, pause above the chair, or hold light weights.
- `equipment`: add a resistance band around thighs for sit-to-stands if knee tracking is solid.
- `at-home`: use a dining chair against a wall, not a wheeled office chair.

## Safety & privacy

Use only a stable chair on a non-slip surface. Stop for dizziness, sharp joint pain, chest pain, or balance loss. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or physical therapist if you have fall risk, recent surgery, or medical limitations.
