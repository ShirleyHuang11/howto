## Goal

You break up sitting with a short routine that restores posture, moves major joints, and adds light strength work without needing workout clothes.

## Preconditions

- Stable chair and a small clear area near the desk.
- Shoes or floor surface that will not slip.
- Enough privacy and space to move arms and step back safely.

## Steps

1. **Stand and reset posture.** Place feet hip-width, unlock knees, and stack ribs over pelvis. → *Expect:* hips and low back feel less compressed.
2. **Breathe and reach.** Take 3 slow breaths while reaching arms overhead without flaring ribs. → *Expect:* upper back and sides expand.
3. **Do chair sit-to-stands.** Stand and sit under control for 8-12 reps. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and you do not drop into the chair.
4. **Do desk incline push-ups.** Use a stable desk edge, keep body straight, and lower chest toward the desk for 6-12 reps. → *Expect:* shoulders stay away from ears.
5. **Do hip hinges.** Place hands on hips, push hips back, and stand tall for 10 reps. → *Expect:* hamstrings stretch and low back stays neutral.
6. **Do standing calf raises.** Hold the desk lightly and rise onto the balls of the feet for 12-20 reps. → *Expect:* ankles move straight and posture stays tall.
7. **Do neck and shoulder resets.** Gently tuck chin, squeeze shoulder blades lightly, and relax. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if any movement causes sharp pain, dizziness, or tingling. → *Expect:* neck feels easier, not forced.

## Decision points

- Desk is unstable → use wall push-ups instead.
- Work clothes restrict movement → shorten range and avoid lunges.
- You feel lightheaded after standing → sit down and resume only when normal.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rushing between meetings:** detect sloppy reps → do fewer reps with better control.
- **F2 Locked knees:** detect back tension while standing → soften knees and shift weight evenly.
- **F3 Shoulder shrugging:** detect neck tension on push-ups → lower shoulders and use the wall.
- **F4 Overstretching neck:** detect pulling or tingling → use gentle range only and stop symptoms.

## Verification

Complete a 10-minute break with breathing reaches, sit-to-stands, incline push-ups, hip hinges, calf raises, and gentle neck/shoulder resets while staying pain-free and work-ready.

## Variations

- `easier`: do seated marches, wall push-ups, and partial sit-to-stands.
- `harder`: add reverse lunges, longer wall sits, or an extra round.
- `equipment`: use a resistance band kept at the desk for pull-aparts or rows.
- `at-home`: use the same routine between long sitting blocks.

## Safety & privacy

Move within your workspace limits and avoid blocking walkways. Stop for dizziness, chest pain, sharp pain, numbness, or tingling. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or ergonomics professional if symptoms persist.
