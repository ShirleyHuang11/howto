## Goal

You perform burpees with controlled landings, a stable plank, and a pace that challenges conditioning without turning sloppy.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space with enough room to step or jump back.
- Ability to squat to a comfortable depth and hold a high plank.
- Warm up ankles, hips, wrists, shoulders, and trunk first.

## Steps

1. **Start tall and brace.** Stand with feet about hip-width, ribs stacked over pelvis, and eyes forward. → *Expect:* you feel balanced over the middle of both feet.
2. **Squat and place hands down.** Hinge and bend knees, placing hands shoulder-width on the floor without collapsing the chest. → *Expect:* hands land flat and knees track over toes.
3. **Move to plank.** [BRANCH: lower impact, step one foot back at a time | higher impact, jump both feet back together] → *Expect:* you arrive in a straight high plank without the low back sagging.
4. **Add the push-up only if clean.** Lower chest toward the floor with elbows angled back, or skip the push-up for conditioning. → *Expect:* shoulders and hips rise together if you press up.
5. **Return feet under hips.** Step or jump feet forward outside or under the hands, landing quietly. → *Expect:* heels or full feet touch down and knees do not cave inward.
6. **Stand or jump.** Stand tall, or add a small vertical jump with soft knees. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop jumping if landings become loud, stiff, or painful. → *Expect:* you finish balanced, breathing hard but controlled.
7. **Repeat at sustainable pace.** Do 3-5 sets of 5-10 reps or 20-45 second intervals. → *Expect:* the final rep still has a stable plank and quiet landing.

## Decision points

- Plank collapses → step back and remove the push-up.
- Knees or ankles hurt on the jump → stand tall instead of jumping.
- Conditioning fails before form → use fewer reps per set and more rest.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Worming the push-up:** detect hips peeling up after the chest → skip the push-up or use an incline.
- **F2 Hard landings:** detect loud feet or locked knees → reduce jump height or step instead.
- **F3 Rounded squat setup:** detect hands reaching from a folded spine → bend knees more and keep chest proud.
- **F4 Rushing breath:** detect gasping after a few reps → slow cadence and exhale when standing.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 8 burpees with hands planted securely, a straight plank, feet returning under control, soft landings, and no sharp wrist, back, knee, or ankle pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: do step-back burpees without a push-up or jump.
- `harder`: add a strict push-up, higher jump, or longer interval.
- `equipment`: use an elevated surface for hands to reduce wrist and trunk demand.
- `at-home`: perform on a mat but keep hands and feet from sliding.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for chest pain, dizziness, sharp joint pain, or loss of landing control. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have cardiovascular, joint, pregnancy-related, or spine concerns.
