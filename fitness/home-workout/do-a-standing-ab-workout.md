## Goal

You train abdominal control while standing, using bracing, rotation control, and balance without floor exercises.

## Preconditions

- Clear standing space and a wall or chair for balance if needed.
- Optional resistance band or light weight.
- Warm up with marching, torso turns, side bends, and shoulder circles.

## Steps

1. **Set tall posture.** Stand with feet hip-width, ribs over pelvis, and knees soft. → *Expect:* abs can brace without locking the knees.
2. **Do standing knee drives.** Lift one knee toward hip height while exhaling and keeping torso tall. → *Expect:* lower abs work without leaning backward.
3. **Do cross-body knee-to-elbow.** Bring opposite elbow and knee toward each other with controlled trunk rotation. → *Expect:* rotation comes through the upper trunk, not a collapsed low back.
4. **Do standing side crunches.** Slide ribs toward hip on one side, then return tall for 8-12 reps each side. → *Expect:* side waist contracts without yanking the neck.
5. **Do anti-rotation holds.** [BRANCH: band available, hold a Pallof press | no band, press palms together hard in front of chest while marching slowly] → *Expect:* trunk resists twisting.
6. **Add balance marches.** Lift knees slowly and pause for 1-2 seconds each side. ⚠️ *Safety:* use support if balance wobbles or the floor is slippery. → *Expect:* hips stay level and feet land quietly.
7. **Complete 2-3 rounds.** Rest 30-45 seconds between drills. → *Expect:* abs feel worked while back and hips remain comfortable.

## Decision points

- Balance is the limiter → hold a chair and reduce knee height.
- Low back feels pinched → reduce rotation and reset ribs over pelvis.
- Too easy → slow each rep, pause longer, or add band resistance.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Leaning backward:** detect ribs flaring on knee drives → lower the knee and exhale before lifting.
- **F2 Neck pulling:** detect head jutting toward elbow → keep hands light and move from the trunk.
- **F3 Locked knees:** detect rigid legs and back tension → soften knees and spread weight through feet.
- **F4 Fast twisting:** detect momentum swinging arms → slow down and keep hips mostly forward.

## Verification

Complete 2-3 rounds of knee drives, cross-body crunches, side crunches, anti-rotation holds, and balance marches with tall posture, controlled breathing, and no back or hip pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: keep toes touching the floor during knee drives.
- `harder`: use a band Pallof press, slower tempo, or longer single-leg pauses.
- `equipment`: hold a light dumbbell at the chest for marches only if posture stays stacked.
- `at-home`: perform beside a wall for balance without needing floor space.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for dizziness, sharp back or hip pain, or balance loss. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or qualified professional if you have spine, vestibular, pregnancy-related, or balance concerns.
