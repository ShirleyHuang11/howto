## Goal

You complete a video-led workout while matching the coach's intent, scaling movements to your current ability, and avoiding rushed or painful reps.

## Preconditions

- A reputable workout video appropriate to your level and space.
- Enough floor area to step, hinge, lunge, and raise your arms without hitting furniture.
- Water, supportive shoes if the workout includes impact, and a warmup included or done beforehand.

## Steps

1. **Preview the workout structure.** Check duration, equipment, impact level, and any movements you cannot safely do. → *Expect:* you know the hard sections before the video starts.
2. **Set the screen and space.** Place the device where you can see it without twisting your neck and clear the floor. → *Expect:* you can glance at the screen while keeping normal posture.
3. **Start with the warmup.** Move through the first 3-5 minutes at lower intensity, using full but comfortable joint range. → *Expect:* breathing and body temperature rise gradually.
4. **Match form before tempo.** Follow the exercise pattern, then choose a speed that lets you control alignment. [BRANCH: easier, reduce range or remove jumping | harder, add range or resistance only if timing and posture stay clean] → *Expect:* reps look consistent even if you are slower than the instructor.
5. **Use planned modifications.** Swap high-impact moves for step-outs, reduce plank time, or use bodyweight instead of weights when form slips. → *Expect:* effort stays challenging without joint pain or breath panic.
6. **Pause when needed.** Take 30-60 seconds if technique breaks, breathing becomes uncontrolled, or you miss instructions. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not chase the video pace through sharp pain, dizziness, or poor landings. → *Expect:* you rejoin with better control.
7. **Complete the cooldown.** Follow the slower breathing, walking, and stretching segment instead of stopping abruptly. → *Expect:* heart rate drops and muscles feel warm rather than cramped.

## Decision points

- Instructor uses a movement you do not know → pause, watch one round, then restart with a simpler version.
- Impact bothers knees, hips, or ankles → switch jumps to step-outs immediately.
- Video feels too easy → choose a harder video next time or add light resistance, not random extra reps midstream.
- Video feels too hard → finish with modifications and pick a beginner session next time.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Neck craning:** detect constant twisting toward the screen → move the device higher or in front of you.
- **F2 Copying speed without control:** detect sloppy landings or partial reps → slow down and follow the movement quality first.
- **F3 Ignoring modifications:** detect pain during jumps, planks, or deep lunges → use the regression shown or substitute a pain-free pattern.
- **F4 Skipping warmup:** detect stiffness and sudden breathlessness → restart with a proper warmup before the main block.

## Verification

You finished the selected video or a scaled version of it, used modifications when needed, kept controlled landings and neutral joint alignment, and ended without sharp pain, dizziness, or uncontrolled breathing.

## Variations

- easier: choose beginner, low-impact, no-equipment videos.
- harder: choose intermediate videos or add light dumbbells only for movements you already perform well.
- equipment: use a mat, chair, or resistance band if the instructor programs them.
- at-home: mute jumping by stepping instead of hopping and using carpet or a mat.

## Safety & privacy

Online workouts vary widely in quality. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or numbness, and avoid giving apps unnecessary health, location, or camera permissions. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
