## Goal

You complete a short full-body workout that raises heart rate and trains major movement patterns without equipment.

## Preconditions

- Clear space about the size of a yoga mat.
- Timer or clock.
- Comfortable clothing and a quick 1-minute warmup of marching, arm circles, and easy squats.

## Steps

1. **Set the interval.** Use 40 seconds work and 20 seconds transition for 10 rounds. → *Expect:* the timer gives clear starts and stops.
2. **Do squats.** Sit hips back and down, stand tall, and keep knees tracking over toes. → *Expect:* legs work evenly and heels stay down.
3. **Do incline or floor push-ups.** Keep body straight and elbows angled back, using a counter if needed. → *Expect:* chest and arms work without shoulder pinching.
4. **Do reverse lunges.** Alternate legs, stepping back softly and pushing through the front foot. → *Expect:* balance stays controlled and front knee tracks cleanly.
5. **Do plank shoulder taps.** From high plank, tap opposite shoulder while resisting hip sway. → *Expect:* hips stay mostly level.
6. **Do low-impact cardio.** March fast, step jacks, or mountain climber steps. → *Expect:* heart rate rises while breathing remains controlled.
7. **Repeat the circuit once.** Perform the same five drills again with cleaner reps than faster reps. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop or slow down if pain or dizziness appears. → *Expect:* fatigue rises but form remains recognizable.
8. **Cool down briefly.** Walk slowly and breathe through the nose or relaxed mouth for 1 minute. → *Expect:* heart rate begins to settle.

## Decision points

- Push-ups break down → elevate hands higher.
- Lunges feel unstable → switch to split squats or supported step-backs.
- You cannot speak a short phrase → reduce pace until breathing is under control.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Racing the timer:** detect partial reps and poor positions → slow down and finish fewer clean reps.
- **F2 Knee collapse:** detect knees moving inward on squats or lunges → reduce depth and track over toes.
- **F3 Plank hip sway:** detect hips rocking side to side → widen feet or hold a regular plank.
- **F4 No warmup:** detect stiff joints in round one → spend another minute marching and mobilizing.

## Verification

Complete 10 minutes using 40:20 intervals with two rounds of squats, push-ups, reverse lunges, shoulder taps, and low-impact cardio while maintaining joint alignment and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use 30 seconds work and 30 seconds rest.
- `harder`: use 45 seconds work, add squat jumps, or use full mountain climbers.
- `equipment`: add a resistance band row between lower-body moves if available.
- `at-home`: choose quiet step jacks instead of jumping in shared housing.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for chest pain, faintness, sharp joint pain, or unusual shortness of breath. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have cardiovascular, respiratory, pregnancy-related, or orthopedic concerns.
