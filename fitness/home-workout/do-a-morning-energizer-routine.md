## Goal

You use a short morning routine to raise alertness, mobilize stiff joints, and start the day without maximal effort.

## Preconditions

- A clear floor area near the bed or in another safe room.
- Comfortable clothes and optional mat.
- You are awake enough to stand steadily; drink water first if you feel dehydrated.

## Steps

1. **Stand and breathe tall.** Place feet hip-width, soften the knees, inhale through the nose, and exhale slowly for 5 breaths. → *Expect:* posture feels upright and breathing steadies.
2. **Mobilize the neck and shoulders.** Make slow shoulder rolls and gentle neck turns without forcing end range. → *Expect:* motion feels smooth, not pinchy.
3. **Open the hips with marching.** March in place for 60 seconds, lifting knees only as high as the pelvis stays level. → *Expect:* balance improves and hip flexors feel active.
4. **Do bodyweight good mornings.** Hands on hips, hinge back with a long spine, then squeeze glutes to stand. → *Expect:* stretch in the hamstrings and no low-back rounding.
5. **Do easy squats.** Sit back to a comfortable depth, keep heels down, and stand smoothly. [BRANCH: easier, squat to a chair | harder, add a 2-second pause at the bottom] → *Expect:* knees track over toes and breathing remains controlled.
6. **Do incline push-ups or wall presses.** Keep the body straight and bend the elbows about 30-45 degrees from the ribs. → *Expect:* chest and arms work without shoulder pinching.
7. **Finish with a brisk reset.** Step side to side or march for 90 seconds, then take 3 slow breaths. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if you feel dizzy, nauseated, or unusually short of breath. → *Expect:* you feel warmer and more awake, not exhausted.

## Decision points

- Morning stiffness is high → reduce range and move more slowly.
- You feel lightheaded after standing → sit down, hydrate, and skip the routine until steady.
- A joint pinches repeatedly → replace that drill with a pain-free mobility motion.
- Routine feels too easy → add one extra round, keeping effort moderate.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Moving too fast immediately:** detect stumbling or breath holding → slow the first two minutes and use smaller ranges.
- **F2 Rounded hinge:** detect low-back flexion during good mornings → bend knees slightly and stop the hinge earlier.
- **F3 Knees collapse in squats:** detect knees drifting inward → reduce depth and press knees over the middle toes.
- **F4 Shoulder discomfort:** detect pinching during presses → move hands higher on the wall and keep elbows closer.

## Verification

You completed 8-10 minutes of breathing, mobility, squats, hinge work, pressing, and light marching with steady balance, controlled breathing, and no sharp pain or dizziness.

## Variations

- easier: do all movements seated or supported by a wall.
- harder: complete two rounds or add light resistance bands.
- equipment: use a chair for supported squats and incline push-ups.
- at-home: keep the routine beside the bed with a mat ready.

## Safety & privacy

Morning movement should feel restorative, not like a max workout. Stop for dizziness, chest pain, sharp joint pain, or unusual shortness of breath. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
