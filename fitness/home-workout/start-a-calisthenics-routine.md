## Goal

You start a sustainable calisthenics routine with balanced pushing, pulling, squatting, hinging, and core work.

## Preconditions

- Floor space and optional pull-up bar, rings, suspension trainer, or resistance band.
- Ability to perform basic bodyweight movements pain-free at an appropriate regression.
- Warm up wrists, shoulders, hips, ankles, and trunk before training.

## Steps

1. **Pick a repeatable schedule.** Train 2-3 nonconsecutive days per week for 30-45 minutes. → *Expect:* recovery days separate hard sessions.
2. **Choose one push.** [BRANCH: beginner, wall or incline push-up | stronger trainee, floor push-up or dip progression] → *Expect:* body stays straight and shoulders feel stable.
3. **Choose one pull.** Use rows, band rows, assisted pull-ups, or negatives. → *Expect:* shoulder blades move under control and elbows track near the body.
4. **Choose one squat or lunge.** Use air squats, split squats, reverse lunges, or step-ups. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and feet stay planted.
5. **Choose one hinge or posterior-chain drill.** Use glute bridges, single-leg hinges, or hamstring walkouts. → *Expect:* glutes and hamstrings work without low-back rounding.
6. **Choose one core drill.** Use dead bugs, hollow holds, side planks, or bird dogs. → *Expect:* trunk resists movement while breathing continues.
7. **Dose the work conservatively.** Do 2-4 sets of 6-12 reps or 15-45 second holds, stopping 1-2 reps before failure. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not force advanced skills before joints tolerate the basics. → *Expect:* form remains consistent across sets.
8. **Progress one variable at a time.** Add reps, sets, range, or harder leverage only after two clean sessions. → *Expect:* progress feels gradual and joints recover between workouts.

## Decision points

- No pull-up bar → prioritize inverted rows, band rows, or towel rows.
- Wrists hurt → use handles, fists, or elevated hands and add wrist prep.
- Progress stalls → increase total weekly volume slightly or improve sleep and recovery.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Only training push-ups:** detect no pulling work in the week → add rows or assisted pulls.
- **F2 Jumping progressions:** detect form breaking when leverage gets harder → return to the last clean variation.
- **F3 Joint irritation:** detect elbow, wrist, or shoulder ache after sessions → reduce volume and check technique.
- **F4 No measurable plan:** detect random workouts → log sets, reps, hold times, and variation used.

## Verification

Complete two weeks of 2-3 sessions including a push, pull, squat/lunge, hinge, and core drill each session, with logged performance and no persistent joint pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use wall, incline, assisted, and shorter-range variations.
- `harder`: progress toward pull-ups, dips, pistol squat regressions, and handstand work gradually.
- `equipment`: rings and bands expand options but require secure anchors.
- `at-home`: build around push-ups, rows under a sturdy table, split squats, bridges, and planks.

## Safety & privacy

Respect tendon and joint recovery; soreness is acceptable, sharp pain is not. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or qualified professional before starting if you have relevant injuries, medical conditions, or are returning after a long layoff.
