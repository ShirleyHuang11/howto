## Goal

You complete a full-body bodyweight circuit that trains legs, pushing strength, trunk control, and conditioning while preserving good form under fatigue.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- Clear floor space, water, and optional timer.
- Ability to squat, hinge, plank, and get up from the floor safely.

## Steps

1. **Set the circuit format.** Choose 4-6 exercises and plan 2-4 rounds with 30-45 seconds work and 15-45 seconds rest. → *Expect:* the session has a defined start, workload, and finish.
2. **Start with squats.** Perform bodyweight squats or chair squats, sitting hips down and back while knees track over toes. → *Expect:* heels stay down and reps remain quiet and controlled.
3. **Add push-ups.** [BRANCH: easier, wall or incline push-ups | standard, knee or full push-ups] keep ribs down and elbows angled about 30-60 degrees from the torso. → *Expect:* chest lowers as one unit with no shoulder pinching.
4. **Train the posterior chain.** Do glute bridges or bodyweight hip hinges, squeezing glutes at the top. → *Expect:* hips extend without low-back cramping.
5. **Add core control.** Perform dead bugs, plank, or side plank with slow breathing and no sagging. → *Expect:* trunk stays steady while limbs move or time passes.
6. **Finish each round with conditioning.** Use marching, step jacks, mountain climbers, or squat-to-reach reps at a sustainable pace. → *Expect:* breathing rises but you can still control landings and alignment.
7. **Rest before form fails.** Take enough rest to keep the next round clean. → *Expect:* later rounds look similar to early rounds, just harder.
8. **Stop unsafe fatigue.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, wheezing that does not settle, or form collapse you cannot correct. → *Expect:* fatigue stays muscular and manageable.

## Decision points

- You cannot finish a round cleanly -> reduce work time or choose easier variations.
- You finish with plenty left -> add one round or shorten rest next time.
- A movement hurts -> swap it for another pattern with no pain.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Squat knees cave:** detect knees drifting inward as you tire -> slow down and press knees over toes.
- **F2 Push-up sag:** detect hips dropping before chest -> elevate hands or use knees.
- **F3 Bridge from low back:** detect back tightness instead of glutes -> reset ribs down and shorten range.
- **F4 Conditioning turns sloppy:** detect loud landings or breath panic -> switch to marching and extend rest.

## Verification

You complete 2-4 rounds of a bodyweight circuit with clean squat alignment, scaled push-ups, controlled core work, sustainable breathing, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- easier: use 20-30 seconds work, longer rest, chair squats, wall push-ups, and dead bugs.
- harder: use 45-60 seconds work, split squats, full push-ups, side planks, and mountain climbers.
- equipment: add a timer, mat, chair, or backpack for progression.
- at-home: choose low-impact conditioning when noise or space is limited.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when fatigue is managed. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest symptoms, unusual shortness of breath, or loss of coordination. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
