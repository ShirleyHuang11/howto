## Goal

You build a quiet, space-efficient workout that fits a small apartment without jumping, equipment clutter, or unsafe furniture use.

## Preconditions

- A clear rectangle of floor roughly the size of a mat.
- Non-slip footwear or mat.
- Timer and optional resistance band.

## Steps

1. **Define the training zone.** Move objects, check overhead clearance, and keep a water glass away from the floor area. → *Expect:* you can step forward, back, and sideways without hitting furniture.
2. **Warm up without impact.** March, reach overhead, hinge at hips, and do gentle side steps for 3 minutes. → *Expect:* breathing rises slightly and joints feel loose.
3. **Use vertical strength moves.** Do squats, wall sits, calf raises, and split squats near a wall for balance. → *Expect:* legs work hard without noise or travel.
4. **Use floor strength moves.** Do incline push-ups on a stable counter, dead bugs, side planks, or glute bridges. → *Expect:* trunk stays controlled and the floor does not slide.
5. **Use quiet cardio intervals.** Alternate fast marching, step jacks, shadow boxing, and low mountain climber steps. → *Expect:* heart rate rises without thudding landings.
6. **Control volume.** Work for 30-45 seconds, rest 15-30 seconds, and repeat 2-4 rounds. → *Expect:* effort builds while neighbors would hear little more than footsteps.
7. **Cool down in place.** Walk slowly, breathe, and stretch calves, hips, and chest. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if tight space makes you compensate or trip. → *Expect:* you finish calm and clear of obstacles.

## Decision points

- Floor noise is high → remove running, jumping, and fast foot switches.
- Space is too narrow for lunges → use split squats or wall sits.
- Motivation drops from repetition → rotate exercises but keep the same movement categories.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hitting furniture:** detect clipped hands or feet → shrink the movement or clear more space.
- **F2 Slippery mat:** detect mat bunching or sliding → remove it or use a grippier surface.
- **F3 Too much impact:** detect floor thuds → switch to step-based cardio.
- **F4 Poor ventilation:** detect overheating quickly → open a window, use a fan, or shorten intervals.

## Verification

Complete a 20-minute low-impact session in a mat-sized space with squats or wall sits, push-ups, core work, quiet cardio, and no collisions, slips, or sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use 20 seconds work and 40 seconds rest.
- `harder`: use slow tempos, longer holds, or resistance bands.
- `equipment`: keep one band, one kettlebell, or adjustable dumbbells if storage allows.
- `at-home`: choose standing drills when floor space is temporarily blocked.

## Safety & privacy

Keep exits clear and avoid moves that require blind backward steps in tight rooms. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or tripping risk. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have relevant health concerns.
