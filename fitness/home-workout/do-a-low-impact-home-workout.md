## Goal

You complete a joint-friendly home workout that builds strength and conditioning without jumping or running.

## Preconditions

- Clear space, stable chair or wall, and non-slip shoes.
- Timer and optional light weights or resistance band.
- Warm up with easy walking, shoulder circles, hip hinges, and ankle mobility.

## Steps

1. **Set a moderate pace.** Use intervals of 40 seconds work and 20 seconds rest, or 10-12 controlled reps. → *Expect:* effort is steady, not frantic.
2. **Do sit-to-stands or squats.** Stand from a chair or squat to comfortable depth. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and breathing stays controlled.
3. **Do wall or incline push-ups.** Keep a straight line from head to heels and lower the chest under control. → *Expect:* arms and chest work without shoulder pain.
4. **Do step-back lunges or supported split squats.** Keep steps quiet and hold a wall if balance is limited. → *Expect:* front foot stays planted and hips stay level.
5. **Do band rows or towel rows.** Pull elbows toward ribs and keep shoulders away from ears. → *Expect:* upper back works more than neck.
6. **Do standing marches.** Drive opposite arm and knee with tall posture. → *Expect:* heart rate rises without impact.
7. **Finish with core control.** Do dead bugs, bird dogs, or standing Pallof presses. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if a low-impact version still causes sharp pain. → *Expect:* trunk resists movement while breathing continues.

## Decision points

- Knees dislike lunges → use glute bridges or shallow split squats.
- Wrists dislike floor work → use wall push-ups and standing core work.
- Heart rate stays too low → increase arm drive, tempo, or interval length without adding jumps.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Turning low-impact into low-quality:** detect rushed partial reps → slow down and use full control.
- **F2 Knee irritation:** detect pain during squat or lunge depth → reduce range and sit back more.
- **F3 Shoulder shrugging:** detect neck tension during rows or push-ups → lower shoulders and reduce resistance.
- **F4 Breathlessness:** detect inability to speak a few words → extend rest or use marching only.

## Verification

Complete 20-25 minutes including squat, push, lunge or bridge, pull, march, and core drills with no jumping, controlled breathing, aligned joints, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use chair support, shorter intervals, and fewer rounds.
- `harder`: add light weights, resistance bands, or slower eccentrics.
- `equipment`: use a backpack, dumbbells, or band for added resistance.
- `at-home`: keep all cardio step-based for apartments or sensitive joints.

## Safety & privacy

Low impact does not mean risk-free. Stop for chest pain, dizziness, sharp joint pain, swelling, or unusual shortness of breath. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have relevant medical conditions.
