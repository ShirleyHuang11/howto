## Goal

You perform pike push-ups to train shoulders and triceps with controlled overhead pressing mechanics.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space and a mat if needed.
- Comfortable wrists and shoulders in a downward-dog-like position.
- Warm up wrists, shoulders, upper back, and hamstrings.

## Steps

1. **Set the pike position.** Place hands shoulder-width, lift hips high, and walk feet in until torso angles toward vertical. → *Expect:* arms line up with ears and weight shifts into hands.
2. **Spread the floor.** Press through fingertips and knuckles, rotate elbow creases slightly forward, and keep shoulders elevated. → *Expect:* hands feel rooted and neck has space.
3. **Lower the head forward.** Bend elbows back at an angle and aim the crown of the head slightly in front of the hands, not between the wrists. → *Expect:* forearms stay near vertical and shoulders load evenly.
4. **Press back to pike.** Push the floor away and return hips high without collapsing the ribs. → *Expect:* elbows straighten and shoulders finish active.
5. **Control range.** [BRANCH: easier, lower partway or use hands on a step | harder, elevate feet] → *Expect:* every rep uses the same path.
6. **Do quality sets.** Perform 3-5 sets of 4-10 reps, resting 60-120 seconds. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if shoulder pinching, wrist pain, or head pressure appears. → *Expect:* shoulders fatigue before joints hurt.

## Decision points

- Hamstrings limit position → bend knees and keep hips high.
- Wrists hurt → use push-up handles or elevate hands.
- Head path drifts between hands → slow down and aim slightly forward.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Turning it into a regular push-up:** detect hips dropping low → walk feet in and lift hips.
- **F2 Elbows flaring wide:** detect elbows pointing straight out → angle elbows back and grip the floor.
- **F3 Collapsed shoulders:** detect shrugging down into ears at the bottom → push tall at the top and reduce depth.
- **F4 Bouncing off the head:** detect contact with the floor → stop short or use a cushion as a depth target, not a rebound.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 6 pike push-ups with hips high, head moving slightly forward of hands, controlled lowering, active shoulders, and no wrist, neck, or shoulder pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: incline pike push-ups with hands elevated.
- `harder`: feet-elevated pike push-ups or deficit reps with stable blocks.
- `equipment`: use parallettes for neutral wrists if you can control the depth.
- `at-home`: use a wall-facing pike position near open space, not near furniture.

## Safety & privacy

Avoid crashing onto the head or forcing shoulder range. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, dizziness, or head pressure. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if you have wrist, shoulder, neck, or blood pressure concerns.
