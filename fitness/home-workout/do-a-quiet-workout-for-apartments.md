## Goal

You complete a strength and conditioning workout in an apartment without jumping, stomping, or dropping equipment.

## Preconditions

- A mat or carpeted space large enough for lunges, planks, and floor work.
- Optional light dumbbells or a backpack.
- A short warmup of marching in place, hip hinges, shoulder circles, and bodyweight squats.

## Steps

1. **Set a quiet base.** Put a mat down and choose soft-soled shoes or bare feet if the floor is safe. → *Expect:* footfalls sound muted during marching.
2. **Warm up with low-impact movement.** March, step side to side, and do gentle good mornings for 4 minutes. → *Expect:* joints feel warmer without bouncing.
3. **Do slow squats.** Stand hip- to shoulder-width, sit the hips back and down, then stand by pressing through the whole foot. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and feet stay quiet on the floor.
4. **Do reverse lunges.** Step one foot back softly, lower until both knees bend comfortably, then drive through the front foot to stand. [BRANCH: easier, hold a chair and reduce depth | harder, add a slow 3-second lower] → *Expect:* the front heel stays down and the back foot lands silently.
5. **Do incline or floor push-ups.** Keep ribs down, hands under shoulders, and lower as one unit. → *Expect:* chest, hips, and thighs move together without sagging.
6. **Do dead bugs.** Lie on your back, brace lightly, lower opposite arm and leg, then return without arching the low back. → *Expect:* the trunk stays still while limbs move.
7. **Finish with a quiet conditioning circuit.** Cycle through step jacks, slow mountain climbers, and shadow boxing for 30 seconds each. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if wrist, shoulder, knee, or back pain becomes sharp. → *Expect:* breathing rises while the floor impact stays low.

## Decision points

- You hear stomping → slow the lowering phase and switch to smaller steps.
- Knees hurt in lunges → use split squats holding support or replace with glute bridges.
- Wrists hurt in plank work → elevate hands on a sturdy surface or use forearms.
- The circuit is too easy → increase time under tension before adding weight.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bouncy reps:** detect heels popping or loud foot contacts → slow down and keep pressure through the full foot.
- **F2 Low-back arch in core work:** detect ribs flaring upward → exhale, brace gently, and shorten the leg reach.
- **F3 Shoulder shrugging in push-ups:** detect neck tension → spread the shoulder blades slightly and keep the neck long.
- **F4 Furniture collisions:** detect shortened, awkward movement paths → clear more space or choose stationary alternatives.

## Verification

You completed 20-25 minutes of low-impact squats, reverse lunges, push-ups, core work, and quiet conditioning with soft foot contacts, stable trunk position, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- easier: use chair-supported squats, wall push-ups, and marching.
- harder: add tempo, pauses, or a loaded backpack.
- equipment: use bands or light dumbbells instead of jumping drills.
- at-home: train on a mat during reasonable hours and avoid dropping weights.

## Safety & privacy

Quiet workouts can still strain joints if fatigue ruins control. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or numbness, and keep enough space to avoid falls. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
