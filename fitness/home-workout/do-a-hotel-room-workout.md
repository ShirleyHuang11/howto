## Goal

You complete a quiet, equipment-light hotel room workout that trains the whole body without relying on a gym.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space away from furniture corners.
- Stable chair, desk, or bed only if it does not slide.
- Shoes if the floor is slippery; towel or mat for floor work.

## Steps

1. **Clear and test the space.** Move luggage aside and press on any chair or desk you might use. → *Expect:* nothing shifts when loaded lightly.
2. **Warm up quietly.** March in place, do arm circles, hip hinges, and ankle rocks for 3 minutes. → *Expect:* joints feel warmer without bothering neighbors.
3. **Do chair or air squats.** Sit back toward a chair or squat freely for 2-3 sets of 10-15 reps. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and feet stay planted.
4. **Do incline push-ups.** Place hands on a stable desk or bed frame, keep body straight, and lower under control for 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps. → *Expect:* chest approaches the surface without shoulder shrugging.
5. **Do reverse lunges.** Step back softly for 2 sets of 6-10 each side, using a wall for balance if needed. → *Expect:* footsteps are quiet and front knee stays stable.
6. **Do suitcase rows if safe.** [BRANCH: sturdy bag, hinge and row a loaded bag close to ribs | no safe load, do prone Y-T raises on the floor] → *Expect:* upper back works without twisting.
7. **Finish with a plank.** Hold 2-3 rounds of 20-45 seconds. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if furniture slips, wrists hurt, or low back sags. → *Expect:* body stays straight and breathing remains steady.

## Decision points

- Furniture is unstable → use floor-only movements.
- Noise is a concern → remove jumps and use slow tempo.
- Travel fatigue is high → cut one set from each move and keep easy effort.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Sliding chair or desk:** detect movement under your hands → stop using it immediately.
- **F2 Back rounding on rows:** detect shoulders and spine curling → lighten the bag and hinge less deeply.
- **F3 Knee wobble in lunges:** detect side-to-side knee motion → hold the wall and shorten the step.
- **F4 Overheating in a small room:** detect dizziness or nausea → open ventilation, hydrate, and stop.

## Verification

Complete squats, incline push-ups, reverse lunges, a row or upper-back substitute, and planks in 20 minutes with quiet landings, stable surfaces, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: do one set of each movement and use a higher incline for push-ups.
- `harder`: add slow 3-second lowering phases or extra rounds.
- `equipment`: use a resistance band packed in luggage for rows and presses.
- `at-home`: the same sequence works in a bedroom or guest room.

## Safety & privacy

Do not load hotel furniture unless it is stable and intended to bear weight. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, or slipping surfaces. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have relevant medical or injury concerns.
