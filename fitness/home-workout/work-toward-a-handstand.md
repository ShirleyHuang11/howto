## Goal

You practice handstand progressions that build wrist tolerance, shoulder elevation, body tension, and safe exits before freestanding attempts.

## Preconditions

- Clear wall space and open floor free of furniture.
- Ability to hold a plank and bear weight through hands without wrist pain.
- Warm up wrists, shoulders, thoracic spine, hamstrings, and core before inversion.

## Steps

1. **Prepare wrists and shoulders.** Do wrist rocks, palm lifts, scapular push-ups, and overhead reaches. → *Expect:* wrists feel warm and shoulders can reach overhead without pinching.
2. **Learn the hollow line.** Stand tall, squeeze glutes, brace abs, and reach arms overhead with ribs down. → *Expect:* body forms a long line without low-back arching.
3. **Practice wall walks.** Start in a plank with feet on the wall, walk feet up and hands back only as far as you can control. → *Expect:* shoulders stack closer over hands while breathing remains steady.
4. **Hold a chest-to-wall position.** Keep hands shoulder-width, push tall through shoulders, and point ribs toward pelvis. → *Expect:* ears sit between arms and body does not banana-arch.
5. **Practice safe exits.** Cartwheel one leg down to the side or step back from a wall walk before fatigue. → *Expect:* you can leave the inversion without falling backward.
6. **Add kick-up practice only after control.** Face away from the wall, kick lightly, and touch one heel to the wall without crashing. → *Expect:* the wall is a target, not a landing pad.
7. **Limit attempts.** Do 5-10 quality holds or kick-ups, resting 60-120 seconds. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if wrists, shoulders, neck, or head feel painful, or if exits become uncontrolled. → *Expect:* every attempt ends deliberately.

## Decision points

- Wrist pain appears → stop hand loading and use wrist prep or elevated pike holds another day.
- Back arches strongly → return to hollow holds and chest-to-wall work.
- Fear blocks exits → practice cartwheel exits from a low wall-walk height before kicking up.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Banana back:** detect ribs flaring and low back arching → squeeze glutes and shorten the hold.
- **F2 Collapsed shoulders:** detect sinking toward the floor → push tall and spread the upper back.
- **F3 Over-kicking:** detect heels slamming into the wall → kick with less force and keep one leg as a brake.
- **F4 Training to failure:** detect shaky arms and late exits → end the set earlier and rest longer.

## Verification

Complete 5 controlled chest-to-wall holds of 10-20 seconds with elevated shoulders, ribs down, safe exits, and no wrist, shoulder, neck, or head pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: practice pike holds with feet on the floor or a low box.
- `harder`: add shoulder taps at the wall or freestanding balance attempts after consistent exits.
- `equipment`: use parallettes only if you can grip and exit safely.
- `at-home`: use a clear wall with enough side space to cartwheel out.

## Safety & privacy

Inversions carry fall, wrist, shoulder, neck, and head risk. Never practice near furniture or when fatigued. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or qualified coach if you have blood pressure, eye pressure, neck, wrist, shoulder, balance, or pregnancy-related concerns.
