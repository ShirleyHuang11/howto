## Goal

You build a balanced no-equipment workout using squat, hinge, push, pull substitute, core, and conditioning patterns that match your current ability.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- Clear floor space, a wall, and optionally a sturdy chair.
- No injury that prevents basic bodyweight movement.

## Steps

1. **Choose the session focus.** Pick strength, conditioning, or balanced full-body work before selecting exercises. → *Expect:* the workout has one clear priority instead of random fatigue.
2. **Select a lower-body squat pattern.** [BRANCH: easier, chair sit-to-stand | standard, bodyweight squat | harder, split squat] keep knees tracking over toes. → *Expect:* legs work through a controlled pain-free range.
3. **Select a hinge pattern.** Use hip hinges, single-leg reaches, or glute bridges with a long spine. → *Expect:* glutes and hamstrings work without low-back strain.
4. **Select a push pattern.** [BRANCH: easier, wall or incline push-up | standard, knee or full push-up] keep shoulders away from ears. → *Expect:* chest and arms work while the trunk stays braced.
5. **Add a pull substitute.** Use prone Y-T-W raises, towel isometric rows around a sturdy post, or reverse snow angels. → *Expect:* upper back engages without neck shrugging.
6. **Add core and conditioning.** Choose dead bugs or planks, then low-impact cardio such as marching, step jacks, or mountain climbers. → *Expect:* trunk stays controlled while breathing rises.
7. **Set volume.** Do 2-4 rounds of 5-15 reps per exercise or 30-45 seconds per station, resting as needed. → *Expect:* the final round is challenging but form remains intact.
8. **Respect your space and joints.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop jumping or fast reps if you cannot land quietly, breathe, or keep joints aligned. → *Expect:* the workout fits your floor, fitness, and recovery.

## Decision points

- Form breaks before the target reps -> regress the exercise or reduce reps.
- Workout feels too easy -> add a round, slow tempo, or a harder variation.
- Joint pain appears -> swap the movement pattern for a pain-free version.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 All conditioning, no strength:** detect only breathless exercises -> include squat, hinge, push, pull substitute, and core.
- **F2 No pulling work:** detect shoulders rounding after many push-ups -> add upper-back raises or towel rows.
- **F3 Reps too fast:** detect bouncing or noisy landings -> slow down and control tempo.
- **F4 Poor progression:** detect random changes every session -> keep exercises stable for 2-4 weeks before progressing.

## Verification

You complete a 20-30 minute workout with at least one squat, hinge, push, pull substitute, core, and conditioning drill, all done with controlled alignment and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- easier: use chair squats, wall push-ups, glute bridges, dead bugs, and marching.
- harder: use split squats, single-leg hinges, full push-ups, longer planks, and faster intervals.
- equipment: add a backpack, towel, or chair while still avoiding gym equipment.
- at-home: keep all movements low-impact if neighbors or flooring are concerns.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when exercise choices match ability. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest symptoms, severe shortness of breath, or loss of coordination. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
