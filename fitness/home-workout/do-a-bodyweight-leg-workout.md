## Goal

You complete a balanced lower-body session at home that trains squatting, hinging, lunging, and calf strength with controlled alignment.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space and optional chair or wall for balance.
- Comfortable shoes or bare feet on a non-slip surface.
- Warm up with 3-5 minutes of brisk marching, hip circles, ankle rocks, and easy squats.

## Steps

1. **Practice air squats.** Sit hips back and down, keep heels planted, and let knees track over toes for 2 sets of 8-12 reps. → *Expect:* thighs and glutes work while your torso stays controlled.
2. **Do reverse lunges.** Step one foot back, lower until both knees bend comfortably, then push through the front foot to stand for 2-3 sets of 6-10 each side. → *Expect:* front knee stays aligned over the middle toes.
3. **Do hip hinges.** Place hands on hips, push hips back with soft knees, then squeeze glutes to stand for 2 sets of 10-15 reps. → *Expect:* hamstrings stretch without low-back rounding.
4. **Add glute bridges.** Lie on your back, feet near hips, press through heels, and lift hips without flaring ribs for 2-3 sets of 10-15 reps. → *Expect:* glutes do the work more than the low back.
5. **Train calves.** Rise onto the balls of the feet, pause, and lower slowly for 2 sets of 12-20 reps. → *Expect:* ankles move straight up and down without rolling outward.
6. **Finish with a wall sit.** Hold 20-45 seconds at a tolerable knee angle. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop any drill that creates sharp knee, hip, ankle, or back pain. → *Expect:* legs feel fatigued but joints feel stable.

## Decision points

- Balance limits lunges → hold a wall or switch to split squats with smaller range.
- Knees cave on squats → reduce depth and actively track knees over toes.
- Workout feels too easy → add a set, slow the lowering phase, or move to single-leg variations.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Heel lift in squats:** detect weight shifting to toes → reduce depth and keep whole-foot pressure.
- **F2 Front knee collapse:** detect knee drifting inward on lunges → shorten the step and slow down.
- **F3 Back-dominant bridges:** detect low-back cramping → tuck pelvis slightly and stop at rib-to-hip alignment.
- **F4 Rushed reps:** detect bouncing or losing balance → use a 2-second lower and 1-second pause.

## Verification

Complete the listed sets with controlled squats, reverse lunges on both sides, hinges, bridges, calf raises, and a wall sit while keeping knees aligned, heels grounded when appropriate, and no sharp joint pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a chair for box squats and supported lunges.
- `harder`: add tempo squats, single-leg bridges, or Bulgarian split squats.
- `equipment`: hold a backpack or dumbbell once bodyweight form is consistent.
- `at-home`: use stairs for calf raises if the edge is secure and you hold a rail.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for sharp pain, numbness, swelling, or instability. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or physical therapist before starting if you have lower-body injuries, joint replacement, or balance concerns.
