## Goal

You perform mountain climbers as a controlled plank-based conditioning drill without sagging hips, bouncing shoulders, or wrist pain.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space and a mat if needed.
- Ability to hold a basic high plank for 20 seconds.
- Warm shoulders, wrists, hips, and ankles before starting.

## Steps

1. **Set a strong high plank.** Put hands under shoulders, spread fingers, step feet back, and brace as if preparing for a push-up. → *Expect:* body forms a straight line from head to heels.
2. **Drive one knee forward.** Bring one knee toward the chest without rounding hard or lifting the hips high. → *Expect:* the moving foot lands lightly or hovers while shoulders stay over wrists.
3. **Switch legs smoothly.** Return the first foot as the other knee comes forward. → *Expect:* feet change places with minimal hip sway.
4. **Choose the right tempo.** [BRANCH: control focus, step each foot quietly | conditioning focus, run the feet faster while keeping the plank] → *Expect:* effort rises while the trunk position stays stable.
5. **Breathe continuously.** Exhale through hard switches and avoid holding your breath. → *Expect:* breathing is rhythmic even as heart rate increases.
6. **Work in intervals.** Do 3-6 rounds of 20-40 seconds with equal or longer rest. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if wrist, shoulder, or low-back pain changes your plank shape. → *Expect:* each round finishes with the same alignment you started with.

## Decision points

- Wrists hurt → elevate hands on a bench, fists, or handles.
- Hips bounce or sag → slow down and shorten the interval.
- Breathing becomes uncontrolled early → use marching mountain climbers instead of running switches.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Sagging hips:** detect low-back dipping → squeeze glutes and shorten the set.
- **F2 Shoulders behind hands:** detect weight rocking backward → shift chest over hands before moving legs.
- **F3 Loud foot strikes:** detect stomping → slow down and place feet quietly.
- **F4 Neck craning:** detect looking forward → look at the floor slightly ahead of hands.

## Verification

Complete 4 rounds of 30 seconds with shoulders stacked over wrists, hips level, quiet foot switches, continuous breathing, and no wrist or back pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: elevate hands on a couch or step.
- `harder`: use cross-body mountain climbers or longer intervals.
- `equipment`: use sliders under feet for slower, higher-tension reps.
- `at-home`: use a towel under each foot on a smooth floor.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for sharp wrist, shoulder, hip, or back pain, dizziness, or inability to maintain plank alignment. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have relevant joint, spine, or cardiovascular conditions.
