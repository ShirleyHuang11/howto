## Goal

You train the core for bracing, anti-rotation, and hip control without relying on crunch volume or low-back strain.

## Preconditions

- Mat or comfortable floor space.
- Ability to get down to and up from the floor safely.
- Warm up with easy marching, cat-cow, hip bridges, and shoulder taps.

## Steps

1. **Set a breathing brace.** Lie on your back with knees bent, exhale gently, and feel ribs settle toward pelvis. → *Expect:* abs tighten lightly without holding breath.
2. **Do dead bugs.** Move opposite arm and leg away while keeping low ribs down for 2-3 sets of 6-10 each side. → *Expect:* low back position stays steady.
3. **Do side planks.** Stack elbow under shoulder and hold knees bent or legs straight. → *Expect:* side waist works and hips do not roll backward.
4. **Do glute bridges.** Press through heels and lift hips until knees, hips, and shoulders align. → *Expect:* glutes work more than low back.
5. **Do bird dogs.** From hands and knees, reach opposite arm and leg without shifting hips. → *Expect:* spine stays still while limbs move.
6. **Do front plank.** Hold elbows under shoulders, glutes lightly squeezed, and neck neutral. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if low back pinches or you cannot breathe. → *Expect:* abs shake before the back hurts.
7. **Cycle the circuit.** Rest 30-60 seconds between drills and complete 2-3 rounds. → *Expect:* control improves or stays consistent across rounds.

## Decision points

- Low back arches during dead bugs → shorten the lever or tap heels instead.
- Side plank bothers shoulder → use a bent-knee side plank or side-lying leg lift.
- Neck strains → look at the floor or ceiling as appropriate and soften the jaw.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Holding breath:** detect pressure in face or chest → exhale during effort and reduce hold length.
- **F2 Rib flare:** detect ribs popping up on dead bugs or bridges → reset with a slow exhale.
- **F3 Hip rotation:** detect pelvis rolling during bird dogs → move slower and shorten reach.
- **F4 Shoulder collapse:** detect sinking in planks → push the floor away and shorten the set.

## Verification

Complete 2 rounds of dead bugs, side planks, glute bridges, bird dogs, and front planks with steady breathing, stable pelvis, no rib flare, and no low-back pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use shorter holds, bent knees, or heel taps.
- `harder`: add longer holds, slow tempo, or hollow-body progressions.
- `equipment`: add a mini band for dead bugs or Pallof presses if available.
- `at-home`: use a folded towel under elbows or knees.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for sharp back, hip, neck, or shoulder pain, numbness, or dizziness. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or physical therapist before starting if you have spine, pelvic floor, pregnancy-related, or post-surgical concerns.
