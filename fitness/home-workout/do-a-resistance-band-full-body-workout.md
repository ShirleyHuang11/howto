## Goal

You complete a full-body resistance band workout with secure anchoring, controlled tension, and balanced movement patterns.

## Preconditions

- Resistance band without cracks, tears, or damaged handles.
- Secure anchor if doing anchored rows or presses.
- Clear space and a warmup for shoulders, hips, ankles, and trunk.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the band.** Stretch it lightly and check for whitening, tears, or weak spots. → *Expect:* the band returns smoothly with no visible damage.
2. **Warm up with light tension.** Do band pull-aparts, bodyweight squats, and hip hinges. → *Expect:* shoulders and hips feel ready before harder sets.
3. **Do band squats.** Stand on the band, hold ends at shoulders, squat to comfortable depth, and stand tall. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and band tension stays even.
4. **Do band rows.** Anchor the band or loop it around feet, pull elbows toward ribs, and pause. → *Expect:* shoulder blades move back without shrugging.
5. **Do band chest presses or push-ups.** Anchor behind you or wrap band across upper back. → *Expect:* pressing feels smooth and shoulders do not pinch.
6. **Do band hinges or deadlifts.** Stand on the band, push hips back, keep spine neutral, and stand by squeezing glutes. → *Expect:* hamstrings and glutes work without back rounding.
7. **Do anti-rotation presses.** Anchor at chest height and press hands forward while resisting twist. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if the anchor slips or the band snaps back unpredictably. → *Expect:* trunk stays square.
8. **Complete 2-4 rounds.** Use 8-15 reps per move with controlled returns. → *Expect:* muscles fatigue while joints feel stable.

## Decision points

- Band feels too light → step farther away, choke up, or use a heavier band.
- Form changes from tension → reduce stretch or use a lighter band.
- Anchor cannot be trusted → choose foot-anchored or unanchored movements.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Band snap risk:** detect cracks or fraying → discard the band.
- **F2 Anchor failure:** detect door or object movement → stop and use a safer anchor.
- **F3 Rushed return:** detect band yanking limbs back → slow the eccentric phase.
- **F4 Shoulder shrugging:** detect neck tension on rows or presses → reduce tension and set shoulders down.

## Verification

Complete 2-4 rounds of band squats, rows, presses, hinges, and anti-rotation presses with secure band condition, controlled tension both directions, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use lighter bands, shorter ranges, or fewer rounds.
- `harder`: use heavier bands, slower tempo, or single-leg variations.
- `equipment`: combine loop bands and handled tube bands if each is inspected.
- `at-home`: use a door anchor only on a door that closes toward you and locks or latches securely.

## Safety & privacy

Bands can snap or anchors can fail. Keep the band away from your face, inspect it often, and stop for sharp pain or equipment movement. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have relevant injuries or medical conditions.
