## Goal

You hold a wall sit with steady lower-body tension, knees tracking cleanly, and no knee or back pain.

## Preconditions

- A clear wall and non-slip floor.
- Shoes with traction, or bare feet if the floor is safe.
- Warm up first with easy marching, air squats, or step-ups for 3-5 minutes.

## Steps

1. **Stand with your back on the wall.** Place feet hip-width apart, 1.5-2 foot lengths away from the wall, toes slightly turned out if comfortable. → *Expect:* your head, upper back, and pelvis touch the wall without arching hard.
2. **Slide down under control.** Bend knees and hips until thighs are near parallel to the floor, stopping higher if your knees complain. → *Expect:* knees point the same direction as toes and stay roughly over the mid-foot.
3. **Set your trunk.** Lightly brace your abs, keep ribs stacked over pelvis, and avoid pressing your low back into an exaggerated arch. → *Expect:* your spine feels long and your breathing stays possible.
4. **Hold the position.** Press through the whole foot, especially heels and big-toe base, and keep thighs active. → *Expect:* quadriceps burn evenly while knees and low back feel stable.
5. **Exit safely.** Push through your feet and slide up the wall before stepping away. → *Expect:* you stand without collapsing inward at the knees.
6. **Repeat for sets.** Do 2-4 holds of 20-45 seconds with 45-90 seconds rest. → *Expect:* each hold ends because of muscle fatigue, not joint pain.

## Decision points

- Knees hurt in the bottom position → hold a higher angle or stop for the day.
- Heels lift or feet slide → move feet slightly farther out or change footwear/surface.
- 45 seconds is easy for all sets → add time, hold a light weight, or use single-leg bias only if form stays clean.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Knees cave inward:** detect knees drifting toward each other → push knees gently toward the second and third toes.
- **F2 Too much knee pressure:** detect sharp kneecap discomfort → shorten the depth and keep shins more vertical.
- **F3 Low-back arching:** detect a hard arch or pinching → brace lightly and bring ribs down.
- **F4 Sliding feet:** detect shoes or socks moving on the floor → switch to a grippier surface before continuing.

## Verification

Complete 3 holds of 30 seconds with thighs at a tolerable depth, heels down, knees tracking over toes, steady breathing, and no sharp knee or back pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: hold higher on the wall or reduce each hold to 10-20 seconds.
- `harder`: hold longer, add a pause at parallel, or hold a light dumbbell at the chest.
- `equipment`: place a small ball between knees only to learn alignment, not to squeeze hard.
- `at-home`: use any clear wall with a floor that does not slide.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for sharp knee pain, numbness, dizziness, or back pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or qualified professional before starting if you have knee, hip, back, cardiovascular, or balance concerns.
