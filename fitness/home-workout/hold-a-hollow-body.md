## Goal

You hold a hollow-body position with ribs down, pelvis controlled, and abdominal tension instead of low-back strain.

## Preconditions

- Comfortable floor or mat.
- Ability to lie on your back and raise arms or legs without pain.
- Warm up with dead bugs, glute bridges, and gentle breathing drills.

## Steps

1. **Set the base position.** Lie on your back, knees bent, feet on floor, and arms by sides. → *Expect:* head, ribs, and pelvis rest comfortably.
2. **Exhale and brace.** Gently draw ribs down and tilt pelvis so the low back becomes heavy on the floor. → *Expect:* abs engage without breath-holding.
3. **Lift shoulders slightly.** Raise head and shoulder blades just off the floor while looking toward knees. → *Expect:* neck stays long and abs work harder.
4. **Choose leg position.** [BRANCH: easier, keep knees bent over hips | harder, extend legs lower only as far as low back stays controlled] → *Expect:* low back does not arch away from the floor.
5. **Choose arm position.** [BRANCH: easier, arms reach toward thighs | harder, arms reach overhead with ribs still down] → *Expect:* tension increases without rib flare.
6. **Hold and breathe.** Hold 10-30 seconds, using small exhales to maintain position. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if low back pinches or neck strain dominates. → *Expect:* abs fatigue before the back does.
7. **Reset between holds.** Bend knees, lower head, and breathe before repeating 3-5 times. → *Expect:* each hold starts with the same low-back control.

## Decision points

- Low back lifts → bend knees more or raise legs higher.
- Neck hurts → support head lightly or use dead bugs instead.
- Hold is easy for 30 seconds → lower legs slightly or reach arms overhead.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rib flare:** detect ribs popping up as arms move overhead → bring arms forward and exhale.
- **F2 Back arch:** detect space under low back → regress leg position immediately.
- **F3 Neck gripping:** detect chin jutting or throat tension → lower head and practice brace only.
- **F4 Breath holding:** detect face pressure → shorten holds and breathe through pursed lips.

## Verification

Complete 4 hollow-body holds of 20 seconds with low back controlled against the floor, ribs down, steady breathing, and no neck or back pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use tuck holds, dead bugs, or arms by sides.
- `harder`: extend legs lower, reach arms overhead, or add hollow rocks only if position holds.
- `equipment`: hold a light band overhead for feedback, not load.
- `at-home`: use a mat or carpet to reduce pressure on the spine.

## Safety & privacy

Stop for sharp back pain, neck pain, numbness, or inability to breathe normally. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or physical therapist if you have spine, hip, pelvic floor, or post-surgical concerns.
