## Goal

You perform inverted rows under a table with a secure setup, strong body line, and upper-back-driven pulling.

## Preconditions

- A heavy, stable table that cannot tip, slide, or break under body weight.
- Clear space under and around the table.
- Warm shoulders, elbows, wrists, and upper back.

## Steps

1. **Test the table cautiously.** Pull upward and backward on the edge while keeping feet on the floor, then check for sliding or tipping. → *Expect:* the table does not move, flex dangerously, or lift.
2. **Position your body.** Lie under the table with chest below the edge, grip firmly, and place feet on the floor. → *Expect:* arms are straight and shoulders are packed, not shrugged.
3. **Set a straight line.** Squeeze glutes and brace abs so shoulders, hips, and knees or heels align. → *Expect:* the torso does not sag before pulling.
4. **Pull chest toward the table.** Drive elbows down and back, bringing shoulder blades together without craning the neck. → *Expect:* upper back and arms do the work, and chest approaches the edge.
5. **Lower slowly.** Return to straight arms over 2-3 seconds while keeping hips lifted. → *Expect:* shoulder blades separate under control.
6. **Adjust difficulty.** [BRANCH: easier, bend knees and keep feet closer | harder, straighten legs or elevate feet] → *Expect:* reps remain full and smooth.
7. **Stop before grip or setup fails.** Do 3-4 sets of 6-12 reps. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop immediately if the table shifts, grip slips, or pain appears. → *Expect:* the table stays still for every rep.

## Decision points

- Table is light or unstable → do not use it; choose band rows or towel rows.
- Hips sag → bend knees and reduce reps.
- Elbows or shoulders ache → reduce range, use a different grip, or stop.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Unsafe anchor:** detect table movement or creaking → abandon the setup.
- **F2 Hip drop:** detect body folding at the hips → squeeze glutes and bend knees.
- **F3 Neck reaching:** detect chin jutting to touch the table → keep neck neutral and pull chest instead.
- **F4 Shoulder shrugging:** detect shoulders near ears → start each rep by setting shoulder blades down.

## Verification

Complete 3 sets of 8 inverted rows with an unmoving table, straight body line, chest pulling toward the edge, controlled lowering, and no grip slip or joint pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: bend knees or use a higher sturdy surface.
- `harder`: straighten legs, elevate feet, or pause at the top.
- `equipment`: use rings, a suspension trainer, or a bar in a rack for a safer adjustable setup.
- `at-home`: choose a table only if it is heavy and structurally solid.

## Safety & privacy

The main risk is falling or pulling unstable furniture onto yourself. Never use glass, folding, lightweight, or damaged tables. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if you have shoulder, elbow, wrist, or back concerns.
