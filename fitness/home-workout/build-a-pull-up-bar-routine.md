## Goal

You build a pull-up bar routine that trains hanging strength, scapular control, pulling volume, and core tension safely.

## Preconditions

- A properly installed pull-up bar rated for your body weight.
- Clear floor below the bar and dry hands.
- Warm shoulders, wrists, elbows, and upper back before hanging.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the bar every session.** Pull down gradually, check doorframe or mounting hardware, and confirm nothing shifts. → *Expect:* the bar stays fixed under a controlled test load.
2. **Start with active hangs.** Hang with straight arms, then gently pull shoulder blades down away from ears for 3-5 reps of 5-10 seconds. → *Expect:* shoulders feel engaged, not jammed near ears.
3. **Choose your pull variation.** [BRANCH: beginner, use foot-assisted or band-assisted pull-ups | stronger trainee, use strict pull-ups or chin-ups] → *Expect:* each rep starts from control and finishes without swinging.
4. **Pull with the upper back first.** Initiate by drawing shoulder blades down and back, then drive elbows toward ribs. → *Expect:* chest rises toward the bar and neck stays neutral.
5. **Lower under control.** Take 2-4 seconds down and return to a full but active hang. → *Expect:* elbows extend without shoulders losing control.
6. **Add horizontal or core work.** Do dead hangs, knee raises, or scapular pulls between sets, keeping ribs down. → *Expect:* abs and lats work without swinging.
7. **Cap quality volume.** Do 3-5 sets, stopping 1-2 reps before form fails. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if you feel sharp elbow, shoulder, or hand pain, or if the bar moves. → *Expect:* the final set looks like the first, just slower.

## Decision points

- Cannot do a strict rep → use assisted reps and slow negatives.
- Grip fails before back muscles → add shorter hangs and more rest.
- Elbows ache → reduce volume, use neutral grip if available, and avoid sudden increases.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Kipping unintentionally:** detect leg swing driving the rep → pause in a dead hang before each pull.
- **F2 Shoulder shrugging:** detect ears rising toward shoulders → practice active hangs before reps.
- **F3 Half reps:** detect not lowering or not reaching chin height → reduce assistance or reps until range is consistent.
- **F4 Bar instability:** detect creaks or movement → stop and reinstall or replace the bar.

## Verification

Complete a routine of active hangs, 3-5 pulling sets, and optional knee raises with stable bar position, controlled lowering, no uncontrolled swing, and no sharp elbow or shoulder pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use foot assistance, a resistance band, or negatives only.
- `harder`: add pauses, slower eccentrics, or weighted pull-ups only after strict reps are reliable.
- `equipment`: use rings or suspension straps for joint-friendly angles if mounted safely.
- `at-home`: doorway bars must be installed exactly as designed and checked each session.

## Safety & privacy

Falls and shoulder or elbow irritation are the main risks. Stop if the bar shifts, grip slips, or pain is sharp. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a professional if you have shoulder, elbow, wrist, or spine issues.
