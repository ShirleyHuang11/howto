## Goal

You complete a first indoor cycling class safely by setting up the bike, pacing your effort, and modifying cues that are too intense.

## Preconditions

- Arrive 10-15 minutes early so the instructor can help with bike setup.
- Water, towel, breathable clothing, and cycling shoes only if required or provided.
- You know you can do moderate cardio for at least 20 minutes.
- Tell the instructor privately about relevant injuries or pregnancy if applicable.

## Steps

1. **Ask for setup help.** Adjust saddle height, saddle position, and handlebar height before class starts. → *Expect:* knees keep a slight bend at the bottom and shoulders feel relaxed.
2. **Learn the resistance knob and brake.** Practice adding, reducing, and stopping the flywheel. → *Expect:* you can change effort without looking down for long.
3. **Start below the room's intensity.** Keep early resistance and cadence comfortable even if others go harder. → *Expect:* breathing rises but you are not overwhelmed in the first song.
4. **Stay seated when needed.** [BRANCH: standing feels unstable, remain seated with moderate resistance | stable, stand only with enough resistance to control the pedals] → *Expect:* hips stay controlled and knees track forward.
5. **Use the beat as optional guidance.** Match cadence only when you can do so smoothly. → *Expect:* you never bounce wildly or lose pedal control.
6. **Ignore unsafe pressure.** ⚠️ *Safety:* reduce resistance, sit down, or stop if you feel dizzy, chest pain, sharp joint pain, or cannot control the pedals. → *Expect:* you finish by managing your own effort, not copying every cue.
7. **Cool down and step off slowly.** Pedal easy, stretch calves and hips lightly, and stand up carefully. → *Expect:* legs feel worked but balance is steady.

## Decision points

- Instructor cue feels too hard → reduce resistance or skip the interval.
- Standing causes knee or back discomfort → stay seated.
- You feel embarrassed modifying → remember class cues are options; safety decides.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bike fit rushed:** detect knee pain or hip rocking → ask for adjustment before continuing.
- **F2 Too much resistance:** detect grinding below a controllable cadence → turn resistance down.
- **F3 Too little resistance standing:** detect bouncing or runaway pedals → add resistance or sit.
- **F4 Heat and dehydration:** detect dizziness or chills → stop, cool down, and hydrate.

## Verification

You complete the class or a self-selected portion with proper bike fit, controllable cadence, knees tracking over feet, no uncontrolled bouncing, and no sharp pain or dizziness.

## Variations

- `easier`: ride seated for the whole class and skip maximal sprints.
- `harder`: follow more standing climbs and intervals after several comfortable classes.
- `equipment`: bring padded shorts or a gel cover if saddle discomfort limits riding.
- `at-home`: follow a recorded class while keeping your own resistance and safety limits.

## Safety & privacy

Low to medium effort risk depending on intensity. Stop for chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath, sharp joint pain, or loss of pedal control. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before intense classes if you have a relevant condition.
