## Goal

You use stairs for a hard but controlled cardio session while protecting knees, ankles, and balance.

## Preconditions

- A clean, dry staircase with sturdy handrail, good lighting, and low traffic.
- Shoes with grip and no loose laces.
- You can climb stairs normally without knee, hip, or Achilles pain.
- Warm up with 5-10 minutes of walking and easy stair climbs.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the stairs.** Check for wet spots, clutter, uneven steps, or people moving through. → *Expect:* the route is predictable and safe to repeat.
2. **Warm up on easy climbs.** Walk up at low effort and walk down slowly using the rail if needed. → *Expect:* breathing rises gradually and legs feel warm.
3. **Choose the interval.** Start with 6-10 rounds of 20-45 seconds climbing and easy walk-down recovery. → *Expect:* work periods are hard but controlled.
4. **Climb with whole-foot pressure.** Step onto each stair with knee tracking over toes, torso slightly inclined, and arms assisting naturally. → *Expect:* foot placement is quiet and stable.
5. **Descend deliberately.** Walk down, keep eyes on the steps, and avoid racing the descent. → *Expect:* breathing recovers while joints stay controlled.
6. **Stop before balance fails.** ⚠️ *Safety:* end the workout if you miss a step, feel dizzy, have sharp knee or Achilles pain, or cannot descend safely. → *Expect:* fatigue never overrides foot accuracy.
7. **Cool down on flat ground.** Walk 3-5 minutes and lightly stretch calves if comfortable. → *Expect:* heart rate and leg tension settle.

## Decision points

- Knee pain appears descending → use fewer rounds, a shorter staircase, or switch to flat cardio.
- You trip or catch toes → slow down and end fast intervals for the day.
- Breathing stays too high after walk-down → lengthen recovery before the next climb.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Skipping warmup:** detect burning legs and breathlessness in round one → restart with easy walking.
- **F2 Racing down:** detect pounding knees or near misses → descend slowly and use the rail.
- **F3 Knee cave:** detect knees collapsing inward on steps → slow down and drive knees over toes.
- **F4 Overreaching steps:** detect pulling with the low back or pushing off awkwardly → use every step rather than bounding.

## Verification

You complete 6-10 stair intervals with stable foot placement, knees tracking over toes, controlled descent, no missed steps, and no sharp knee, ankle, Achilles, hip, or chest symptoms.

## Variations

- `easier`: walk intervals on a small flight with longer recovery.
- `harder`: increase rounds or climb duration before adding speed.
- `equipment`: use a stair machine if real stairs are crowded or unsafe.
- `at-home`: use a single low step for controlled step-ups instead of full stair sprints.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because fatigue on stairs increases fall risk. Keep descents slow, use the rail when needed, and stop for dizziness, sharp pain, or loss of coordination. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before stair intervals if you have a relevant condition.
