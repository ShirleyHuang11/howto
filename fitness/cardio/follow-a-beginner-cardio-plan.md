## Goal

Build a consistent beginner cardio habit over 28 days using manageable sessions and gradual progression.

## Preconditions

- A low-impact cardio option such as walking, cycling, elliptical, rowing, or easy jogging.
- Three to five available training days per week.
- Comfortable shoes or equipment adjusted to fit.
- Medical clearance if you have a relevant health condition or have been inactive for a long time.

## Steps

1. **Choose one primary mode.** Pick the cardio option you can repeat with minimal joint irritation. → *Expect:* you have a default workout that is easy to start.
2. **Set your easy intensity.** Move at a pace where you can speak in full sentences. → *Expect:* effort feels like 3-4 out of 10, not breathless.
3. **Complete week 1 with short sessions.** Do 3 sessions of 15-20 minutes, including 5 minutes easy at the start and end. → *Expect:* you finish each session feeling you could do a little more.
4. **Add frequency in week 2.** Do 3-4 sessions of 20-25 minutes, keeping most work easy. → *Expect:* breathing is steady and soreness does not alter your gait.
5. **Add duration in week 3.** Do 4 sessions of 25-30 minutes, with one optional 5-minute moderate block in the middle. → *Expect:* the moderate block raises breathing but remains controlled.
6. **Consolidate in week 4.** Do 4-5 sessions of 25-35 minutes and keep at least one rest day after the hardest session. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop and seek care for chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath, or sharp joint pain. → *Expect:* your weekly total rises without accumulating pain or exhaustion.

## Decision points

- Sessions feel too hard → repeat the previous week instead of progressing.
- Joint pain changes your stride → switch to lower-impact cardio and reduce duration.
- Energy and sleep worsen → add a rest day and keep all sessions easy.
- Easy sessions feel consistently comfortable → add 5 minutes to one or two sessions.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Progressing too fast:** detect soreness lasting more than 48 hours or declining performance → repeat or reduce the week.
- **F2 Training every session hard:** detect breathlessness on all workouts → return to conversational pace.
- **F3 Poor equipment fit:** detect numb feet, saddle pain, or shoe rubbing → adjust fit before adding volume.
- **F4 Skipping warmups:** detect tight calves, knees, or hips early → start every session with 5 easy minutes.

## Verification

After 28 days, you completed at least 12 cardio sessions, built up to 25-35 minutes per session, and maintained conversational form without sharp pain.

## Variations

- Easier: start with 10-minute sessions and progress every two weeks.
- Harder: add one moderate interval session per week after week 4.
- Equipment: bike, treadmill, rowing machine, elliptical, jump rope, or walking route.
- At-home: step-ups, marching, stationary bike, or low-impact follow-along cardio.

## Safety & privacy

Beginner cardio should leave you better recovered, not depleted. Stop for chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness, or sharp pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
