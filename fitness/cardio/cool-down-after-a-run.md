## Goal

You bring breathing, heart rate, and muscle tone down after a run without abruptly stopping from high effort.

## Preconditions

- You have finished a run or interval session.
- A safe area for walking slowly.
- Water and dry clothing available if weather is cold or hot.
- No severe symptoms requiring immediate medical attention.

## Steps

1. **Ease out of running.** Slow from running to very easy jogging or brisk walking for 2-5 minutes. → *Expect:* breathing starts to come down without dizziness.
2. **Walk until speech is easy.** Continue walking with tall posture and relaxed arms. → *Expect:* you can speak in full sentences again.
3. **Scan for unusual symptoms.** Notice chest pain, faintness, sharp pain, or confusion. → *Expect:* normal fatigue feels muscular and general, not alarming or localized.
4. **Add gentle mobility.** Do light calf, quad, hamstring, and hip flexor stretches without forcing end range. → *Expect:* muscles feel less tight while joints remain comfortable.
5. **Rehydrate and regulate temperature.** Drink to thirst and change layers if you are chilled or overheated. → *Expect:* sweating, shivering, or overheating begins to settle.
6. **Log important notes.** Record distance, effort, pain, fueling, heat, or route issues if you track training. → *Expect:* you have useful information for the next run.

## Decision points

- You feel dizzy after stopping → keep walking slowly, sit if needed, and seek help if it does not resolve.
- A muscle feels crampy → walk, breathe, and use gentle range of motion before stretching harder.
- Sharp pain appeared during the run → do not stretch aggressively; rest and assess.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Abrupt stop:** detect lightheadedness after a hard finish → walk until breathing normalizes.
- **F2 Forced stretching:** detect joint pain or nerve-like tingling → back off and use gentle mobility.
- **F3 Ignoring heat or cold:** detect chills, goosebumps, or overheating → change layers and cool or warm gradually.
- **F4 Missing warning signs:** detect chest pain, fainting, confusion, or severe breathlessness → seek medical help.

## Verification

Within 10-15 minutes after the run, you have walked down to easy speech, breathing is controlled, temperature feels stable, and no sharp pain, dizziness, or severe symptoms are present.

## Variations

- `easier`: walk only and skip stretching if you are exhausted.
- `harder`: after intense workouts, add 5 minutes of very easy jogging before walking.
- `equipment`: use a watch timer, water bottle, towel, and dry layer.
- `at-home`: finish treadmill runs with a slow belt walk before stepping off.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but do not dismiss severe symptoms after exercise. Stop stretching if pain is sharp or tingling. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor for chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or persistent injury symptoms.
