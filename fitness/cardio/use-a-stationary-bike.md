## Goal

You set up and ride a stationary bike for cardio with stable posture, smooth pedaling, and appropriate resistance.

## Preconditions

- A stationary bike with adjustable seat, handlebars, and resistance.
- Shoes that stay secure on pedals or clipped in if you know how to unclip.
- Water and a towel nearby.
- Warm up with 5 minutes of very easy pedaling.

## Steps

1. **Set seat height.** Adjust the saddle so your knee keeps a slight bend at the bottom of the pedal stroke. → *Expect:* hips do not rock side to side while pedaling.
2. **Set fore-aft and handlebars.** Place the seat so the front knee is roughly over the pedal spindle when pedals are level, then set handlebars for a comfortable reach. → *Expect:* shoulders are relaxed and wrists are not bearing excessive weight.
3. **Start with low resistance.** Pedal easily for 5 minutes while checking hip, knee, and foot alignment. → *Expect:* knees track forward over the feet without wobbling inward.
4. **Find a smooth cadence.** Aim for a cadence you can control, often 70-95 rpm for general cardio. → *Expect:* the pedal stroke feels circular rather than stomped.
5. **Add resistance gradually.** Increase resistance until breathing rises but posture remains steady. → *Expect:* effort comes from legs and lungs, not bouncing or grinding.
6. **Keep the trunk quiet.** Sit tall or hinge slightly forward with a neutral spine and light hands. → *Expect:* hips stay planted and upper body stays calm.
7. **Cool down before stopping.** Reduce resistance and pedal easily for 3-5 minutes. → *Expect:* breathing and leg tension decrease.

## Decision points

- Knees hurt at the front → check seat height and avoid heavy low-cadence grinding.
- Hips rock at the bottom of the stroke → lower the saddle slightly.
- Numb hands or neck tension appear → raise handlebars or lighten your grip.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Seat too low:** detect cramped knees and front-knee discomfort → raise the saddle in small increments.
- **F2 Seat too high:** detect hip rocking or toe-pointing to reach the pedal → lower the saddle slightly.
- **F3 Resistance too heavy:** detect bouncing, grinding, or knee strain → reduce resistance and spin smoothly.
- **F4 Collapsed posture:** detect rounded back and locked elbows → lift the chest and soften the arms.

## Verification

You ride 20-30 minutes with smooth cadence, slight knee bend at the bottom, knees tracking over feet, relaxed shoulders, no bouncing, and no sharp knee, hip, back, or foot pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: use 10-15 minutes at low resistance and conversational breathing.
- `harder`: add 5-8 controlled intervals of 1-3 minutes with easy pedaling between.
- `equipment`: upright, recumbent, and spin bikes all work if adjusted correctly.
- `at-home`: use a fan and keep the bike on a stable floor surface.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for most people when setup is correct. Stop for chest pain, faintness, sharp joint pain, numbness that does not resolve after adjustment, or unusual shortness of breath. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
