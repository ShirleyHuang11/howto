## Goal

You complete a steady aerobic session at an intensity that builds endurance while remaining recoverable.

## Preconditions

- A cardio mode you can sustain: walking, running, cycling, rowing, elliptical, or swimming if competent.
- Optional heart-rate monitor or perceived-effort scale.
- Water and a cool environment if indoors.
- Warm up for 5-10 minutes before settling into the main effort.

## Steps

1. **Choose a low-skill modality.** Pick the option that lets you move continuously without technical breakdown. → *Expect:* effort is limited by aerobic work, not balance or joint discomfort.
2. **Warm up gradually.** Start very easy and increase effort over 5-10 minutes. → *Expect:* breathing deepens smoothly and muscles feel warmer.
3. **Find Zone 2 effort.** Use a pace where you can speak in full sentences but would not want to sing. → *Expect:* breathing is steady, controlled, and sustainable.
4. **Hold the effort for 25-40 minutes.** Keep speed, resistance, or incline stable and avoid surging. → *Expect:* heart rate or perceived effort stays mostly flat after the first several minutes.
5. **Adjust for drift.** If breathing climbs or form worsens, reduce speed or resistance slightly. → *Expect:* you return to conversational effort within 1-2 minutes.
6. **Cool down easily.** Move at very low intensity for 5 minutes. → *Expect:* breathing and heart rate trend downward before you stop.

## Decision points

- You cannot speak in full sentences → lower intensity.
- The session feels too easy but heart rate is stable → extend duration before increasing intensity.
- Joint discomfort appears → switch to a lower-impact modality like cycling or rowing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Turning it into tempo:** detect hard breathing and chasing pace → slow down until speech returns.
- **F2 Cardiac drift ignored:** detect effort creeping up in heat or fatigue → reduce output and hydrate.
- **F3 Poor modality fit:** detect pain from the chosen machine or movement → switch tools rather than forcing it.
- **F4 No warmup:** detect abrupt breathlessness early → restart easier and ramp gradually.

## Verification

You complete 25-40 minutes of continuous cardio at conversational effort, with stable form, no sharp pain, and a cool-down that brings breathing down.

## Variations

- `easier`: start with 15-20 minutes and add 5 minutes per week.
- `harder`: extend to 60-90 minutes before adding intensity.
- `equipment`: use a heart-rate monitor, bike, rower, treadmill, or elliptical.
- `at-home`: use brisk walking, step-ups to a low step, or a stationary bike.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for most people, but stop for chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath, or sharp pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant medical condition.
