## Goal

Complete a brisk walking workout that raises heart rate without needing running or gym equipment.

## Preconditions

- Comfortable walking shoes and a safe route or treadmill.
- Enough space to walk continuously for 20-40 minutes.
- Light warmup walking already started.

## Steps

1. **Begin at an easy pace.** Walk 5 minutes with tall posture, relaxed shoulders, and arms swinging naturally. → *Expect:* joints feel loose and breathing begins to deepen without strain.
2. **Set brisk posture.** Stand tall through the crown of the head, lightly brace the abdomen, and look 10-20 feet ahead. → *Expect:* your chest stays open and your stride feels smooth.
3. **Increase pace without overstriding.** Push the ground behind you, keep steps quick, and land with the foot close to under your body. → *Expect:* breathing is moderately hard but you can speak short sentences.
4. **Use the arms to support cadence.** Bend elbows about 90 degrees and swing front-to-back instead of across the body. → *Expect:* pace rises without shoulders tensing.
5. **Hold the main effort.** Walk briskly for 15-25 minutes, checking that knees track forward and feet roll heel to toe. ⚠️ *Safety:* slow down or stop for chest pain, dizziness, severe shortness of breath, or sharp joint pain. → *Expect:* effort stays steady and sustainable.
6. **Cool down gradually.** Slow to an easy walk for 5 minutes and let breathing return toward normal. → *Expect:* heart rate lowers and legs feel worked but not painful.

## Decision points

- Too easy → add hills, increase pace, or extend time by 5 minutes.
- Too hard → use intervals of 2 minutes brisk and 2 minutes easy.
- Knee or hip discomfort → shorten stride and choose flatter ground.
- Foot blisters → change socks, shoe fit, or route distance.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Overstriding:** detect loud heel strikes or braking sensation → shorten steps and quicken cadence.
- **F2 Rounded posture:** detect looking down with shoulders forward → lift gaze and stack ribs over pelvis.
- **F3 Arm crossing:** detect hands swinging across the midline → drive elbows back and keep arms parallel.
- **F4 Starting too fast:** detect early breathlessness → back off to a pace you can hold.

## Verification

You completed 20-35 minutes including warmup and cooldown, with 15 minutes at brisk effort, upright posture, quick controlled steps, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- Easier: shorten the brisk block or use flat indoor walking.
- Harder: add incline, carry light hand-free load, or use brisk/easy intervals.
- Equipment: treadmill, walking poles, reflective gear, or a watch for timing.
- At-home: walk laps indoors or march briskly in place with arm swing.

## Safety & privacy

Use well-lit routes, stay aware of traffic, and stop for chest pain, dizziness, faintness, or sharp joint pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
