## Goal

You use jump rope intervals to train coordination, rhythm, and cardiovascular fitness with low, controlled contacts.

## Preconditions

- A rope sized so the handles reach roughly armpit height when you stand on the middle.
- A flat, forgiving surface with overhead clearance.
- Supportive shoes and no current calf, Achilles, or foot injury.
- Warm up ankles, calves, hips, and shoulders before jumping.

## Steps

1. **Set the rope length and grip.** Hold handles lightly at hip height with elbows close to the ribs. → *Expect:* wrists can turn the rope without big shoulder circles.
2. **Practice the bounce without the rope.** Hop 1-2 cm off the floor with knees soft and ankles springy. → *Expect:* landings are quiet and heels barely kiss down.
3. **Turn from the wrists.** Swing the rope with small wrist circles while keeping shoulders relaxed. → *Expect:* the rope passes smoothly without arms flaring wide.
4. **Use short intervals.** Jump 20-30 seconds, then rest 30-60 seconds; repeat 6-10 times. → *Expect:* rhythm stays consistent and breathing rises gradually.
5. **Keep jumps low and aligned.** Land on the balls of the feet with knees tracking over toes and torso tall. → *Expect:* contacts are quiet and centered, not stomping.
6. **Reset after misses.** If the rope catches, pause, stand tall, and restart calmly. → *Expect:* misses do not turn into frantic, high jumps.
7. **Stop for tendon warning signs.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if calf, Achilles, shin, or foot pain becomes sharp or worsens during the session. → *Expect:* normal calf work feels muscular, not stabbing or localized at the tendon.

## Decision points

- Rope hits your toes repeatedly → shorten the jump timing or check rope length.
- Calves fatigue before cardio → reduce interval length and total contacts.
- Knees cave inward on landing → slow down and practice low pogo hops without the rope.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Jumping too high:** detect loud landings and fast fatigue → jump only high enough for the rope to pass.
- **F2 Shoulder spinning:** detect wide arm circles → tuck elbows and rotate from wrists.
- **F3 Locked knees:** detect stiff landings → keep knees soft and ankles elastic.
- **F4 Too much volume:** detect next-day tendon soreness → cut rounds in half and build gradually.

## Verification

You complete 6-10 controlled jump-rope intervals with low quiet landings, elbows near the ribs, wrist-driven turns, knees tracking over toes, and no sharp calf, shin, foot, or Achilles pain.

## Variations

- `easier`: do rope-free pogo hops or single skips with longer rest.
- `harder`: add boxer step, alternating feet, or longer intervals after basic rhythm is solid.
- `equipment`: use a beaded rope for feedback or a speed rope only after coordination improves.
- `at-home`: jump on a mat or low-impact surface with enough ceiling clearance.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because repeated jumping can irritate calves, Achilles tendons, feet, and shins. Stop for sharp pain or swelling. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or clinician before starting if you have relevant tendon, bone, joint, heart, or balance concerns.
