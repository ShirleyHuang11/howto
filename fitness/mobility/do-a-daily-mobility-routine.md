## Goal

Complete a short daily mobility routine that keeps common joints moving through comfortable, controlled ranges.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space and optional mat.
- Enough time to move slowly without rushing.
- No acute injury requiring rest or medical evaluation.

## Steps

1. **Breathe and scan posture.** Stand tall or sit upright, take 3 slow breaths, and notice neck, shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles. → *Expect:* you identify the stiffest area without forcing movement.
2. **Move the neck gently.** Turn left and right, then nod within a comfortable range. → *Expect:* motion is smooth with no dizziness or radiating symptoms.
3. **Circle shoulders and wrists.** Roll shoulders, then move wrists through flexion, extension, and circles. → *Expect:* joints feel lubricated and hands stay relaxed.
4. **Mobilize spine and hips.** Do cat-cow, open-book rotations, and hip circles or 90-90 switches. → *Expect:* trunk and hips move without pinching.
5. **Move knees and ankles.** Do slow bodyweight squats to a comfortable depth, calf raises, and knee-over-toe ankle rocks. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp pain, numbness, tingling, dizziness, or joint instability. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and heels stay controlled.
6. **Finish with your tightest area.** Spend 1-2 extra minutes on the stiffest safe stretch or drill. → *Expect:* you end with easier movement than when you started.

## Decision points

- Morning stiffness is high → keep range small and gentle.
- A joint pinches → change angle or skip that drill.
- Routine feels too easy → slow down and add active holds.
- You train later that day → keep mobility dynamic rather than long passive holds.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rushing reps:** detect bouncing or momentum → slow down and control the full range.
- **F2 Forcing painful range:** detect sharp pain or pinching → back off and choose a smaller motion.
- **F3 Compensating through low back:** detect rib flare in hip or shoulder drills → brace lightly and reduce range.
- **F4 Skipping consistency:** detect doing long sessions rarely → use a shorter daily version.

## Verification

You completed 8-12 minutes covering neck, shoulders, spine, hips, knees, and ankles, with controlled breathing, smooth motion, and no sharp pain or nerve symptoms.

## Variations

- Easier: do the routine seated or supported by a wall.
- Harder: add controlled articular rotations and end-range isometric holds.
- Equipment: mat, chair, wall, foam roller, or resistance band.
- At-home: perform the routine beside a chair for balance support.

## Safety & privacy

Daily mobility should feel restorative, not provocative. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, tingling, dizziness, swelling, or joint instability. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or clinician before starting if you have a relevant condition.
