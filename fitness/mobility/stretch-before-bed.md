## Goal

You complete a calm bedtime stretch routine that reduces general tension without raising effort or irritating joints.

## Preconditions

- Quiet floor or bed space with a mat, blanket, or pillows.
- No severe pain, acute injury, or dizziness.
- Keep intensity mild; this is relaxation work, not flexibility testing.

## Steps

1. **Dim effort and slow breathing.** Sit or lie down and take 5 slow breaths with relaxed jaw and shoulders. → *Expect:* heart rate and muscle tone begin to settle.
2. **Stretch the hips gently.** Lie on your back and hold a figure-four stretch for 45 seconds per side. → *Expect:* stretch is felt in the outer hip without knee pain.
3. **Lengthen the hamstrings.** Use a towel behind one thigh and straighten the knee only to a mild stretch. → *Expect:* pelvis stays heavy and breathing remains easy.
4. **Release the low back.** Hug both knees lightly or rock knees side to side. → *Expect:* low back feels broad and comfortable.
5. **Open the chest.** Lie with arms out in a low goalpost shape or clasp hands behind the back while seated. → *Expect:* chest and front shoulders open without tingling.
6. **Finish with supported breathing.** Rest on your back or side for 1-2 minutes and breathe quietly. → *Expect:* body feels calmer, not energized.
7. **Avoid aggressive end range.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp pain, numbness, tingling, or stretches that make symptoms worse after release. → *Expect:* each stretch feels mild and reversible.

## Decision points

- Stretching wakes you up -> reduce intensity and hold only comfortable positions.
- Joint discomfort appears -> support the limb with pillows or skip that stretch.
- Muscle cramp occurs -> ease out, move gently, and reduce range.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Forcing flexibility:** detect breath holding or grimacing -> back off to a mild stretch.
- **F2 Twisting the knee in figure-four:** detect knee pressure -> flex the foot and reduce hip angle.
- **F3 Pulling the neck forward:** detect neck strain during supine work -> rest the head on a pillow.
- **F4 Cold-room tension:** detect shivering or guarding -> use a blanket and smaller ranges.

## Verification

You complete 10 minutes of mild stretching with steady breathing, no sharp pain, and a calmer resting state afterward.

## Variations

- easier: do all stretches in bed with pillows for support.
- harder: hold each stretch up to 90 seconds while keeping intensity mild.
- equipment: use a towel, pillow, blanket, or yoga strap.
- at-home: perform the sequence beside the bed or on the bed if the surface is supportive.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when gentle. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, tingling, dizziness, or symptoms that worsen after stretching. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
