## Goal

You use pigeon pose to stretch the outer hip and glute region while protecting the front knee and low back.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- A mat, yoga block, cushion, or folded towel.
- No sharp knee pain, hip pinching, or recent hip or knee injury.

## Steps

1. **Enter from hands and knees.** Bring one knee forward behind the same-side wrist and angle the shin only as far as comfortable. → *Expect:* the front knee is not twisted or painful.
2. **Extend the back leg.** Slide the rear leg back with the top of the foot down and hips facing mostly forward. → *Expect:* pelvis is supported and not dropping hard to one side.
3. **Support the front hip.** Place a block or cushion under the hip if it does not reach the floor easily. → *Expect:* you can relax into the stretch without torquing the knee.
4. **Choose torso height.** [BRANCH: tight, stay upright on hands | comfortable, lower to forearms] keep the spine long. → *Expect:* stretch is felt in the outer front hip or glute, not inside the knee.
5. **Hold and breathe.** Stay 45-90 seconds, breathing slowly and keeping the front foot lightly flexed. → *Expect:* intensity settles from strong to manageable.
6. **Exit with control.** Press into your hands, tuck the rear toes if needed, and return to hands and knees before switching. → *Expect:* the knee feels unchanged or better after leaving the pose.
7. **Protect the knee.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop immediately for sharp knee pain, twisting pressure, numbness, or hip pinching. → *Expect:* all sensation remains muscular around the hip.

## Decision points

- Front knee hurts -> switch to a figure-four stretch on your back.
- Hip cannot rest comfortably -> add support under the hip.
- Low back feels compressed -> stay upright and square the pelvis less aggressively.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Forcing the shin parallel:** detect knee torque -> let the foot come closer to the opposite hip.
- **F2 Unsupported hip drop:** detect pelvis hanging in space -> add a block or cushion.
- **F3 Twisting the back leg:** detect rear hip rolling open -> realign gently or reduce depth.
- **F4 Holding through numbness:** detect tingling or deadness -> exit immediately.

## Verification

You hold 45-90 seconds per side with supported hips, a pain-free front knee, steady breathing, and stretch localized to the outer hip or glute.

## Variations

- easier: do a supine figure-four stretch instead.
- harder: lower to forearms or add a gentle forward fold only with no knee symptoms.
- equipment: use blocks, bolsters, cushions, or a folded towel.
- at-home: use a couch cushion under the front hip.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when the knee is protected. Stop for knee pain, numbness, tingling, hip pinching, or sharp back pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
