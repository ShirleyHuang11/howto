## Goal

You move major joints through comfortable ranges after sleep and start the day with less stiffness and better posture.

## Preconditions

- Floor space and a mat or carpet.
- Mild morning stiffness only, not acute injury pain.
- Move slowly at first; tissues may feel less ready immediately after waking.

## Steps

1. **Begin with nasal breathing.** Lie on your back with knees bent and take 5 slow breaths into the ribs. → *Expect:* your trunk relaxes and breathing becomes steady.
2. **Mobilize the spine with cat-cow.** Move from hands and knees, rounding and extending gently one vertebral segment at a time. → *Expect:* motion feels smoother with each rep.
3. **Open the hips.** Rock back toward child's pose, then return forward and alternate low lunges. → *Expect:* hips and low back warm without pinching.
4. **Rotate the upper back.** From hands and knees, reach one arm under the body, then open it toward the ceiling. → *Expect:* rotation comes through the upper back, not by twisting the hips.
5. **Wake up the ankles and calves.** Step into downward-dog-like pedaling or standing calf rocks. → *Expect:* calves lengthen and heels move closer to the floor over time.
6. **Stand and squat lightly.** Use a supported squat or sit-to-stand pattern with knees tracking over toes. → *Expect:* legs feel more awake and balance improves.
7. **Finish tall.** Stand, reach overhead without flaring ribs, then walk for 30 seconds. → *Expect:* posture feels stacked and movement feels less stiff.
8. **Keep symptoms quiet.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for dizziness, sharp pain, or symptoms that worsen as you move. → *Expect:* the flow leaves you more comfortable, not irritated.

## Decision points

- Very stiff on waking -> spend longer breathing and use smaller ranges.
- Wrist pressure in floor work -> use fists, forearms, or a raised surface.
- Energy is low -> keep the flow gentle instead of turning it into a workout.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rushing immediately:** detect breath holding and jerky reps -> slow down for the first 2 minutes.
- **F2 Low-back pinching:** detect pain during extension -> reduce arching and brace lightly.
- **F3 Knee cave in squats:** detect knees drifting inward -> hold support and press knees over toes.
- **F4 Shoulder shrugging overhead:** detect neck tension -> lower arms and keep ribs down.

## Verification

You complete 10 minutes of continuous easy movement, can squat or sit-to-stand comfortably, rotate both directions, and walk away with reduced stiffness and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- easier: do the flow seated or standing with a chair.
- harder: add a few push-ups, lunges, or longer holds after the mobility work.
- equipment: use a mat, yoga blocks, or chair.
- at-home: perform beside the bed with enough room for hands-and-knees movement.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when kept gentle. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest symptoms, or neurological symptoms. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
