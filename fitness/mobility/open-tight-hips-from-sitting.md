## Goal

You restore comfortable hip extension, rotation, and glute activation after long sitting so walking, squatting, and training feel less restricted.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first if you have been inactive: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- Floor space, a mat, and optional support for balance.
- No sharp hip pinching, groin pain, or recent hip injury.

## Steps

1. **Start with hip flexor breathing.** Kneel in a half-kneeling stance, gently tuck the pelvis, and breathe for 5 slow breaths per side. → *Expect:* stretch appears at the front of the rear hip without low-back arching.
2. **Add glute bridges.** Lie on your back, feet hip-width, ribs down, and lift hips by squeezing glutes. → *Expect:* hips rise without hamstring cramping or back extension.
3. **Use 90/90 switches.** Sit with both knees bent, rotate both legs side to side, and keep your torso tall. → *Expect:* hips rotate while knees stay comfortable.
4. **Practice adductor rocks.** From hands and knees, extend one leg to the side and rock hips back gently. → *Expect:* inner-thigh stretch appears without twisting the knee.
5. **Rehearse a squat pry.** Hold a support, sit into a comfortable squat, and shift gently side to side with heels down. → *Expect:* hips feel open while knees track over toes.
6. **Stand and walk.** Walk for 1 minute with long relaxed steps and glutes finishing each stride. → *Expect:* hip extension feels easier than before the sequence.
7. **Avoid joint pinching.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop or reduce range if the front of the hip pinches sharply. → *Expect:* all sensations stay muscular and controllable.

## Decision points

- Front hip pinches in deep flexion -> use a higher squat or skip the pry.
- Knees complain during 90/90 -> sit on a cushion and make the angles wider.
- Low back takes over in bridges -> shorten the lift and reset ribs down.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Arching to feel a stretch:** detect low-back compression -> retuck pelvis and squeeze the rear glute.
- **F2 Forcing hip rotation:** detect knee torque -> reduce range and support the hands behind you.
- **F3 Bridge hamstring cramps:** detect back-of-thigh cramping -> bring feet slightly closer and drive through midfoot.
- **F4 Collapsed squat:** detect arches dropping and knees caving -> hold support and press knees over toes.

## Verification

You complete 10 minutes, walk with a longer comfortable stride, and perform a supported squat with heels down, knees tracking over toes, and no sharp hip pinching.

## Variations

- easier: do the sequence with hands on a chair and sit on a cushion for 90/90 work.
- harder: add loaded goblet squat pries or longer bridge holds.
- equipment: use a mat, cushion, chair, or light kettlebell.
- at-home: perform the full sequence beside a couch or sturdy table.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when you keep ranges pain-free. Stop for sharp groin pain, catching, numbness, or worsening symptoms. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
