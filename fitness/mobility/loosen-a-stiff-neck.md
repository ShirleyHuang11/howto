## Goal

You reduce mild neck stiffness by restoring gentle motion, improving shoulder blade position, and avoiding aggressive stretching of sensitive tissues.

## Preconditions

- A chair or standing space with relaxed posture.
- Mild stiffness only; no trauma, severe headache, fever, arm weakness, or numbness.
- Ability to move slowly and stop before pain.

## Steps

1. **Reset your posture.** Sit or stand tall, feet grounded, ribs relaxed, and chin level. → *Expect:* the back of the neck feels long rather than compressed.
2. **Do small neck nods.** Glide the chin slightly back, then nod as if saying yes through a tiny range. → *Expect:* movement happens high in the neck without jaw clenching.
3. **Rotate gently.** Turn your head right and left only to the first mild stretch, keeping shoulders down. → *Expect:* each side feels smoother after several slow reps.
4. **Add side bends without pulling.** Tip one ear toward the same-side shoulder while the opposite shoulder stays heavy. → *Expect:* a mild stretch along the side of the neck, not sharp pain.
5. **Move the shoulder blades.** Perform 10 slow shoulder rolls and 10 scapular retractions, sliding shoulder blades back and down. → *Expect:* upper-trap tension decreases and posture feels easier.
6. **Use heat or gentle self-massage.** Apply warmth or massage the upper traps for 1-2 minutes without pressing on the front of the neck. → *Expect:* muscles feel less guarded.
7. **Screen for warning signs.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop and seek medical care for trauma, severe or sudden headache, dizziness, fever, numbness, weakness, or pain traveling down the arm. → *Expect:* only ordinary muscular stiffness is being addressed.

## Decision points

- Pain increases with motion -> reduce range or stop.
- Stiffness came after an accident -> do not self-treat; get evaluated.
- Desk posture triggers symptoms -> add frequent position changes and monitor height.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Pulling the head hard:** detect hand-assisted yanking -> remove the hand and use active motion.
- **F2 Shrugging:** detect shoulders creeping up -> exhale and let them drop.
- **F3 Moving too fast:** detect dizziness or guarding -> slow down and reduce range.
- **F4 Ignoring nerve signs:** detect tingling or weakness -> stop and seek professional care.

## Verification

You complete 7 minutes of gentle neck and shoulder motion, can turn and side-bend through a comfortable range, and have no sharp pain, dizziness, numbness, or arm symptoms.

## Variations

- easier: perform only chin glides and shoulder rolls.
- harder: add thoracic extension over a chair after symptoms calm.
- equipment: use a warm towel or massage ball only on the upper back, not the front of the neck.
- at-home: do the sequence seated at a desk.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for mild stiffness, but the neck has important nerves and blood vessels. Stop for neurological symptoms, severe headache, trauma, fever, or worsening pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
