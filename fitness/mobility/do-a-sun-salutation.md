## Goal

You complete a controlled sun salutation sequence that links breath with spinal, shoulder, hip, and hamstring movement.

## Preconditions

- A non-slip mat or clear floor space.
- Wrists, shoulders, and low back tolerate basic floor positions.
- Move at a pace where breathing stays controlled.

## Steps

1. **Stand in mountain pose.** Feet grounded, ribs stacked over pelvis, arms relaxed. → *Expect:* posture feels tall and balanced.
2. **Reach overhead on an inhale.** Sweep arms up without shrugging or flaring the ribs. → *Expect:* shoulders flex while the neck stays relaxed.
3. **Fold forward on an exhale.** Hinge at the hips, bend knees as needed, and bring hands toward shins, blocks, or floor. → *Expect:* stretch is in the hamstrings, not a sharp back pull.
4. **Lengthen halfway.** Place hands on shins or thighs and extend the spine forward. → *Expect:* back is long and chest reaches forward.
5. **Step or hop to plank.** [BRANCH: basic, step one foot back at a time | advanced, light hop back] land with shoulders over wrists. → *Expect:* body forms a straight line from head to heels or knees.
6. **Lower with control.** Lower knees first if needed, keep elbows near the ribs, and descend only as far as shoulders stay comfortable. → *Expect:* chest lowers smoothly without shoulder pinching.
7. **Lift into a gentle backbend.** Press into cobra or upward-facing variation with glutes lightly active and ribs controlled. → *Expect:* extension is spread through the spine, not jammed in the low back.
8. **Press back and return to stand.** Move through downward dog or hands-and-knees, step forward, fold, and rise. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for wrist pain, shoulder pinching, dizziness, or sharp back pain. → *Expect:* one complete round flows smoothly with steady breath.

## Decision points

- Wrists hurt in plank -> use fists, blocks, or perform the sequence from an incline.
- Low back pinches in cobra -> lower the chest and use a smaller backbend.
- Hamstrings pull hard in folds -> bend knees more and use blocks.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Breath racing:** detect gasping or rushing -> pause in standing and restart slower.
- **F2 Shoulder collapse:** detect shoulders sinking in plank -> push the floor away.
- **F3 Forced straight knees:** detect low-back rounding and strain -> bend knees.
- **F4 Elbows flaring on the lower:** detect shoulders rolling forward -> lower knees and keep elbows closer.

## Verification

You complete 3 controlled rounds with steady breathing, stable plank alignment, pain-free forward folds, and no wrist, shoulder, or back symptoms.

## Variations

- easier: step instead of hop, lower knees, and use cobra.
- harder: add controlled chaturanga and upward-facing dog only if shoulders tolerate it.
- equipment: use blocks under hands or a mat for grip.
- at-home: do the sequence on a carpet or mat with enough length for plank.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when positions are scaled. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, numbness, or joint pinching. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
