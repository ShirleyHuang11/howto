## Goal

Improve shoulder comfort and range of motion with controlled stretches for the chest, lats, posterior shoulder, and upper back.

## Preconditions

- Wall, doorway, or sturdy surface.
- Shoulders warmed with arm circles, easy rows, or light movement.
- No acute shoulder injury, instability, or unexplained numbness.

## Steps

1. **Check pain-free range.** Raise arms overhead, reach across the body, and rotate gently. → *Expect:* you know which motions are stiff versus painful.
2. **Open the chest in a doorway.** Place forearm on a doorframe with elbow below or near shoulder height, then rotate the torso away slightly. → *Expect:* stretch is felt across the chest, not in the front of the shoulder joint.
3. **Stretch the lats.** Hold a wall or counter, sit hips back, and keep ribs from flaring. → *Expect:* stretch runs along the side body and armpit while neck stays relaxed.
4. **Stretch the posterior shoulder.** Bring one arm across the chest and pull gently above the elbow, keeping the shoulder blade down. → *Expect:* stretch is behind the shoulder without tingling.
5. **Add upper-back rotation.** Do open-book rotations or thread-the-needle slowly. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for numbness, tingling, sharp pain, catching, or a sense of instability. → *Expect:* motion comes from the upper back, not forced shoulder twisting.
6. **Retest overhead reach.** Move through the same check from step 1. → *Expect:* range feels smoother without joint irritation.

## Decision points

- Front shoulder pinches → lower elbow angle or skip that stretch.
- Neck tightens → reduce effort and relax shoulders down.
- Numbness or tingling appears → stop and seek evaluation if it persists.
- Mobility improves but control feels poor → add light strength work afterward.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Forcing end range:** detect grimacing or joint pinching → reduce range immediately.
- **F2 Shrugging:** detect shoulders near ears → exhale and let shoulder blades settle.
- **F3 Rib flare:** detect low-back arch during overhead work → brace lightly and keep ribs down.
- **F4 Pulling on the elbow joint:** detect elbow discomfort during cross-body stretch → hold above the elbow on the upper arm.

## Verification

You completed chest, lat, posterior shoulder, and upper-back stretches for both sides, with smoother overhead reach and no numbness, tingling, sharp pain, or shoulder pinching.

## Variations

- Easier: perform stretches seated with smaller ranges.
- Harder: add controlled shoulder CARs or light banded mobility after stretching.
- Equipment: wall, doorway, dowel, towel, or resistance band.
- At-home: use a doorframe and kitchen counter.

## Safety & privacy

Shoulder stretching should not create joint pain, nerve symptoms, or instability. Stop for numbness, tingling, catching, sharp pain, or weakness. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or clinician before starting if you have a relevant condition.
