## Goal

You improve hamstring flexibility with controlled hip hinges, active range, and mild stretching while avoiding nerve irritation and low-back strain.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- A towel or strap and optional blocks or chair.
- No acute hamstring tear, severe back pain, numbness, or radiating symptoms.

## Steps

1. **Check your hinge.** Stand tall, soften knees, push hips back, and stop at the first hamstring stretch. → *Expect:* spine stays long and stretch is in the back of the thighs.
2. **Use supported forward folds.** Rest hands on a chair, blocks, or shins and breathe for 30 seconds. → *Expect:* tension stays mild and even.
3. **Add active knee extensions.** From the supported fold, bend and straighten each knee slowly for 8 reps. → *Expect:* range increases without bouncing.
4. **Train supine control.** Lie down, hold behind one thigh or use a strap, and straighten the knee until a mild stretch appears. → *Expect:* pelvis stays still and foot does not tingle.
5. **Strengthen the new range.** Do 8 slow bodyweight Romanian deadlift reps, reaching hips back and standing by squeezing glutes. → *Expect:* hamstrings load gently through the available range.
6. **Retest the hinge.** Repeat your standing hinge and compare depth and comfort. → *Expect:* you reach slightly farther or feel less resistance at the same depth.
7. **Avoid nerve tension.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for burning, tingling, electric pain, numbness, or symptoms below the knee. → *Expect:* all sensation remains muscular.

## Decision points

- Back rounds before hamstrings stretch -> elevate hand support and hinge less deeply.
- Stretch feels like nerve tension -> bend the knee and reduce ankle dorsiflexion.
- Flexibility gains vanish quickly -> add light hinge strength work 2-3 times weekly.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Locked-knee forcing:** detect hard knee lock and shaking -> soften knees.
- **F2 Bouncing:** detect momentum at end range -> pause and breathe.
- **F3 Pelvis rotating:** detect one hip hiking in supine work -> lower the leg and square the pelvis.
- **F4 Ignoring soreness:** detect next-day tendon pain near sit bones -> reduce intensity and volume.

## Verification

You complete 12 minutes and perform 8 controlled hip hinges with a long spine, mild hamstring tension, stable pelvis, and no nerve symptoms.

## Variations

- easier: keep knees bent and hands supported on a chair.
- harder: add light dumbbell Romanian deadlifts once bodyweight hinges are clean.
- equipment: use a strap, towel, blocks, chair, or light weights.
- at-home: use a bath towel as a strap and a chair for support.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when stretching is mild and active. Stop for sharp pain, bruising, popping, numbness, tingling, or radiating symptoms. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
