## Goal

You improve squat depth by addressing ankle dorsiflexion, hip control, and trunk position while keeping knees, feet, and spine organized.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- A wall, post, rack upright, or doorframe for support.
- No sharp knee, hip, or back pain during bodyweight squats.

## Steps

1. **Assess your baseline squat.** Squat slowly with feet about shoulder-width and toes slightly turned out. → *Expect:* you identify the first limiter: ankles, hips, balance, or trunk collapse.
2. **Mobilize ankles.** Do knee-to-wall rocks, driving the knee over the toes while the heel stays down. → *Expect:* shin angle improves without the arch collapsing.
3. **Open hips with supported pries.** Hold a support, sit into a comfortable squat, and gently shift weight side to side. → *Expect:* hips settle lower while knees keep tracking over toes.
4. **Use counterbalance squats.** Hold a light object in front, brace lightly, and squat slowly. → *Expect:* torso stays more upright and heels remain grounded.
5. **Pause in your best position.** Hold the lowest clean squat for 3 breaths without relaxing into the joints. → *Expect:* feet stay active and spine stays long.
6. **Practice controlled reps.** Stand fully, then repeat 5-8 slow squats to the same clean depth. → *Expect:* each rep matches depth without knee collapse or heel lift.
7. **Do not force depth under load.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop increasing depth if the low back rounds hard, knees cave, heels lift uncontrollably, or joints pinch. → *Expect:* range improves only where control is maintained.

## Decision points

- Heels lift early -> prioritize ankle rocks and use a small heel wedge temporarily.
- Hips pinch -> widen stance slightly or reduce depth.
- Back rounds at the bottom -> stop just above that point and strengthen there.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Knee cave:** detect knees dropping inward -> press knees over the second or third toes.
- **F2 Arch collapse:** detect feet rolling inward -> tripod the foot: big toe, little toe, heel.
- **F3 Butt wink beyond control:** detect pelvis tucking hard -> use a higher depth and build control.
- **F4 Falling backward:** detect toes lifting and balance loss -> use counterbalance or hold support.

## Verification

You complete 5-8 controlled bodyweight squats to your best clean depth with heels down, knees tracking over toes, active feet, and no sharp hip, knee, or back pain.

## Variations

- easier: hold a doorframe and squat to a box.
- harder: add goblet squats with a light weight after bodyweight control is consistent.
- equipment: use a wedge, box, kettlebell, or rack upright.
- at-home: use a doorframe and a backpack held in front.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk unloaded, but forced depth can irritate knees, hips, or back. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness, or loss of control. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
