## Goal

You move the spine gently through flexion and extension to reduce stiffness and improve awareness of spinal position.

## Preconditions

- A mat or soft floor for hands and knees.
- Wrists, knees, and shoulders tolerate quadruped position.
- No acute spinal injury, severe pain, or neurological symptoms.

## Steps

1. **Set hands and knees.** Place hands under shoulders, knees under hips, and spread fingers. → *Expect:* weight is even through both hands and knees.
2. **Find neutral.** Lengthen from crown of head to tailbone with ribs gently drawn in. → *Expect:* spine feels long, not sagging or rounded.
3. **Move into cat.** Exhale, press the floor away, tuck the tail slightly, and round the upper and lower back. → *Expect:* shoulder blades spread and the back broadens.
4. **Move into cow.** Inhale, tip the pelvis forward, lift the chest, and gently extend the spine. → *Expect:* front body opens without pinching the low back.
5. **Repeat slowly.** Flow for 8-12 reps, initiating each direction from the pelvis and letting the rest of the spine follow. → *Expect:* motion becomes smoother and more coordinated.
6. **Add small circles if comfortable.** Shift hips and ribs in slow circles while keeping pressure even. → *Expect:* side-to-side stiffness decreases without wrist strain.
7. **Stop if symptoms travel.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp back pain, numbness, tingling, or pain radiating into an arm or leg. → *Expect:* the drill feels gentle and relieving.

## Decision points

- Wrists hurt -> use fists, forearms, or hands on a bench.
- Knees hurt -> add padding or do seated spinal flexion and extension.
- Low back pinches in cow -> reduce extension and brace lightly.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Neck overmoving:** detect head throwing up or dropping hard -> keep the neck following the spine gently.
- **F2 Locked elbows:** detect elbow discomfort -> soften the elbows slightly.
- **F3 Sagging into cow:** detect low-back compression -> make the range smaller.
- **F4 Moving only one segment:** detect stiff upper back or pelvis -> initiate from the pelvis and breathe.

## Verification

You complete 8-12 slow reps with even hand and knee pressure, smooth spinal rounding and extension, and no sharp pain or radiating symptoms.

## Variations

- easier: perform seated cat-cow with hands on thighs.
- harder: add thread-the-needle rotations after the basic reps.
- equipment: use a mat, knee pad, or bench.
- at-home: use a folded blanket under knees.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when motion is gentle. Stop for sharp pain, radiating symptoms, numbness, tingling, or recent injury pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
