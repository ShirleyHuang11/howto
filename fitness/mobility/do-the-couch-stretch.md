## Goal

You stretch the hip flexors and quadriceps while keeping the pelvis controlled so the low back does not substitute for hip extension.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- A couch, wall, bench, or padded surface for the rear knee.
- No sharp knee pain or recent hip flexor strain.

## Steps

1. **Pad the rear knee.** Place a folded towel under one knee and put that foot up against a couch or wall. → *Expect:* the knee is comfortable enough to hold position without guarding.
2. **Set the front foot.** Step the other foot forward into a half-kneeling stance with the front shin near vertical. → *Expect:* you feel balanced, not twisted.
3. **Tuck the pelvis gently.** Squeeze the rear-side glute and draw the belt line slightly upward. → *Expect:* the stretch moves to the front of the rear hip and thigh rather than the low back.
4. **Bring the torso upright gradually.** [BRANCH: tight, keep hands on the floor or front thigh | comfortable, lift the torso tall] → *Expect:* stretch intensity increases without back arching.
5. **Breathe and hold.** Hold 45-60 seconds, keeping ribs down and glute lightly active. → *Expect:* the stretch stays steady or eases slightly.
6. **Switch sides carefully.** Come out by leaning forward, lowering the rear foot, and changing legs. → *Expect:* both sides are trained with the same pelvic control.
7. **Avoid forcing the knee.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if the rear knee has sharp pressure, pinching, or instability. → *Expect:* any discomfort remains muscular in the front thigh or hip.

## Decision points

- Low back arches -> move farther from the wall and retuck the pelvis.
- Knee pressure dominates -> add padding or use a standing quad stretch.
- Front hip pinches -> widen stance slightly and reduce depth.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rib flare:** detect chest lifted and low back arched -> exhale, stack ribs over pelvis, and squeeze the rear glute.
- **F2 Knee jammed into hard floor:** detect kneecap pain -> use thicker padding.
- **F3 Foot cramping:** detect rear foot tension -> move slightly away from the wall.
- **F4 Twisted pelvis:** detect one hip rotating open -> square both hip points forward.

## Verification

You hold 45-60 seconds per side with the rear glute active, ribs down, front shin stable, and no sharp knee, hip, or back pain.

## Variations

- easier: move the rear knee farther from the wall or use a standing quad stretch.
- harder: bring the torso upright and lightly reach the same-side arm overhead.
- equipment: use a wall, couch, bench, or pad.
- at-home: use a sofa cushion and folded towel.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when knee pressure and spinal extension are controlled. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, or joint pinching. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
