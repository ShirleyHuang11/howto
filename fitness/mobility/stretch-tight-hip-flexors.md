## Goal

Stretch tight hip flexors while keeping the pelvis controlled so the low back does not take over.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space, mat, or pad for the back knee.
- Warmup from light walking or gentle squats.
- No sharp hip, groin, or low-back pain.

## Steps

1. **Set up half-kneeling.** Place one knee on a pad and the other foot forward, both knees about 90 degrees. → *Expect:* you feel balanced with the front foot flat.
2. **Stack ribs over pelvis.** Gently tuck the pelvis as if bringing belt buckle toward ribs, and lightly brace the abdomen. → *Expect:* low back stops arching before the stretch begins.
3. **Shift forward slightly.** Move the hips forward a few inches without losing the pelvic tuck. → *Expect:* stretch appears at the front of the rear hip or thigh.
4. **Add same-side reach if appropriate.** Reach the arm on the kneeling side overhead and slightly away. → *Expect:* stretch increases along the front of the hip without back pinching.
5. **Hold with glute engaged.** Lightly squeeze the rear-side glute for 20-45 seconds. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp groin pain, hip pinching, numbness, or low-back pain. → *Expect:* front hip stretches while lumbar spine stays quiet.
6. **Switch sides and reassess.** Stand, walk a few steps, and compare hip extension. → *Expect:* stride feels easier and more upright.

## Decision points

- Low back arches → reduce forward shift and retuck pelvis.
- Front knee feels stressed → widen stance or use a chair-supported lunge.
- Hip pinches in front → back out and change angle; do not force it.
- Stretch is too mild → add glute squeeze or overhead reach.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Lumbar extension substitute:** detect ribs flaring and low-back arching → brace and tuck pelvis.
- **F2 Leaning too far:** detect front knee drifting far with balance loss → use a smaller shift.
- **F3 No glute engagement:** detect stretch mostly in low back → squeeze rear glute lightly.
- **F4 Kneeling discomfort:** detect knee pressure → add padding or use standing version.

## Verification

You held 2-3 hip flexor stretches per side for 20-45 seconds with pelvis tucked, rear glute lightly engaged, and no hip pinching or low-back pain.

## Variations

- Easier: standing split-stance hip flexor stretch with hands on a wall.
- Harder: rear foot elevated couch stretch if knees and quads tolerate it.
- Equipment: mat, towel pad, bench, couch, or wall.
- At-home: use a couch cushion under the knee and a wall for balance.

## Safety & privacy

Do not force hip extension through the low back. Stop for sharp groin pain, hip pinching, numbness, or increasing back pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or clinician before starting if you have a relevant condition.
