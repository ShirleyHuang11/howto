## Goal

You prepare the wrists, forearms, and shoulders to tolerate push-up positions with less discomfort and better hand pressure.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- A mat and optional push-up handles or dumbbells.
- No acute wrist sprain, swelling, numbness, or sharp pain with weight bearing.

## Steps

1. **Warm the hands.** Open and close the fingers, circle the wrists, and shake out tension for 60 seconds. → *Expect:* hands feel warmer and less stiff.
2. **Load wrist extension gradually.** Place hands on a table or wall, fingers forward, and lean in lightly. → *Expect:* stretch appears in the forearms without pinching at the wrist.
3. **Progress to quadruped rocks.** On hands and knees, spread fingers, press through knuckles, and rock shoulders slightly past wrists. → *Expect:* pressure is spread across the whole hand, not dumped into the heel of the palm.
4. **Train wrist flexion lightly.** Place backs of hands on the floor only if comfortable and rock back gently. → *Expect:* mild forearm stretch without sharp joint pain.
5. **Add scapular support.** In a high plank from knees or toes, push the floor away so shoulder blades are stable. → *Expect:* wrists feel supported by active shoulders.
6. **Test push-up position.** [BRANCH: uncomfortable, use handles or fists | comfortable, use flat palms] hold 10-20 seconds with hands under shoulders. → *Expect:* wrists tolerate the angle without pain increasing.
7. **Do not push through joint pain.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp wrist pain, numbness, tingling, swelling, or weakness. → *Expect:* discomfort stays mild and decreases after unloading.

## Decision points

- Wrist extension is limited -> elevate hands and build range over weeks.
- Palm pressure hurts -> use push-up handles while continuing mobility work.
- Numbness or tingling appears -> stop and seek professional evaluation if it persists.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Collapsed knuckles:** detect weight only on palm heels -> spread fingers and press knuckles down.
- **F2 Locked elbows with sagging shoulders:** detect shoulder blades sinking -> push the floor away.
- **F3 Too much range too soon:** detect pain that lingers -> return to wall or table loading.
- **F4 Hands too far forward:** detect wrists far ahead of shoulders -> stack shoulders over wrists.

## Verification

You can hold a 10-20 second knee or full plank with hands under shoulders, fingers spread, knuckles grounded, active shoulders, and no sharp wrist pain.

## Variations

- easier: use wall or incline push-up positions.
- harder: add slow wrist rocks in full plank after pain-free holds.
- equipment: use push-up handles, dumbbells, parallettes, or a folded towel.
- at-home: use a sturdy countertop for elevated practice.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when load is gradual. Stop for sharp pain, swelling, numbness, tingling, or loss of grip strength. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
