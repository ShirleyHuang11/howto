## Goal

Move the hips through flexion, extension, rotation, and adduction ranges to improve comfort for squatting, walking, running, and sitting.

## Preconditions

- Mat or clear floor space.
- Hips warmed with light walking, marching, or bodyweight squats.
- No sharp hip, groin, or back pain.

## Steps

1. **Start with hip CARs.** Stand near a wall, brace lightly, and draw slow circles with one knee without moving the trunk. → *Expect:* motion comes from the hip socket while pelvis stays mostly still.
2. **Do 90-90 switches.** Sit with both knees bent, rotate both legs side to side, and use hands behind you if needed. → *Expect:* hips rotate without knee pain.
3. **Add a half-kneeling hip flexor stretch.** Tuck pelvis, squeeze rear glute, and shift forward slightly. → *Expect:* front of the rear hip stretches without low-back arching.
4. **Use an adductor rock-back.** Start on hands and knees, extend one leg to the side, and rock hips back slowly. → *Expect:* inner thigh stretches while spine stays neutral.
5. **Finish with figure-four glute stretch.** Lie on your back and pull one thigh toward you with the other ankle crossed over it. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp groin pain, knee pain, hip pinching, numbness, or tingling. → *Expect:* stretch is felt in the outer hip, not the knee.
6. **Retest a squat or stride.** Stand and move through an easy squat or walking lunge pattern. → *Expect:* hips feel freer and movement stays controlled.

## Decision points

- Knee hurts in 90-90 → widen angles, use hands, or skip it.
- Low back arches in hip flexor stretch → reduce range and retuck pelvis.
- Hip pinches in flexion → back out and use smaller circles.
- Routine feels easy → slow the tempo and add active holds.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Moving from the spine:** detect torso twisting during hip CARs → brace and shrink the circle.
- **F2 Knee torque:** detect knee discomfort in rotation drills → reposition and support with hands.
- **F3 Forcing deep range:** detect pinching or guarding → use smaller ranges and stop before pain.
- **F4 Holding breath:** detect bracing too hard → breathe slowly through each rep.

## Verification

You completed 10-20 minutes covering hip rotation, flexion, extension, adduction, and glute stretching, with controlled pelvis position and no sharp hip, knee, or back pain.

## Variations

- Easier: perform all drills supported by a wall, chair, or hands.
- Harder: add active end-range lifts in 90-90 positions.
- Equipment: mat, yoga block, wall, chair, or pad.
- At-home: complete the routine on a carpeted floor with a folded towel for knee padding.

## Safety & privacy

Hip mobility should not be forced through pinching or knee torque. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, tingling, catching, or symptoms that worsen after the routine. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or clinician before starting if you have a relevant condition.
