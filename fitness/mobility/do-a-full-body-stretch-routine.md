## Goal

Move through a balanced full-body stretch routine that improves comfort and range of motion without forcing joints.

## Preconditions

- Clear floor space and optional mat.
- Body warm from light walking, easy cycling, or a few minutes of movement.
- No acute injury that makes stretching painful.

## Steps

1. **Start with gentle breathing.** Lie or sit tall, inhale through the nose, and exhale slowly while relaxing the jaw and shoulders. → *Expect:* muscle tone drops and stretching feels less guarded.
2. **Stretch calves and hamstrings.** Use a wall calf stretch, then hinge at the hips with a soft knee for hamstrings. → *Expect:* stretch is felt in the back of the lower leg and thigh, not sharp behind the knee.
3. **Open hip flexors and glutes.** Do a half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, then a figure-four stretch. Keep pelvis level and ribs down. → *Expect:* front hip and outer hip stretch without low-back pinching.
4. **Mobilize the spine.** Move through cat-cow and open-book rotations slowly. → *Expect:* motion spreads through the upper back without forcing the neck.
5. **Stretch chest and shoulders.** Use a doorway chest stretch and cross-body shoulder stretch with relaxed neck. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if you feel numbness, tingling, joint pinching, or sharp pain. → *Expect:* stretch is broad across muscle, not inside the shoulder joint.
6. **Finish with easy movement.** Stand, walk, and reassess posture and breathing. → *Expect:* motion feels freer but stable, not overstretched or weak.

## Decision points

- Stretch feels sharp or nervy → back off immediately and change angle.
- One side is tighter → spend extra time there without forcing symmetry.
- You are cold or stiff → add more light movement before static holds.
- Flexibility is improving → increase hold time before increasing intensity.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Forcing range:** detect breath-holding or grimacing → reduce intensity to mild-moderate stretch.
- **F2 Stretching joints instead of muscles:** detect pinching or pressure inside a joint → reposition or skip that stretch.
- **F3 Rounded low back in hamstring work:** detect lumbar flexion instead of hip hinge → bend knees and hinge from hips.
- **F4 Neck tension during shoulder stretches:** detect shrugging → lower shoulders and relax the jaw.

## Verification

You completed 15-25 minutes covering calves, hamstrings, hips, spine, chest, and shoulders, with slow breathing, no sharp pain, and improved comfortable range.

## Variations

- Easier: hold each stretch 15-20 seconds and use supports.
- Harder: hold 45-60 seconds or add active end-range contractions.
- Equipment: mat, yoga strap, towel, wall, or chair.
- At-home: perform the whole routine beside a wall and doorway.

## Safety & privacy

Stretching should feel like tolerable muscle tension, not pain. Stop for numbness, tingling, sharp pain, dizziness, or joint pinching. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor or clinician before starting if you have a relevant condition.
