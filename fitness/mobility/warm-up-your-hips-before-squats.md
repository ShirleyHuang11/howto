## Goal

You prepare the hips for squatting by activating glutes, opening hip flexion and rotation, and rehearsing the exact squat pattern you will train.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- Clear space, optional mini-band, and a rack, post, or doorframe for support.
- No sharp hip, knee, or back pain with unloaded squats.

## Steps

1. **Activate glutes with bridges.** Do 10 controlled glute bridges, ribs down and feet hip-width. → *Expect:* glutes work more than hamstrings or low back.
2. **Open hip flexors.** Hold a half-kneeling hip flexor position for 5 breaths per side with the rear glute squeezed. → *Expect:* front of the rear hip stretches without back arching.
3. **Mobilize rotation.** Do 6 slow 90/90 switches or supported hip airplanes at a small range. → *Expect:* hips rotate while knees stay comfortable.
4. **Practice lateral control.** [BRANCH: with band, do 10 mini-band side steps | no band, do 10 slow lateral lunges] keep feet active. → *Expect:* hips control knee position without collapsing inward.
5. **Pry the squat.** Hold support, sit into a comfortable squat, and shift gently side to side. → *Expect:* hips settle lower while heels stay down.
6. **Rehearse warmup squats.** Do 5-8 bodyweight squats with the stance you plan to use for training. → *Expect:* depth and knee tracking are consistent.
7. **Do not load poor positions.** ⚠️ *Safety:* if hip pinching, knee cave, heel lift, or back rounding appears unloaded, fix that before adding weight. → *Expect:* loaded work begins only after clean bodyweight reps.

## Decision points

- Hips feel pinchy at depth -> widen stance slightly or reduce depth.
- Knees drift inward -> add glute activation and slow the descent.
- Squat still feels cold -> repeat bodyweight reps before loading.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bridge from the back:** detect low-back cramping -> tuck pelvis slightly and squeeze glutes first.
- **F2 Band steps with foot drag:** detect sloppy steps -> keep toes forward and control each step.
- **F3 Unsupported squat collapse:** detect heels lifting and torso folding -> hold a support.
- **F4 Forcing hip rotation:** detect knee torque in 90/90 -> reduce range and use hand support.

## Verification

You complete the 10-minute sequence and perform 5-8 bodyweight squats to your training depth with heels down, knees tracking over toes, active hips, and no sharp pain.

## Variations

- easier: use only bridges, hip flexor stretch, and supported squats.
- harder: add light goblet squats as the final rehearsal.
- equipment: use a mini-band, rack upright, post, or light kettlebell.
- at-home: use a doorframe and bodyweight lateral lunges.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when unloaded and controlled. Stop for sharp hip pinching, knee pain, back pain, numbness, or loss of control. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
