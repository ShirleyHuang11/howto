## Goal

You cool down after a run and stretch calves, hip flexors, quads, hamstrings, and glutes without irritating tired tissues.

## Preconditions

- Finish the run with no acute injury, chest symptoms, or dizziness.
- A wall, curb, bench, or clear floor space.
- Hydration available if the run was hot, long, or intense.

## Steps

1. **Walk before stretching.** Walk 3-5 minutes until breathing comes down. → *Expect:* heart rate drops gradually instead of stopping abruptly.
2. **Stretch calves.** Use a wall calf stretch with back heel down and toes forward, then bend the back knee for the deeper calf. → *Expect:* stretch is muscular, not sharp at the Achilles.
3. **Stretch hip flexors.** Step into a half-kneeling or standing lunge, tuck the pelvis, and squeeze the rear glute. → *Expect:* stretch is at the front of the rear hip.
4. **Stretch quads.** Stand tall, hold one ankle or pant leg, and keep knees near each other with ribs down. → *Expect:* front thigh stretches without low-back arching.
5. **Stretch hamstrings.** Put one heel on a low step, soften the knee, and hinge from the hips. → *Expect:* back of thigh stretches while spine stays long.
6. **Stretch glutes.** Use a standing or seated figure-four stretch with the foot flexed. → *Expect:* outer hip stretches without knee pressure.
7. **Keep it gentle.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop for sharp pain, limping, swelling, or pain that worsens after cooling down. → *Expect:* legs feel calmer, not more irritated.

## Decision points

- You finished a hard workout -> keep stretches shorter and prioritize food, fluids, and easy walking.
- Achilles or tendon pain appears -> avoid aggressive calf stretching and get persistent pain assessed.
- You are cramping -> reduce stretch intensity and rehydrate.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Stopping suddenly:** detect lightheadedness after the run -> walk first before floor stretches.
- **F2 Overstretching tired tissue:** detect shaking or pain -> back off to mild intensity.
- **F3 Arching in quad stretch:** detect low-back compression -> tuck pelvis and lower the knee.
- **F4 Twisting the knee in glute stretch:** detect knee pressure -> flex the foot and reduce depth.

## Verification

You walk 3-5 minutes and complete 20-45 seconds per major stretch on each side with steady breathing, no sharp pain, and no limp afterward.

## Variations

- easier: stretch standing while holding a wall.
- harder: add foam rolling after the basic cool-down if it feels useful.
- equipment: use a curb, bench, wall, strap, or foam roller.
- at-home: walk indoors for a few minutes before stretching on a mat.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when gentle. Stop for chest symptoms, dizziness, sharp pain, swelling, limping, or suspected injury. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
