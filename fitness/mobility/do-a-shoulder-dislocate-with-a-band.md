## Goal

You improve shoulder flexion and extension tolerance by moving a band around the body with controlled shoulder blades and ribs.

## Preconditions

- Warm up first: `fitness/do-a-dynamic-warmup`.
- A light resistance band, PVC pipe, broomstick, or towel.
- No current shoulder dislocation, instability, sharp impingement pain, or recent surgery.

## Steps

1. **Choose a wide grip.** Hold a light band with hands much wider than shoulders and elbows straight but not locked. → *Expect:* the band has light tension and shoulders feel relaxed.
2. **Stack ribs over pelvis.** Stand tall, squeeze glutes lightly, and keep the front ribs down. → *Expect:* your low back does not arch to fake shoulder range.
3. **Raise the band overhead.** Move slowly from thighs to overhead while shoulder blades rotate upward. → *Expect:* arms pass overhead without shrugging hard.
4. **Continue behind the body only if smooth.** Let the band travel behind you until it reaches the back of the hips or your comfortable limit. → *Expect:* stretch is broad across chest and shoulders, not sharp in the joint.
5. **Return the same way.** Bring the band back overhead to the front with steady control. → *Expect:* both directions feel symmetrical and unhurried.
6. **Adjust grip width.** [BRANCH: too intense, widen grip | too easy, narrow grip slightly] keep reps pain-free. → *Expect:* range challenges the shoulders without compensation.
7. **Stop for instability or pinching.** ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if the shoulder feels like it may slip, catches sharply, or pain increases with each pass. → *Expect:* only mild stretching and muscle work occur.

## Decision points

- Low back arches -> widen grip and brace lightly.
- Shoulder pinches overhead -> stop before that point or use wall slides instead.
- One side lags badly -> keep the grip wide and move at the tighter side's pace.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Narrowing too soon:** detect elbow bend or pain -> widen the grip.
- **F2 Rib flare:** detect belly forward and back arch -> exhale and reset posture.
- **F3 Fast swinging:** detect momentum carrying the band -> slow each pass to 3-4 seconds.
- **F4 Shrugging:** detect neck tension -> lower the shoulders and reduce range.

## Verification

You complete 8-12 slow band passes with straight arms, ribs down, no shoulder pinching, and no feeling of instability.

## Variations

- easier: use a towel or very wide grip and stop at overhead.
- harder: narrow the grip slightly only after pain-free full passes.
- equipment: use a band, PVC pipe, broomstick, or towel.
- at-home: use a towel if no band is available.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for healthy shoulders when range is not forced. Stop for sharp pain, catching, numbness, or instability. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
