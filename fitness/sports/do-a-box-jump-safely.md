## Goal

You perform box jumps with a stable takeoff, quiet landing, and controlled step-down so the drill builds power without careless impact.

## Preconditions

- A sturdy box that will not slide, starting below knee height.
- Flat shoes, clear floor space, and a warmup with squats, hinges, calf raises, and easy jumps.
- Pain-free knees, hips, ankles, and low back.

## Steps

1. **Choose a conservative height.** Pick a box you can land on with knees slightly bent, not tucked to your chest. → *Expect:* the height feels easy enough to land quietly.
2. **Stand close but not cramped.** Set feet hip-width about one foot-length from the box. → *Expect:* you can swing arms without hitting the box.
3. **Dip into an athletic quarter squat.** Push hips back, keep chest proud, and let arms swing behind you. → *Expect:* knees track over toes and heels stay down.
4. **Jump through the floor.** Extend ankles, knees, and hips together while swinging arms forward. → *Expect:* your body rises as one unit, not from a knee tuck alone.
5. **Land softly on the box.** Place both feet fully on top, knees bent, hips back, and knees aligned over toes. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if you land with locked knees, knees caving, heels hanging off, or stumble. → *Expect:* the landing is quiet and balanced.
6. **Stand tall to finish.** Extend hips fully after landing before leaving the box. → *Expect:* the rep ends in control, not in a crouch.
7. **Step down, do not jump down.** Step one foot then the other to the floor to reduce impact. → *Expect:* knees and ankles feel fresh for the next rep.
8. **Keep reps low quality-high.** Do 2 to 4 sets of 3 to 5 jumps with full rest. → *Expect:* every jump looks as crisp as the first.

## Decision points

- Landing is loud or deep → lower the box or reduce reps.
- Knees cave inward → pause box jumps and practice squats and low pogo jumps.
- You need a huge knee tuck to make it → the box is too high for power training.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Ego height:** detect landing in a deep squat → use a lower box and jump higher, not farther.
- **F2 Forward crash:** detect shins hitting or body falling onto the box → stand slightly farther back and drive upward.
- **F3 Knee valgus:** detect knees collapsing inward → cue knees over second and third toes and reduce height.
- **F4 Jumping down:** detect extra impact on the floor → step down every rep.

## Verification

You complete 3 sets of 5 box jumps on a conservative box with quiet two-foot landings, full-foot contact, knees tracking over toes, upright control, and step-downs after every rep.

## Variations

- `easier`: do step-ups or low snap-down landings before full box jumps.
- `harder`: use a slightly higher box only when landings remain quiet and stable.
- `equipment`: use a soft plyo box or stable wooden box that cannot tip.
- `at-home`: use stairs only for step-ups, not jumps, unless the surface is wide, stable, and clear.

## Safety & privacy

Box jumps carry fall and joint-impact risk. Stop for sharp pain, unstable landings, toe catches, dizziness, or fatigue that changes mechanics, and keep the box height conservative. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
