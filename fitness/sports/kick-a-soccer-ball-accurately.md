## Goal

You pass or shoot a soccer ball more accurately by planting well, locking the ankle, striking the correct surface, and following through to the target.

## Preconditions

- A soccer ball, flat field or turf, and a safe target area.
- Shoes with enough traction for the surface.
- Space clear of people, windows, and obstacles.

## Steps

1. **Choose the kick type.** [BRANCH: inside-foot pass for accuracy | instep drive for a firmer shot] → *Expect:* you know which part of the foot will strike the ball.
2. **Approach at a slight angle.** Take controlled steps toward the ball rather than running straight through it. → *Expect:* your final step gives room for the kicking leg to swing.
3. **Plant beside the ball.** Place the non-kicking foot next to the ball, toes pointing at the target. → *Expect:* the plant foot line predicts the ball's direction.
4. **Set the kicking ankle.** Lock the ankle firm and keep the toe position matched to the strike. → *Expect:* the foot feels like a solid striking surface.
5. **Strike the center line.** Hit through the middle of the ball for a low accurate pass, or slightly below center for controlled lift. → *Expect:* the ball travels without wobbling wildly.
6. **Keep head steady through contact.** Watch the foot meet the ball before looking up. → *Expect:* contact feels clean and centered.
7. **Follow through at the target.** Let the kicking leg continue in the intended direction. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if groin, hip flexor, knee, or ankle pain appears. → *Expect:* your body finishes balanced over the plant leg.
8. **Repeat from short range.** Make 10 accurate passes before increasing distance or power. → *Expect:* accuracy holds before intensity rises.

## Decision points

- Ball goes wide → check plant foot direction before changing the swing.
- Ball pops up unintentionally → strike closer to center and keep chest slightly over the ball.
- Contact hurts toes → use the laces or inside foot, not the toe tip.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Loose ankle:** detect weak or painful contact → lock the ankle before the swing.
- **F2 Plant too far back:** detect ball lifting or mishit → plant beside the ball, not behind it.
- **F3 Leaning away:** detect high, uncontrolled kicks → bring chest over the ball.
- **F4 Looking up early:** detect topped or sliced contact → keep eyes on contact.

## Verification

You complete 20 kicks from short range with a stable plant foot, locked ankle, eyes through contact, balanced follow-through, and at least 15 balls reaching a target gate or marked zone.

## Variations

- `easier`: use inside-foot passes to a large wall target from close range.
- `harder`: strike a moving ball or use a smaller target gate.
- `equipment`: use cones, bottles, or bags as target gates.
- `at-home`: use a soft ball against a safe wall or practice plant-foot positioning without kicking.

## Safety & privacy

Kick only in a clear area and stop for sharp groin, hip, knee, ankle, or foot pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
