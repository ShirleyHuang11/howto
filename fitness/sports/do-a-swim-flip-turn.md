## Goal

You perform a freestyle flip turn that keeps momentum into the wall, plants both feet, and pushes off in a streamlined position.

## Preconditions

- A supervised lap pool with enough lane space and a visible wall or T marker.
- Comfort swimming freestyle and somersaulting in water.
- Goggles and a clear lane free of other swimmers close behind.

## Steps

1. **Approach on freestyle rhythm.** Swim toward the wall at controlled speed, watching the pool floor T rather than lifting your head. → *Expect:* your head stays neutral and speed carries into the wall.
2. **Take the last stroke.** When your head passes the T, take one final stroke and leave both arms near your sides. → *Expect:* your hands are by your hips as the turn begins.
3. **Tuck the chin and somersault.** Drop the head, pull knees toward chest, and let momentum rotate you forward. → *Expect:* hips rise over the head without pushing down with the hands.
4. **Plant both feet on the wall.** Place feet about hip-width apart, knees bent, toes pointing sideways or slightly upward. → *Expect:* both soles contact the wall at the same time.
5. **Set streamline before push-off.** Bring arms overhead, squeeze ears between upper arms, and stack hands. → *Expect:* your body is long before the legs drive.
6. **Push off on your back or side.** Drive powerfully from both legs and rotate toward your stomach during the glide. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop practicing if you hit the wall with your head, feel disoriented, or cannot find the surface. → *Expect:* you leave the wall in a narrow line without bending at the waist.
7. **Dolphin kick or flutter into breakout.** Add a few small kicks, then start the first stroke before slowing too much. → *Expect:* you surface smoothly and resume freestyle rhythm.
8. **Reset distance if needed.** Adjust the final stroke count so you neither jam the wall nor reach for it. → *Expect:* foot plant feels close and compact, not cramped or stretched.

## Decision points

- Feet miss the wall → start the flip closer or delay the tuck less.
- You land too deep → tuck tighter and push off horizontally, not downward.
- You lose air → exhale gently through the nose during rotation.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Head lift before turn:** detect looking forward at the wall → keep eyes on the floor marker and trust the count.
- **F2 Wide, slow somersault:** detect legs swinging around sideways → tuck knees tighter and keep rotation forward.
- **F3 Pushing before streamline:** detect arms still moving during push-off → pause the drill until arms lock overhead before extending legs.
- **F4 Crooked push-off:** detect crossing lane lines → plant both feet evenly and squeeze the streamline.

## Verification

You complete 8 flip turns in a supervised lane with no head contact, both feet planted on the wall, a tight streamline before push-off, and a smooth breakout back into freestyle.

## Variations

- `easier`: practice somersaults away from the wall, then add a wall plant from a slow glide.
- `harder`: do flip turns at race pace while maintaining the same stroke count into the wall.
- `equipment`: use fins only for early rhythm practice, then remove them.
- `at-home`: no true at-home option; use a supervised pool.

## Safety & privacy

Lap swimming and underwater rotation carry water and collision risks. Practice in a supervised pool, leave space from other swimmers, avoid prolonged underwater breath-holding, and stop for dizziness, panic, headache, or neck pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
