## Goal

You hit a tennis backhand with early preparation, stable spacing, clean contact, and a balanced follow-through.

## Preconditions

- A tennis racket, tennis balls, court or hitting wall, and appropriate shoes.
- Enough space around you for a full swing.
- Warm shoulders, wrists, hips, and trunk rotation.

## Steps

1. **Choose your backhand style.** [BRANCH: two-handed backhand for stability | one-handed backhand for reach and slice/topspin options] → *Expect:* grip and contact point match the chosen style.
2. **Split step before the feed.** Hop lightly as the opponent or partner strikes the ball. → *Expect:* you land ready to move either direction.
3. **Turn the shoulders early.** Unit turn with the racket taken back before the ball bounces. → *Expect:* your chest is sideways to the net before the swing starts.
4. **Move to proper spacing.** Use small adjustment steps so the ball is not jammed into your body. → *Expect:* contact will happen slightly in front of the lead hip.
5. **Swing low to high for topspin.** Drop the racket head below the ball and drive through the back of it. → *Expect:* the ball clears the net with forward spin.
6. **Keep the head steady at contact.** Watch the contact zone and avoid pulling up early. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if wrist, elbow, or shoulder pain appears. → *Expect:* contact feels centered on the strings.
7. **Follow through fully.** Finish high and balanced, with hips and shoulders rotating naturally. → *Expect:* the racket path continues toward the target before wrapping.
8. **Recover to ready position.** Push back toward the middle after the shot. → *Expect:* you are ready for the next ball.

## Decision points

- Ball flies long → add topspin and check that the racket face is not open.
- Ball hits the net → swing from lower to higher and finish through the ball.
- Contact feels jammed → prepare earlier and create more side spacing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Late shoulder turn:** detect rushed arm-only swings → call "turn" as soon as the ball leaves the opponent.
- **F2 Open racket face:** detect floating shots → adjust grip and keep strings slightly closed through contact.
- **F3 Pulling the head:** detect mishits off the frame → keep eyes in the contact zone longer.
- **F4 Overreaching:** detect loss of balance → move feet first, then swing.

## Verification

You hit 20 backhands from controlled feeds with early shoulder turn, contact in front, balanced finish, and at least 12 balls landing past the service line and inside the singles court.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a two-handed backhand from slow feeds or a wall.
- `harder`: hit cross-court and down-the-line targets from moving feeds.
- `equipment`: use a grip size that lets the wrist stay relaxed without squeezing.
- `at-home`: shadow-swing the unit turn and follow-through in clear space.

## Safety & privacy

Use court shoes on court surfaces, keep spacing from other players, and stop for sharp wrist, elbow, shoulder, back, hip, knee, or ankle pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
