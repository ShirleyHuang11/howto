## Goal

You roll a golf putt on your intended line with stable posture, centered contact, and distance control.

## Preconditions

- A putter, golf balls, and a practice green or flat putting mat.
- Safe space where rolling balls will not trip anyone.
- Basic ability to stand comfortably in a slight hip hinge.

## Steps

1. **Read the putt.** Look from behind the ball toward the target and choose a start line. → *Expect:* you have one clear aim point.
2. **Set the putter face first.** Place the face square to the start line before setting your feet. → *Expect:* the face points where you intend the ball to start.
3. **Build a stable stance.** Feet about hip-width, eyes near over the ball, arms relaxed from the shoulders. → *Expect:* posture feels still and balanced.
4. **Make a pendulum stroke.** Move the putter with shoulders and upper torso, keeping wrists quiet. → *Expect:* the backswing and follow-through are smooth and similar in rhythm.
5. **Strike the center of the face.** Keep the head steady until after contact. → *Expect:* the ball starts rolling without hopping or skidding excessively.
6. **Control distance with stroke length.** Make longer strokes for longer putts while keeping tempo consistent. [BRANCH: easier, practice 3-foot putts | harder, lag putt from 20-30 feet] → *Expect:* the ball finishes close even when it misses.
7. **Hold the finish.** Keep the putter low and still after impact. ⚠️ *Safety:* keep balls and clubs away from walkways. → *Expect:* the start line reflects the face angle at impact.

## Decision points

- Ball starts off line → check putter face alignment before changing stroke.
- Ball consistently short → lengthen the stroke slightly without hitting harder.
- Ball rolls past too far → shorten the backswing and keep tempo calm.
- Wrists flip → reduce grip pressure and rock shoulders.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Head lift:** detect pulled or pushed putts after watching the ball too soon → keep eyes on the original ball spot after contact.
- **F2 Wrist jab:** detect inconsistent speed → make small shoulder-driven strokes.
- **F3 Face misalignment:** detect good roll on wrong line → aim the face before placing feet.
- **F4 Decelerating:** detect weak, wobbly rolls → use a shorter backswing and continue through the ball.

## Verification

You rolled 20 putts with the face aimed first, stable posture, quiet wrists, centered contact, and at least 15 balls starting on or very near the intended line.

## Variations

- easier: practice straight 3-foot putts on a flat surface.
- harder: add breaking putts and lag distance targets.
- equipment: use a putting gate or two tees for start-line feedback.
- at-home: use a putting mat or carpet with a safe backstop.

## Safety & privacy

Putting has low physical risk, but balls and clubs can create trip or impact hazards. Keep the area clear and stop if back or wrist discomfort persists. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
