## Goal

You field a ground ball with low body position, soft hands, clean transfer, and a balanced throwing setup.

## Preconditions

- A glove, baseball or softball, and a flat practice area.
- A partner, coach, or wall for controlled ground balls.
- Warm legs, hips, shoulders, elbows, and wrists.

## Steps

1. **Start in ready position.** Feet wider than hips, knees bent, chest forward, glove out in front. → *Expect:* you can move left, right, or forward quickly.
2. **Move to the ball.** Take short steps to get your body in front of the grounder when possible. → *Expect:* the ball is centered between your feet rather than reaching far to the side.
3. **Get low early.** Lower the hips, keep the back flat, and place the glove on the ground with fingers down. → *Expect:* there is no gap under the glove for the ball to pass through.
4. **Use two hands.** Keep the throwing hand above or beside the glove to secure the ball. → *Expect:* the ball enters the pocket and is covered immediately.
5. **Funnel to the center.** Bring the glove and ball toward the chest as the feet prepare to throw. → *Expect:* the transfer path is short and controlled.
6. **Step into the throw.** Replace the feet, point the front shoulder toward target, and throw from balance. [BRANCH: easier, roll slow grounders | harder, add forehand/backhand approaches] → *Expect:* the throw starts after secure possession.
7. **Recover after the play.** Follow the throw and reset ready. ⚠️ *Safety:* avoid bad-hop drills without protective gear or supervision. → *Expect:* you finish balanced and aware of the next ball.

## Decision points

- Ball goes under the glove → get lower earlier and set glove fingers on the ground.
- Ball pops out → soften the hands and cover with the throwing hand.
- You field on your heels → widen stance and keep weight on the balls of the feet.
- Bad hops are frequent → slow the drill or move to a smoother surface.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Glove too high:** detect balls rolling beneath it → start with the glove touching the ground.
- **F2 Stiff hands:** detect rebounds out of the pocket → give slightly with the ball and funnel inward.
- **F3 Reaching across the body:** detect awkward catches outside the feet → move the feet first.
- **F4 Rushed transfer:** detect drops before throwing → secure the ball, then transfer.

## Verification

You cleanly fielded 15 controlled ground balls with low hips, glove on the ground, two-hand securement, clean funnel to the chest, and balanced throwing footwork.

## Variations

- easier: use slow rollers from short distance.
- harder: add lateral movement, forehand, backhand, or faster hops.
- equipment: use a softer training ball for beginners.
- at-home: roll a soft ball against a wall only where impact marks are acceptable.

## Safety & privacy

Ground balls can hit fingers, shins, face, or teeth if hops are unpredictable. Use appropriate balls, keep the field clear, and stop for sharp pain or vision issues after impact. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
