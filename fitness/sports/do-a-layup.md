## Goal

You perform a controlled basketball layup using the correct footwork, upward knee drive, and soft finish off the backboard.

## Preconditions

- A basketball hoop with clear space around the lane.
- A basketball and shoes with court traction.
- Basic dribbling control; warm up ankles, knees, hips, and shoulders.

## Steps

1. **Start at a 45-degree angle.** Stand outside the lane on the right side for a right-hand layup or left side for a left-hand layup. → *Expect:* you can see the square on the backboard clearly.
2. **Approach under control.** Take a slow dribble toward the basket with shoulders facing the backboard. → *Expect:* speed feels manageable before the gather.
3. **Gather the ball.** For a right-hand layup, gather as the right foot steps, then step left; reverse for the left side. → *Expect:* you avoid traveling and feel a clear one-two rhythm.
4. **Jump off the outside foot.** Drive the inside knee upward and lift toward the rim. → *Expect:* the body rises vertically instead of drifting under the backboard.
5. **Finish with the outside hand.** Extend the shooting hand and place the ball softly off the upper corner of the square. [BRANCH: easier, practice from one step without dribble | harder, add a defender or speed] → *Expect:* the ball kisses the backboard and drops toward the rim.
6. **Land balanced.** Land on two feet or the takeoff foot with knees bent and eyes up. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if the landing area is crowded or slippery. → *Expect:* you can decelerate without stumbling.
7. **Practice both sides.** Repeat on the left using left-hand finish and mirrored footwork. → *Expect:* both sides follow the same rhythm even if one is less accurate.

## Decision points

- You jump from the wrong foot → slow to walking pace and rehearse the final two steps.
- Ball hits too hard → reduce speed and use a softer wrist finish.
- You drift under the rim → jump upward earlier and aim higher on the backboard.
- Contact makes you lose control → practice without defense until footwork is automatic.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Traveling on the gather:** detect extra steps after picking up the ball → count "gather, one, two" slowly.
- **F2 Same-side knee stays down:** detect a flat, weak jump → drive the opposite knee up with the finish hand.
- **F3 Finishing with the inside hand:** detect exposed ball near defenders → use the outside hand away from the middle.
- **F4 Hard backboard hit:** detect rebounds flying long → slow the approach and release with softer fingers.

## Verification

You made or cleanly attempted 10 right-side and 10 left-side layups with legal footwork, outside-hand finish, upward knee drive, soft backboard contact, and controlled landings.

## Variations

- easier: start from one step and no dribble.
- harder: add a live dribble, speed, or light defensive pressure.
- equipment: use a lower hoop or smaller ball for youth players.
- at-home: rehearse footwork without a ball in a driveway or hallway with enough clearance.

## Safety & privacy

Layups involve jumping near other players, so check the lane before taking off and land under control. Stop for ankle, knee, or hip pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
