## Goal

You pass a volleyball with a stable platform, leg-driven lift, and controlled ball direction.

## Preconditions

- A volleyball and open court, gym, or grass space.
- A partner or wall for gentle tosses.
- Warm wrists, shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles.

## Steps

1. **Start in ready posture.** Feet wider than hips, knees bent, hips back, arms relaxed in front. → *Expect:* you can shuffle before the ball arrives.
2. **Move behind the ball.** Shuffle so the ball comes to your midline rather than reaching late. → *Expect:* shoulders face the target before contact.
3. **Build the platform.** Place one hand over the other or thumbs side by side, straighten elbows, and press forearms together. → *Expect:* the forearms form a flat, even surface.
4. **Angle to target.** Tilt the platform slightly toward where you want the ball to travel. → *Expect:* the ball path changes with platform angle, not a swinging arm motion.
5. **Lift with the legs.** Contact the ball on the forearms and extend knees and hips gently. [BRANCH: easier, receive soft underhand tosses | harder, pass served balls] → *Expect:* the ball rises smoothly without a hard arm swing.
6. **Hold the platform through contact.** Keep elbows locked and shoulders quiet until the ball leaves. → *Expect:* the ball does not spin wildly off uneven arms.
7. **Reset quickly.** Separate hands, move feet, and prepare for the next ball. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if wrist, thumb, or shoulder pain becomes sharp. → *Expect:* you return to ready posture immediately.

## Decision points

- Ball goes too far → reduce leg drive and soften the platform angle.
- Ball dies short → move feet earlier and lift with legs.
- Ball sprays sideways → square shoulders and even out the forearms.
- Forearms hurt excessively → reduce ball speed and volume.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Swinging arms:** detect hard, low-control passes → freeze the platform and use legs.
- **F2 Bent elbows:** detect ball popping unpredictably → straighten elbows before contact.
- **F3 Late feet:** detect reaching outside the body → shuffle first, then pass.
- **F4 Uneven platform:** detect side spin → align thumbs and press forearms together.

## Verification

You passed 20 tosses with feet behind the ball, straight-elbow platform, forearm contact, leg-driven lift, and at least 12 balls traveling toward the intended target.

## Variations

- easier: use balloon or beach ball progressions for timing.
- harder: receive serves or pass to a moving setter target.
- equipment: use a softer training volleyball for beginners.
- at-home: practice platform shape and gentle self-tosses in a safe outdoor area.

## Safety & privacy

Volleyball passing can bruise forearms and irritate wrists, thumbs, or shoulders if contact is hard or frequent. Stop for sharp pain and reduce speed for beginners. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
