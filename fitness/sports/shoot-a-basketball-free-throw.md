## Goal

You shoot free throws with a repeatable stance, smooth force from the legs, and clean follow-through toward the rim.

## Preconditions

- A regulation or practice basketball hoop, a basketball, and a marked free-throw line.
- Court space free of loose balls around your feet.
- Warm shoulders, wrists, hips, and ankles before repeated shots.

## Steps

1. **Set your stance.** Place the shooting-side foot slightly ahead, toes toward the rim, feet about shoulder-width. → *Expect:* you feel balanced without leaning sideways.
2. **Grip the ball consistently.** Put the shooting hand under and slightly behind the ball, guide hand on the side, fingers spread. → *Expect:* the ball rests on finger pads, not jammed into the palm.
3. **Find the target.** Pick the back-center of the rim or a consistent visual cue. → *Expect:* your eyes settle before the shot begins.
4. **Load with the legs.** Bend ankles, knees, and hips slightly while keeping chest tall. → *Expect:* weight stays centered over both feet.
5. **Lift and release in one motion.** Extend the legs, raise the elbow under the ball, and release with wrist flexion and backspin. → *Expect:* the ball leaves the index and middle fingers with a high arc.
6. **Hold the follow-through.** Keep the shooting arm extended and wrist relaxed until the ball reaches the rim. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not chase rebounds under other players' feet. → *Expect:* fingers point down toward the target and shoulders stay square.
7. **Repeat the same routine.** Dribble, breathe, set, and shoot with identical timing for each attempt. → *Expect:* misses cluster predictably instead of spraying left and right.

## Decision points

- Shots miss short → use more leg drive and a higher arc before adding arm force.
- Shots miss left or right → check guide-hand interference and square the shooting elbow.
- Backspin is absent → finish through the fingertips with a relaxed wrist.
- Fatigue changes mechanics → pause before practicing more reps.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Guide-hand push:** detect sidespin or misses opposite the guide hand → remove the guide hand just before release.
- **F2 Flat arc:** detect hard front-rim misses → release higher with elbow above eye level.
- **F3 Off-balance landing:** detect stepping sideways after the shot → reset stance and shoot with less sway.
- **F4 Inconsistent routine:** detect rushed shots after makes or misses → use the same breath and dribble count every time.

## Verification

You attempted 25 free throws with the same routine, balanced stance, upward leg drive, straight follow-through, visible backspin, and no unsafe rebound collisions.

## Variations

- easier: start closer to the hoop and keep the same mechanics.
- harder: shoot sets of 10 while tracking percentage under mild fatigue.
- equipment: use the correct ball size for your age and league.
- at-home: practice form shooting against a wall only where ball marks are allowed.

## Safety & privacy

Repeated shooting can irritate wrists, elbows, or shoulders if volume jumps too quickly. Stop for sharp pain and watch for other players when retrieving rebounds. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
