## Goal

You throw darts with a stable stance, repeatable release, and consistent follow-through so groups tighten around the target.

## Preconditions

- A regulation dartboard fixed securely at safe height and a clear throwing area.
- Proper darts with intact tips and flights.
- No one standing near the board or between the oche and board.

## Steps

1. **Stand side-on and balanced.** Place your lead foot near the throw line, toes angled slightly toward the board, with most weight on the lead leg. → *Expect:* you can stay still without rocking.
2. **Align eye, dart, and target.** Raise the dart in front of your dominant eye and choose a precise spot, not a general scoring zone. → *Expect:* the dart tip appears on the same visual line as the target.
3. **Grip lightly.** Hold the barrel with enough pressure to control it, keeping wrist neutral and fingers relaxed. → *Expect:* the dart does not wobble in your hand or feel squeezed.
4. **Keep the upper arm quiet.** Hinge mainly at the elbow as you draw the dart back toward the side of your face. → *Expect:* your shoulder stays still and the dart travels on one line.
5. **Accelerate smoothly forward.** Extend the elbow toward the target without snapping the shoulder. → *Expect:* the dart leaves on a flat, direct path.
6. **Release before full lockout.** Let the fingers open cleanly as the forearm points at the target. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if elbow pain or tingling appears. → *Expect:* the dart leaves without sticking to the fingers or diving.
7. **Follow through.** Finish with fingers pointing down the target line and hold the position briefly. → *Expect:* your hand ends in the same place after each throw.
8. **Score the group, not one dart.** Throw three darts with the same routine before adjusting. → *Expect:* the cluster shows your actual pattern.

## Decision points

- Darts land low → release slightly earlier or reduce wrist collapse.
- Darts spray sideways → simplify grip and keep the shoulder still.
- Good groups but wrong location → move your aim point gradually rather than changing the whole throw.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Tight grip:** detect fishtailing or darts sticking to fingers → reduce pressure and use the same finger placement each throw.
- **F2 Shoulder throw:** detect whole-arm pushing → practice elbow-only throws close to the board.
- **F3 Falling forward:** detect stepping over the line → reset weight over the lead foot and hold the finish.
- **F4 Rushed routine:** detect different pauses and release timing → use one breath, aim, throw, and follow-through rhythm.

## Verification

You throw five rounds of three darts from the line, hold balance after each throw, and keep at least three rounds within a hand-width group on your chosen target area.

## Variations

- `easier`: practice at a shorter safe distance before moving to regulation distance.
- `harder`: call an exact segment and count only darts landing there.
- `equipment`: try different barrel weights only after your stance and release are consistent.
- `at-home`: use a soft-tip board with a protected wall area.

## Safety & privacy

Throw only when the board area is clear, retrieve darts after all throws are finished, and stop if elbow or shoulder pain develops. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
