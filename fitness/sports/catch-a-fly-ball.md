## Goal

You catch a fly ball by reading its arc early, moving to the spot, setting your feet, and securing the ball with two hands.

## Preconditions

- A baseball or softball glove, ball, and open field.
- A partner or machine hitting or throwing manageable fly balls.
- Awareness of fences, holes, other players, sun position, and wind.

## Steps

1. **Start in a ready stance.** Stand on the balls of the feet with knees bent, glove side forward, and eyes on the hitter or thrower. → *Expect:* you can move on the first ball flight cue.
2. **Take a first step back if uncertain.** Open the hips and move back before charging forward. → *Expect:* the ball stays in front of you instead of sailing over your head.
3. **Run to the projected landing spot.** Turn and run normally, glancing at the ball while covering ground. → *Expect:* you arrive early enough to slow down.
4. **Get behind the ball.** Position yourself slightly behind the landing spot so you can move forward into the catch. → *Expect:* momentum comes toward the infield or target.
5. **Raise the glove late but ready.** Present the glove above the eyes for high balls, pocket open to the ball. → *Expect:* you see the ball into the glove.
6. **Use two hands when possible.** Cover the ball with the throwing hand as it enters the pocket. ⚠️ *Safety:* call loudly and peel away if another fielder has priority or collision risk. → *Expect:* the ball is secured before transfer.
7. **Catch with soft hands.** Let the glove give slightly instead of stabbing at the ball. → *Expect:* the ball sticks in the pocket with minimal bounce-out.
8. **Finish ready to throw.** Bring the ball to the throwing hand and square toward the target. → *Expect:* you can make the next play without rushing.

## Decision points

- Ball is over your head often → start deeper or take a stronger first step back.
- Sun blocks vision → use sunglasses, brim angle, or shift body position when possible.
- Wind pushes the ball → keep moving your feet until the ball is caught.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Drifting under the ball:** detect catching flat-footed while backpedaling → turn and run, then circle behind it.
- **F2 Glove stabbing:** detect bounce-outs → soften the glove and receive the ball.
- **F3 Early glove block:** detect losing sight behind the glove → keep the glove lower until tracking is clear.
- **F4 Silent collision risk:** detect two players converging → call "ball" clearly and follow team priority rules.

## Verification

You catch 8 of 10 routine fly balls in open space while taking an early route, arriving balanced, seeing the ball into the glove, securing with two hands when possible, and avoiding collisions.

## Variations

- `easier`: start with tennis balls or short pop-ups thrown by a partner.
- `harder`: practice balls hit over each shoulder and in light wind.
- `equipment`: use sunglasses and a properly broken-in glove.
- `at-home`: practice tracking by tossing a tennis ball high in a safe yard.

## Safety & privacy

Scan the field, communicate loudly, avoid blind collisions, and stop for dizziness, eye impact, finger injury, or joint pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
