## Goal

You make a basic golf swing with safe spacing, balanced posture, coordinated rotation, and clean contact through the ball.

## Preconditions

- A golf club, practice balls, and a range bay, net, or open practice area designed for golf.
- No people, pets, windows, or fragile objects within swing range.
- Warm wrists, shoulders, trunk, hips, and hamstrings.

## Steps

1. **Check the swing area.** Confirm a full club-length plus margin around you and downrange. → *Expect:* nothing can be struck by the club or ball.
2. **Grip the club neutrally.** Place hands together with light-to-moderate pressure and the clubface square. → *Expect:* wrists feel mobile and the face is not twisted open or closed.
3. **Set athletic posture.** Feet about shoulder-width, knees soft, hinge from hips, spine long, arms hanging naturally. → *Expect:* weight sits midfoot and the club rests behind the ball.
4. **Turn into the backswing.** Rotate shoulders around the spine while hips turn less and the lead arm stays comfortably extended. → *Expect:* you feel coil through the torso without swaying far off the ball.
5. **Start down from the ground.** Shift pressure toward the lead foot, rotate hips, then bring the club down. → *Expect:* the club approaches from a repeatable path instead of chopping with the arms.
6. **Swing through the ball.** Let the clubhead accelerate past impact and rotate to a balanced finish. [BRANCH: easier, make half swings | harder, progress to full swings after solid contact] → *Expect:* contact happens near the center of the clubface and divot or brush mark is after the ball.
7. **Hold the finish.** Finish facing the target with most weight on the lead foot. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if back, wrist, elbow, or shoulder pain becomes sharp. → *Expect:* you can balance for 2 seconds.

## Decision points

- Contact is topped → stay in posture and brush the ground after the ball.
- Contact is fat → reduce sway and keep pressure moving to the lead foot.
- Ball slices → check grip, face angle, and out-to-in swing path.
- Back feels strained → shorten the swing and reduce speed.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Sway instead of turn:** detect the head and hips sliding far sideways → rotate around a stable spine angle.
- **F2 Overgripping:** detect tense forearms → reduce grip pressure and let wrists hinge naturally.
- **F3 Early standing up:** detect thin shots → keep hip hinge through impact.
- **F4 Casting from the top:** detect weak, scooped contact → start down with lower-body pressure shift before throwing the hands.

## Verification

You made 20 practice swings or shots with clear swing space, stable posture, controlled backswing, ground contact after the ball on turf shots, and a balanced 2-second finish.

## Variations

- easier: rehearse half swings or use a short iron.
- harder: add target changes and full-speed swings after contact is consistent.
- equipment: use foam balls or a practice net for limited spaces.
- at-home: do slow shadow swings only with enough ceiling and side clearance.

## Safety & privacy

Golf clubs and balls can seriously injure bystanders or damage property. Always check the area before swinging, stop for sharp pain, and use proper practice facilities. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
