## Goal

You deliver a bowling ball with a repeatable approach, square release, and target-focused aim so more first balls hit the pocket.

## Preconditions

- A bowling lane, bowling shoes, and a ball light enough to swing without shoulder strain.
- Dry hands and a ball with finger holes that fit without pinching.
- Enough clear approach space to take three to five steps.

## Steps

1. **Choose a controllable ball.** Use the heaviest ball you can swing smoothly without gripping hard or dropping your shoulder. → *Expect:* the ball hangs from relaxed fingers and your arm can pendulum freely.
2. **Set your starting position.** Stand with feet parallel, bowling-side foot slightly back, and shoulders square to your intended target arrow. → *Expect:* your body points down-lane without twisting at the waist.
3. **Pick an arrow, not the pins.** Aim over a lane arrow that matches your pocket line instead of staring at the headpin. → *Expect:* your eyes stay fixed on a close target through release.
4. **Start the pushaway smoothly.** Move the ball forward as your first step begins, then let it drop into a relaxed backswing. → *Expect:* the ball swing matches your steps instead of being muscled.
5. **Keep the swing close to your body.** Let the arm travel like a pendulum beside your hip, with wrist firm and elbow relaxed. → *Expect:* the ball does not loop behind your back or drift far outside your shoulder.
6. **Slide and release beside the ankle.** On the final step, slide on the non-bowling foot and release the ball near that ankle with the thumb exiting before the fingers. → *Expect:* the ball rolls onto the lane quietly, not lofted or slapped down.
7. **Follow through toward the target.** Let your hand finish upward along the target line, palm facing generally toward the pins. → *Expect:* your arm finishes balanced and your chest still faces the lane.
8. **Hold the finish.** Stay in your release position until the ball crosses the arrows. → *Expect:* you can see whether the ball crossed your intended arrow.

## Decision points

- Ball pulls left or right before the arrows → slow the approach and keep the swing under the shoulder.
- Ball drops with a thud → choose a lighter ball or release closer to the ankle.
- Missing the pocket consistently → move your starting feet a board or two opposite the miss while keeping the same target.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Forced arm swing:** detect shoulder tension or a jerky backswing → reduce ball weight and let gravity create the swing.
- **F2 Drifting feet:** detect your slide foot finishing far from the starting line → walk a straight approach without a ball, then add the ball.
- **F3 Early head lift:** detect eyes jumping to the pins before release → keep eyes on the arrow until the ball passes it.
- **F4 Overgripping:** detect thumb sticking or wrist fatigue → dry your hand, check fit, and relax pressure.

## Verification

You complete 10 first-ball deliveries with a balanced finish, quiet roll near the slide ankle, eyes on the same arrow, and at least 7 balls crossing within one board of the target arrow.

## Variations

- `easier`: use a three-step approach and a lighter house ball.
- `harder`: add a controlled hook release after your straight-line delivery is repeatable.
- `equipment`: use wrist support only if it helps maintain a neutral wrist without pain.
- `at-home`: rehearse the footwork and pendulum swing with no ball in a clear hallway.

## Safety & privacy

Use shoes with clean soles, keep fingers out of the return machine, and stop if shoulder, wrist, or knee pain appears. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
