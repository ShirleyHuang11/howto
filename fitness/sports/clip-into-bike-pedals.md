## Goal

You clip into and out of clipless bike pedals reliably while stationary and rolling, reducing low-speed fall risk.

## Preconditions

- Bike with clipless pedals, compatible cycling shoes and cleats, helmet, and a clear practice area.
- Pedal release tension set low enough for learning.
- Basic bike balance and braking skills.

## Steps

1. **Practice the release motion off the bike.** Hold one shoe in your hand, engage the cleat, and twist the heel outward to release. → *Expect:* you understand the angle and force needed before riding.
2. **Set up next to support.** Sit on the bike beside a wall, rail, or trainer with one hand supported. → *Expect:* the bike cannot tip while you learn.
3. **Clip in one foot.** Place the cleat nose into the pedal, press down until it clicks, and keep the other foot on the ground. → *Expect:* the shoe is attached and does not lift free.
4. **Unclip deliberately.** Twist the clipped heel outward until the cleat releases. Repeat both sides. → *Expect:* release happens before you need to put the foot down.
5. **Start with one foot clipped.** Clip the dominant foot, push off, coast slowly, then bring the second pedal into position. [BRANCH: easier, keep practicing beside support | harder, clip both feet while rolling] → *Expect:* you can ride straight while preparing the second foot.
6. **Clip the second foot while moving.** Look ahead, place the cleat, and press down smoothly. → *Expect:* the click happens without staring down or swerving.
7. **Unclip before stopping.** Twist one heel out early, coast, brake gently, and put that foot down. ⚠️ *Safety:* do not wait until the bike is almost stopped to unclip; stop practicing after any fall with head impact or sharp pain. → *Expect:* one foot is free before the bike loses balance.

## Decision points

- You cannot release easily → lower pedal tension or check cleat compatibility.
- You look down and swerve → practice clipping in beside support and then at very low speed.
- You tip at stops → unclip earlier and always choose a default foot.
- Cleat feels loose or misaligned → tighten bolts and adjust position before riding.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Late unclipping:** detect panic at stops → twist out several bike lengths before stopping.
- **F2 Wrong release direction:** detect pulling upward without release → twist the heel outward, not up.
- **F3 High pedal tension:** detect excessive force needed → reduce tension according to pedal instructions.
- **F4 Cleat misplacement:** detect knee discomfort or trouble engaging → check cleat alignment and bolt tightness.

## Verification

You clipped in and unclipped each foot 10 times beside support, then started, clipped both feet while rolling, unclipped one foot early, and stopped safely 5 times without tipping.

## Variations

- easier: practice on an indoor trainer or grass field with low pedal tension.
- harder: practice starts on mild inclines only after flat starts are reliable.
- equipment: different pedal systems release differently, so learn your exact mechanism.
- at-home: use a stationary trainer for repetitions before riding outdoors.

## Safety & privacy

Clipless pedals have medium risk from low-speed falls, especially at intersections and stops. Wear a helmet, practice away from traffic, unclip early, and stop after head impact or sharp pain. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
