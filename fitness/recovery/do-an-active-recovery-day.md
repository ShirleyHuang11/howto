## Goal

You complete a low-intensity recovery session that increases blood flow and restores movement without adding meaningful fatigue.

## Preconditions

- Mild soreness or general fatigue, not acute injury or illness.
- Comfortable shoes or equipment for walking, cycling, swimming, or mobility.
- A route or space where you can move easily and stop if needed.

## Steps

1. **Check readiness.** Rate soreness, sleep, mood, and joint pain before starting. → *Expect:* discomfort is mild and does not change your walking pattern.
2. **Choose easy movement.** [BRANCH: walk, easy bike, easy swim, gentle mobility] Use an option that feels smooth and familiar. → *Expect:* effort stays conversational.
3. **Warm up gradually.** Start with 5 minutes of very easy movement. → *Expect:* stiffness decreases rather than increases.
4. **Hold low intensity.** Stay around 2 to 4 out of 10 effort, breathing through the nose or speaking easily. → *Expect:* you finish feeling better or the same, not drained.
5. **Move through gentle ranges.** Add light hip, ankle, thoracic, and shoulder mobility without forcing end ranges. → *Expect:* motion feels easier after several reps.
6. **Avoid hidden workouts.** Do not add sprints, heavy lifting, hard hills, or long mileage. ⚠️ *Safety:* stop if soreness becomes sharp pain, swelling increases, or movement quality worsens. → *Expect:* the session remains restorative.
7. **Cool down and hydrate.** Finish with 3 to 5 easy minutes and drink to thirst. → *Expect:* heart rate settles quickly.
8. **Record the effect.** Note whether soreness, energy, and range of motion improved. → *Expect:* you know whether the recovery dose was appropriate.

## Decision points

- You feel better after 10 minutes → continue up to the planned easy duration.
- You feel worse after 10 minutes → stop and use rest instead.
- Soreness changes your gait or lifting mechanics → treat the day as rest and monitor symptoms.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Turning recovery into training:** detect sweating hard or chasing pace → slow down immediately.
- **F2 Ignoring joint pain:** detect sharp or localized pain → stop and avoid loading that area.
- **F3 Too much duration:** detect heavy legs afterward → cut the next recovery day by half.
- **F4 Forced stretching:** detect guarding or pain at end range → use smaller, smoother motion.

## Verification

You complete 20 to 40 minutes of easy movement at conversational effort, with no increase in pain, no limp or altered mechanics, and equal or improved stiffness afterward.

## Variations

- `easier`: take a 10-minute walk and do gentle breathing only.
- `harder`: add light technique drills, but keep effort below moderate.
- `equipment`: use a bike, pool, rower, or elliptical if walking irritates joints.
- `at-home`: do an easy mobility circuit and short walk around the house or block.

## Safety & privacy

Active recovery should not mask injury. Stop for sharp pain, swelling, fever, chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or symptoms that worsen during movement. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have a relevant condition.
