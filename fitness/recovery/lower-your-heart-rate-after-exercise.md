## Goal

You cool down after exercise so breathing and heart rate return toward baseline without abrupt dizziness or unnecessary stress.

## Preconditions

- A safe space to walk, pedal easily, or sit upright.
- Water available if the session was sweaty.
- No chest pain, faintness, or severe shortness of breath requiring urgent care.

## Steps

1. **Reduce intensity gradually.** Walk, pedal, or move easily for 3-10 minutes instead of stopping suddenly. → *Expect:* breathing slows and you can speak in full sentences.
2. **Keep the posture open.** Stand or sit tall with relaxed shoulders rather than collapsing forward for long periods. → *Expect:* breathing feels easier and less compressed.
3. **Use slow nasal or relaxed breathing.** Inhale calmly and lengthen the exhale without forcing breath holds. → *Expect:* heart rate trends down and tension decreases.
4. **Add gentle mobility if desired.** Move through easy ranges for the hips, calves, chest, or back. → *Expect:* muscles feel less keyed up without pain.
5. **Rehydrate and transition slowly.** Sip fluid and wait until lightheadedness is absent before showering, driving, or lying flat. → *Expect:* you feel steady when walking away.

## Decision points

- Heart rate drops steadily and you feel normal → end the cool-down.
- Lightheadedness appears → keep moving gently or sit upright and breathe slowly.
- Chest pain, fainting, irregular heartbeat, or severe breathlessness occurs → seek urgent medical help.
- Heat exposure was high → prioritize shade, cooling, and fluids.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Abrupt stop after hard effort:** detect dizziness or nausea → walk slowly until symptoms settle.
- **F2 Breath holding:** detect pressure in the chest or head → use easy continuous breathing.
- **F3 Overstretching immediately:** detect cramping or sharp pain → switch to gentle movement first.
- **F4 Ignoring red flags:** detect chest pain, fainting, or severe shortness of breath → stop and get urgent help.

## Verification

Within 5-10 minutes, you can speak comfortably, walk steadily, and feel your heart rate and breathing clearly declining without dizziness, chest pain, or severe breathlessness.

## Variations

- `easier`: walk slowly for 5 minutes and skip stretching.
- `harder`: use a heart-rate monitor to learn how long different workouts take to settle.
- `equipment`: use a fan, shade, or cool towel after hot sessions.
- `at-home`: step in place or pace indoors until breathing normalizes.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for normal post-exercise recovery. Stop and seek urgent care for chest pain, fainting, blue lips, confusion, severe shortness of breath, or an irregular heartbeat that does not settle. This is general fitness guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor before starting if you have heart, lung, blood-pressure, or fainting conditions.
