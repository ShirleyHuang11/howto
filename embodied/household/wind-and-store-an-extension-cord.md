## Goal

Unplug, wind, and store an extension cord without damaging insulation, creating trip hazards, or leaving it energized.

## Preconditions

- Devices powered by the cord can be turned off.
- A dry storage location is available.
- The cord is cool and reachable along its full length.

## Steps

1. **Turn off connected devices.** Switch off tools, lamps, or appliances before unplugging. → *Expect:* connected devices stop drawing power.
2. **Unplug from the wall.** Pull by the plug body, not the cord. → *Expect:* metal prongs are out of the outlet.
3. **Unplug attached devices.** Disconnect the far end and keep plugs off wet or dirty surfaces. → *Expect:* the cord is fully de-energized and free.
4. **Inspect the cord.** Look for cuts, exposed wire, heat marks, crushed plugs, or loose sockets. → *Expect:* the cord is safe to store or set aside for disposal.
5. **Straighten twists.** Walk the cord out or shake gentle loops loose. → *Expect:* the cord lies without tight kinks.
6. **Wind in loose loops.** Coil using hand-to-elbow or over-under loops without forcing tight bends. → *Expect:* the coil is even and not twisted.
7. **Secure the coil.** Use a cord strap, hook-and-loop tie, or loose tie around the coil, not around one tight spot. → *Expect:* the cord stays coiled without pinching.
8. **Store dry and off walkways.** Hang or place the coil away from water, heat, doors, and foot traffic. → *Expect:* no trip hazard or strain on plugs remains.

## Decision points

- Insulation is damaged or prongs are bent → do not use the cord; label and dispose or repair according to policy.
- Cord is wet → dry it fully before storage and do not energize it.
- Cord is still warm → let it cool before coiling.
- Storage hook is sharp or too small → choose a broader support to avoid cutting insulation.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Cord still plugged in:** detect tension from outlet or lit device → stop and unplug before coiling.
- **F2 Tight kink:** detect sharp bend or twisted loop → unwind that section and recoil loosely.
- **F3 Damaged jacket:** detect exposed conductor or cracked insulation → remove from service immediately.
- **F4 Trip hazard:** detect coil left on floor path → move it to storage hook or shelf.

## Verification

The cord is unplugged at both ends, inspected, loosely coiled, secured, and stored dry outside any walking path.

## Variations

- Outdoor cord: clean and dry mud or moisture before storage.
- Reel storage: wind evenly on the reel without crossing over the plug.
- Heavy-gauge cord: use larger loops to reduce strain.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from electrical shock, fire, and tripping. Never coil an energized, wet, hot, or visibly damaged cord.
