## Goal

The whiteboard writing surface is visibly clean and ready for new marker writing.

## Preconditions

- A dry eraser or clean microfiber cloth is available.
- The board surface is reachable without overextension.
- Important writing has been recorded elsewhere if needed.

## Steps

1. **Grip the eraser flat.** Hold the eraser with the wiping face parallel to the board. → *Expect:* the eraser face covers the writing area without edge-only contact.
2. **Start at the upper left.** Press lightly against the board and wipe horizontally across the top band. → *Expect:* marker residue fades along the wiped path.
3. **Overlap the next pass.** Move the eraser down by half its height and wipe back in the opposite direction. → *Expect:* no unwiped stripe remains between passes.
4. **Continue downward.** Repeat overlapping horizontal passes until the written area is covered. → *Expect:* only light haze or small edge marks remain.
5. **Detail stubborn marks.** Use a clean corner of the cloth or eraser with small circular motions and moderate pressure. → *Expect:* the mark lightens without scratching the surface.
6. **Clean the tray edge.** Wipe loose dust from the lower board tray toward a collected corner. → *Expect:* residue gathers in one removable line or pile.
7. **Inspect from an angle.** Step or tilt view to catch reflected light across the board. → *Expect:* the surface appears uniformly clean with no readable writing.

## Decision points

- Writing must be preserved → stop and ask for confirmation before wiping.
- Dry erase marks remain after wiping → use approved whiteboard cleaner on a cloth.
- Board is glass or coated → avoid abrasive pads and use only soft cloth.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Permanent marker remains:** detect by dark writing unchanged by dry wiping → apply whiteboard cleaner or trace over with dry-erase marker, then wipe again.
- **F2 Smearing spreads:** detect by broad gray haze after each pass → switch to a clean cloth face and reduce liquid.
- **F3 Board moves:** detect by frame shifting under pressure → stabilize the frame and wipe with lighter force.

## Verification

No readable marker strokes remain on the main writing surface when viewed straight on and at a shallow angle.

## Variations

- Magnetic boards: move magnets and notes before wiping, then replace them.
- Shared office boards: confirm content can be erased before step 2.

## Safety & privacy

Whiteboards can contain private plans or names; preserve required content before erasing. Use only light pressure on wall-mounted boards.
