## Goal

Books stand upright on a shelf with their spines visible and the row supported.

## Preconditions

- Shelf has enough height and width for the books.
- Books are closed and dry.
- A bookend or existing row is available if the shelf is not full.

## Steps

1. **Clear shelf space.** Move loose objects aside and make a gap slightly wider than the book group. → *Expect:* the shelf surface is visible and unobstructed.
2. **Pick up the first book.** Grip both covers near the spine with the bottom edge facing downward. → *Expect:* the pages stay closed and aligned.
3. **Set the book upright.** Lower the bottom edge onto the shelf with the spine facing outward. → *Expect:* the book stands vertical when supported by the hand.
4. **Add adjacent books.** Place each next book with its bottom edge on the shelf and spine aligned to the row. → *Expect:* covers touch lightly without bending.
5. **Compress the row gently.** Press the outer book toward the row with flat palm pressure. → *Expect:* gaps close and the row remains upright.
6. **Add side support.** Slide a bookend or heavy book against the exposed side of the row. → *Expect:* the last book no longer leans when released.
7. **Align fronts and spines.** Push the row backward or forward as a group until spines form an even line. → *Expect:* spines are readable and the row is straight.

## Decision points

- Shelf is too short → rotate oversized books flat or choose another shelf.
- Row leans outward → add or move a bookend before letting go.
- Book jacket catches → smooth the jacket and insert with less side pressure.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Book tips over:** detect by book rotating away from vertical → catch it, rebuild the row, and add side support sooner.
- **F2 Pages fan open:** detect by pages separating during grip → close the book, grip nearer the fore edge, and retry.
- **F3 Shelf overloaded:** detect by visible sag or row too tight to move → remove heavy books and distribute weight.

## Verification

Books stand upright with bottom edges fully on the shelf, spines facing outward, and no unsupported book leaning more than a few degrees.

## Variations

- Tall art books: store flat if upright height clearance is insufficient.
- Children's books: use lower shelves and a larger bookend for irregular sizes.

## Safety & privacy

Avoid pinching fingers between books and shelf sides. Book titles may reveal personal interests; keep spines private if needed.
