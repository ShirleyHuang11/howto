## Goal

Wet clothes are hung on a drying line with enough spacing and support to dry without falling.

## Preconditions

- Drying line is clean, anchored, and reachable.
- Clothes are washed and not dripping excessively.
- Clothespins or hangers are available if needed.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the line.** Tug downward lightly at the midpoint and check for dirt or slack. → *Expect:* the line holds tension and stays attached.
2. **Pick up one garment.** Grasp the garment at two corners or shoulder points and lift it clear of the pile. → *Expect:* the garment hangs unfolded from the grip.
3. **Shake out folds.** Give one or two short downward shakes while keeping the garment away from the floor. → *Expect:* major wrinkles open and edges are visible.
4. **Drape over the line.** Place the garment midpoint or top edge across the line with balanced weight on both sides. → *Expect:* the garment remains on the line when lightly released.
5. **Clip the garment.** Pinch a clothespin open, capture fabric and line together, and release at a reinforced edge. → *Expect:* the pin clamps fabric to the line without tearing.
6. **Space the next garment.** Leave a hand-width gap before hanging the next item. → *Expect:* adjacent garments do not overlap.
7. **Check the full row.** Look along the line and adjust sagging or bunched items. → *Expect:* all clothes are supported, separated, and clear of the floor.

## Decision points

- Garment is delicate → drape over a hanger or use wider support instead of tight pins.
- Line sags near the floor → reduce load or move heavy garments to a sturdier rack.
- Windy area → use extra pins at both corners.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Garment falls:** detect by fabric slipping from line → rehang with more overlap and add two clothespins.
- **F2 Clothespin marks delicate fabric:** detect by compression marks at pin site → move pins to seams or use a hanger.
- **F3 Line overload:** detect by severe sag or anchor movement → remove heavy items and redistribute.

## Verification

Each garment remains attached after a light tug, has airflow space around it, and hangs clear of the floor.

## Variations

- Indoor rack: use rack bars as the line and balance garments across parallel bars.
- Shirts: place on hangers first, then hook hangers over the line.

## Safety & privacy

Do not overload the line or climb on unstable furniture to reach it. Clothing may reveal personal information; choose a private drying location when needed.
