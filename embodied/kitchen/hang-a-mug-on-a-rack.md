## Goal

A mug is suspended by its handle on a rack hook without touching neighboring mugs.

## Preconditions

- Mug is empty, dry, and cool.
- Rack hook is fixed, visible, and has enough clearance.
- Mug handle is intact.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the hook.** Confirm the hook is clear and points upward or outward enough to retain the handle. → *Expect:* the hook has open space at its tip.
2. **Grasp the mug body.** Hold the mug around the side wall with the handle exposed. → *Expect:* the mug is stable and the handle opening is visible.
3. **Orient the handle to the hook.** Rotate the mug so the handle loop is coaxial with the hook tip. → *Expect:* the hook can pass through the handle opening without hitting the rim.
4. **Lift to hook height.** Raise the mug slowly until the lower edge of the handle is just above the hook tip. → *Expect:* the handle clears the hook by a small margin.
5. **Slide handle onto hook.** Move the mug inward until the hook enters the handle loop, then lower gently. → *Expect:* the handle settles onto the hook and carries the mug weight.
6. **Release gradually.** Reduce grip force while keeping a hand near the mug for one second. → *Expect:* the mug remains hanging and does not swing into neighbors.
7. **Check spacing.** Observe the mug from front and side. → *Expect:* the mug hangs vertical with no contact against adjacent mugs or wall.

## Decision points

- Hook is loose → do not hang; place the mug on a shelf.
- Mug is too heavy for the hook → choose a stronger hook or shelf storage.
- Neighboring mugs are close → rotate handles or move one mug before release.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Handle misses hook:** detect by hook outside the handle loop → lift, realign the handle opening, and retry with slower lateral motion.
- **F2 Mug swings:** detect by pendulum motion after release → steady the mug body until motion stops and increase spacing.
- **F3 Hook bends:** detect by visible hook deflection → lift the mug off immediately and use a sturdier storage point.

## Verification

The mug remains suspended by the handle for 5 seconds, hangs vertical, and has clearance from neighboring objects.

## Variations

- Under-cabinet hooks: approach from below and keep the rim clear of the cabinet underside.
- S-hooks: hold the hook steady with the other hand if it rotates during placement.

## Safety & privacy

Ceramic mugs are fragile. Use controlled lifting and never release until the handle is fully seated on the hook.
