## Goal

The chip bag opening is folded and clipped closed to reduce air exposure and spilling.

## Preconditions

- A clean bag clip is available.
- The bag opening is intact enough to fold.
- Chips are below the fold line.

## Steps

1. **Stand the bag upright.** Hold both upper corners and settle chips below the opening. → *Expect:* loose chips drop away from the top edge.
2. **Flatten the top air pocket.** Press the front and back panels together above the chips with light palm pressure. → *Expect:* the upper bag is flat and free of trapped chips.
3. **Fold the top edge down.** Make a straight fold 2-4 cm below the opening. → *Expect:* the open edge is hidden inside the fold.
4. **Add one more fold.** Roll or fold downward once more while keeping side edges aligned. → *Expect:* the folded band is firm and even.
5. **Open the clip.** Squeeze the clip handles until the jaws separate wider than the folded band. → *Expect:* the clip jaws are fully open.
6. **Place clip across the fold.** Center the clip over the folded band and release slowly. → *Expect:* the jaws clamp both layers across most of the bag width.
7. **Test the seal.** Lift the bag by the body and tilt it slightly side to side. → *Expect:* the fold stays closed and no chips spill.

## Decision points

- Bag is nearly full → make a smaller single fold and use a wider clip.
- Clip is too narrow → use two clips spaced across the fold.
- Bag is torn below the fold → transfer chips to a sealed container.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Clip slips off:** detect by clip sliding upward or falling → refold with a thicker band and clamp lower on the fold.
- **F2 Chips trapped in fold:** detect by crunching or bulge at top → reopen, shake chips downward, and refold.
- **F3 Bag tears:** detect by new rip at side seam → stop pulling and move contents to another bag or container.

## Verification

The bag remains closed when tilted gently, with the clip centered over a folded band and no chips caught in the seal.

## Variations

- No clip: fold tightly and place the bag fold-side down in a bin.
- Resealable bag: press the zipper track closed after removing trapped crumbs.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Use light force to avoid crushing chips or tearing thin packaging.
