## Goal

Dry rice is transferred from its bag into a jar and the jar is closed without spilling.

## Preconditions

- Jar is clean, dry, and large enough for the rice.
- Rice bag is open or can be opened without scattering grains.
- Counter is dry and stable; funnel is available if the jar mouth is narrow.

## Steps

1. **Place and stabilize the jar.** Set the jar upright on the counter and hold it near the base. → *Expect:* the jar does not slide when nudged.
2. **Add a funnel if needed.** Insert the funnel tip into the jar mouth and center it. → *Expect:* the funnel rests upright without blocking the opening.
3. **Grip the rice bag.** Hold the bag with one hand near the lower body and one hand near the open top. → *Expect:* the bag opening is controlled and points upward.
4. **Move opening over the jar.** Position the bag mouth above the funnel or jar centerline. → *Expect:* any falling grains would land inside the opening.
5. **Tilt gradually.** Rotate the bag slowly until rice begins to flow in a narrow stream. → *Expect:* grains enter the jar without hitting the counter.
6. **Control the fill level.** Reduce tilt as rice approaches 2-3 cm below the jar rim. → *Expect:* the flow slows before the jar overfills.
7. **Stop the pour.** Rotate the bag upright and hold it over the opening until the last grains fall. → *Expect:* rice flow stops and no grains spill from the bag mouth.
8. **Remove funnel and close jar.** Lift the funnel vertically, place the lid on the jar, and tighten or press until sealed. → *Expect:* the lid seats fully and the jar can be moved without rice escaping.

## Decision points

- Jar mouth is narrow → use a funnel or scoop instead of free pouring.
- Rice bag is too heavy → pour in smaller batches with a scoop.
- Jar is glass and unstable → move to a lower, non-slip surface.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Rice spills:** detect by grains on counter → stop pouring, upright the bag, sweep grains away from the jar base, and continue slower.
- **F2 Funnel clogs:** detect by rice backing up in the funnel → pause pouring and tap the funnel side lightly.
- **F3 Jar overfills:** detect by rice reaching the rim → stop, remove a small amount with a scoop, and close with headspace.

## Verification

Rice is inside the jar below the rim, the lid is fully seated, and no active spill remains around the jar.

## Variations

- No funnel: pinch the bag opening into a narrow spout before tilting.
- Bulk bin scoop: scoop rice into the jar in repeated small transfers.

## Safety & privacy

Glass jars are fragile. Spilled rice is a slip hazard on hard floors, so clean loose grains promptly.
