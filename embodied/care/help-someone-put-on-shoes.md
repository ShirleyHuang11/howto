## Goal

Help a person put on shoes with feet protected, fasteners secure, and no painful forcing.

## Preconditions

- The person agrees to help and is seated or otherwise stable.
- The shoes match left and right feet and are the correct size.
- Socks, braces, or orthotics required by the person are available.

## Steps

1. **Ask about comfort and order.** Confirm which foot to start with and whether any foot is painful. → *Expect:* the person consents and identifies limits.
2. **Prepare the shoe.** Loosen laces, straps, or tongue and remove objects inside. → *Expect:* the opening is wide and clear.
3. **Support the foot.** Lift only as much as needed, keeping the ankle aligned. → *Expect:* the foot is stable without pulling the leg.
4. **Slide the shoe on.** Guide toes into the toe box and ease the heel in without crumpling the back. → *Expect:* toes and heel are fully inside.
5. **Check fit before fastening.** Ask if toes are cramped or seams pinch. → *Expect:* the person reports acceptable comfort or asks for adjustment.
6. **Fasten securely.** Tie laces, close straps, or zip so the shoe stays on without cutting circulation. → *Expect:* fasteners are snug and flat.
7. **Repeat for the other foot.** Use the same consent and comfort checks. → *Expect:* both shoes are on the correct feet.
8. **Confirm standing safety.** Check soles, laces, and foot placement before the person stands. → *Expect:* no loose lace or unstable heel remains.

## Decision points

- Foot swelling or wound is visible → stop and ask a caregiver or clinician before forcing the shoe.
- Shoe will not fit over brace or orthotic → use prescribed footwear or pause.
- Person reports pain → remove or adjust the shoe immediately.
- Laces cannot be tied safely → use alternate shoes or secure the loose ends away from walking.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong foot:** detect shoe curve opposite the foot → remove and swap shoes.
- **F2 Heel collapsed:** detect folded heel counter under foot → pull shoe back and reseat the heel.
- **F3 Circulation tightness:** detect color change, numbness, or deep marks → loosen fastener or remove shoe.
- **F4 Loose lace:** detect dangling loop or lace end → retie or tuck it before walking.

## Verification

Both shoes are on the correct feet, fastened securely, comfortable by the person's report, and free of loose parts that could cause tripping.

## Variations

- Slip-on shoes: use a shoehorn if available and check the heel is not folded.
- Compression socks: avoid bunching inside the shoe.
- One-sided weakness: start with the weaker or more painful side.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from skin injury, pain, and falls. Ask before touching feet, maintain dignity, and pause if wounds, swelling, or unexplained pain are present.
