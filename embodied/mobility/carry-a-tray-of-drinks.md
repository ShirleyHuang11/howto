## Goal

Carry multiple drinks on a tray to a destination without spilling, dropping glassware, or bumping people.

## Preconditions

- The tray is clean, dry, and large enough for the drinks.
- Cups or glasses are not overfilled.
- The destination has a clear landing surface.

## Steps

1. **Arrange drinks near the tray center.** Place heavier or fuller cups closest to the middle and leave edges clear. → *Expect:* the tray does not tip when lightly nudged.
2. **Wipe wet bases.** Dry cup bottoms and tray surface before lifting. → *Expect:* cups do not slide when the tray tilts slightly.
3. **Choose the carry grip.** Use two hands under or at opposite sides of the tray. → *Expect:* wrists can keep the tray level.
4. **Lift straight up and pause.** Raise the tray a few centimeters before walking. → *Expect:* no cup slides, tips, or leaks.
5. **Scan the path.** Identify doors, people, pets, rugs, and tight turns before moving. → *Expect:* a clear route or waiting point is known.
6. **Walk slowly with level tray.** Keep the tray near torso height and turn the body instead of twisting wrists. → *Expect:* drink surfaces ripple but do not reach rims.
7. **Set the tray down fully.** Place it on the destination surface before removing any drink. → *Expect:* the tray is stable and supported.
8. **Deliver drinks one at a time.** Move each cup by its safest grip point and confirm the recipient or table supports it before release. → *Expect:* each drink ends upright and controlled.

## Decision points

- A drink is hot → announce "hot drink" and keep it away from tray edges.
- Path becomes crowded → stop at a stable surface or wait with the tray held close.
- Tray feels overloaded → make two trips.
- Destination is cluttered → ask for or create a clear landing spot before approaching.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Sliding cup:** detect movement across the tray → stop, level the tray, and reset the cup positions.
- **F2 Spill starts:** detect liquid over a rim → stop and set the tray down before wiping.
- **F3 Grip fatigue:** detect shaking hands or lowering tray → set it on the nearest stable surface and rest.
- **F4 Collision risk:** detect person entering path → pause and let them pass.

## Verification

All drinks arrive upright on the destination table or in recipients' hands, with no broken glass, burn, or active spill.

## Variations

- Round serving tray: keep one hand under the center and one steadying the rim.
- Lidded cups: still keep hot drinks centered because lids can leak.
- Stairs: avoid carrying a full drink tray on stairs unless no safer route exists.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from hot liquid, slippery spills, and broken glass. Slow near people, warn about hot drinks, and clean spills before anyone walks through them.
