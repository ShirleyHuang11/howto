## Goal

Sublet an apartment lawfully by getting required approval, screening a subtenant, signing clear terms, and documenting condition and payments.

## Preconditions

- You have the lease, sublet clause, landlord contact, rent amount, deposit rules, move-in/move-out dates, and local subletting law.
- You know whether you remain liable for rent, damage, utilities, and rule violations.
- You can screen applicants consistently and lawfully.
- You can document condition, keys, furniture, and house rules.

## Steps

1. **Read the lease and local rules.** Check whether subletting is allowed, requires consent, has fees, bans short-term rentals, or requires specific forms. → *Expect:* you know the approval path and restrictions.
2. **Ask the landlord for written consent if required.** Provide proposed dates, reason, and process before advertising if the lease requires approval. → *Expect:* the landlord confirms the requirements or sends an application form.
3. **Set sublet terms.** Decide rent, deposit, utilities, furniture, pets, parking, mail, move-in date, move-out date, and access to shared spaces. → *Expect:* the offer is clear and does not exceed lease limits.
4. **Advertise and screen lawfully.** Use accurate listing details, apply the same criteria to applicants, and avoid discriminatory preferences. → *Expect:* you have a qualified candidate and screening records.
5. **Submit the candidate for approval.** Send application, ID, income proof, references, and consent forms only as required by landlord or law. → *Expect:* approval, denial with reason, or request for more information is documented.
6. **Sign a written sublease.** Include parties, premises, dates, rent, deposit, utilities, rules, furniture, maintenance reporting, no holdover, and lease attachment. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* signing can leave you liable to both landlord and subtenant, so confirm approval and dates before signing. → *Expect:* all parties have signed copies.
7. **Collect permitted payments.** Take deposit and first rent using traceable payment methods and provide receipts. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* improper deposit handling can create penalties, so follow local deposit rules before accepting funds. → *Expect:* payments are recorded and stored correctly.
8. **Document move-in condition and keys.** Photograph the unit, inventory furniture, record meter readings if relevant, and list keys/fobs/cards transferred. → *Expect:* condition and access items are agreed at handover.
9. **Monitor and close the sublet.** Track rent, notices, repairs, rule compliance, move-out condition, key return, and deposit accounting. → *Expect:* the apartment returns to you or the landlord with documented condition and balances.

## Decision points

- Lease bans sublets → ask about assignment, lease break, roommate addition, or written exception instead.
- Landlord unreasonably refuses → local law may provide remedies; get tenant advice before proceeding anyway.
- Candidate wants short-term rental use → check lease, zoning, building rules, and platform bans.
- You will be abroad or unreachable → appoint a contact for emergencies and notices.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Unauthorized sublet discovered:** detect landlord notice or lease violation → recover by seeking retroactive approval, ending the sublet, or negotiating assignment.
- **F2 Subtenant stops paying:** detect missed rent → recover using the sublease notice process and pay landlord if needed to protect your lease.
- **F3 Damage dispute:** detect move-out damage or missing items → recover with move-in photos, inventory, repair estimates, and deposit accounting.
- **F4 Overstay:** detect subtenant refuses to leave → recover through lawful notice or court process; do not lock them out.
- **F5 Deposit mishandled:** detect commingled funds or missed return deadline → recover by correcting accounting and following local return rules immediately.

## Verification

You have landlord approval if required, a signed sublease, payment receipts, move-in condition evidence, and a tracked end date.

## Variations

- `us`: consent rights, rent caps, deposit handling, short-term rental rules, and eviction process vary by state and city.
- `rent-stabilized`: special rules may limit rent charged and require official forms.
- `roommate-share`: adding a roommate may differ from subletting the whole unit.
- `assignment`: transferring the lease can release you only if the landlord agrees in writing.

## Safety & privacy

High risk from eviction, unpaid rent, property damage, and illegal lockout claims. Do not hand over keys without written approval where required, signed terms, and traceable payment records.
