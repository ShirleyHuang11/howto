## Goal

You manage a roommate's move-out so rent, deposits, utilities, keys, belongings, and replacement-roommate obligations are settled in writing.

## Preconditions

- Lease, roommate agreement, move-out notice requirements, and landlord or HOA rules.
- Current ledger for rent, utilities, deposits, shared purchases, and damages.
- Move-out date, forwarding address, and replacement-roommate plan if needed.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the legal move-out path.** Read the lease and roommate agreement for notice, subletting, assignment, replacement approval, and joint liability. → *Expect:* everyone knows whether the departing roommate remains liable.
2. **Put notice and dates in writing.** Record move-out date, last rent period, utility responsibility, and room availability. → *Expect:* there is no ambiguity about timing.
3. **Notify landlord or management if required.** Submit roommate change, sublet, or lease amendment paperwork before someone new moves in. → *Expect:* property approval process is underway or completed.
4. **Settle money before move-out.** Calculate rent, utilities, deposit, shared items, damage, and cleaning obligations. → *Expect:* balances owed and due dates are documented.
5. **Inspect the room and shared areas together.** Take dated photos, compare condition to move-in records, and list repairs or cleaning. → *Expect:* deposit deductions are evidence-based.
6. **Collect keys and access devices.** Retrieve keys, fobs, parking passes, mailbox keys, garage remotes, and alarm or smart-lock access. → *Expect:* the departing roommate no longer has unauthorized access after move-out.
7. **Transfer utilities and accounts.** Remove or replace the roommate on utilities, internet, subscriptions, renter's insurance, and payment apps. → *Expect:* bills will not continue in the wrong person's name.
8. **Return deposit or document deductions.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before final payout, confirm unpaid bills, damages, keys, and local deposit-return deadlines. → *Expect:* final accounting is sent with any refund or amount owed.

## Decision points

- Roommate is on the lease → landlord approval may be required to release them from liability.
- Roommate leaves without notice → mitigate damages, document abandonment, and follow local rules for belongings and deposits.
- Replacement roommate needed → use a lawful screening process and written approval before key handoff.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Departing roommate still owes rent:** detect unpaid balance → send written ledger and payment deadline; use agreed dispute process if needed.
- **F2 Utilities remain in their name:** detect shutoff warning or account closure → transfer service immediately and settle final charges.
- **F3 Keys not returned:** detect missing key/fob → change codes, ask landlord about rekeying, and document cost allocation.
- **F4 Abandoned property:** detect items left behind → follow local notice and storage rules before disposal.

## Verification

The move-out date, lease status, final ledger, deposit accounting, utilities, access devices, and forwarding address are documented, and any replacement roommate is approved before moving in.

## Variations

- `joint-and-several-lease`: remaining tenants may owe the full rent if one roommate leaves unless the landlord releases them.
- `informal-housemate`: even without a formal lease, local notice and property rules may apply.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from housing liability, deposits, access control, and disputes. Keep communications written, protect forwarding and financial information, and change access promptly when appropriate.
