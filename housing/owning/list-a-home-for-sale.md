## Goal

You publish an accurate, legally compliant home listing with strong photos, correct terms, and a showing process that can produce qualified offers.

## Preconditions

- You are authorized to sell the property.
- Price, disclosures, photos, showing access, and included items are ready.
- You have chosen an agent, flat-fee listing service, or for-sale-by-owner platform.

## Steps

1. **Complete required seller disclosures.** Disclose known defects, HOA details, lead-based paint where applicable, permits, and other required forms. → *Expect:* listing documents are complete before buyers rely on them.
2. **Approve photos and listing media.** Use accurate professional photos and remove images that misrepresent condition or include private items. → *Expect:* media shows the real property clearly.
3. **Write the listing description.** Highlight factual features, upgrades, layout, location strengths, and included items without exaggerating or hiding defects. → *Expect:* buyers understand what is being sold.
4. **Enter accurate MLS or platform data.** Verify address, parcel, square footage source, beds, baths, HOA fees, taxes, school information source, parking, utilities, and showing instructions. → *Expect:* data fields match records or documented sources.
5. **Set showing and offer rules.** Decide notice required, access method, open house times, offer deadline if any, and how offers will be reviewed. → *Expect:* buyers and agents know how to tour and submit.
6. **Publish only after final review.** Check price, remarks, photos, documents, exclusions, compensation fields if applicable, and contact information. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once syndicated, incorrect listing data can spread quickly, so confirm facts before publishing. → *Expect:* the listing is live with correct information.
7. **Monitor launch performance.** Track views, saves, showings, feedback, and questions during the first several days. → *Expect:* you can tell whether the market is responding.

## Decision points

- Listing has sensitive items visible → remove or retake photos before publishing.
- Property has known defects → disclose clearly and price or negotiate accordingly.
- Low launch traffic → revisit price, photos, remarks, and showing access.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Incorrect facts:** detect wrong square footage, taxes, HOA fee, or school data → correct immediately and notify interested parties if material.
- **F2 Missing disclosure:** detect a required form omitted → pause showings or offers until disclosures are provided.
- **F3 Showing access problem:** detect agents cannot schedule or enter → fix lockbox, instructions, or showing software.
- **F4 Misleading photo:** detect buyer complaints about edited or old photos → replace with accurate current images.

## Verification

The listing is live on the intended platform, all required disclosures are attached or available, showing instructions work, and key facts match documented sources.

## Variations

- `us`: MLS rules, compensation display, disclosure forms, and lead-based paint requirements vary by jurisdiction and property age.
- FSBO: you must handle inquiries, disclosures, showings, negotiation, and contract coordination yourself.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from legal disclosure duties, private information exposure, and strangers touring the home. Remove valuables and personal documents, disclose known material facts, and control showing access.
