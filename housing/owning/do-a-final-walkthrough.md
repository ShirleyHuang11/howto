## Goal

You verify the home is in the agreed condition just before closing, with repairs complete, included items present, and no new damage.

## Preconditions

- Closing is scheduled and you have access for a walkthrough.
- You have the purchase contract, repair addendum, inspection repair receipts, and included/excluded item list.
- Utilities are on unless the contract says otherwise.

## Steps

1. **Bring the right documents and tools.** Bring the contract, repair list, phone charger, flashlight, outlet tester if available, and camera. → *Expect:* you can verify obligations room by room.
2. **Check required repairs first.** Compare each agreed repair to receipts, permits if relevant, and visible work. → *Expect:* every repair is complete or a remaining issue is documented.
3. **Confirm included items remain.** Check appliances, fixtures, window treatments, keys, remotes, manuals, and any negotiated personal property. → *Expect:* the property matches the contract inventory.
4. **Test normal systems.** Run water, flush toilets, test lights, appliances, garage doors, HVAC, fans, and visible leaks. → *Expect:* systems operate normally for a brief functional check.
5. **Look for new damage or vacancy problems.** Check floors, walls, ceilings, windows, basement, attic access, exterior, and signs of water intrusion. → *Expect:* no new damage beyond contract condition, or photos show the issue.
6. **Document anything wrong immediately.** Send photos and a written list to your agent, attorney, lender, or closing officer. → *Expect:* every unresolved issue is in writing before signing.
7. **Resolve before closing when material.** [BRANCH: minor issue, negotiate credit or holdback if allowed | serious issue, delay closing or require repair] ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once you close, leverage usually drops, so confirm material issues before signing closing papers. → *Expect:* the closing plan reflects any walkthrough issue.

## Decision points

- Seller belongings remain → require removal, written permission, or a disposal credit.
- Agreed repair is incomplete → request completion, escrow holdback, credit, or delayed closing.
- New major damage appears → involve your agent or attorney before signing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Utilities off:** detect that systems cannot be tested → request utilities on and repeat the walkthrough.
- **F2 Missing appliance:** detect an included item removed → demand return, replacement value, or written credit before closing.
- **F3 New leak or damage:** detect water, stains, broken glass, or vandalism → photograph, notify all parties, and pause closing until resolved.
- **F4 Rushed walkthrough:** detect pressure to skip checks → slow down and document; the walkthrough is your last practical check.

## Verification

You have completed a room-by-room check, documented any exceptions in writing, and either approved closing or have a written repair, credit, holdback, or delay agreement.

## Variations

- `us`: escrow holdbacks are allowed only when the lender and closing agent approve them.
- New construction: use a punch list and builder warranty process in addition to the final walkthrough.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because closing transfers responsibility for the property. Do not climb roofs, enter unsafe crawlspaces, or ignore major defects under time pressure; confirm unresolved issues in writing before closing.
