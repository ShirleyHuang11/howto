## Goal

You inspect the home after winter or the wet season, catch water damage early, and prepare exterior and mechanical systems for warmer months.

## Preconditions

- Snow, freeze risk, or heavy seasonal rain has mostly passed.
- You can safely walk the property exterior and access basement, attic entry, and utility areas.
- You have a phone camera, flashlight, and basic cleaning supplies.

## Steps

1. **Walk the exterior slowly.** Check siding, trim, foundation, grading, steps, railings, decks, fences, and driveways for damage or movement. → *Expect:* visible winter or water damage is photographed and listed.
2. **Inspect roof edges and gutters from the ground.** Look for missing shingles, sagging gutters, clogged downspouts, and water staining. → *Expect:* roof or gutter issues are noted without unsafe climbing.
3. **Check drainage.** Confirm downspouts discharge away from the foundation and soil slopes away from the house. → *Expect:* water paths move away from the structure.
4. **Look inside for leaks.** Check ceilings, attic access area, basement, crawlspace entry, under sinks, around windows, and near appliances. → *Expect:* dampness, staining, or musty smells are identified early.
5. **Restart seasonal systems.** [BRANCH: irrigation present, inspect heads and leaks before full use | hose bibs present, turn on slowly and check inside for leaks] → *Expect:* seasonal water systems work without hidden leaks.
6. **Prepare cooling and ventilation.** Replace HVAC filters, clear debris around outdoor units, test air conditioning on a mild day, and clean bathroom fan grilles. → *Expect:* cooling and ventilation are ready before peak heat.
7. **Update maintenance records.** Schedule contractors for roof, gutter, HVAC, pest, drainage, or exterior repairs as needed. → *Expect:* findings become dated tasks, not forgotten observations.

## Decision points

- Water is entering the home → prioritize source identification and repair before cosmetic fixes.
- Roof damage is suspected → hire a roofer rather than climbing onto the roof.
- Air conditioning fails test → schedule service before the first heat wave.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hidden hose bib leak:** detect water inside wall or basement after turning on exterior faucet → shut it off and call a plumber.
- **F2 Foundation drainage problem:** detect pooling near the house → extend downspouts and correct grading.
- **F3 Pest entry:** detect droppings, nests, or chewed material → seal entry points and use professional pest control if needed.
- **F4 Ignored small leak:** detect recurring stain or damp smell → investigate promptly before mold or rot spreads.

## Verification

You have completed an exterior, drainage, interior leak, seasonal water, and HVAC check, with dated photos and scheduled repairs for any problems.

## Variations

- Cold climate: include ice dam, freeze-thaw, and snow-load damage checks.
- Wet climate: inspect drainage, crawlspaces, gutters, and exterior caulk more frequently.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from ladders, electrical equipment, pests, and water damage. Stay off unsafe roofs, turn water on slowly, and call licensed professionals for electrical, structural, or major moisture issues.
