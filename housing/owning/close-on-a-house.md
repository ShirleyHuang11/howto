## Goal

You sign the final purchase and loan documents, transfer funds safely, and receive legal possession according to the contract.

## Preconditions

- Your lender has issued final approval or "clear to close" if you are financing.
- You have reviewed the Closing Disclosure or settlement statement.
- Final walkthrough issues are resolved or documented.
- You have verified payment instructions with the closing agent using a trusted phone number.

## Steps

1. **Confirm closing status with all parties.** Ask the lender, closing agent, attorney, and agent whether documents, funds, and title work are ready. → *Expect:* a confirmed closing time and no known open conditions.
2. **Review final numbers before arrival.** Compare cash to close, loan terms, seller credits, prorations, and payoff amounts to the latest disclosure. → *Expect:* you understand the final amount and any changes.
3. **Bring required identification and funds.** Bring government ID and the verified cashier's check or wire confirmation requested by the closing agent. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* a real estate wire may be unrecoverable, so verify instructions by trusted phone call before sending. → *Expect:* the closing office confirms acceptable ID and funds.
4. **Read documents before signing.** Review the deed, note, mortgage or deed of trust, settlement statement, tax forms, affidavits, and title documents. → *Expect:* names, address, price, loan terms, and vesting are correct.
5. **Sign only when errors are resolved.** Ask for corrections if the interest rate, payment, escrow setup, credits, or legal names are wrong. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* signed closing documents create binding legal and financial obligations, so confirm key terms before signing. → *Expect:* corrected documents or written explanation before signatures.
6. **Wait for funding, recording, or disbursement.** The closing agent confirms when lender funds are received and the deed is recorded or submitted according to local practice. → *Expect:* the transaction is legally closed or scheduled for recording.
7. **Receive keys and closing package.** Get keys, garage remotes, access codes, copies of signed documents, and contact information for post-closing questions. → *Expect:* possession transfers according to the contract.

## Decision points

- Final loan terms differ from what you accepted → stop and call the lender before signing.
- Walkthrough problem remains unresolved → require written agreement, credit, holdback, or delay before closing.
- Wire instructions change at the last minute → treat as fraud until independently verified.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wire fraud:** detect changed email instructions or urgency → stop, call the closing office at a verified number, and notify your bank if funds were sent.
- **F2 Document error:** detect wrong name, address, rate, term, or vesting → refuse to sign that document until corrected.
- **F3 Funding delay:** detect that lender funds have not arrived → wait for confirmed funding before relying on possession.
- **F4 Unresolved title issue:** detect missing payoff, lien, or judgment → ask the closing agent or attorney what must clear before recording.

## Verification

The closing agent confirms funding and recording or legal closing, you receive keys or possession as contracted, and you have a complete signed closing package.

## Variations

- `us`: closing customs vary by state; some use escrow companies, some attorneys, and some record before keys are released.
- Remote closing: identity verification, notarization, and wet-signature rules depend on state and lender requirements.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because signing and wiring money can be irreversible. Verify wire instructions independently, read before signing, protect identity documents, and involve an attorney or closing professional for unclear legal terms.
