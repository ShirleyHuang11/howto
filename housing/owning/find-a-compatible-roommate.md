## Goal

You screen and choose a roommate whose budget, lifestyle, reliability, and housing needs fit the home before anyone moves in or signs.

## Preconditions

- Clear rent, utilities, deposit, room, move-in date, lease terms, and household rules.
- Permission from landlord, HOA, or co-owners if required.
- Safe way to advertise and communicate without exposing sensitive personal information.

## Steps

1. **Define the must-haves before advertising.** Include budget, move-in date, lease length, pets, smoking, parking, work schedule, guests, cleanliness, and shared-space expectations. → *Expect:* you know what compatibility means.
2. **Write an accurate listing.** Describe the room, total monthly cost, neighborhood, transit/parking, housemates, restrictions, and application process. → *Expect:* applicants can self-select realistically.
3. **Use safer contact channels.** Use platform messaging or a separate email at first; avoid posting exact unit numbers or access codes. → *Expect:* inquiries arrive without exposing the household.
4. **Screen for basic fit.** Ask about income source, move-in timing, pets, smoking, schedule, guest habits, and why they are moving. → *Expect:* incompatible applicants are filtered early.
5. **Meet before committing.** Use a video call or public first meeting, then show the home with another roommate present when possible. → *Expect:* both sides can assess communication and comfort.
6. **Verify financial and rental reliability lawfully.** With consent, use landlord-approved application, references, proof of income, credit/background screening where legal, and identity verification. → *Expect:* screening is documented and compliant.
7. **Discuss house rules directly.** Cover cleaning, quiet hours, guests, food, bills, conflict style, and move-out notice. → *Expect:* likely conflicts surface before signing.
8. **Put the arrangement in writing.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before handing over keys or accepting deposit, confirm lease permission, signed agreement, payment terms, and move-in date. → *Expect:* the chosen roommate has completed required paperwork and payment.

## Decision points

- Applicant resists written terms or verification → decline politely.
- Existing lease requires landlord approval → do not allow move-in until approval is complete.
- Multiple strong candidates → choose reliability and lifestyle fit over fastest move-in.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Scam applicant or fake payment:** detect overpayment, urgency, or refusal to meet → stop and use verified payment and identity processes.
- **F2 Discriminatory screening risk:** detect decisions based on protected characteristics → use neutral criteria tied to rent, lease terms, and household rules.
- **F3 Undisclosed pet or guest pattern:** detect mismatch after move-in → enforce written rules and revise agreement if everyone consents.
- **F4 Landlord rejects roommate:** detect failed application → return funds as required and continue search.

## Verification

The selected roommate is approved where required, has signed the lease or roommate agreement, has paid agreed move-in funds through a verified method, and has acknowledged house rules before receiving keys.

## Variations

- `owner-occupied`: local rules may treat lodgers differently; still use written terms and fair screening.
- `shared-lease`: all tenants may be jointly responsible for full rent, so financial screening matters more.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from personal safety, housing access, discrimination law, and money. Meet safely, verify before key handoff, protect applicants' personal documents, and apply consistent screening criteria.
