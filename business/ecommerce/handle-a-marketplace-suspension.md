## Goal

Respond to a marketplace account or listing suspension with a factual appeal and corrective action plan that maximizes reinstatement chances.

## Preconditions

- Access to the seller account, suspension notice, affected listings, order history, and policy pages.
- Supplier invoices, tracking records, authenticity documents, safety certificates, or other evidence.
- Authority to pause listings, change processes, and communicate with marketplace support.

## Steps

1. **Read the suspension notice exactly.** Identify whether the issue is account-level, listing-level, payment, performance, intellectual property, safety, or verification. → *Expect:* the cited policy and requested action are known.
2. **Stop risky activity.** Pause affected listings, ads, or fulfillment actions that could worsen the violation. → *Expect:* no new orders are accepted for disputed or noncompliant items.
3. **Collect evidence.** Gather invoices, photos, tracking, customer messages, safety documents, authorization letters, and process logs. → *Expect:* evidence directly addresses the stated issue.
4. **Find the root cause.** Determine what failed: listing content, supplier verification, late shipments, counterfeit complaint, restricted product, or account identity mismatch. → *Expect:* a clear cause without blaming the marketplace or customer.
5. **Write the corrective action plan.** State what happened, what was fixed immediately, and what process prevents recurrence. → *Expect:* the appeal is concise, factual, and tied to policy.
6. **Submit the appeal.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm all evidence is attached and the appeal answers the exact notice before submission, because repeated weak appeals can reduce review quality. → *Expect:* the marketplace confirms receipt or shows appeal under review.
7. **Track and respond once.** Wait for a decision, answer any specific follow-up, and avoid duplicate tickets unless the platform instructs it. → *Expect:* all communication is in one documented case thread.

## Decision points

- Notice asks for identity verification → complete verification before writing a policy appeal.
- IP complaint came from a rights owner → seek retraction or authorization evidence if legitimate.
- Product is actually prohibited → remove it permanently instead of arguing for reinstatement.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Generic appeal denied:** detect canned rejection → rewrite with specific evidence and root-cause corrections.
- **F2 Missing documents:** detect request for invoices or certificates → obtain originals from suppliers and resubmit clearly labeled files.
- **F3 Duplicate case confusion:** detect multiple open appeals → stop opening new cases and consolidate references.
- **F4 Funds held:** detect payout reserve after suspension → read release terms and keep proof of fulfilled orders for reserve review.

## Verification

The marketplace appeal is submitted under the correct case or appeal flow with required evidence attached, affected listings paused or corrected, and a case ID or review status recorded.

## Variations

- `amazon`: use a plan of action with root cause, corrective action, and preventive measures.
- `etsy`: handmade, vintage, and IP policy evidence may matter more than fulfillment metrics.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because account access and payouts can be lost. Do not falsify invoices, do not create duplicate accounts to evade suspension, and protect supplier and customer data in appeal attachments.
