## Goal

You decline a wedding invitation promptly and kindly, so the couple can plan accurately and still feel your goodwill.

## Preconditions

- The invitation, RSVP deadline, and requested response method.
- Your final decision not to attend.
- Any gift, card, or private note you want to send separately.

## Steps

1. **Decide before the RSVP deadline.** Do not leave the couple guessing while you try to keep options open. → *Expect:* you have a firm yes-or-no decision.
2. **Use the requested RSVP channel.** Reply by card, wedding website, email, or text exactly as requested. → *Expect:* their guest list updates in the system they are using.
3. **Keep the reason brief.** Give a simple explanation only if it helps; avoid long apologies or sensitive details. → *Expect:* the message centers on care, not excuses.
4. **Express warmth and congratulations.** Say you are happy for them and sorry to miss it. → *Expect:* the decline feels personal and gracious.
5. **Send any separate note or gift.** If you want, mail a card or send a gift before or soon after the wedding. → *Expect:* your support is clear even though you will not attend.
6. **Do not renegotiate after declining unless invited.** If your situation changes, ask whether changes are still possible rather than assuming. → *Expect:* you avoid disrupting seating, catering, or lodging plans.

## Decision points

- You are declining because of cost → keep it private or say you cannot make the trip.
- You have a family conflict with the couple → do not use the RSVP to process it.
- You missed the deadline → respond immediately and apologize for the late reply.
- You are invited to related events only → RSVP separately for each event.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Overexplaining:** detect a long message about logistics, money, or guilt → shorten it to a warm decline.
- **F2 Ambiguous reply:** detect wording like "probably cannot" → send a clear no.
- **F3 Wrong channel:** detect that you texted but the couple requested the wedding site → also submit the official RSVP.
- **F4 Delayed response:** detect that the deadline passed → respond now and acknowledge the delay.

## Verification

The RSVP is recorded as declined through the requested channel, and any personal note clearly congratulates the couple without asking them to manage your feelings.

## Variations

- `formal`: use full names and a short handwritten card.
- `close-friend`: add one specific line about celebrating with them another time if that is genuine.
- Template:
  "We are so sorry we cannot be there on [date], but we are thrilled for you both.
  Thank you for including us, and we will be thinking of you with so much happiness.
  With love, [Names]"

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Avoid sharing private health, money, immigration, or family details unless you truly want the couple to know them.
