## Goal

You submit a warranty claim that clearly proves the product, defect, coverage period, and remedy you are requesting.

## Preconditions

- Product name, model, serial number, purchase date, seller, and receipt or order number.
- Warranty terms, return merchandise authorization rules, and support contact.
- Photos, video, error messages, or troubleshooting notes showing the defect.

## Steps

1. **Confirm warranty coverage.** Read the written warranty for duration, exclusions, proof requirements, and remedy options. → *Expect:* you know whether repair, replacement, refund, or parts are available.
2. **Collect purchase and product evidence.** Save receipt, order confirmation, serial number, photos, and the warranty page. → *Expect:* the claim packet proves ownership and timing.
3. **Document the defect objectively.** Describe what fails, when it began, and what normal use looked like. → *Expect:* the manufacturer can reproduce or understand the issue.
4. **List troubleshooting already attempted.** Include reasonable steps such as reset, battery change, firmware update, or support chat if applicable. → *Expect:* the claim does not get delayed by basic scripted questions.
5. **State the remedy requested.** Ask for the specific covered remedy and any shipping label or RMA number needed. → *Expect:* the company knows exactly what resolution you seek.
6. **Attach evidence and send through the official channel.** [BRANCH: web form, upload files and save confirmation | email, attach PDFs/photos and keep sent copy | mail, send copies not originals by trackable mail] → *Expect:* the claim is submitted with proof.
7. **Track deadlines and responses.** Note claim number, promised response date, and next follow-up date. → *Expect:* you can escalate if the company misses its timeline.
8. **Ship only after authorization if required.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm the RMA, destination, packing rules, and tracking before sending the product away. → *Expect:* the item is shipped only under documented claim instructions.

## Decision points

- Warranty expired but defect is safety-related → still contact the manufacturer and check recalls.
- Seller and manufacturer both offer coverage → use the path with clearer rights and faster remedy.
- The product is expensive → preserve all evidence and consider consumer-protection options if denied.
- Company asks for original receipt by mail → send a copy unless the terms explicitly require original documents.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missing proof of purchase:** detect claim rejection for no receipt → provide bank statement, order history, warranty registration, or seller invoice if accepted.
- **F2 Exclusion cited:** detect denial for misuse, wear, or unauthorized repair → compare the denial to the written warranty and respond with evidence.
- **F3 Lost shipment:** detect no scan or delivery issue → file with carrier using tracking and packing photos.
- **F4 No response:** detect missed promised response date → follow up with claim number and escalate to supervisor or consumer-protection agency.

## Verification

The warranty claim has a claim or RMA number, includes proof of purchase and defect evidence, and the company has confirmed the next action: repair, replacement, refund, inspection, or denial in writing.

## Variations

- `us`: the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act governs consumer product warranties, but practical enforcement often starts with the company, retailer, credit-card benefits, or state consumer agency.
- `eu-uk`: statutory consumer rights may be stronger than a manufacturer's voluntary warranty; the seller may be responsible.
- Template:
  "I am requesting warranty service for [product/model/serial], purchased on [date] from [seller].
  The defect is [specific failure], which began on [date] during normal use.
  Please provide [repair/replacement/refund] under the warranty and send any RMA or shipping instructions."

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because money and ownership rights are involved. Do not send original documents unless required, redact unrelated payment details, and confirm shipping authorization before releasing the product.
