## Goal

You negotiate price by email with a clear rationale, respectful tone, and written record of any final terms.

## Preconditions

- The item, service, quote, or contract being negotiated.
- Market comparisons, budget limit, competing quote, defect information, or scope details supporting your offer.
- Your walk-away price and any non-price terms that matter.

## Steps

1. **Define your target and floor.** Decide your ideal price, acceptable range, and walk-away point before writing. → *Expect:* you can negotiate without improvising under pressure.
2. **Identify the reason for your offer.** Use market price, quantity, timing, condition, scope, loyalty, or competing quote. → *Expect:* the request has a business basis.
3. **Open courteously.** Thank them for the quote or listing and signal genuine interest. → *Expect:* the tone invites negotiation rather than conflict.
4. **Make a specific counteroffer.** State the price or discount you are asking for and, if useful, what you can do in return. → *Expect:* the seller can accept, reject, or counter.
5. **Keep non-price terms visible.** Mention shipping, taxes, delivery date, warranty, payment timing, scope, or cancellation terms. → *Expect:* agreement will not hide costs elsewhere.
6. **Respond to counters deliberately.** Compare each counter to your target and total cost before replying. → *Expect:* you do not concede beyond your plan.
7. **Confirm final terms in writing.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before paying, signing, or scheduling, confirm total price, included items, deadlines, and refund/cancellation terms. → *Expect:* both sides have the same written agreement.
8. **Pay only through safe channels.** Use the agreed invoice, platform, or traceable method. → *Expect:* payment matches confirmed terms.

## Decision points

- Seller says price is firm → decide whether non-price terms can still improve the deal.
- You have a competing quote → mention it honestly without fabricating.
- The seller pressures immediate payment → pause and verify legitimacy before proceeding.
- The price drops but scope changes → compare the full terms, not only the number.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Unsupported lowball:** detect an offer with no reason and a large discount → add a fair rationale or raise the offer.
- **F2 Hidden total cost:** detect shipping, fees, taxes, or add-ons after agreement → ask for an all-in written price.
- **F3 Verbal agreement only:** detect phone or chat terms not reflected in invoice → send a recap and wait for confirmation.
- **F4 Scam payment request:** detect wire, gift card, crypto, or off-platform pressure → stop and use protected payment or walk away.

## Verification

The final email or invoice states the agreed total price, scope or item, included fees, payment method, delivery or service date, and any cancellation or refund terms before money changes hands.

## Variations

- `freelance-services`: negotiate scope, revision rounds, timeline, and payment milestones along with price.
- `used-item`: condition, pickup logistics, and payment clearance matter as much as the offer.
- Template:
  "Thank you for the quote for [item/service]. I am interested, but my budget is closer to [amount] based on [reason].
  Would you be able to do [amount] if [prompt payment/flexible timing/reduced scope]?
  If so, please confirm the all-in price and included terms in writing."

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because negotiation can lead to payment, contracts, or scams. Do not fabricate competing offers, do not pay before written terms are clear, and avoid unsafe payment methods.
