## Goal

You respond to a wedding invitation clearly, on time, and with the exact attendance details the couple needs for planning.

## Preconditions

- The invitation, RSVP card, wedding website, or message with deadline and response method.
- Names of invited guests in your party.
- Meal choices, accessibility needs, and schedule conflicts if requested.

## Steps

1. **Read who is invited.** Check the envelope, invitation, website, or RSVP card for named guests and whether a plus-one or children are included. → *Expect:* you know exactly who may attend.
2. **Decide before the deadline.** Confirm travel, childcare, work, and budget quickly enough to answer on time. → *Expect:* you can give a firm yes or no.
3. **Use the requested RSVP method.** [BRANCH: RSVP card, fill and mail it | website, submit the form | text/email, reply directly to the named contact] → *Expect:* the response reaches the planning system the couple chose.
4. **Provide all requested details.** Include attendee names, acceptance or regrets, meal selections, song request if asked, and accessibility or allergy notes. → *Expect:* the couple has complete count and logistics information.
5. **Keep the message warm and brief.** Congratulate them whether accepting or declining. → *Expect:* the response is gracious without adding unnecessary explanation.
6. **Update promptly if an emergency changes your answer.** Contact the couple or designated planner as soon as possible. → *Expect:* they can adjust seating, meals, or guest count if still possible.

## Decision points

- Invitation names only you → do not add a guest unless the couple explicitly offers one.
- Children are not named → assume they are not invited unless the invitation or website says otherwise.
- You are declining → no long explanation is needed; a kind regret is enough.
- Dietary restriction is serious → state the restriction clearly, not just a preference.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missed RSVP deadline:** detect the date has passed → respond immediately and apologize briefly.
- **F2 Website submission unclear:** detect no confirmation screen or email → message the listed contact with the same details.
- **F3 Need to change from yes to no:** detect illness, emergency, or immovable conflict → notify the couple promptly and accept any costs already incurred.
- **F4 Added uninvited guest:** detect you submitted too many attendees → correct it immediately and apologize.

## Verification

The RSVP has been submitted by the requested method and includes attendance status, guest names, meal choices if requested, and any essential accommodation or allergy note.

## Variations

- `accepting`: "With joy, Alex Rivera accepts. Meal choice: salmon. I am so happy to celebrate with you."
- `declining`: "With warm regrets, Alex Rivera is unable to attend. I am so sorry to miss it and will be thinking of you both."
- `family invitation`: "The Rivera family accepts for Alex and Jordan; Sam and Lee are unable to attend. Meals: two vegetarian."

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Respect the named guest list, avoid sharing private reasons for declining unless you want to, and communicate serious allergies or accessibility needs clearly.
