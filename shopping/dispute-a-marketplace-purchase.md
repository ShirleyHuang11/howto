## Goal

Dispute a marketplace order that was not received, not as described, counterfeit, damaged, or incorrectly refunded.

## Preconditions

- You have the order number, seller name, listing, messages, payment receipt, and tracking.
- You know the marketplace dispute deadline and seller response window.
- You can photograph the item, packaging, labels, and damage if received.

## Steps

1. **Read the marketplace policy.** Find deadlines for item-not-received, not-as-described, counterfeit, damaged, return, and appeal claims. → *Expect:* the correct claim type and deadline are known.
2. **Collect evidence.** Save listing screenshots, photos, tracking, delivery photo, packaging, serial numbers, messages, and payment record. → *Expect:* the evidence supports your specific issue.
3. **Contact the seller in-platform.** Explain the problem, requested remedy, and evidence without moving to email or text. → *Expect:* the seller response is recorded by the marketplace.
4. **Wait only the required period.** Give the seller the platform's stated response time, not an indefinite extension. → *Expect:* you know when escalation is allowed.
5. **Open the marketplace dispute.** [BRANCH: item not received | not as described | damaged | counterfeit | refund issue] select the accurate reason and upload evidence. → *Expect:* the case number and deadline appear.
6. **Follow return instructions exactly.** If return is required, use the provided label, photograph the packed item, and keep drop-off receipt. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once shipped back, you lose possession, so document condition and tracking first. → *Expect:* tracking proves return handoff.
7. **Respond to case messages.** Upload requested evidence before each deadline and keep communication in-platform. → *Expect:* the case remains active.
8. **Escalate or appeal if needed.** If seller refuses or the decision ignores evidence, use marketplace appeal before deadline. → *Expect:* a reviewer receives the full record.
9. **Consider payment dispute last.** If marketplace process fails, compare card or payment-service dispute deadlines and risks. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* chargebacks can affect marketplace account standing, so use them only with saved evidence. → *Expect:* you choose escalation knowingly.

## Decision points

- Tracking says delivered but you did not receive it → check address, carrier proof, delivery photo, and marketplace item-not-received rules.
- Item is counterfeit → include authenticity proof, serial check, or expert statement where possible.
- Seller offers partial refund → accept only if it fairly reflects the defect and does not waive needed rights.
- Marketplace requires police report or carrier claim → provide only accurate reports and keep copies.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Deadline missed:** detect claim window closed, recover by checking payment-card dispute deadlines or seller goodwill.
- **F2 Off-platform messages:** detect seller asks to leave platform, recover by restating that all communication must stay in the case.
- **F3 Return tracking lost:** detect no scan, recover with drop-off receipt and carrier inquiry.
- **F4 Evidence too vague:** detect case denied for insufficient proof, recover by adding photos, serials, listing screenshots, and timeline.
- **F5 Refund short:** detect fees or shipping not refunded, recover by citing policy and reopening or appealing.

## Verification

The marketplace case shows refund, replacement, return acceptance, or written final decision, and your payment account reflects any approved refund.

## Variations

- Local marketplace pickup: inspect before payment because platform protection may be limited.
- International order: customs, return shipping, and seller response times can alter remedies.
- Payment-service dispute: deadlines and required evidence differ from marketplace rules.

## Safety & privacy

Keep messages on-platform, do not share verification codes or full card numbers, and document items before returning them.
