## Goal

Buy a refrigerator, washer, dryer, dishwasher, range, or similar appliance that fits the space, utilities, delivery path, budget, and service needs.

## Preconditions

- You can measure the installation space, doorways, stairs, and utility connections.
- You know budget, must-have features, and desired delivery date.
- You can access retailer policies for delivery, installation, haul-away, returns, and warranty.

## Steps

1. **Measure the real space.** Record width, height, depth, ventilation clearance, door swing, hoses, plugs, gas line, water line, and drain location. → *Expect:* a model can be rejected before purchase if it will not fit.
2. **Measure the delivery path.** Measure doorways, hallways, elevators, turns, stairs, and old appliance removal path. → *Expect:* delivery risk is known before scheduling.
3. **Define required features.** List capacity, fuel type, voltage, finish, noise, efficiency rating, accessibility, and smart features you actually need. → *Expect:* comparison filters match the household use.
4. **Compare total cost.** Add item price, sales tax, delivery, installation kit, haul-away, permit if needed, restocking fee, warranty, and financing cost. → *Expect:* the final cost is not just the shelf price.
5. **Check reliability and service.** Read recent reviews for failure patterns, parts availability, local repair support, and warranty length. → *Expect:* weak models or hard-to-service brands are flagged.
6. **Review policies.** Confirm return window, damage reporting deadline, installation exclusions, old-appliance disconnection rules, and cancellation fees. → *Expect:* you know what must be ready on delivery day.
7. **Place the order.** Choose the exact model, accessories, delivery slot, installation, haul-away, and payment method. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* custom orders, installed appliances, and delivery fees may be hard to refund, so confirm measurements and policies before paying. → *Expect:* receipt shows model number, services, fees, and delivery date.
8. **Inspect at delivery.** Check model number, visible damage, missing parts, and fit before signing or accepting installation. → *Expect:* defects are documented before the crew leaves.
9. **Register and save records.** Save receipt, serial number, warranty, manuals, and installer notes. → *Expect:* warranty support can be started later without searching.

## Decision points

- The appliance barely fits → choose a smaller model or confirm clearance with installer before ordering.
- Installation requires gas, electrical, plumbing, or cabinet work → schedule a qualified professional before delivery.
- Financing is offered → compare promotional terms, deferred interest, and fees against paying cash or card.
- Delivery arrives damaged → refuse delivery or document damage immediately under retailer policy.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Fit failure:** detect blocked installation or door swing, recover by refusing delivery if allowed or arranging exchange quickly.
- **F2 Hidden installation fee:** detect added kit, haul-away, permit, or labor charge, recover by reviewing invoice before approval.
- **F3 Delivery damage:** detect dents, broken glass, or missing parts, recover by photographing and reporting within the policy deadline.
- **F4 Utility mismatch:** detect wrong plug, gas type, or hose connection, recover by pausing installation and getting correct parts or trade help.
- **F5 Warranty confusion:** detect unclear coverage, recover by reading manufacturer warranty before buying extended protection.

## Verification

The installed appliance matches the receipt model number, operates through a basic cycle or startup check, and all delivery, warranty, and payment records are saved.

## Variations

- `us`: appliance delivery, haul-away, installation licensing, and rebate programs vary by retailer, utility, and state.
- Rental home: landlord approval may be required before installation or removal.
- Built-in appliances: cabinet dimensions and professional installation matter more than freestanding models.

## Safety & privacy

Major appliances involve heavy objects, electricity, water, and gas. Do not bypass installation requirements, and protect payment and address details in delivery messages.
