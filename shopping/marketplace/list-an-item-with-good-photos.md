## Goal

You produce marketplace photos that accurately show an item's value, condition, size, and defects so the listing gets serious buyers and fewer disputes.

## Preconditions

- The item cleaned and ready to stage.
- A phone or camera with enough storage and battery.
- A plain background, good light, and any accessories included in the sale.

## Steps

1. **Clean and stage the item.** Remove dust, personal items, clutter, and unrelated objects from the frame. → *Expect:* the item looks presentable without hiding condition.
2. **Choose honest lighting.** Use bright indirect daylight or neutral indoor light; avoid filters that change color. → *Expect:* photos show the true color and surface condition.
3. **Capture the required angles.** Photograph front, back, sides, top/bottom, labels, ports, accessories, and packaging. → *Expect:* a buyer can inspect the full item remotely.
4. **Show scale and fit.** Include measurements, a ruler, model number, size tag, or context object when relevant. → *Expect:* the buyer can judge dimensions or compatibility.
5. **Document every flaw.** Photograph scratches, dents, stains, missing parts, repairs, or wear close-up and in context. → *Expect:* defects are visible before a buyer commits.
6. **Check sharpness and crop.** Retake blurry, dark, overexposed, or cut-off images. → *Expect:* all photos are clear on a phone screen and desktop view.
7. **Remove private information.** Hide addresses, license plates, barcodes tied to accounts, faces, documents, and reflections. → *Expect:* photos disclose the item, not personal data.
8. **Upload photos in buyer-priority order.** Put the strongest full-item image first, then condition, details, flaws, and included extras. → *Expect:* the listing gallery tells a complete condition story.
9. **Publish with matching text.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before making the listing live, confirm the description mentions the flaws shown in photos. → *Expect:* photos and written condition agree.

## Decision points

- Item is high value → add serial/model photos for proof, but obscure sensitive numbers if theft or warranty misuse is a risk.
- Color is hard to capture → include a daylight photo and describe the color plainly.
- Item is large → show access constraints, disassembly, and pickup condition.
- Item is reflective → angle the camera to avoid showing faces, rooms, or documents.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Buyer says flaw was hidden:** detect dispute after sale → point to flaw photos and improve first-gallery visibility next time.
- **F2 Low interest from poor main photo:** detect views but no inquiries → replace the first photo with a brighter full-item shot.
- **F3 Personal data leak:** detect address or reflection in uploaded photo → remove the photo immediately and upload a cropped replacement.
- **F4 Misleading scale:** detect repeated size questions → add measurements and a scale photo.
- **F5 Platform rejects image:** detect upload or moderation error → remove watermarks, prohibited content, or excessive text overlays.

## Verification

The listing has a complete photo gallery showing the whole item, included accessories, measurements or scale where needed, and all material flaws, with no private information visible.

## Variations

- Clothing: include size tags, measurements, fabric texture, and fit view if available.
- Electronics: show ports, model label, powered-on screen, accessories, and damage.
- Furniture: show dimensions, underside, fabric wear, and pickup access constraints.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because photos can reveal home details and because misleading photos cause disputes. Remove private information, show flaws clearly, and confirm gallery accuracy before publishing.
