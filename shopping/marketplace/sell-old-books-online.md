## Goal

You identify, price, list, pack, and ship used books so they sell for a realistic net return without condition disputes.

## Preconditions

- Books you own and are allowed to sell.
- Access to ISBNs, edition details, condition information, and shipping materials.
- A marketplace, buyback site, or bookstore trade-in account.

## Steps

1. **Sort books by resale path.** Separate textbooks, recent trade books, rare/collectible books, damaged books, and donations. → *Expect:* each book has a likely sale, buyback, collectible, or donation path.
2. **Identify exact editions.** Use ISBN, title, author, edition, format, publisher, publication year, and any access-code status. → *Expect:* each sellable book is matched to the correct edition.
3. **Grade condition honestly.** Note highlighting, writing, water damage, odors, missing dust jackets, broken spines, ex-library marks, and access codes. → *Expect:* every book has a defensible condition grade and flaw list.
4. **Compare net prices, not headline prices.** Check sold listings, buyback quotes, fees, shipping cost, and expected time to sell. → *Expect:* you know the best net channel for each book.
5. **List or accept buyback offers.** [BRANCH: marketplace listing | instant buyback] Enter exact edition, condition, photos for flaws, price, shipping, and handling time. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* accepting a buyback quote or publishing a listing creates buyer expectations; confirm edition and condition first. → *Expect:* books are live for sale or accepted into a buyback shipment.
6. **Pack books to prevent transit damage.** Use rigid mailers or boxes, moisture protection where appropriate, and padding so corners do not crush. → *Expect:* packed books cannot slide or bend in transit.
7. **Ship with tracking by the deadline.** Buy the correct label, upload tracking, and keep the receipt. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once shipped, recovery depends on carrier and marketplace rules; confirm buyer/address/contents before mailing. → *Expect:* tracking is active and attached to the order.
8. **Monitor delivery, payout, and disputes.** Respond to condition questions with photos and listing text. → *Expect:* payout is released or any issue is addressed with evidence.

## Decision points

- Book has no ISBN or appears collectible → research specialized book marketplaces and edition points before pricing.
- Shipping cost exceeds expected proceeds → bundle, donate, or use local sale instead.
- Textbook includes an access code → state whether it is unused, used, missing, or unknown.
- Buyback quote is condition-sensitive → downgrade honestly before shipping to avoid rejected payouts.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong edition sold:** detect buyer complaint or ISBN mismatch → offer cancellation before shipping or accept return according to policy.
- **F2 Condition downgrade:** detect buyback site pays less than quoted → review their photos/reason and decide whether to accept return or reduced payout.
- **F3 Transit damage:** detect bent or wet book on delivery → file carrier claim if insured and provide packing evidence.
- **F4 Slow-moving inventory:** detect no views or offers after the target period → lower price, bundle, switch platform, or donate.
- **F5 Access-code dispute:** detect buyer expected a valid code → point to the listing disclosure or resolve through return/refund if unclear.

## Verification

Each book is either sold and shipped with tracking, accepted by a buyback program with shipment proof, or removed from the sale pile, and any sold-book payout is received or scheduled.

## Variations

- Textbooks: semester timing matters; list before classes start for better demand.
- Collectible books: photos of edition points, dust jacket, signatures, and defects are essential.
- Local sale: avoid shipping costs but require safe meetup and payment confirmation.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because payments, addresses, and condition disputes are involved. Remove bookmarks or personal papers, photograph flaws, use tracked shipping, and do not overstate edition, signature, or access-code value.
