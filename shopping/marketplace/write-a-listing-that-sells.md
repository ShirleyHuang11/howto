## Goal

You write a marketplace listing that is searchable, honest, and specific enough to attract serious buyers while preventing avoidable disputes.

## Preconditions

- The item details, photos, condition notes, measurements, and included accessories.
- A target platform and understanding of its required fields.
- A private minimum price and preferred handoff or shipping method.

## Steps

1. **Name the item precisely.** Use brand, model, size, color, material, year, compatibility, or part number in the title. → *Expect:* buyers searching for the exact item can find it.
2. **Lead with the essential facts.** State condition, what is included, whether it works, and why it is being sold if helpful. → *Expect:* the first lines answer the buyer's main questions.
3. **Disclose flaws plainly.** List scratches, stains, missing parts, repairs, odors, defects, or limitations without softening them. → *Expect:* the description matches the photos and reduces disputes.
4. **Add measurements and compatibility.** Include dimensions, size, model numbers, system requirements, or fit notes where relevant. → *Expect:* buyers can verify the item fits their need.
5. **State sale terms.** Include price firmness, pickup area, shipping availability, payment method, bundle options, and whether holds are allowed. → *Expect:* buyers know how to complete the purchase.
6. **Avoid risky claims.** Do not use brand names, authenticity claims, medical claims, or warranty promises you cannot support. → *Expect:* the listing is accurate and policy-compliant.
7. **Format for scanning.** Use short paragraphs or bullets, no keyword stuffing, and no aggressive all-caps pressure. → *Expect:* the listing is easy to read on mobile.
8. **Review before publishing.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm title, price, condition, flaws, and terms before listing goes live. → *Expect:* the listing can be published without immediate corrections.

## Decision points

- Item has known defects → mention them in both description and photos, then price accordingly.
- Buyers commonly ask the same question → add the answer to the listing rather than repeating it in messages.
- Platform limits title length → prioritize brand, model, size, and condition keywords.
- You need a fast sale → write "priced for pickup this week" only if you will honor that price.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Too vague:** detect repeated basic questions → add model, measurements, included items, and pickup/shipping terms.
- **F2 Misleading condition:** detect buyer complaint or return request → compare against your wording and revise future listings more explicitly.
- **F3 Policy violation:** detect listing removal → remove prohibited claims, restricted goods, or keyword misuse before relisting.
- **F4 Low-quality inquiries:** detect many unserious messages → tighten price firmness, pickup rules, and buyer requirements.
- **F5 Search invisibility:** detect few views → improve title keywords and category selection.

## Verification

The listing text includes exact item identity, condition, flaws, included items, price, and transaction terms, and it can be published with photos that support every material claim.

## Variations

- Local marketplace: include pickup area and safe-meetup preference.
- Shipping marketplace: include handling time, carrier, return terms, and package contents.
- Collectibles: include provenance and condition grade only when supportable.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because inaccurate listings can trigger disputes and unsafe buyers. Do not include home address or personal contact details, and confirm all claims before publishing.
