## Goal

You set a marketplace price that is low enough to sell quickly but still above your minimum acceptable net proceeds.

## Preconditions

- The item identity, condition, included accessories, and urgency.
- A target platform or local market.
- An estimate of platform fees, payment fees, shipping cost, and packing cost.

## Steps

1. **Define your deadline and floor.** Decide when the item must sell and the minimum net amount you will accept. → *Expect:* a clear speed target and walk-away number.
2. **Research sold prices.** Find recent completed sales for the same item in similar condition and location or shipping terms. → *Expect:* a sold-price range, not just asking prices.
3. **Calculate net proceeds.** Subtract platform fees, payment fees, shipping subsidies, packaging, travel, and expected discounts. → *Expect:* a realistic net amount at several price points.
4. **Adjust for condition and completeness.** Price lower for damage, missing parts, no box, weak battery, odors, or uncertain compatibility. → *Expect:* your price reflects buyer risk.
5. **Choose a fast-sale price.** Set the listing below the median comparable sold price while staying above your floor. → *Expect:* the price is visibly competitive against similar listings.
6. **Build in offer strategy.** [BRANCH: firm price for urgent low-hassle sale | slightly higher price with room to negotiate] → *Expect:* your public price and private acceptance range are defined.
7. **Check total buyer cost.** Include shipping, taxes, pickup effort, or delivery fees in the buyer's perspective. → *Expect:* the all-in price remains attractive.
8. **Publish or update the listing.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm net proceeds and minimum before making the price live. → *Expect:* the listing shows the chosen price and terms.
9. **Monitor early signal.** After the first view/message window, compare interest against deadline. → *Expect:* you know whether the price is working.

## Decision points

- Many views but no messages → price may be high or photos weak; improve listing or reduce price.
- Immediate flood of full-price buyers → price may be too low; honor accepted commitments but adjust unsold duplicate items.
- Shipping makes total too high → offer local pickup or choose a lower-cost carrier if safe.
- Deadline is today → price below comparable sold listings and require immediate pickup or payment.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hidden fee loss:** detect payout below floor after sale → include all fees in future pricing; do not accept offers without net math.
- **F2 Anchored to purchase price:** detect no interest because you priced by what you paid → reprice based on current sold comps.
- **F3 Race-to-bottom mistake:** detect a fast sale below needed value → use a written minimum before publishing next time.
- **F4 Buyer negotiates after agreement:** detect on-site lowball → restate agreed price or walk away.
- **F5 Stale comps:** detect old prices from a different season or model cycle → refresh comps and reprice.

## Verification

The item is listed at a price with calculated net proceeds at or above your minimum, below or competitive with recent comparable sold prices, and aligned with the required sale deadline.

## Variations

- Local pickup: discount for buyer travel and no shipping risk.
- High-demand drops: price against current market depth and beware fake high asks.
- Bulky goods: factor moving effort and delivery into the buyer's all-in cost.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because pricing decisions affect money and negotiation pressure. Set a private floor, calculate net proceeds before accepting, and do not let urgency override safe payment or meetup practices.
