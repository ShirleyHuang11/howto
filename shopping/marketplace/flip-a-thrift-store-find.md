## Goal

You evaluate, buy, prepare, and resell a thrift-store item only when the expected resale profit justifies the cost, effort, and risk.

## Preconditions

- A resale platform account or local marketplace access.
- A budget limit for thrift purchases.
- A phone for sold-comps research and condition photos.

## Steps

1. **Scan only categories you understand.** Focus on brands, materials, electronics, books, home goods, or clothing where you can judge demand and defects. → *Expect:* you avoid random speculative purchases.
2. **Identify a candidate item.** Record brand, model, size, material, condition, completeness, and price tag. → *Expect:* enough information exists to research resale value.
3. **Check sold comps in the store.** Search recent sold listings for the exact or closest item and condition. → *Expect:* a realistic resale range.
4. **Estimate all costs.** Include thrift price, cleaning, repairs, batteries, packaging, shipping, platform fees, returns, and your time. → *Expect:* a maximum buy price and expected profit.
5. **Inspect condition hard.** Check stains, cracks, missing pieces, battery corrosion, odors, authenticity, and whether electronics power on if allowed. → *Expect:* hidden costs are found before purchase.
6. **Decide buy or pass.** Buy only if expected net profit exceeds your target and the item can be listed honestly. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm condition and max buy price before checkout because returns may be limited. → *Expect:* you either buy a qualified item or leave it.
7. **Clean and test after purchase.** Remove dust, launder if appropriate, test functions, and gather included parts. → *Expect:* the item is ready for accurate photos.
8. **List with thrift-specific disclosure.** Photograph condition, disclose flaws, set a competitive price, and avoid unsupported authenticity claims. → *Expect:* the listing is live with profit target preserved.
9. **Ship or hand off after payment.** Use safe marketplace payment and tracked shipping or public meetup. → *Expect:* the sale is completed with evidence.
10. **Calculate actual profit.** Subtract every cost from payout. → *Expect:* you know whether the flip met the target.

## Decision points

- Sold comps are high but rare → buy only if you can wait or price lower for faster sale.
- Item needs repair → buy only if repair cost and skill are known.
- Authenticity is uncertain → pass unless you can authenticate before resale.
- Profit is small and item is bulky → pass because storage and shipping risk erase margin.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bad comp:** detect listed prices but no sold prices → reprice based on actual sale data or skip future similar items.
- **F2 Hidden defect:** detect problem after purchase → disclose and sell for parts, return if allowed, or donate.
- **F3 Overlooked shipping cost:** detect parcel cost kills margin → switch to local sale or avoid bulky items.
- **F4 Counterfeit risk:** detect suspicious tags or construction → do not sell as authentic; seek return if possible.
- **F5 Slow sell-through:** detect item sits past target window → reprice, bundle, or clear inventory.

## Verification

The thrift item was purchased at or below the calculated maximum buy price, listed accurately, sold with verified payment, and produced actual net profit at or above your target.

## Variations

- Clothing: check fabric content, seams, stains, size tags, and current style demand.
- Electronics: test power, ports, battery compartments, and missing accessories before buying.
- Books/media: scan ISBNs or barcodes and check edition, condition, and fees.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because upfront spending can become dead inventory. Keep a buy cap, avoid counterfeit or unsafe goods, disclose defects, and verify payment before releasing the item.
