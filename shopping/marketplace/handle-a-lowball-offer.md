## Goal

You respond to a lowball marketplace offer without wasting time, dropping below your floor, or escalating into an unsafe or unproductive negotiation.

## Preconditions

- An active listing and a buyer offer below asking price.
- A private minimum net amount and current comparable sale range.
- Platform messaging access.

## Steps

1. **Pause before replying.** Compare the offer against your minimum net, fees, shipping, and urgency. → *Expect:* you know whether the offer is unacceptable, possible, or acceptable.
2. **Classify the offer.** Decide if it is a serious opening offer, a bulk/bundle opportunity, or a time-waster. → *Expect:* your response effort matches buyer quality.
3. **Send a concise counter or decline.** Use one clear price, such as "I can do X if picked up today" or "No thanks, price is firm." → *Expect:* the buyer has a concrete next step.
4. **Avoid defending the price at length.** Point to condition, comps, included extras, or pickup convenience once if needed. → *Expect:* the conversation stays focused.
5. **Set a deadline for special terms.** If you offer a discount for fast pickup or bundle, make the deadline explicit. → *Expect:* the buyer cannot keep a discounted offer open indefinitely.
6. **Stop if pressure continues.** Do not keep negotiating with insults, guilt, or repeated below-floor offers. → *Expect:* your time and price floor are protected.
7. **Accept only if terms are safe.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before accepting, confirm payment method, handoff/shipping, and net proceeds remain above your floor. → *Expect:* any accepted low offer is still a safe deal.

## Decision points

- Offer is below floor by a lot → decline or ignore depending on platform norms.
- Offer is low but buyer can pick up immediately → counter at the lowest fast-sale price you can accept.
- Buyer bundles multiple items → evaluate total net, not each discount separately.
- Buyer changes terms after acceptance → return to your last safe offer or cancel.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Emotional discounting:** detect you are tempted to accept below floor → step away and recalculate net.
- **F2 Endless counter loop:** detect repeated small increases below floor → state final price and stop responding.
- **F3 Pickup lowball:** detect buyer brings less money in person → do not hand over unless the new price is acceptable.
- **F4 Scam masked as lowball:** detect offer plus off-platform payment or courier story → decline and report.
- **F5 Missed better buyer:** detect you held item too long for a lowballer → use short holds only after firm commitment.

## Verification

The lowball offer is declined, countered, or accepted in writing, and any accepted deal preserves your minimum net proceeds and safe payment/handoff terms.

## Variations

- Fixed-price platform: use formal counteroffer tools and expiration settings.
- Local marketplace: discounts can be tied to same-day pickup.
- Clearance goal: lower your floor intentionally before negotiating, not during pressure.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because rushed negotiation can lead to bad payment or unsafe handoff. Keep a private floor, do not reveal desperation, and verify payment before releasing the item.
