## Goal

You diagnose why a listing has not sold and adjust price, terms, or presentation so it has a realistic path to sale without dropping below your minimum.

## Preconditions

- An active marketplace listing that has not sold.
- Access to listing analytics, messages, saved comps, and fee/shipping estimates.
- A private minimum net amount or a decision to clear the item regardless of price.

## Steps

1. **Record current performance.** Note days listed, views, saves, messages, offers, current price, and total buyer cost. → *Expect:* a baseline for deciding what to change.
2. **Refresh comparable sales.** Search recent sold listings for the same item, condition, location, and shipping terms. → *Expect:* current market value is known.
3. **Diagnose the blocker.** Compare price, photos, title, description, shipping, pickup distance, and condition disclosure against sold comps. → *Expect:* the most likely reason for no sale is identified.
4. **Recalculate your floor.** Include fees, shipping, packaging, storage cost, and urgency. → *Expect:* a revised minimum acceptable net.
5. **Choose the adjustment.** [BRANCH: lower price | improve photos/title | offer bundle | add shipping or local pickup | accept offers] → *Expect:* a specific change tied to the blocker.
6. **Apply the reprice or listing edit.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm the new visible price and net proceeds before saving because buyers can act immediately. → *Expect:* the live listing reflects the updated price or terms.
7. **Message active watchers when appropriate.** Use platform tools to send an offer or price-drop notification without pressuring buyers off-platform. → *Expect:* interested buyers receive the updated offer.
8. **Set a next review date.** Decide when to lower again, bundle, donate, trade in, or remove the item. → *Expect:* the listing has a clear next action if it remains unsold.

## Decision points

- High views and saves, no offers → reduce price or send offers to watchers.
- Low views → improve title, category, and first photo before cutting deeply.
- Many questions about shipping or pickup → clarify logistics or add a shipping option.
- New comp prices fell sharply → reprice now rather than waiting on stale value.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Price below floor:** detect new net would lose money → cancel the edit and choose a non-price improvement.
- **F2 Repricing too often:** detect buyers waiting for repeated drops → set scheduled reductions instead of reactive cuts.
- **F3 Hidden shipping deterrent:** detect item price is fine but all-in cost is high → adjust package size, carrier, or local pickup option.
- **F4 Condition mismatch:** detect buyers disengage after details → add honest flaw photos and price accordingly.
- **F5 Platform stale listing:** detect search placement has collapsed → relist only if allowed and accurate, or create a refreshed listing per policy.

## Verification

The live listing has an updated price or terms based on current sold comps, expected net proceeds remain at or above your floor, and a dated next action is set if it still does not sell.

## Variations

- eBay: use offers to watchers or promoted listing tools only after checking fee impact.
- Facebook Marketplace: renewing or deleting/relisting may be available depending on category and policy.
- Seasonal goods: reprice before the demand window ends.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because repricing can trigger immediate purchase or loss. Confirm net proceeds before saving, keep negotiation inside the platform, and avoid misleading urgency claims.
