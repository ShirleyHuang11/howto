## Goal

You organize, advertise, price, and run a garage sale so unwanted items convert to cash without unsafe meetups, pricing chaos, or unresolved cleanup.

## Preconditions

- Items you are allowed to sell, separated from anything you intend to keep.
- A legal place to host the sale, such as your garage, driveway, yard, or an approved community sale location.
- Small bills, coins, tables or boxes, price stickers, bags, and a phone with a payment app if you choose to accept digital payments.
- Awareness of any local permit, HOA, apartment, or signage rules.

## Steps

1. **Check local rules and choose the sale window.** Confirm whether permits, time limits, sign restrictions, or neighborhood rules apply, then pick a date with a rain backup. → *Expect:* a permitted sale date, start time, end time, and backup plan.
2. **Sort items into sell, donate, discard, and keep piles.** Remove recalled, unsafe, counterfeit, personal-data-bearing, or legally restricted items. → *Expect:* only sale-eligible items remain in the sell pile.
3. **Price items before sale day.** Use simple tiers, visible stickers, and bundle pricing for low-value goods; mark high-value items individually. → *Expect:* each item or category has a clear price, plus a private minimum for negotiable items.
4. **Prepare payment handling.** Start with change, decide whether to accept payment apps, and set one person as cashier. → *Expect:* cash box, change, and accepted payment methods are ready.
5. **Advertise with boundaries.** Post the sale date, hours, general location, item highlights, and photos on approved local channels; avoid posting private household details beyond what buyers need. → *Expect:* the sale is findable and the public listing shows correct hours and location.
6. **Stage the sale for browsing and supervision.** Put valuables near the cashier, group similar items, keep walkways clear, and block access to private areas. → *Expect:* buyers can browse without entering private spaces or handling unsupervised valuables.
7. **Open on time and track sales.** Greet buyers, answer condition questions honestly, and record high-value sales or digital payments. → *Expect:* transactions are logged enough to reconcile cash and payment-app receipts.
8. **Negotiate without losing control.** [BRANCH: fixed-price item | negotiable item] Decline offers below your private floor and use bundle discounts to move low-value inventory. → *Expect:* accepted prices meet your minimums or the item remains available.
9. **Confirm payment before releasing goods.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once a buyer leaves with an item, recovery is unlikely; count cash or verify digital payment inside your own app first. → *Expect:* the buyer leaves only after real payment is confirmed.
10. **Close, reconcile, and dispose of leftovers.** Remove signs, count cash, compare digital receipts, mark donations, and secure unsold valuables. → *Expect:* sale proceeds are counted, signs are down, and every leftover item has a next destination.

## Decision points

- Rain or unsafe weather → postpone using the advertised backup date instead of moving buyers through unsafe conditions.
- Buyer wants to enter the home for an item → bring the item outside or decline; do not invite strangers into private rooms.
- High-value item attracts haggling → require payment confirmation and a written receipt with item condition stated.
- Local rules prohibit signs or sales → use approved community boards or a hosted community sale instead.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Payment app spoof:** detect a screenshot or notification but no balance change in your account → keep the item until the payment appears in your own app.
- **F2 Theft or item switching:** detect missing valuables or swapped labels → keep expensive items supervised and pause the sale if you need to reset layout.
- **F3 Neighbor or rule complaint:** detect a warning from HOA, landlord, police, or city staff → remove signs, reduce crowding, or close early rather than escalating.
- **F4 Pricing confusion:** detect buyers disputing unlabeled prices → pause that category, add visible labels, and restart with a consistent rule.

## Verification

The sale has ended, all public signs/posts are removed or updated, proceeds are counted, digital payments are confirmed in your accounts, and all unsold items are assigned to keep, donate, relist, or discard.

## Variations

- `us`: some cities require a garage-sale permit or limit the number of sales per year; check city or county rules before advertising.
- Community sale: the host may control hours, booth space, payment rules, and cleanup deadlines.
- Apartment or condo: use approved common areas only and confirm elevator, parking, and guest-access policies.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because strangers come near your home and money changes hands. Keep private doors locked, do not display documents with personal data, supervise valuables, avoid holding large visible cash, and confirm every payment before releasing goods.
