## Goal

You create a Depop listing that attracts the right buyer, keeps style and condition claims honest, and ships only through a protected payment flow.

## Preconditions

- A Depop account with payment and payout settings completed.
- Clothing or accessories cleaned, measured, and ready to ship.
- Packaging, scale, and carrier access.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the item closely.** Check stains, holes, repairs, odors, label condition, and wear. → *Expect:* you know what must be disclosed.
2. **Define the style and buyer.** Identify era, cut, brand, fabric, color, and searchable aesthetic terms without making false vintage or designer claims. → *Expect:* a keyword list grounded in the actual item.
3. **Measure and photograph.** Capture front/back, worn or flat-lay view, tags, fabric, flaws, and measurements in notes. → *Expect:* photos and measurements support the description.
4. **Research sold and active comps.** Compare Depop and other resale platforms for similar brand, condition, and style. → *Expect:* a list price and private minimum after fees and shipping.
5. **Create the listing.** Enter category, size, condition, price, shipping method, and relevant hashtags. → *Expect:* the draft reaches the intended buyers and has no required fields missing.
6. **Write a transparent description.** Include measurements, fit notes, fabric, flaws, shipping timing, and whether offers are welcome. → *Expect:* buyers understand fit and condition before messaging.
7. **Publish the listing.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm price, shipping responsibility, size, and condition details before going live. → *Expect:* the listing appears in your Depop shop.
8. **Manage messages and offers.** Keep communication on Depop, answer fit questions, and decline off-app payment or trades you cannot verify. → *Expect:* any deal is documented in the app.
9. **Ship after confirmed payment.** Use the agreed shipping method, upload tracking if required, and keep drop-off proof. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* verify the Depop order status before mailing. → *Expect:* tracking is active and visible to the buyer.
10. **Release payout records.** Track sale price, fees, shipping cost, and payout availability. → *Expect:* proceeds are available or scheduled and the order has no dispute.

## Decision points

- Buyer wants a lower price plus free shipping → calculate total net before accepting.
- Item is handmade, altered, or unbranded → describe it precisely and avoid brand stuffing.
- Buyer asks to pay by friends-and-family transfer → decline because seller protection is lost.
- Condition is poor but stylish → disclose flaws prominently and price for repair or wear.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Off-app scam:** detect requests for external payment, email, or shipping labels → refuse and report inside Depop.
- **F2 Misdescribed vintage claim:** detect a buyer challenging age or authenticity → provide only evidence you have; revise future listing language.
- **F3 Shipping delay:** detect missed handling time → message the buyer in-app and ship with tracking immediately or cancel if you cannot fulfill.
- **F4 Item-not-received claim:** detect a delivery dispute → submit tracking and drop-off receipt.
- **F5 Color or fit complaint:** detect buyer dissatisfaction → refer to measurements and daylight photos; accept platform-directed outcomes.

## Verification

The Depop order shows paid and shipped with valid tracking, the payout is available or scheduled above your minimum, and there is no open dispute or off-platform obligation.

## Variations

- Vintage: include approximate era only when supported by tags, construction, or provenance.
- International shipping: quote carrier cost before enabling it and account for customs forms.
- Handmade or upcycled: disclose materials, alterations, and care limits clearly.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk due to payment, addresses, and counterfeit or misrepresentation claims. Keep the sale on Depop, avoid personal contact sharing, and verify payment before shipping.
