## Goal

You buy resale inventory only when expected profit, demand, legality, and operational effort justify the risk.

## Preconditions

- A resale niche or platform you understand.
- A budget cap and storage space.
- Access to sold-comps research and a way to inspect items before buying.

## Steps

1. **Choose a narrow sourcing target.** Define category, brands, sizes/models, condition limits, and platforms where you can resell. → *Expect:* you know what to buy and what to ignore.
2. **Set a buy formula.** Estimate resale price, platform fees, shipping, repairs, cleaning, supplies, taxes, and desired profit margin. → *Expect:* a maximum buy price for each target item.
3. **Check legal and platform restrictions.** Avoid counterfeit goods, recalled items, restricted products, stolen goods, and categories you cannot authenticate. → *Expect:* the sourcing list is compliant.
4. **Search sourcing channels.** [BRANCH: thrift stores | estate sales | clearance racks | local marketplaces | auctions | wholesale lots] Compare asking prices against your formula. → *Expect:* candidate items are filtered by profit potential.
5. **Inspect before buying.** Check condition, completeness, authenticity markers, serials, odors, damage, and sell-through demand. → *Expect:* only items meeting condition and authenticity thresholds remain.
6. **Negotiate or walk away.** Offer at or below your maximum buy price and leave if the seller will not meet it. → *Expect:* purchase price preserves expected profit.
7. **Buy and record inventory.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm max price, resale legality, and condition before paying because liquidation may be difficult. → *Expect:* purchased item has cost, source, date, and notes recorded.
8. **Prepare for listing.** Clean, test, photograph, and store the item safely. → *Expect:* inventory is ready to list without losing parts or condition evidence.
9. **Review sourcing performance.** Track sell-through time, profit, defects, returns, and dead inventory. → *Expect:* future sourcing decisions improve from actual results.

## Decision points

- Profit depends on perfect condition → buy only if you can inspect thoroughly.
- Item requires authentication you cannot perform → skip it or use a trusted authenticator before purchase.
- Large lot has mixed quality → price based on the worst likely sellable portion, not the best item.
- Storage or cash is tight → prioritize fast sell-through over high theoretical margin.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Counterfeit purchase:** detect failed authentication or suspect markers → do not resell as authentic; seek refund from source if possible.
- **F2 Hidden damage:** detect defect during testing → sell for parts with disclosure or return if allowed.
- **F3 Dead inventory:** detect no sale after planned window → reprice, bundle, donate, or stop sourcing that category.
- **F4 Fee miscalculation:** detect profit lower than expected → update buy formula with actual costs.
- **F5 Restricted product:** detect platform removal or recall → remove listing and dispose or return according to rules.

## Verification

Each sourced item has a recorded purchase price at or below its maximum buy price, passes legality and condition checks, and has an expected net profit margin after fees, shipping, and prep costs.

## Variations

- Thrift sourcing: inspect in-store because returns may be limited.
- Online auctions: include buyer premium and shipping in the maximum bid.
- Wholesale lots: sample quality and sell-through before buying volume.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because you are spending money before revenue exists. Use strict buy caps, avoid counterfeit or restricted goods, and keep records for taxes, disputes, and profitability.
