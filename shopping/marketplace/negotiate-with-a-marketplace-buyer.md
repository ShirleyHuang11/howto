## Goal

You negotiate with a buyer using a clear minimum price, safe communication, and firm payment terms so the sale closes without avoidable risk.

## Preconditions

- An active listing and a buyer inquiry or offer.
- A private minimum net price and preferred payment/handoff method.
- Knowledge of platform rules on offers, messages, and off-platform transactions.

## Steps

1. **Classify the buyer message.** Identify whether it is a specific question, serious offer, lowball, scam pattern, or logistics request. → *Expect:* you know whether to engage, counter, or ignore.
2. **Check your numbers before replying.** Recalculate fees, shipping, travel, bundle value, and your minimum net. → *Expect:* a firm acceptable range is ready.
3. **Respond with facts and boundaries.** Answer legitimate questions and state price, pickup/shipping, and payment terms clearly. → *Expect:* the buyer understands what is negotiable.
4. **Counter once with a reasoned offer.** If the buyer is below your target but above possible floor, counter with a specific price and deadline. → *Expect:* there is a concrete next price, not vague haggling.
5. **Keep communication on-platform.** Decline requests to move to text, email, wire, gift cards, or external payment. → *Expect:* the negotiation record remains protected.
6. **Confirm the complete deal.** Price, included items, payment method, pickup time/location or shipping address, and who pays shipping must all be explicit. → *Expect:* both sides have the same agreement in writing.
7. **Accept the offer or schedule handoff.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before accepting, confirm the final net is above your floor and the payment method is safe. → *Expect:* the platform shows an accepted offer or the meetup plan is set.
8. **Close or walk away.** If the buyer changes terms, pressures you, or fails to commit, move to the next buyer. → *Expect:* you either have a safe sale path or no obligation to that buyer.

## Decision points

- Offer is below floor → decline or counter once; do not justify endlessly.
- Buyer agrees on price but changes payment method → reset the agreement or stop.
- Buyer wants a hold → hold only with platform deposit/payment or a very short pickup window.
- Buyer is rude or pressuring → end the negotiation and preserve safety.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Overpayment scam:** detect buyer offers extra and asks refund or courier payment → decline and report.
- **F2 Endless haggling:** detect repeated tiny counters → state final price and deadline.
- **F3 Terms changed at pickup:** detect buyer brings less money → accept only if above floor; otherwise leave.
- **F4 Off-platform pressure:** detect request for phone, email, or alternative checkout → keep platform terms or cancel.
- **F5 Misunderstood inclusions:** detect buyer expected accessories not included → point to written agreement; update listing if unclear.

## Verification

The buyer has agreed in writing to a final price, included items, payment method, and handoff or shipping plan that keeps your expected net proceeds at or above your minimum.

## Variations

- Local cash sale: negotiate price before meeting and do not renegotiate in unsafe settings.
- Platform offers: use formal offer/counteroffer tools when available.
- Bundles: negotiate total net across all items, not discount percentage alone.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from scams, payment reversals, and personal meetups. Keep messages on-platform, set a private floor, verify payment independently, and do not share unnecessary personal details.
