## Goal

You combine related marketplace items into a bundle that increases buyer value, reduces shipping or meetup friction, and improves your total net proceeds.

## Preconditions

- Multiple items you own and are willing to sell together.
- Individual item values, conditions, and photos.
- Platform rules on bundles, combined shipping, and offers.

## Steps

1. **Group compatible items.** Choose items that serve the same buyer, fit together, or make shipping/pickup more efficient. → *Expect:* each bundle has a clear reason to exist.
2. **Price items individually first.** Estimate sold value, fees, and shipping for each item on its own. → *Expect:* you know the unbundled baseline.
3. **Calculate bundle economics.** Add combined shipping, packaging, platform fees, and discount needed to motivate a buyer. → *Expect:* a bundle price that beats separate sales without going below your total floor.
4. **Photograph the set.** Show everything together plus individual condition and flaws for each item. → *Expect:* buyers can verify all included pieces.
5. **Write the bundle listing.** List every included item, condition, sizes/models, missing pieces, and whether you will split the bundle. → *Expect:* the listing prevents confusion about contents.
6. **Set bundle terms.** Decide price, pickup/shipping, whether offers are allowed, and minimum accepted if buyer asks to remove items. → *Expect:* negotiation boundaries are defined.
7. **Publish or send a bundle offer.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm included items and total price before making the offer live. → *Expect:* the buyer sees a single clear bundle deal.
8. **Pack or hand over as one deal.** Confirm every item is included and payment covers the full bundle before release. → *Expect:* the buyer receives the complete set and you receive agreed payment.
9. **Update inventory.** Mark all bundled items sold or remove individual listings to avoid double-selling. → *Expect:* no unavailable item remains active.

## Decision points

- One item is valuable alone → bundle only if total net improves or clears slow inventory.
- Buyer wants to split → quote a separate price based on remaining item value and effort.
- Shipping becomes heavy or fragile → compare local pickup versus separate shipments.
- Bundle reduces search visibility → keep individual listings but clearly state bundle availability if platform allows.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Double sale:** detect an individual item sold after bundle agreement → cancel or replace only with buyer consent; remove duplicate listings promptly.
- **F2 Missing item dispute:** detect buyer says a piece was absent → use packing photos and item checklist.
- **F3 Over-discounting:** detect bundle payout below separate liquidation value → reprice or split the bundle.
- **F4 Shipping overweight:** detect label cost exceeds estimate → remeasure before accepting and adjust shipping.
- **F5 Confusing contents:** detect repeated buyer questions → rewrite included/excluded list and add labeled photos.

## Verification

The bundle listing or offer clearly states included items and total terms, expected net proceeds exceed your bundle floor, and all included items are marked sold or removed after payment.

## Variations

- Clothing bundles: group by size, style, season, or brand.
- Electronics bundles: include compatibility, cables, and tested status for each component.
- Local moving sale: bundle for pickup speed and disclose "must take all" terms.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because bundles involve higher total value and inventory confusion. Confirm contents, price, and payment before release, and remove duplicate listings immediately after sale.
