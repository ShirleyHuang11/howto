## Goal

You arrange and complete an in-person marketplace handoff in a public, controlled way that protects your safety, privacy, item, and payment.

## Preconditions

- A buyer who has agreed on the item, price, time window, and payment method.
- The item ready to inspect, with accessories and condition as described.
- A charged phone and transportation plan.

## Steps

1. **Screen the buyer before scheduling.** Look for normal account history, specific questions, and agreement on price and payment method. → *Expect:* the buyer appears legitimate enough to meet.
2. **Pick a public safe-exchange location.** Prefer a police-station exchange zone, bank lobby, busy store entrance, or camera-covered public place during daylight. → *Expect:* the meeting location is public, well-lit, and easy to leave.
3. **Share a limited plan with the buyer.** Confirm location, time, item, price, and payment method; do not share your home address unless unavoidable for large items. → *Expect:* both parties have the same meetup details.
4. **Tell a trusted person where you are going.** Share buyer name/profile, location, time, and expected return. → *Expect:* someone else knows the handoff plan.
5. **Prepare the item for quick inspection.** Bring only the item being sold, remove personal data, and keep expensive accessories controlled until payment. → *Expect:* inspection can happen without exposing unrelated belongings.
6. **Meet in the public area and keep boundaries.** Stay outside private vehicles and decline last-minute location changes to isolated places. → *Expect:* the buyer inspects the item in the agreed safe location.
7. **Confirm payment before handoff.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once the buyer leaves with the item, recovery is unlikely; count cash or verify payment in your own app first. → *Expect:* payment is confirmed before the item changes hands.
8. **Leave promptly and update the listing.** Do not count cash in public for longer than needed; mark the item sold after leaving if possible. → *Expect:* you exit safely and the marketplace status reflects the sale.

## Decision points

- Buyer asks to meet at your home → use a public location unless the item's size makes that impossible; if home pickup is necessary, keep buyers outside and have another adult present.
- Buyer changes location at the last minute → accept only if the new place is equally public and safe; otherwise reschedule.
- Buyer renegotiates after inspecting → accept only if the new price meets your private floor.
- Buyer brings extra people → continue only if the setting remains public and comfortable.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 No-show:** detect the buyer misses the agreed window → leave after your stated grace period and offer the item to the next buyer.
- **F2 Unsafe location pressure:** detect requests for a parking lot corner, home entry, or car handoff → move to a public spot or cancel.
- **F3 Fake payment:** detect payment only on the buyer's screen → keep the item until your own account confirms funds.
- **F4 Aggressive negotiation:** detect pressure, intimidation, or bait-and-switch pricing → end the meeting and leave with the item.

## Verification

The handoff occurs at the agreed public location, payment is verified in your own possession or account, the buyer receives only the sold item, and the listing is marked sold or unavailable.

## Variations

- Large furniture: stage the item near an exterior door or garage, keep private rooms closed, and require payment before loading.
- Electronics: meet where the buyer can test basic function without accessing your personal accounts.
- High-value goods: choose a bank or police exchange zone and bring another person.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from personal contact, money, and location exposure. Keep meetings public, preserve your home address, do not enter vehicles, and cancel when the buyer pushes against agreed safety boundaries.
