## Goal

You buy a refurbished device from a trustworthy source with clear condition, warranty, return rights, and post-delivery checks that catch defects early.

## Preconditions

- Exact device model, required specs, carrier/region compatibility if relevant, and maximum price.
- A list of acceptable refurbishers or retailer programs.
- Payment method with dispute rights and a delivery address.

## Steps

1. **Define required specs and compatibility.** Record model number, storage/RAM, carrier bands or lock status, power plug region, operating-system support, and battery threshold. → *Expect:* you can reject incompatible listings quickly.
2. **Choose reputable refurb sources.** Prefer manufacturer-certified, authorized retailer, or marketplace programs with written inspection standards, warranty, and returns. → *Expect:* candidate sellers have enforceable protections.
3. **Compare refurbished, open-box, used, and new prices.** Include warranty length, return window, accessory quality, and battery replacement cost. → *Expect:* refurbished savings justify the reduced certainty versus new.
4. **Inspect listing terms before checkout.** Confirm condition grade, exact SKU, included accessories, warranty provider, return window, restocking fee, and seller identity. → *Expect:* the listing meets your required specs and protection standard.
5. **Pay through a protected checkout.** Avoid wire, gift card, crypto, or off-platform invoice requests. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* payment authorizes at checkout; confirm seller, model, condition, warranty, and final total first. → *Expect:* order confirmation number appears.
6. **Document unboxing and device identity.** Photograph package condition, serial/IMEI where appropriate, included accessories, and any visible damage. → *Expect:* you have evidence from arrival.
7. **Run functional checks immediately.** Test power, screen, keyboard/buttons, cameras, speakers, ports, battery health, network lock, activation lock, storage, and charging. → *Expect:* the device either passes your acceptance checklist or issues are documented.
8. **Register warranty or start return quickly.** If the device passes, save warranty proof; if not, open return/support before the window closes. → *Expect:* warranty is active or return authorization is started.

## Decision points

- Seller warranty is shorter than your risk tolerance → choose another source or lower the price cap.
- Phone/tablet may be carrier locked → verify unlock status and IMEI compatibility before buying.
- Battery health is not disclosed → assume replacement risk and reduce max price.
- Marketplace seller asks for off-platform payment → decline.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Activation or account lock:** detect setup blocked by previous owner or enterprise management → return immediately as unusable.
- **F2 Counterfeit or mismatched device:** detect serial/model differs from listing → document and open a dispute/return.
- **F3 Battery below standard:** detect poor battery health or shutdowns → request replacement, refund, or return under refurb warranty.
- **F4 Hidden liquid/impact damage:** detect corrosion indicators, port issues, or screen defects → stop using and file return with photos.
- **F5 Warranty not honored:** detect provider cannot validate purchase → escalate with receipt and listing terms or dispute if misrepresented.

## Verification

The refurbished device is purchased from an acceptable seller at or below the maximum price, passes the post-delivery functional checklist, and has an active warranty or an open return authorization.

## Variations

- Manufacturer certified: usually costs more but has clearer parts, inspection, and warranty standards.
- Marketplace refurbished: verify whether the platform or individual seller backs the warranty.
- Phones: IMEI, carrier lock, eSIM support, and blacklist status are critical checks.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because devices can be defective, locked, counterfeit, or privacy-invasive. Use protected payment, avoid off-platform deals, inspect immediately, wipe/reset if needed, and do not enter sensitive accounts until the device passes basic trust checks.
