## Goal

Submit a warranty claim with the documents and evidence needed for repair, replacement, refund, or denial review.

## Preconditions

- You have the product, receipt or order proof, serial number, model number, and warranty terms.
- You can describe the defect and when it started.
- You have photos, videos, diagnostic messages, or service records if relevant.

## Steps

1. **Find the warranty terms.** Read coverage period, covered defects, exclusions, required maintenance, shipping duties, and claim channel. → *Expect:* you know whether the issue may be covered.
2. **Confirm purchase proof.** Locate receipt, invoice, order confirmation, gift receipt, card statement, or warranty registration. → *Expect:* purchase date and seller are documented.
3. **Document the defect.** Photograph or record the issue, error codes, serial number label, and product condition. → *Expect:* evidence shows both defect and product identity.
4. **Try required troubleshooting.** Follow only safe manufacturer steps such as reset, cleaning, firmware update, or diagnostic test. → *Expect:* you can report what did and did not work.
5. **Submit the claim.** [BRANCH: online form | phone | retailer | manufacturer] provide model, serial, purchase date, seller, defect, and evidence. → *Expect:* you receive a claim number or support ticket.
6. **Review shipping or service terms.** Check who pays shipping, whether packaging is required, whether a hold is placed on your card, and expected timeline. → *Expect:* costs and responsibilities are clear.
7. **Send the item only when authorized.** Use the provided label, packing instructions, and tracking. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once shipped for warranty service, you may lose possession for weeks, so confirm claim number and tracking first. → *Expect:* tracking or service appointment confirms handoff.
8. **Track the outcome.** Watch for repair estimate, replacement approval, refund, denial, or request for more information. → *Expect:* the claim status is current.
9. **Verify the remedy.** Test repaired or replacement item and save final paperwork. → *Expect:* the defect is fixed or the denial reason is documented.

## Decision points

- The issue is accidental damage → check extended warranty, card benefit, or insurance instead of manufacturer defect warranty.
- The warranty has expired → ask about goodwill repair, recall, parts, or paid service.
- Shipping costs exceed item value → compare claim value against replacement cost.
- Claim is denied for misuse → request the evidence and appeal with photos or maintenance records.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missing receipt:** detect no purchase proof, recover by downloading order history or card statement.
- **F2 Serial number unreadable:** detect worn label, recover by checking box, app, device settings, or registration email.
- **F3 Claim stalled:** detect no update beyond promised timeline, recover by escalating with claim number and tracking.
- **F4 Shipping damage:** detect new damage after service, recover by using packing photos and carrier claim process.
- **F5 Denial unsupported:** detect generic denial, recover by asking for written policy basis and appeal path.

## Verification

You have a claim number and final written outcome showing repair, replacement, refund, or denial, with proof of shipping or service if applicable.

## Variations

- Retailer warranty: the store may handle exchange during a short return or protection-plan window.
- Manufacturer warranty: shipping and diagnostic requirements often apply.
- Credit-card benefit: card issuers may require the original warranty, receipt, and claim denial.

## Safety & privacy

Warranty claims can expose address, phone, serial numbers, photos, and purchase history. Remove personal data from devices before service and keep tracking records.
