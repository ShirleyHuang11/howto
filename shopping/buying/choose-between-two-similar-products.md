## Goal

You choose between two similar products using requirements, total cost, risk, and evidence rather than brand familiarity or marketing copy.

## Preconditions

- You have two candidate products that appear to satisfy the same need.
- You know your must-have features, nice-to-have features, budget, and deadline.
- You can access product specs, reviews, warranty, and return details.

## Steps

1. **Define the job to be done.** Write the specific use case, must-haves, dealbreakers, and maximum total cost. → *Expect:* a decision checklist independent of either product.
2. **Normalize the specs.** Compare model numbers, size, compatibility, capacity, materials, power, warranty, and included accessories in the same format. → *Expect:* differences are visible without relying on marketing language.
3. **Calculate total ownership cost.** Include purchase price, required accessories, consumables, shipping, taxes, subscriptions, maintenance, and expected replacement parts. → *Expect:* both products have an all-in cost estimate.
4. **Evaluate evidence quality.** Prefer professional tests, verified owner reviews, long-term reports, and return data over sponsored blurbs. → *Expect:* known strengths and recurring complaints are listed.
5. **Check support and returns.** Compare warranty length, customer support reputation, restocking fees, and return window. → *Expect:* downside risk is known for each option.
6. **Score against must-haves first.** Eliminate any product that fails a requirement, then choose the better value among remaining options. → *Expect:* one product wins for explicit reasons or neither qualifies.
7. **Buy the chosen product.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* before paying, confirm exact model, seller, total price, and return policy. → *Expect:* the order confirmation matches the chosen product and budget.

## Decision points

- One product fails a must-have → remove it even if cheaper.
- Evidence is weak or reviews conflict → delay purchase or buy the option with easier returns.
- Higher upfront price lowers ownership cost → choose it if cash budget allows.
- Both products are equivalent → choose the lower delivered price from the safer seller.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Spec mismatch:** detect wrong model or region version in cart → remove and select exact product.
- **F2 Review manipulation:** detect repetitive or incentivized reviews → rely on independent tests and negative-review patterns.
- **F3 Accessory surprise:** detect required add-ons after purchase → return unopened if total cost breaks the budget.
- **F4 Warranty gap:** detect seller is unauthorized → cancel or buy from authorized seller.

## Verification

The selected product satisfies all must-have requirements, has the best justified value among the two candidates, and the order confirmation shows the exact chosen model within budget.

## Variations

- `electronics`: include firmware support, ports, battery replacement, and regional warranty.
- `appliances`: include delivery, installation, haul-away, and repair network.
- `apparel`: include fit risk, fabric care, and return shipping cost.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because payment and returns are involved. Avoid sellers with unclear identity, confirm model numbers carefully, and preserve order evidence until the return window closes.
