## Goal

Screen an online listing for scam signs before sending money, personal information, or arranging pickup.

## Preconditions

- You have a listing URL, seller profile, photos, price, and item description.
- You know normal market price for the item or can compare similar listings.
- You can use platform messaging and protected checkout if available.

## Steps

1. **Compare market price.** Check several sold or current listings for the same model, condition, age, and accessories. → *Expect:* the price is plausible or suspiciously low.
2. **Inspect seller history.** Review account age, ratings, completed sales, location consistency, and copied profile text. → *Expect:* seller trust level is clearer.
3. **Check photos and description.** Look for stock photos, mismatched backgrounds, blurry serials, inconsistent specs, or copied wording. → *Expect:* listing originality is assessed.
4. **Ask specific questions.** Request a new photo with date, serial or model label where safe, condition details, pickup area, and included items. → *Expect:* a real seller can answer concretely.
5. **Keep communication on platform.** Refuse moves to text, email, payment apps, or external checkout unless the platform itself requires it. → *Expect:* platform protections remain available.
6. **Review payment method.** [BRANCH: platform checkout | local pickup | shipped direct] prefer protected checkout, in-person inspection with safe payment, or decline unprotected shipping. → *Expect:* refund or dispute options are known.
7. **Check pressure tactics.** Watch for urgency, overpayment, courier pickup, deposit demands, fake escrow, or requests for verification codes. → *Expect:* scam patterns are identified before payment.
8. **Buy only if protections are adequate.** Submit payment only through the agreed protected method after confirming item and seller. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* wire, crypto, gift card, cash app friends payments, and deposits may be unrecoverable. → *Expect:* receipt or meetup plan preserves evidence and protection.

## Decision points

- Seller refuses platform checkout or safe meetup → walk away.
- Price is far below market with urgency → assume high scam risk unless strong proof appears.
- Item is high-value electronics, tickets, pets, rentals, or vehicles → require extra verification and avoid deposits.
- Seller asks for a code sent to your phone → stop; that is often account-takeover fraud.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Fake tracking:** detect tracking not addressed to you or wrong weight, recover by opening a platform claim quickly.
- **F2 Counterfeit item:** detect serial mismatch or authenticity failure, recover with photos and expert or manufacturer proof.
- **F3 Off-platform payment loss:** detect seller disappears after external payment, recover by contacting payment provider and reporting to platform.
- **F4 Account takeover seller:** detect sudden odd messages from a good profile, recover by insisting on platform checkout and fresh proof.
- **F5 Unsafe meetup:** detect remote or changing location, recover by meeting at a public safe-exchange location or canceling.

## Verification

Before payment, the listing has plausible price, consistent seller history, original proof, protected payment or safe pickup terms, and no unresolved scam signals.

## Variations

- Local pickup: meet in daylight at a public location and inspect before paying.
- Tickets or digital goods: use official transfer systems and avoid screenshots as proof.
- Rentals: verify ownership or management company before application fees or deposits.

## Safety & privacy

Do not share verification codes, passwords, full address, or unnecessary ID. Use protected payments and keep all messages, screenshots, and receipts.
