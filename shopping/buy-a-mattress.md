## Goal

Buy a mattress that fits your body, bed frame, sleep preferences, return window, and total budget.

## Preconditions

- You know mattress size, bed-frame support, room access, and budget.
- You can compare return trial, warranty, delivery, setup, and old-mattress removal.
- You know major comfort needs such as firmness, cooling, motion isolation, and pressure relief.

## Steps

1. **Measure bed and room.** Confirm mattress size, frame slat spacing, stairways, doorway clearance, and elevator limits. → *Expect:* the mattress and delivery method fit the home.
2. **Define sleep needs.** Note sleep position, body support needs, partner motion, heat sensitivity, allergies, and edge support. → *Expect:* unsuitable materials and firmness levels are filtered out.
3. **Compare mattress types.** [BRANCH: foam | innerspring | hybrid | latex | adjustable] compare feel, durability, heat, weight, and price. → *Expect:* you know which type best matches the need.
4. **Check real total cost.** Add mattress, foundation, delivery, setup, removal, mattress protector, return fees, financing, and taxes. → *Expect:* the purchase fits the full budget.
5. **Read return trial terms.** Check minimum break-in period, return window, pickup fee, exchange-only rules, stain requirements, and refund method. → *Expect:* you know how to return it if sleep quality is poor.
6. **Read warranty terms.** Check sag depth threshold, required support, stain exclusions, shipping cost, and prorated coverage. → *Expect:* warranty promises are realistic.
7. **Buy from a traceable seller.** Use the official brand, known retailer, or marketplace listing sold by an authorized seller. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* clearance, floor models, and final-sale mattresses may not be returnable, so confirm trial terms before payment. → *Expect:* receipt lists model, size, price, trial, and delivery details.
8. **Inspect on delivery.** Check size, label, damage, odor, and packaging before discarding materials required for return. → *Expect:* any defect is documented immediately.
9. **Test through the required period.** Use a protector and track comfort until the return or exchange decision date. → *Expect:* you can keep, exchange, or return before the window closes.

## Decision points

- You need pressure relief but dislike sinking → compare hybrid or latex instead of soft all-foam.
- Return fees are high → weigh local store exchange options against online trial convenience.
- The bed frame is unsupportive → upgrade support before blaming the mattress.
- Financing includes deferred interest → pay before promo end or choose another payment method.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Too firm or soft:** detect pain after break-in, recover by using the trial exchange or return process.
- **F2 Return denied for stain:** detect policy requiring clean condition, recover by using a protector from day one.
- **F3 Sag warranty denied:** detect unsupported frame or sag below threshold, recover by documenting support and measuring indentation.
- **F4 Fake discount:** detect inflated original price, recover by comparing price history and multiple sellers.
- **F5 Delivery problem:** detect damaged or wrong size mattress, recover by photographing and reporting immediately.

## Verification

The mattress is the correct model and size, fits the frame, has saved receipt and trial deadline, and either sleeps acceptably or is scheduled for return within the window.

## Variations

- In-store: lie on each candidate long enough to test normal sleep positions.
- Online bed-in-a-box: expansion time and off-gassing periods vary.
- Medical needs: ask a clinician about support needs rather than relying only on marketing labels.

## Safety & privacy

Mattresses are heavy and can trigger allergies or respiratory irritation during unpacking. Protect payment information and confirm return rules before buying final-sale items.
