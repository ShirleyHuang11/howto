## Goal

You enroll in the Medicare parts you need on time and keep proof of your choices and effective dates.

## Preconditions

- Your Social Security number, date of birth, citizenship or lawful presence information, and current health coverage details.
- A my Social Security account or ability to apply through SSA by phone or office.
- Your Initial Enrollment Period, Special Enrollment Period, or other enrollment window identified.

## Steps

1. **Confirm your enrollment window.** Most people first qualify at 65; disability, ALS, or ESRD can qualify earlier. → *Expect:* you know the first and last dates you can enroll without risking penalties.
2. **Decide whether you need Part A and Part B now.** If you have active employer group coverage, confirm with HR and Medicare whether delaying Part B is allowed; retiree, COBRA, and marketplace coverage usually do not protect against Part B penalties. → *Expect:* you know whether to enroll now or use a valid delay.
3. **Apply through Social Security.** Use ssa.gov/medicare/sign-up, call SSA, or visit an SSA office. → *Expect:* SSA provides an application confirmation.
4. **Review the Medicare card and effective dates.** When approved, check your Part A and Part B start dates and name/address accuracy. → *Expect:* your Medicare record shows the correct coverage.
5. **Choose how to fill coverage gaps.** [BRANCH: Original Medicare, consider Medigap and Part D | Medicare Advantage, compare Part C plans that include medical coverage and often drugs] → *Expect:* you understand which route you are choosing.
6. **Compare drug coverage.** Enter your prescriptions and pharmacies in Medicare Plan Finder or a trusted counseling program. → *Expect:* plans are ranked by estimated yearly cost and formulary fit.
7. **Enroll in selected supplemental coverage.** Apply for Part D, Medicare Advantage, or Medigap within the applicable window. → *Expect:* each plan gives a confirmation number and effective date.
8. **Cancel or coordinate old coverage only after confirmation.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* ending employer, marketplace, or retiree coverage can create gaps; confirm new Medicare coverage is active first. → *Expect:* no coverage gap is created.

## Decision points

- Still working with employer coverage → ask HR whether the plan is primary and whether Medicare enrollment should be delayed.
- Low income or high drug costs → check Medicare Savings Programs, Extra Help, Medicaid, or State Health Insurance Assistance Program counseling.
- You missed a deadline → contact SSA or Medicare immediately about General Enrollment or Special Enrollment options.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Late enrollment penalty risk:** detect enrollment after the allowed window → ask SSA/Medicare whether a Special Enrollment Period applies and document prior coverage.
- **F2 Wrong effective date:** detect a Medicare card or notice with an unexpected start date → call SSA before canceling any other coverage.
- **F3 Drug not covered:** detect a formulary denial or high pharmacy price → ask the plan about formulary exceptions, alternatives, or switching during an allowed period.
- **F4 Duplicate/conflicting plan:** detect enrollment in both Medicare Advantage and separate incompatible coverage → call Medicare or SHIP to clarify before using services.

## Verification

You have Medicare approval, correct Part A/Part B effective dates, plan confirmation numbers for any Part D/Medicare Advantage/Medigap coverage, and copies of all notices.

## Variations

- `us`: Medicare enrollment is federal, but Medigap rules and Medicaid/Medicare Savings Programs vary by state.
- Railroad retiree: use the Railroad Retirement Board for Medicare enrollment questions.
- Employer coverage: large-employer, small-employer, COBRA, retiree, and marketplace rules differ; verify before delaying Part B.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because missed deadlines can cause penalties and coverage gaps. Use official SSA.gov, Medicare.gov, 1-800-MEDICARE, or SHIP counseling, and never give Medicare numbers to unsolicited callers.
