## Goal

You replace a failed headlight bulb with the correct part and confirm the beam works without damaging the housing or electrical connector.

## Preconditions

- The vehicle is parked, off, cooled down, and secured with the parking brake.
- You have the correct bulb type for the exact year, make, model, trim, and headlight position.
- You have gloves or a clean cloth; halogen bulb glass should not be touched with bare fingers.

## Steps

1. **Confirm which bulb is out.** Turn headlights on briefly and check low beam, high beam, and daytime running light positions. → *Expect:* the failed bulb and side are identified.
2. **Open access safely.** Turn the lights and ignition off, open the hood, and locate the rear of the headlight assembly or access cover. → *Expect:* you can see the connector or service cover for the failed bulb.
3. **Disconnect the electrical connector.** Press the locking tab rather than pulling on wires. → *Expect:* the connector releases without broken tabs or stretched wiring.
4. **Remove the old bulb.** Twist, unclip, or release the retaining spring according to the vehicle design. → *Expect:* the bulb comes out intact and the socket/housing remains seated.
5. **Compare old and new bulbs.** Match base shape, tabs, wattage/spec, and connector style. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* installing the wrong bulb can overheat wiring or misalign the beam; confirm the part before inserting it. → *Expect:* the replacement matches the removed bulb.
6. **Install the new bulb cleanly.** Handle by the base, keep glass clean, seat it fully, and lock the retainer. → *Expect:* the bulb is flush and cannot rotate or wobble loose.
7. **Reconnect and test.** Reattach the connector, turn on the headlights, and verify low/high beam as appropriate. → *Expect:* the new bulb illuminates steadily.
8. **Close covers and check aim impression.** Reinstall dust caps and ensure the beam pattern is not wildly higher or lower than the other side. → *Expect:* the housing is sealed and beam height appears consistent.

## Decision points

- Bulb is HID, LED module, or sealed assembly → check manufacturer instructions; replacement may require a module, ballast, or professional service.
- Access requires bumper or wheel-well removal → decide whether you have the tools and time; do not force plastic clips blindly.
- New bulb does not light → test fuse, connector, ground, and bulb orientation before replacing more parts.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Connector stuck:** detect no movement after pressing the tab → inspect for a secondary lock, use gentle rocking, and avoid pulling wires.
- **F2 Bulb glass touched:** detect fingerprints on halogen glass → clean with alcohol and let dry before installing.
- **F3 Retainer clip pops loose:** detect spring or ring out of place → photograph the other side for reference or consult the service manual.
- **F4 Moisture after replacement:** detect fog or droplets in the housing → reseat dust caps and inspect seals.

## Verification

The correct headlight position illuminates steadily, the connector and dust cover are secured, and the beam appears aligned with the opposite side or manufacturer mark.

## Variations

- Halogen: do not touch the glass; oils shorten bulb life.
- LED retrofit: legality and beam pattern vary; use road-legal parts designed for the housing.
- Some vehicles: replace bulbs in pairs if color and brightness mismatch matters, but keep the working spare if still usable.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from poor night visibility, hot bulbs, and electrical shorts. Confirm the lights are off before disconnecting parts, and do not drive at night with a failed required headlight.
