## Goal

You replace a flat tire with a usable spare or temporary spare so the vehicle can be moved safely to repair.

## Preconditions

- Spare tire, jack, lug wrench, wheel lock key if needed, and owner's manual.
- The vehicle is on firm, level ground away from traffic.
- Hazard lights are on and passengers are out of the vehicle in a safe place.

## Steps

1. **Choose a safe location.** Pull well off the road, avoid soft shoulders and slopes, set hazards, and call roadside assistance if traffic is close. → *Expect:* the vehicle is stable and away from moving traffic.
2. **Secure the vehicle.** Shift to Park or gear, set the parking brake, and chock the wheel diagonally opposite the flat if possible. → *Expect:* the vehicle will not roll.
3. **Find the jack point.** Check the owner's manual or marked pinch weld near the flat tire. → *Expect:* the jack is placed under a manufacturer-approved lift point.
4. **Loosen lug nuts slightly.** Turn counterclockwise before lifting; do not remove them yet. → *Expect:* each lug nut breaks free while the tire is still on the ground.
5. **Raise the vehicle.** Jack until the flat tire is off the ground. ⚠️ *Hazard:* never put any body part under a vehicle supported only by a jack. → *Expect:* the wheel spins freely and the jack remains vertical.
6. **Remove lug nuts and flat tire.** Keep lug nuts together and pull the wheel straight off. → *Expect:* the hub is exposed and hardware is not lost.
7. **Mount the spare.** Align holes, push the spare fully onto the hub, and hand-thread lug nuts. → *Expect:* every lug nut turns by hand without cross-threading.
8. **Snug the lug nuts in a star pattern.** Tighten enough to seat the wheel, but final torque waits until the tire is on the ground. → *Expect:* the spare sits flush against the hub.
9. **Lower the vehicle and final-tighten.** Lower completely, remove the jack, then tighten lug nuts firmly in a star pattern. → *Expect:* all lug nuts are tight and the vehicle rests normally.
10. **Check spare limits.** Read the spare's speed and distance limits, usually around 50 mph/80 km/h for compact spares. → *Expect:* you know the maximum safe route to a repair shop.

## Decision points

- Location is unsafe → stay belted if safer, call roadside assistance, and wait away from traffic when possible.
- Spare is flat or missing → call roadside assistance or tow service.
- Lug nuts will not loosen → use roadside assistance rather than jumping on the wrench near traffic.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Jack shifts or sinks:** lower immediately, reposition on firm ground, and use a stable base if approved.
- **F2 Lug nut cross-threads:** stop, back it off, and hand-thread again; seek service if threads are damaged.
- **F3 Wheel stuck to hub:** with lug nuts loosely installed, lower slightly and rock the tire; call for help if it stays stuck.
- **F4 Vibration after driving:** pull over safely and recheck lug tightness; get professional inspection.

## Verification

The spare tire is mounted flush, all lug nuts are tight in a star pattern, tools and flat tire are secured, and the vehicle is driven only within the spare's stated limits.

## Variations

- `run-flat`: the vehicle may be drivable for a limited distance at limited speed; follow the tire and vehicle manual.
- `wheel-locks`: use the wheel lock key before lifting; keep it with the lug wrench.
- `no-spare`: many vehicles have sealant kits instead; follow the kit instructions or tow.

## Safety & privacy

High risk from traffic and vehicle collapse. Do not work close to moving lanes, do not go under a jacked vehicle, and treat compact spares as temporary emergency equipment.
