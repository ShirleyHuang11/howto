## Goal

You park parallel to the curb in a legal space without hitting other vehicles, the curb hard, cyclists, or pedestrians.

## Preconditions

- A legal space at least several feet longer than your vehicle.
- Turn signal and mirrors work.
- You can stop traffic briefly without creating an unsafe situation.

## Steps

1. **Choose a suitable space.** Check signs, hydrants, driveways, bus stops, and curb markings. → *Expect:* the space is legal and large enough.
2. **Signal and pull alongside the front car.** Stop about 2-3 feet away, with rear bumpers roughly aligned. → *Expect:* other road users see your intent and you are positioned to reverse.
3. **Check all around.** Look for pedestrians, cyclists, scooters, and approaching traffic. → *Expect:* the path behind and beside you is clear.
4. **Reverse slowly and turn toward the curb.** Back up until your rear wheel or bumper begins entering the space. → *Expect:* the rear of your vehicle angles into the spot.
5. **Straighten briefly.** When your front bumper clears the rear bumper of the front car, straighten the wheel while continuing slowly. → *Expect:* the vehicle moves deeper into the space.
6. **Turn away from the curb.** Steer the front end into the space while watching both bumpers. → *Expect:* the vehicle becomes parallel to the curb.
7. **Adjust within the space.** Pull forward or back to center the vehicle and leave room for others. → *Expect:* both nearby cars can exit reasonably.
8. **Settle and secure the car.** Shift to Park or gear, set parking brake on slopes, and curb wheels where required. → *Expect:* the car is legally parked and stable.

## Decision points

- Traffic is too fast or close → skip the space and find a safer one.
- Space is tight but possible → use small corrections and stop before contact.
- You touch the curb lightly → pull forward and correct; avoid climbing the curb.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Approaching cyclist enters the gap:** stop immediately and wait.
- **F2 Angle is wrong:** pull out if safe and restart rather than making many blind corrections.
- **F3 Tire hits curb hard:** inspect tire sidewall and wheel before driving far.
- **F4 Parking sensor alarms constantly:** stop, look directly, and use mirrors/camera as aids, not substitutes.

## Verification

The vehicle is inside the legal parking space, parallel to the curb, not blocking traffic or driveways, and has clearance from surrounding vehicles.

## Variations

- `one-way-street-left-side`: the maneuver mirrors to the left curb where legal.
- `hill-parking`: turn wheels according to local rules and set the parking brake.
- `backup-camera`: use it to judge distance, but still check mirrors and blind spots.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from collisions with pedestrians, cyclists, vehicles, and curbs. Move slowly, signal early, and stop whenever you lose track of a person or obstacle.
