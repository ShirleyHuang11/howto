## Goal

You load cargo onto a roof rack within weight limits and secure it so it cannot shift, fall, or damage the vehicle.

## Preconditions

- The vehicle has installed crossbars or a rack rated for the load.
- You know the vehicle roof limit, rack limit, and cargo weight; the lowest rating controls.
- You have proper straps, tie-downs, padding, and a way to reach the roof safely.

## Steps

1. **Confirm weight and fit.** Add cargo, rack, box, and accessory weights and compare them to the lowest rated limit. ⚠️ *Hazard:* overloading the roof raises rollover risk and can detach cargo. → *Expect:* the planned load is within all limits.
2. **Inspect the rack.** Check towers, clamps, locks, crossbars, and mounting points for looseness or corrosion. → *Expect:* the rack is solid before cargo touches it.
3. **Place cargo centered and balanced.** Put heavier items low and centered between crossbars; avoid blocking doors, hatch, antennae, or sunroof. → *Expect:* weight is evenly distributed front-to-back and side-to-side.
4. **Protect contact points.** Use padding where cargo could rub paint, glass, or sharp rack edges. → *Expect:* cargo rests on stable, protected surfaces.
5. **Strap to the rack structure.** Use rated cam straps or ratchet straps around cargo and crossbars; do not rely on bungee cords as primary restraint. → *Expect:* cargo cannot lift or slide when pushed.
6. **Secure front and rear overhang.** Use bow/stern or front/rear tie-downs for long items such as lumber, kayaks, or ladders. → *Expect:* long cargo cannot yaw, bounce, or spear forward.
7. **Bundle strap tails.** Tie off loose ends so they do not flap, enter doors, or contact wheels. → *Expect:* no loose strap ends can move in the wind.
8. **Do a shake and short-drive check.** Push the load hard, then stop after a few minutes and retighten. → *Expect:* straps stay tight and cargo position is unchanged.

## Decision points

- Load exceeds any rating → use a trailer, delivery, or another vehicle.
- Cargo is wider or longer than legal limits → add required flags/lights or choose another transport method.
- Weather includes high wind or heavy rain → reduce speed, shorten the trip, or delay.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Strap loosens:** detect flapping, humming, or cargo movement → pull over safely and retighten or add straps.
- **F2 Rack shifts:** detect crossbar or tower movement → unload and fix the rack before driving.
- **F3 Door seal leak or strap noise:** detect water or loud vibration → reroute straps outside weather seals when possible and twist flat straps slightly to reduce hum.
- **F4 Cargo damages roof:** detect rubbing or dents → stop, add padding, and redistribute weight.

## Verification

The load is under the lowest weight limit, strapped to structural rack points, does not move during a forceful shake, and remains unchanged after a short retightening stop.

## Variations

- Roof box: obey the box's internal weight limit and lock all latches.
- Kayak/canoe: use cradles or foam blocks plus bow and stern lines.
- Lumber or ladder: tie individual pieces into one bundle and mark overhang as required locally.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because falling roof cargo can injure or kill others. Confirm weight limits, strap condition, and overhang rules before driving, and stop immediately if the load moves or makes new noise.
