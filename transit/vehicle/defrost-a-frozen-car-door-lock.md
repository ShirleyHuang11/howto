## Goal

You thaw a frozen car door lock or latch without breaking the key, damaging seals, or forcing the door.

## Preconditions

- Lock de-icer, isopropyl alcohol spray, or a warm key method is available.
- You have the mechanical key if the fob battery or electronic lock is not working.
- The vehicle is parked safely.

## Steps

1. **Check other doors first.** Try another door or hatch gently. → *Expect:* you may find an unfrozen entry point.
2. **Inspect whether the lock or door seal is frozen.** The key may not insert/turn if the lock is frozen; the handle may move but the door sticks if seals are frozen. → *Expect:* you know which part needs thawing.
3. **Apply lock de-icer to the keyway.** Spray a small amount into the lock and wait. → *Expect:* ice inside the cylinder starts melting.
4. **Warm the key if needed.** Warm the metal key with body heat or warm water, dry it, then insert gently. ⚠️ *Hazard:* do not use an open flame near the vehicle or key fob. → *Expect:* the key inserts farther or turns with light pressure.
5. **Work the key gently.** Turn only with moderate pressure and repeat de-icer if needed. → *Expect:* the cylinder turns without bending the key.
6. **Free frozen door seals carefully.** Press inward around the door edge, brush ice from the seam, and use de-icer around the frame. → *Expect:* the seal releases without tearing.
7. **Start the vehicle and warm the cabin.** Once inside, run heat and defrost. → *Expect:* remaining ice softens from inside.
8. **Dry and lubricate later.** After thawing, use lock lubricant or silicone seal conditioner as appropriate. → *Expect:* the lock and seals are less likely to refreeze.

## Decision points

- Key starts bending → stop immediately and use more de-icer or professional help.
- Electronic fob fails too → replace fob battery or use the hidden mechanical key.
- Door opens but will not latch → warm the latch mechanism and avoid driving until it closes securely.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Key breaks in lock:** do not push fragments deeper; call locksmith or roadside assistance.
- **F2 Door seal tears:** avoid driving in heavy precipitation and replace damaged weatherstripping.
- **F3 Lock refreezes repeatedly:** dry the lock and apply proper lock lubricant after thawing.
- **F4 De-icer unavailable:** use a warm key and hand warmth patiently; avoid hot water on paint and glass.

## Verification

The key turns or electronic lock releases, the door opens without torn seals, and the door latches securely before driving.

## Variations

- `keyless-entry`: many fobs hide a mechanical key and the car may have a concealed lock cylinder under a handle cover.
- `garage-after-thaw`: leave doors open briefly in a dry safe place to vent moisture.
- `preventive`: apply silicone to door seals before freezing rain, keeping it off glass and paint as directed.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but forcing frozen parts can break keys, handles, and seals. Do not use open flames, do not force a bending key, and do not drive with a door that will not latch.
