## Goal

You reverse into a parking space smoothly so the vehicle is centered, clear of lines, and easier to drive out safely.

## Preconditions

- A legal parking space with enough room to maneuver.
- Mirrors, rear window, and camera if equipped are clean.
- You can pause without blocking traffic dangerously.

## Steps

1. **Choose the space and signal.** Slow early and signal toward the space. → *Expect:* nearby drivers understand you are parking.
2. **Pull past the space.** Position the vehicle about one space width away, with your rear bumper slightly past the target space. → *Expect:* you have room to arc backward.
3. **Check all directions.** Look for pedestrians, carts, cyclists, and moving vehicles. → *Expect:* the reversing path is clear.
4. **Shift to reverse and begin slowly.** Keep your foot ready on the brake. → *Expect:* reverse lights and camera or sensors activate if equipped.
5. **Turn toward the space.** Steer gradually while backing, watching the inside rear corner and opposite front corner. → *Expect:* the vehicle enters the space without crossing into obstacles.
6. **Straighten when aligned.** As the vehicle becomes parallel with the lines, straighten the wheel. → *Expect:* both sides have roughly equal clearance.
7. **Back to the proper depth.** Stop before hitting a curb, wall, wheel stop, or pedestrian path. → *Expect:* the vehicle is fully in the space without overhanging unsafely.
8. **Correct if needed.** Pull forward slightly, straighten, and back again. → *Expect:* the final position is centered and within lines.
9. **Secure the vehicle.** Shift to Park or gear and set the parking brake if needed. → *Expect:* the vehicle is stationary and safely parked.

## Decision points

- A person walks behind the car → stop and wait until they are fully clear.
- Space is narrow → fold mirrors if appropriate after parking, not while maneuvering.
- Rear camera view is blocked → clean it or rely on direct sight and mirrors.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Front corner swings toward another car:** stop, pull forward, and use a wider setup.
- **F2 Vehicle ends diagonal:** pull forward until straight, then reverse again.
- **F3 Sensors disagree with visual clearance:** stop and inspect directly before continuing.
- **F4 Traffic pressures you:** keep moving slowly or abandon the space if the situation becomes unsafe.

## Verification

The vehicle is centered between the lines, fully inside the space, clear of obstacles, and positioned to exit forward safely.

## Variations

- `angled-space`: approach from the direction that lets the vehicle follow the angle naturally.
- `garage`: use fixed visual reference points and stop before stored objects or the wall.
- `large-vehicle`: use wider setup distance and mirrors to track rear wheels.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because reversing has blind spots. Move slowly, check directly, and stop immediately if a person or object enters the path.
