## Goal

You adjust speed, visibility, spacing, and route choices so heavy rain does not turn into a loss-of-control or low-visibility crash.

## Preconditions

- Wipers, tires, headlights, brakes, and defroster are functional.
- You are sober, alert, and able to delay the trip if conditions are severe.
- The route does not require crossing known flood-prone roads if alternatives exist.

## Steps

1. **Decide whether the trip can wait.** Check weather alerts and flood warnings before departure. → *Expect:* you know whether driving is reasonable or should be delayed.
2. **Turn on headlights.** Use low beams, not hazard lights while moving unless local law and conditions require them. → *Expect:* your vehicle is visible to others.
3. **Increase following distance.** Leave at least 4-6 seconds behind the vehicle ahead. → *Expect:* you have more time to brake on wet pavement.
4. **Reduce speed smoothly.** Drive below the posted limit when visibility, standing water, or traffic requires it. → *Expect:* steering and braking feel controlled.
5. **Avoid sudden inputs.** Brake, accelerate, and steer gradually. → *Expect:* tires maintain traction.
6. **Use defog and wipers proactively.** Set wipers for steady clearing and use defrost if windows fog. → *Expect:* windshield and mirrors remain usable.
7. **Do not drive through floodwater.** ⚠️ *Hazard:* turn around if water covers the road, depth is unknown, or current is moving. → *Expect:* you avoid water that could stall, float, or sweep the vehicle.
8. **Recover calmly from hydroplaning.** Ease off the accelerator, keep steering straight, and avoid hard braking until traction returns. → *Expect:* the tires reconnect with the road.
9. **Pull over if visibility collapses.** Exit the roadway to a safe lot or shoulder away from travel lanes. → *Expect:* you wait where other drivers are unlikely to hit you.

## Decision points

- Water is flowing across the road → turn around and choose another route.
- Wipers cannot keep up → slow down or stop somewhere safe.
- You feel the car float or steering go light → ease off the accelerator and hold course.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Vehicle stalls in water:** do not restart; exit if safe and call for help because engine damage is possible.
- **F2 Brakes feel weak after puddles:** gently apply brakes to dry them while driving slowly.
- **F3 Windows fog suddenly:** switch to defrost, A/C, fresh air, and high fan.
- **F4 Another driver tailgates:** avoid speeding up; move right or let them pass when safe.

## Verification

You complete the drive or stop safely without crossing floodwater, losing traction, or driving with impaired visibility.

## Variations

- `night`: reduce speed more because glare and standing water are harder to judge.
- `highway`: avoid cruise control in heavy rain and watch for pooled water in ruts.
- `mountain-road`: expect runoff, debris, and rockfall after intense rain.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because flooding, hydroplaning, and low visibility can be fatal. Do not cross unknown water depth, and delay travel when warnings indicate dangerous conditions.
