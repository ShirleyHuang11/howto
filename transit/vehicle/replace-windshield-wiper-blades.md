## Goal

You replace worn windshield wiper blades with blades of the correct length and connector type.

## Preconditions

- Replacement blades matched to the vehicle year, make, model, and wiper positions.
- The vehicle is parked with ignition off.
- A towel or cardboard is available to protect the windshield.

## Steps

1. **Confirm blade sizes and connector type.** Check the parts catalog, package, owner's manual, or existing blades. → *Expect:* each replacement blade matches its position.
2. **Lift one wiper arm.** Place a towel on the windshield beneath it. → *Expect:* the glass is protected if the arm snaps down.
3. **Release the old blade.** Press the tab, slide the blade off the hook, or open the connector cover depending on type. → *Expect:* the old blade separates from the arm.
4. **Keep hold of the bare arm.** Do not let it spring against the glass. → *Expect:* the arm stays controlled while no blade is attached.
5. **Attach the new blade.** Slide or click it into the connector until it locks. → *Expect:* a firm click or locked cover confirms attachment.
6. **Lower the arm gently.** Set the new blade on the windshield. → *Expect:* the rubber sits evenly on the glass.
7. **Repeat for the other blades.** Replace driver, passenger, and rear blades as needed. → *Expect:* all worn blades are replaced.
8. **Test with washer fluid.** Spray the windshield and run the wipers. → *Expect:* the blades clear water smoothly without streaking, chatter, or detaching.

## Decision points

- Blade will not lock → compare adapter pieces and connector orientation before forcing it.
- Wiper arm uses a specialty connector → use the included adapter or buy the correct blade type.
- Wipers still streak after replacement → clean the windshield and blade rubber; inspect for bent arms.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wiper arm hits glass:** inspect for cracks immediately and replace damaged glass if needed.
- **F2 New blade flies loose during test:** stop the wipers, reinstall until locked, and verify the adapter.
- **F3 Wrong blade length:** exchange for the correct size; a blade that hits trim or another blade is unsafe.
- **F4 Chattering persists:** clean glass, remove wax buildup, and inspect arm spring pressure.

## Verification

Each new blade is locked to the arm and clears washer fluid across its sweep without detaching, streaking heavily, or striking trim.

## Variations

- `beam-blades`: many include multiple adapters; use only the adapter specified for the arm type.
- `rear-wiper`: rear blades often use different connectors and shorter sizes.
- `service-position`: some vehicles require a wiper service mode before arms can be lifted.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but bare wiper arms can crack windshields. Keep control of each arm and test blades while parked before relying on them in rain.
