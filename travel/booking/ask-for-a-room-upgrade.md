## Goal

You ask for a better hotel room politely and increase your chance of receiving a useful upgrade without paying unexpected fees.

## Preconditions

- Confirmed hotel reservation and ID/payment card for check-in.
- Loyalty status, special occasion note, or service issue details if relevant.
- A private maximum amount you would pay for a confirmed upgrade, if any.

## Steps

1. **Check availability before arrival.** Look at the hotel's same-night room inventory and price differences. → *Expect:* you know which room categories appear available and what paid upgrades cost.
2. **Join or add loyalty details.** Attach your loyalty number and note any elite status or direct-booking benefits. → *Expect:* the reservation shows your membership.
3. **Choose the right timing.** Ask through chat shortly before arrival or at check-in when staff can see actual inventory. → *Expect:* a staff member can evaluate available rooms.
4. **Make a specific, courteous request.** Ask for a quiet room, higher floor, view, larger room, or early check-in rather than a vague "best room." → *Expect:* the agent understands the upgrade that matters to you.
5. **Disclose your constraints.** If you cannot pay, say you would appreciate any complimentary option; if willing, ask the exact upgrade price before accepting. → *Expect:* the offer is clearly free or priced.
6. **Confirm the final assignment.** Verify room type, bed count, fees, lounge/breakfast access, and checkout time. → *Expect:* key packet or app check-in shows the upgraded room without surprise charges.
7. **Inspect the room promptly.** Check bed type, noise, cleanliness, and promised features before unpacking. → *Expect:* the room matches the accepted upgrade or you can return to the desk quickly.

## Decision points

- Complimentary upgrade unavailable → ask for a quieter room, better view, breakfast credit, or late checkout.
- Paid upgrade exceeds your cap → decline politely and keep the booked room.
- Special occasion is important → request in advance, but do not depend on it for essential needs.
- Assigned room has a defect → report immediately and ask for a comparable fix.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Surprise upgrade fee:** detect a charge at checkout → show the chat or desk note that stated complimentary terms.
- **F2 Worse room despite upgrade label:** detect smaller room, bad noise, or wrong bed → ask to move before using the room heavily.
- **F3 Accessibility need treated as upgrade:** detect required accessible feature unavailable → escalate as an accommodation issue, not a luxury request.
- **F4 Inventory sold out:** detect no available alternatives → ask for waitlist note and check again after standard checkout time.

## Verification

The hotel app, key packet, or folio shows the accepted room assignment and any upgrade charge is either zero or at or below your pre-set cap.

## Variations

- `resort`: view and club upgrades may carry daily fees; confirm inclusions.
- `business-hotel`: Sunday or holiday arrivals may have more empty premium rooms.
- `mobile-check-in`: messaging the hotel before selecting a room can preserve upgrade options.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but payment details are on file. Confirm any paid upgrade amount before accepting and avoid sharing special-occasion personal details beyond what is useful.
