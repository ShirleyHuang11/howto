## Goal

Complete an overnight bus ride with your ticket, sleep, valuables, temperature, bathroom needs, and arrival plan under control.

## Preconditions

- You have a confirmed ticket, departure station, seat assignment if available, arrival address, and offline map.
- Your valuables, documents, medication, charger, water, layers, earplugs, and toilet paper are in a small bag kept at your seat.
- You know whether the bus has toilets, scheduled stops, seat belts, and luggage tags.

## Steps

1. **Verify the operator and platform early.** Match bus company, route number, destination, license plate or staff uniform, and platform before loading luggage. → *Expect:* you board the correct bus, not a similar departure.
2. **Keep valuables on your body or lap.** Put passport, wallet, phone, and medication in a money belt, inside pocket, or small bag looped to you. → *Expect:* theft or luggage access does not expose critical items.
3. **Tag and photograph stored luggage.** Use the bus luggage tag if provided and photograph the bag going into the hold. → *Expect:* you can prove which bag was loaded.
4. **Settle for sleep before departure.** Buckle up if belts exist, layer for cold air-conditioning, set alarms before rest stops and arrival, and dim screens. → *Expect:* your body is ready to rest despite noise and stops.
5. **Use stops strategically.** Bring the valuables bag off the bus, note the bus location, use the restroom, buy water, and return before the stated time. → *Expect:* you do not get left behind or lose the bus in a crowded lot.
6. **Prepare before arrival.** Wake early, check the map, gather trash and chargers, and confirm the arrival terminal or roadside stop. → *Expect:* you exit alert with belongings in hand.
7. **Recover at the destination.** Move to a lit public area, arrange onward transport through official channels, and avoid major decisions while exhausted. → *Expect:* the next leg starts safely despite poor sleep.

## Decision points

- Seat choice available → choose a solo, window, or upper-deck front seat only if safety and motion comfort are acceptable.
- Border crossing overnight → keep documents reachable and expect wake-ups for immigration checks.
- Arrival before dawn → pre-book lodging, station storage, or a cafe plan rather than wandering with bags.
- Motion sickness history → take preventive medication before boarding according to label instructions.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong bus:** destination or operator does not match the ticket → do not load luggage and ask station staff for the correct platform.
- **F2 Rest-stop confusion:** you cannot find the bus after a stop → show the ticket and bus photo to staff immediately before departures resume.
- **F3 Valuables tampered with:** bag position or zipper changes while you slept → check essentials, alert staff, and document missing items at the next safe stop.
- **F4 Unsafe arrival:** the drop-off area is dark or chaotic → stay near staff or other passengers and use official taxi, rideshare, or station transport.

## Verification

You arrive at the correct destination with documents, valuables, luggage, and onward transport plan intact, and no rest stop or arrival creates an avoidable safety gap.

## Variations

- Sleeper buses: check whether berths are shared, whether shoes are removed, and how luggage is secured.
- Premium coaches: Wi-Fi and toilets help comfort but do not replace offline maps and water.
- Rural routes: stops may be informal, so bus photos and alarms matter more.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from theft, fatigue, road safety, and late-night arrivals. Keep critical items attached to you, avoid sedating yourself deeply, and do not share your destination hotel with strangers.
