## Goal

Pack a compact day bag for transit or sightseeing with essentials accessible and valuables protected.

## Preconditions

- You know the day's weather, activities, transit mode, and expected time away from lodging.
- You have a comfortable bag that closes securely.
- You have required documents, medication, phone, charger, water, and payment method.

## Steps

1. **Choose the bag.** Pick a small backpack, crossbody, or tote that zips and fits under a seat or on your body. → *Expect:* the bag is comfortable and not overlarge.
2. **Pack documents and money securely.** Put passport, ID, transit card, one payment card, and small cash in an inner pocket or pouch. → *Expect:* essentials are reachable but not exposed.
3. **Add health and comfort basics.** Pack medications, hand sanitizer, tissues, sunscreen, lip balm, and any child or period supplies needed for the day. → *Expect:* predictable personal needs are covered.
4. **Add power and navigation.** Bring phone, charging cable, power bank, offline map, and address of lodging. → *Expect:* you can navigate even with weak service.
5. **Pack weather layers.** [BRANCH: heat | rain | cold] add water and hat for heat, shell or umbrella for rain, or warm layer for cold. → *Expect:* the bag matches the forecast.
6. **Include food and water.** Add a refillable bottle and sturdy snack, especially for flights, trains, hikes, or children. → *Expect:* delays will not immediately cause hunger or dehydration.
7. **Reduce theft and bulk.** Remove full wallets, extra keys, jewelry, and duplicate electronics; keep the bag closed and balanced. → *Expect:* the bag carries only what you need today.
8. **Do a final access check.** Place high-use items near the top and emergency items in a consistent pocket. → *Expect:* you can find tickets, phone, medication, and water without unpacking everything.

## Decision points

- Museums or venues restrict bag size → use a smaller crossbody or check storage rules.
- Traveling with kids → add snacks, wipes, spare clothes, comfort item, and a lightweight activity.
- Pickpocketing risk is high → use inner pockets and keep the bag in front in crowds.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bag too heavy:** detect shoulder strain before leaving → remove duplicates, guidebooks, extra bottles, and nonessential gear.
- **F2 Medication inaccessible:** detect medication buried or checked elsewhere → move it to a top inner pocket.
- **F3 Phone dies:** detect low battery early → switch to low power mode and charge during meals or transit.
- **F4 Valuables exposed:** detect open outer pockets or visible passport → move items inside and close the bag.

## Verification

Before leaving lodging, the day bag contains documents, money, phone power, medication, weather protection, water, and a snack, with valuables minimized and secured.

## Variations

- `flight-day`: add liquids bag, headphones, documents, and one spare layer.
- `city-sightseeing`: prioritize anti-theft carry, water, sunscreen, and transit card.
- `outdoor-day`: add first aid, map, headlamp, and extra water.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, mostly theft, lost documents, dehydration, and inaccessible medication. Keep identity documents private, carry medications with you, and avoid exposing addresses or travel plans unnecessarily.
