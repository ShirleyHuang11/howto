## Goal

You gate-check a stroller, car seat, or carry-on item so it is accepted at the gate and returned where you expect it.

## Preconditions

- Boarding pass and the item to be gate-checked.
- Airline rules for stroller, car seat, mobility item, or carry-on gate checking.
- Protective bag or cover if available.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the item is eligible.** Check airline rules for dimensions, weight, and whether the item is free to gate-check. → *Expect:* the item is allowed at the gate or you know it must be checked earlier.
2. **Remove loose and valuable items.** Take out toys, cups, electronics, passports, medication, wallets, and detachable accessories. → *Expect:* only the checkable item itself remains.
3. **Collapse or prepare the item.** Fold the stroller, attach straps, protect wheels, and bag the item if using a cover. → *Expect:* the item can be handled without loose parts dangling.
4. **Request a gate-check tag early.** Ask the gate agent before boarding starts, not at the aircraft door if avoidable. → *Expect:* the agent prints or writes a tag for the item.
5. **Verify destination of return.** Ask whether the item will be returned at the aircraft door, jet bridge, baggage claim, or final destination. → *Expect:* you know exactly where to retrieve it.
6. **Attach the tag securely.** Put the tag where staff can see it and keep the receipt stub if provided. → *Expect:* the item has visible routing and claim information.
7. **Leave the item at the instructed spot.** Place it at the end of the jet bridge or hand it to ramp staff only when instructed. → *Expect:* staff accept it before you board.
8. **Wait at the correct pickup point.** After landing, wait in the jet bridge or go to baggage claim according to the tag instructions. → *Expect:* the item is returned before you leave that area.

## Decision points

- Tight connection → confirm whether pickup at the aircraft door will delay you or whether through-checking is safer.
- Item is expensive or fragile → use a padded cover and photograph condition before handoff.
- Stroller needed during connection → insist the tag shows return at the connection point if the airline allows it.
- Item contains batteries → ask whether batteries must be removed or carried onboard.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Item sent to baggage claim unexpectedly:** detect no return at jet bridge → ask gate staff to check the tag and proceed to the baggage office or carousel.
- **F2 Tag lost or torn:** detect missing tag before handoff → return to the gate agent for a new tag.
- **F3 Item damaged:** detect damage on return → photograph it immediately and report to the airline before leaving the airport.
- **F4 Agent refuses gate check:** detect item exceeds rules → check it at the counter if time allows or repack into compliant carry-on.

## Verification

The item has a gate-check tag with the correct destination, you know the pickup point, and you keep any claim stub until the item is returned.

## Variations

- `stroller`: many airlines allow strollers to be checked free, but size and return location vary.
- `car-seat`: a car seat may be used onboard only if approved and assigned to a paid seat; otherwise it may be gate-checked.
- `regional-jet`: roller bags may be valet-checked at the aircraft and returned at the jet bridge.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but damage and delay are common. Remove valuables and essentials before handoff, photograph expensive items, and do not leave identity documents or medication in a gate-checked bag.
