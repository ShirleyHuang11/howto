## Goal

Book a last-minute trip that is legal, affordable enough, logistically coherent, and not undone by missing documents or impossible timing.

## Preconditions

- You know the traveler names, dates, destination flexibility, budget ceiling, passport or ID validity, and reason for travel.
- You can book flights, lodging, and ground transport quickly with a payment method.
- You have realistic packing capacity and can leave for the airport or station on short notice.

## Steps

1. **Check document feasibility first.** Verify passport validity, visa needs, vaccination or entry forms, and ID rules before searching fares deeply. → *Expect:* only legal destinations remain on the list.
2. **Set hard constraints.** Define latest departure, latest arrival, maximum price, minimum hotel standard, and cancellation tolerance. → *Expect:* search choices have clear stop rules.
3. **Search bundled logistics.** Price flight or train, lodging, airport transfers, arrival time, and first meal together rather than as separate bargains. → *Expect:* the total trip cost and first-night plan are visible.
4. **Prefer direct and refundable where possible.** Book direct with airlines, hotels, and major operators when disruption help matters. → *Expect:* changes can be handled by the provider responsible for the service.
5. **Confirm location details before paying.** Check hotel address, late check-in, airport distance, local transit end times, and neighborhood safety at arrival hour. → *Expect:* the arrival path works after delays or after dark.
6. **Pay after one final identity review.** Match names, dates, airports, baggage, and room dates, then complete the purchases. → *Expect:* confirmations arrive for transport and lodging with correct details.
7. **Build the departure checklist.** Add check-in deadline, packing minimums, chargers, medication, documents, weather layer, and transport to the airport or station. → *Expect:* the trip can start without another planning session.

## Decision points

- International paperwork uncertain → choose a domestic or no-visa destination rather than gambling at the border.
- Cheapest flight arrives after midnight → price taxis, hotel late check-in, and safety before accepting it.
- Hotel inventory is poor → change destination or dates before accepting unsafe or badly located lodging.
- Weather event or strike is active → buy flexible fares or postpone because last-minute disruption options sell out fast.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Visa impossible:** destination requires approval you cannot get in time → pivot to a destination with legal entry for your passport today.
- **F2 Hidden cost spike:** bags, transfers, and lodging exceed the cap → abandon the itinerary before payment and search a simpler route.
- **F3 Confirmation delay:** payment posts but email is missing → retrieve booking in the provider portal before buying a duplicate.
- **F4 Late arrival problem:** hotel or transport cannot receive you → call before departure and switch to a 24-hour front desk or airport hotel.

## Verification

You have valid entry documents, confirmed transport and lodging, a feasible arrival plan, correct traveler details, and a packed essentials list before leaving.

## Variations

- Emergency family travel: prioritize direct routing and changeability over savings.
- Weekend escape: destination flexibility can save more than fare hunting.
- Award travel: last-minute availability may be strong, but taxes, positioning flights, and cancellation rules still matter.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because rushed purchases invite identity errors, unsafe lodging, and nonrefundable costs. Use official providers, verify documents before payment, and avoid announcing an empty home publicly.
