## Goal

Enroll in a trusted-traveler program that fits your travel pattern and receive a Known Traveler Number or equivalent approval you can use on future bookings.

## Preconditions

- You have a valid passport or government ID, your legal name history, five years of address and employment history, and a payment card.
- You can attend an in-person interview or document check at an enrollment center, airport, or approved partner location.
- You understand that approval is discretionary and can be denied for criminal, customs, immigration, or incomplete-application issues.

## Steps

1. **Choose the right program.** Pick TSA PreCheck for mostly US domestic security, Global Entry for international arrivals plus TSA PreCheck eligibility, or a local trusted-traveler equivalent if you are not US-based. → *Expect:* one program matches your routes, passport, and willingness to attend an interview.
2. **Create the official account.** Use the government program's official site, not an ad or third-party form, and save the login in your password manager. → *Expect:* you are inside the official application portal with your own account.
3. **Enter identity and history carefully.** Copy names, birth date, passport or ID numbers, address history, employment, and travel history from documents instead of memory. → *Expect:* the draft application matches your documents and has no unexplained gaps.
4. **Review disclosures and pay.** Confirm the fee, refund policy, eligibility statements, and consent to background checks before submitting. → *Expect:* the portal shows a paid submitted application or conditional approval workflow.
5. **Schedule the appointment.** Search several enrollment centers, airports, and date ranges; for Global Entry, consider interview-on-arrival after an international trip if appointments are scarce. → *Expect:* an appointment or interview-on-arrival plan is recorded with location, date, and required documents.
6. **Attend with original documents.** Bring the same passport or ID used in the application, proof of address if required, and any residency or visa documents that apply. → *Expect:* the officer verifies your documents, photo, and fingerprints where required.
7. **Save and use the number.** After approval, copy the Known Traveler Number or PASSID into airline profiles and future reservations exactly as shown. → *Expect:* boarding passes on eligible itineraries display the trusted-traveler indicator after the number is attached.

## Decision points

- Mostly domestic flights → TSA PreCheck is usually simpler and cheaper than Global Entry.
- Frequent international arrivals → Global Entry usually pays back through faster immigration kiosks and includes TSA PreCheck eligibility.
- No appointment nearby → check early mornings, cancellation openings, nearby airports, and interview-on-arrival before paying for travel to a distant center.
- Name differs across documents → correct the document mismatch before applying because trusted-traveler vetting depends on exact identity matching.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Appointment unavailable:** no local slots appear for weeks → check adjacent cities and cancellation windows, or use airport interview-on-arrival if the program allows it.
- **F2 Name mismatch:** the portal or officer flags inconsistent names → update the application or bring legal name-change proof before the interview proceeds.
- **F3 Conditional approval stalls:** the application stays pending beyond the stated range → use the portal's status tools and official help channels, not a third-party expeditor.
- **F4 Number missing on boarding pass:** the airline profile has the number but the pass lacks the indicator → verify name, birth date, and number in the reservation, then ask the airline to reissue the boarding pass.

## Verification

The program portal shows approved status, you have saved the assigned traveler number, and at least one eligible airline reservation accepts the number without a name or birth-date mismatch.

## Variations

- `us`: TSA PreCheck uses enrollment providers; Global Entry uses the Trusted Traveler Programs portal and customs interviews.
- `canada`: NEXUS can be better for US-Canada travel but has its own interview and eligibility path.
- Family travel: every traveler, including children in many programs, needs separate eligibility or a program-specific family rule.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because the application exposes passport data, addresses, employment history, fingerprints, and payment details. Use only official sites, avoid public Wi-Fi for the application, and verify the fee before submitting.
