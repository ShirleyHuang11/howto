## Goal

Store luggage for a day between checkout, arrival, activities, and onward travel without losing access, valuables, or proof of custody.

## Preconditions

- You know bag count, bag size, storage duration, pickup deadline, and the area where you will need the bags again.
- Valuables, passports, medication, electronics, and fragile items can stay with you.
- You can identify staffed storage, station lockers, hotel storage, or reputable app-based partners.

## Steps

1. **Choose storage near the next movement.** Prioritize your departure station, hotel, airport rail stop, or final neighborhood over the cheapest listing across town. → *Expect:* pickup will not require an extra stressful transfer.
2. **Check hours and size rules.** Confirm opening hours, lunch closures, maximum dimensions, prohibited items, and same-day pickup deadline. → *Expect:* the bags can be accepted and recovered before your next departure.
3. **Remove critical items.** Take passport, wallet, phone, laptop, medication, chargers, keys, and fragile items before handoff. → *Expect:* the stored bag contains only replaceable travel items.
4. **Document the handoff.** Photograph the bag, tag, locker number, receipt, QR code, and storefront or station area. → *Expect:* you can prove which bag was stored and where.
5. **Set a pickup alarm.** Add travel time back to the storage point plus a buffer before closing or departure. → *Expect:* your day plan contains a protected luggage-retrieval window.
6. **Retrieve and inspect.** Present the claim check, open the bag enough to verify contents and damage, then leave the storage area. → *Expect:* bags return in the same condition before the next leg.

## Decision points

- Hotel will store bags after checkout → use it if pickup location still fits your onward route.
- Station lockers are full → switch to staffed left-luggage or a reputable storage network rather than leaving bags unattended.
- Flight departs late → airport storage can simplify security timing but may cost more.
- You need access during the day → choose staffed storage because lockers may not allow repeated opening without repaying.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Storage closed:** posted hours or holiday closure blocks pickup → call the provider, station staff, or app support and rebook transport only after escalation.
- **F2 Claim ticket lost:** receipt or QR code is missing → use photos, payment record, ID, and bag description to prove custody.
- **F3 Bag rejected:** size or contents violate rules → remove prohibited items or find oversized/staffed storage.
- **F4 Damage or missing items:** bag condition changed → photograph immediately and file a claim before leaving the premises.

## Verification

You have a storage receipt or locker proof, pickup alarm, critical items remain with you, and the bag is retrieved and inspected before onward travel.

## Variations

- Train stations: lockers may require coins, transit cards, or QR codes and can fill by midday.
- Museums and attractions: cloakrooms often reject suitcases even when they accept backpacks.
- App-based storage: location quality varies, so reviews and exact hours matter.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because stored luggage can expose belongings and travel plans. Keep documents and electronics with you, avoid storing illegal or high-value items, and do not post claim tags publicly.
