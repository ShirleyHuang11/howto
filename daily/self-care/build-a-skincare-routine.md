## Goal

Create a simple skincare routine that cleanses, moisturizes, and protects skin without overloading it with products.

## Preconditions

- Gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and broad-spectrum sunscreen for daytime.
- Patch test area available, such as behind the ear or inner arm.
- No severe rash, infection signs, burns, or rapidly changing lesions; seek medical care for those.

## Steps

1. **Identify skin needs.** Note whether skin usually feels oily, dry, sensitive, acne-prone, or combination. → *Expect:* product choices match observed skin behavior.
2. **Cleanse gently.** Wash with lukewarm water and a mild cleanser, then pat dry. → *Expect:* skin feels clean but not tight or burning.
3. **Moisturize.** Apply a simple fragrance-free moisturizer while skin is slightly damp. → *Expect:* skin feels comfortable after a few minutes.
4. **Use sunscreen in the morning.** Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen to exposed skin when going outdoors. → *Expect:* face and exposed areas have even coverage.
5. **Add only one active at a time.** If using acne, retinoid, exfoliating, or pigment products, introduce one product slowly. → *Expect:* any reaction can be linked to a single new product.
6. **Patch test new products.** Apply a small amount for several days before using broadly. → *Expect:* no redness, itching, swelling, or burning appears at the test site.
7. **Review after four weeks.** Judge dryness, irritation, acne, and comfort after consistent use. → *Expect:* the routine is either helping or clearly needs adjustment.

## Decision points

- Skin burns or peels -> stop new active products and return to cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen.
- Acne is painful, scarring, or persistent -> see a dermatologist or clinician.
- Pregnancy or trying to conceive -> ask a clinician before retinoids or strong actives.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Too many new products:** detect by irritation with no clear cause -> recover by stopping actives and reintroducing one at a time.
- **F2 Over-cleansing:** detect by tight, shiny, or stinging skin after washing -> recover by using a gentler cleanser and washing less often.
- **F3 Skipping sunscreen:** detect by daytime routine ending before sun protection -> recover by placing sunscreen beside the morning moisturizer.

## Verification

For two weeks, cleanser and moisturizer are used consistently, sunscreen is used on outdoor days, and no new product causes ongoing burning, swelling, or severe irritation.

## Variations

- Very dry skin: use creamier moisturizer and avoid foaming cleansers.
- Oily skin: choose lightweight non-comedogenic products without stripping the skin.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when products are introduced slowly. Consult a professional for severe acne, infection, allergic reactions, changing moles, burns, or persistent rashes. Skin photos and app logs can expose health information.
