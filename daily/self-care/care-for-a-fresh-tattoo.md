## Goal

A fresh tattoo is cleaned, moisturized lightly, protected from contamination, and monitored during early healing.

## Preconditions

- Tattoo was applied by a professional or with sterile equipment.
- Artist aftercare instructions, mild fragrance-free soap, clean paper towels, and fragrance-free moisturizer are available.
- No fever, spreading redness, severe swelling, or allergic reaction is present.

## Steps

1. **Follow wrap timing.** Leave the original bandage or film on for the artist's instructed time. → *Expect:* the tattoo stays protected during the initial weeping period.
2. **Wash hands first.** Clean hands with soap and water before touching the tattoo. → *Expect:* hands are clean and dry.
3. **Remove covering gently.** Peel film or bandage slowly; wet stuck gauze if needed. → *Expect:* covering comes away without tearing skin.
4. **Wash the tattoo.** Use lukewarm water and mild soap with fingertips, not a washcloth. → *Expect:* plasma, ink residue, and ointment rinse away.
5. **Pat dry.** Blot with a clean paper towel and let air dry for several minutes. → *Expect:* tattoo is dry without lint stuck to it.
6. **Apply a thin moisturizer layer.** Spread a small amount of fragrance-free lotion or artist-recommended ointment. → *Expect:* skin looks lightly hydrated, not shiny or greasy.
7. **Protect from friction.** Wear loose clean clothing and avoid scratching, picking, swimming, and soaking. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* picking scabs can remove ink and scar skin; leave flakes attached. → *Expect:* tattoo is not rubbed or submerged.
8. **Repeat routine.** Clean and moisturize as directed, usually 1-2 times daily at first. → *Expect:* peeling and mild tenderness gradually decrease.

## Decision points

- Second-skin film leaks or fills with fluid → remove or replace only as the artist instructed.
- Redness spreads, heat increases, pus appears, or fever develops → seek medical care.
- Rash or intense itching occurs around adhesive → contact the artist or clinician about adhesive reaction.
- Tattoo is on hands, feet, or joints → expect more friction and protect it carefully.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Over-moisturized tattoo:** surface is sticky or pimply → wash gently, dry, and use less product.
- **F2 Scab lifted:** inked skin bleeds or opens → clean gently, stop picking, and monitor for infection.
- **F3 Clothing sticks:** dried fluid glued fabric down → wet the fabric with clean water before removing.
- **F4 Infection signs:** spreading redness, heat, swelling, pus, fever, or worsening pain → seek medical care.

## Verification

The tattoo is clean, lightly moisturized, not submerged or picked, and shows no spreading redness, heat, pus, fever, or worsening pain.

## Variations

- Adhesive film aftercare: follow the artist's exact wear time and replacement rules.
- Large tattoo: wash in sections with clean hands so residue is fully removed.
- Workplace exposure: cover only with clean breathable clothing unless the artist instructs otherwise.

## Safety & privacy

Medium health risk. Fresh tattoos are open wounds; avoid pools, hot tubs, sun exposure, shared towels, dirty hands, and unverified ointments.
