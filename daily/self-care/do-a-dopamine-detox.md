## Goal

Run a one-day reset from compulsive high-stimulation habits and replace them with lower-friction offline activities.

## Preconditions

- A normal day without urgent digital obligations, or clear exceptions for work, school, caregiving, and safety.
- List of target behaviors, such as social media, short videos, gaming, shopping, porn, or constant news.
- Offline alternatives prepared, such as walking, chores, reading, cooking, journaling, or meeting someone.

## Steps

1. **Define the scope.** Choose which apps, sites, or behaviors are paused and which essentials remain allowed. → *Expect:* the rules fit on one short list.
2. **Remove easy access.** Log out, move apps off the home screen, block sites, or put devices in another room. → *Expect:* the target habit requires extra effort to start.
3. **Plan replacement blocks.** Schedule meals, movement, chores, quiet work, and rest. → *Expect:* empty time has a default activity.
4. **Start with one day.** Follow the rules from waking until bedtime, allowing only defined essentials. → *Expect:* urges rise and pass without automatic checking.
5. **Use urge surfing.** When an urge hits, wait 10 minutes and do a prepared offline action. → *Expect:* the urge changes intensity without being obeyed immediately.
6. **Review the pattern.** At the end, write which triggers were strongest and which replacements worked. → *Expect:* tomorrow's boundaries are based on evidence.

## Decision points

- The behavior involves gambling, substances, self-harm, or severe compulsion -> seek professional support rather than relying on a reset day.
- Total abstinence disrupts work or caregiving -> allow defined utility use and block only the compulsive loop.
- The day improves focus -> convert one boundary into a daily rule.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Vague rules:** detect by debating whether an app counts -> recover by writing allowed and paused categories before starting.
- **F2 Empty-time rebound:** detect by boredom leading straight back to scrolling -> recover by starting the next scheduled offline block.
- **F3 Shame spiral:** detect by treating one slip as proof of failure -> recover by logging the trigger and continuing the day.

## Verification

By bedtime, the chosen high-stimulation behaviors stayed paused except for declared essentials, urges were handled with at least one delay, and a written review identifies triggers and next boundaries.

## Variations

- Half-day reset: use noon to bedtime when a full day is unrealistic.
- Social version: tell a friend the rules and meet offline during the hardest block.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when framed as behavior design, not medical detox. For addiction, severe anxiety, depression, or unsafe urges, consult a qualified professional. App blockers and logs may reveal browsing habits.
