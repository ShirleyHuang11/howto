## Goal

Reduce screen-related eye fatigue by adjusting distance, lighting, breaks, and dryness triggers.

## Preconditions

- A screen used for work, school, or leisure.
- Ability to adjust brightness, text size, seating, or lighting.
- Eye pain, sudden vision changes, new flashes, or injury are absent; seek eye care urgently if present.

## Steps

1. **Set screen distance.** Place the screen about an arm's length away with the top near or slightly below eye level. → *Expect:* eyes look slightly downward without neck strain.
2. **Match brightness to the room.** Adjust screen brightness so it is not a glowing rectangle or too dim to read. → *Expect:* text is clear without squinting.
3. **Increase readable size.** Raise zoom or font size until normal text is easy at the chosen distance. → *Expect:* you can read without leaning forward.
4. **Control glare.** Turn the screen away from windows or bright lamps and use indirect lighting. → *Expect:* reflections no longer cover the working area.
5. **Use break cycles.** Every 20 minutes, look at something far away for about 20 seconds. → *Expect:* eye focusing relaxes before fatigue builds.
6. **Blink and handle dryness.** Blink fully during breaks and consider preservative-free artificial tears if dryness is common. → *Expect:* burning or gritty sensation decreases.
7. **Recheck after one work block.** After 45 to 60 minutes, note strain, posture, and readability. → *Expect:* symptoms are milder than before the setup change.

## Decision points

- You wear glasses or contacts -> confirm the prescription is current, especially for computer distance.
- Dryness persists -> ask an eye-care professional about dry-eye causes and treatment.
- Headaches or vision changes continue -> schedule an eye exam rather than only adjusting screens.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Breaks forgotten:** detect by working more than an hour without looking away -> recover by setting a repeating timer.
- **F2 Glare remains:** detect by seeing windows or lamps reflected on the screen -> recover by repositioning the screen or light source.
- **F3 Symptoms suggest more than strain:** detect by pain, double vision, sudden changes, or flashes -> recover by seeking professional eye care promptly.

## Verification

After a normal screen session, text remains readable at arm's length, glare is controlled, breaks occurred at least twice per hour, and eye discomfort is reduced or absent.

## Variations

- Multiple monitors: center the primary screen and keep secondary screens at similar height and distance.
- Night work: dim room lights gently but avoid using a bright screen in a dark room.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Screen adjustments are not treatment for eye disease; sudden or severe symptoms need professional care. No personal data is required unless using tracking software for breaks.
