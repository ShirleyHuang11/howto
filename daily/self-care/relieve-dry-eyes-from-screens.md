## Goal

Screen-related dry eye discomfort is reduced by blinking, resting focus, improving airflow, and using lubricating drops when appropriate.

## Preconditions

- Symptoms are mild dryness, grittiness, or tired eyes during screen use.
- Preservative-free artificial tears are available if drops are needed.
- No eye injury, chemical exposure, severe pain, sudden vision loss, or new light sensitivity is present.

## Steps

1. **Pause and assess symptoms.** Look away from the screen and note pain, redness, vision change, or discharge. → *Expect:* symptoms fit mild dryness rather than urgent eye trouble.
2. **Blink deliberately.** Close eyes fully for 2 seconds, squeeze gently for 2 seconds, then open; repeat 5 times. → *Expect:* the eye surface feels more coated.
3. **Use distance breaks.** Every 20 minutes, look at something far away for about 20 seconds. → *Expect:* focusing muscles relax and blinking increases.
4. **Lower the screen slightly.** Position the screen so your gaze is slightly downward. → *Expect:* eyelids cover more of the eye surface.
5. **Reduce drying airflow.** Move fans, vents, or heaters away from your face. → *Expect:* eyes feel less exposed to moving air.
6. **Use artificial tears if needed.** Put in preservative-free lubricating drops according to the label. → *Expect:* grittiness eases without stinging beyond a few seconds.
7. **Clean contact lenses if worn.** Follow lens care instructions or switch to glasses for the session. → *Expect:* lens-related dryness decreases.

## Decision points

- Symptoms include severe pain, vision loss, injury, or chemical exposure → seek urgent medical care.
- Redness, discharge, or light sensitivity is present → stop self-treatment and contact an eye professional.
- Drops are needed many times daily → ask an eye clinician about dry eye management.
- Contact lenses worsen symptoms → remove lenses and use glasses until comfortable.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Drops sting strongly:** product may not suit your eyes → rinse with lubricating drops and try preservative-free tears or seek advice.
- **F2 Dryness returns quickly:** environment is still drying → adjust airflow, humidity, screen height, and break frequency.
- **F3 Vision blurs after drops:** excess tears are on the surface → blink gently and wait a few minutes.
- **F4 Symptoms are one-sided and painful:** dryness may not be the cause → contact a clinician.

## Verification

After a 10-minute screen break routine, eyes feel less gritty, blinking is comfortable, and no urgent symptoms such as severe pain or vision loss are present.

## Variations

- Contact lens wearer: use drops labeled compatible with contacts or remove lenses first.
- Dry room: add humidity or move away from vents.
- Long work session: schedule recurring breaks rather than waiting for discomfort.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk for mild dryness. Eye pain, sudden vision changes, chemical exposure, trauma, or light sensitivity needs urgent professional evaluation.
