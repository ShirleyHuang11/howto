## Goal

Shift the body clock toward a stable sleep-wake schedule using consistent wake time, light timing, meals, and evening dimming.

## Preconditions

- A target wake time and bedtime that allow adequate sleep.
- Access to morning outdoor light or a bright light source.
- No untreated sleep apnea signs, severe insomnia, mania symptoms, or safety-critical drowsiness; consult a clinician for those.

## Steps

1. **Set the wake anchor.** Choose one wake time and keep it within 30 minutes daily. → *Expect:* mornings have a fixed reference point.
2. **Get morning light.** Seek outdoor light for 10 to 30 minutes within an hour of waking. → *Expect:* alertness rises earlier in the day.
3. **Move meals earlier.** Eat breakfast or a first meal after waking and avoid heavy meals close to bedtime. → *Expect:* digestion cues align with the new day.
4. **Use daytime activity.** Add walking or exercise earlier in the day when possible. → *Expect:* sleep pressure builds naturally by evening.
5. **Cut late caffeine.** Stop caffeine at least 8 hours before target bedtime. → *Expect:* stimulant effects are less likely at lights-out.
6. **Dim the evening.** Lower bright lights and reduce stimulating screens for the last 1 to 2 hours. → *Expect:* the environment signals night.
7. **Shift gradually.** Move wake time by 15 to 30 minutes every few days if the gap is large. → *Expect:* the change is tolerable enough to maintain.
8. **Hold for two weeks.** Keep the same anchor through weekends and one bad night. → *Expect:* sleepiness begins arriving near the target bedtime.

## Decision points

- Need to shift earlier -> morning light and fixed wake time matter most.
- Need to shift later -> evening light exposure may help, but protect total sleep and safety.
- Persistent insomnia, loud gasping snoring, or dangerous sleepiness -> seek sleep-medicine advice.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Weekend drift:** detect by wake times more than 60 minutes later on days off -> recover by returning to the anchor the next morning.
- **F2 Bed too early:** detect by lying awake more than 20 to 30 minutes -> recover by getting up briefly in dim light and trying again when sleepy.
- **F3 Bright nights:** detect by late screens and overhead lights -> recover by setting a dimming alarm and charging devices away from bed.

## Verification

For seven consecutive days, wake time stays within 30 minutes of target, morning light happens within an hour, caffeine cutoff is met, and sleepiness appears closer to the planned bedtime.

## Variations

- Shift work: protect a consistent anchor around workdays and use blackout curtains for daytime sleep.
- Jet lag: use destination morning light and meals after arrival.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but drowsy driving and untreated sleep disorders are serious. Consult a clinician for severe insomnia, apnea signs, mood elevation with reduced sleep, or persistent impairment. Sleep trackers may collect sensitive health data.
