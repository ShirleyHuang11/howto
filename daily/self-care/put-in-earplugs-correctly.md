## Goal

Foam or reusable earplugs are inserted cleanly and deeply enough to reduce sound while staying comfortable.

## Preconditions

- Clean hands and clean earplugs are available.
- Earplugs are the correct type for sleep, noise, swimming, or hearing protection.
- No ear pain, drainage, suspected infection, or recent ear surgery is present.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the earplugs.** Check that foam is clean and rebounds, or reusable plugs are intact. → *Expect:* plugs are clean and undamaged.
2. **Wash and dry hands.** Clean hands before touching the part that enters the ear. → *Expect:* fingers are dry and free of lotion.
3. **Compress foam if used.** Roll a foam plug into a narrow crease-free cylinder. → *Expect:* the plug is thin enough to enter the canal.
4. **Open the ear canal.** Reach over your head and gently pull the top of the ear up and back. → *Expect:* the ear canal straightens.
5. **Insert and hold.** Place the plug into the canal and hold it while foam expands, or seat reusable flanges with gentle pressure. → *Expect:* the plug seals without pain.
6. **Check fit.** Cup hands over ears and release them while listening to steady room noise. → *Expect:* sound changes little because the plugs are already sealing.
7. **Remove gently.** Twist slowly to break the seal before pulling out. → *Expect:* the plug comes out without a painful suction feeling.

## Decision points

- Plug causes pain → remove it and try a smaller size or different style.
- Sound reduction is weak → reinsert deeper after rerolling foam.
- Ear is wet or infected → avoid nonmedical earplug use and seek care if symptoms persist.
- Using for hazardous noise → use plugs with an appropriate noise reduction rating and fit test if available.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Plug backs out:** foam expanded before insertion → reroll smaller and insert promptly.
- **F2 Muffled pressure hurts:** plug is too deep or large → remove slowly and use a smaller plug.
- **F3 Dirty plug irritates ear:** reusable plug was not cleaned → wash and dry it or replace disposable foam.
- **F4 Poor seal:** ear was not pulled open → straighten the canal and reinsert.

## Verification

Each plug sits mostly inside the ear canal, feels comfortable, and noticeably reduces steady sound without pain.

## Variations

- Foam plugs: roll, insert, and hold for 20-30 seconds.
- Flanged reusable plugs: insert with a gentle twisting motion and do not force.
- Children: use only plugs sized for children and supervise removal.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Do not insert earplugs with ear pain, drainage, infection, or after ear surgery unless a clinician says it is safe.
