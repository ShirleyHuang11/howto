## Goal

Recover from a hard workout by restoring fluids, food, mobility, and sleep while watching for injury signs.

## Preconditions

- The workout is complete and cooldown can begin.
- Water, food, and a chance to sleep are available.
- No collapse, chest pain, heat illness, severe swelling, or dark urine; seek urgent care for those.

## Steps

1. **Cool down gradually.** Walk, pedal easy, or move lightly for 5 to 10 minutes. → *Expect:* heart rate and breathing come down steadily.
2. **Rehydrate.** Drink water and include electrolytes if the session was long, hot, or very sweaty. → *Expect:* thirst decreases and urine trends pale over time.
3. **Eat a recovery meal.** Include protein and carbohydrate within a few hours. → *Expect:* hunger is addressed and energy starts returning.
4. **Use gentle mobility.** Stretch lightly or move sore areas through comfortable range. → *Expect:* stiffness decreases without provoking sharp pain.
5. **Plan the next day easy.** Choose rest, walking, light cycling, or technique work instead of another max effort. → *Expect:* recovery has a protected slot.
6. **Sleep deliberately.** Set up a normal bedtime and reduce late caffeine or alcohol. → *Expect:* the highest-value recovery block is protected.
7. **Check symptoms the next morning.** Compare soreness, swelling, range of motion, and energy. → *Expect:* normal soreness is diffuse and improving, not sharp or worsening.

## Decision points

- Soreness is normal and improving -> keep light movement and resume training gradually.
- Pain is sharp, localized, swollen, or changes walking -> stop loading that area and seek professional assessment if it persists.
- You feel sick, overheated, or unusually weak -> rest and consider medical advice.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Skipping food:** detect by lingering shakiness or strong hunger -> recover by eating a balanced meal or snack with protein and carbohydrate.
- **F2 Training hard again too soon:** detect by planned high intensity while still very sore -> recover by replacing it with active recovery.
- **F3 Injury mistaken for soreness:** detect by sharp, one-sided, swollen, or function-limiting pain -> recover by resting the area and consulting a clinician if not quickly improving.

## Verification

Within 24 to 48 hours, hydration, food, sleep, and easy movement occurred, soreness is stable or improving, and no red-flag injury or illness signs are present.

## Variations

- Endurance session: emphasize carbohydrate, fluids, and electrolytes.
- Heavy lifting: emphasize protein, sleep, and at least 48 hours before repeating the same hard muscle group.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because severe exertion can reveal injury, heat illness, or rhabdomyolysis. Seek urgent care for dark cola-colored urine, severe swelling, confusion, chest pain, fainting, or symptoms that worsen rapidly.
