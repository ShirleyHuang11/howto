## Goal

Prepare the body for exercise with gradual heat, joint motion, and activity-specific practice.

## Preconditions

- Enough room to move without tripping.
- The planned workout or sport is known.
- No acute injury, fever, chest pain, or dizziness; get medical guidance before training if those are present.

## Steps

1. **Start easy movement.** Walk, pedal, row, or jog lightly for 3 to 5 minutes. → *Expect:* body temperature rises and breathing becomes slightly deeper.
2. **Move major joints.** Circle ankles, hips, shoulders, and wrists through comfortable ranges. → *Expect:* joints feel smoother without pinching.
3. **Add dynamic mobility.** Do leg swings, arm swings, lunges, inchworms, or gentle squats instead of long static holds. → *Expect:* muscles feel awake rather than stretched cold.
4. **Practice the workout pattern.** Do lighter versions of the main movement, such as bodyweight squats before loaded squats or brisk walking before running. → *Expect:* coordination improves before intensity rises.
5. **Ramp intensity gradually.** Add speed, load, or range in two or three small steps. → *Expect:* the first hard set or interval does not feel abrupt.
6. **Check readiness.** Scan for sharp pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness, or joint instability. → *Expect:* only normal effort sensations remain before starting.

## Decision points

- Strength training -> include lighter warmup sets of the exact lift.
- Running or field sport -> include skips, strides, or direction changes only after easy movement.
- Sharp pain appears -> stop and assess rather than stretching through it.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Static stretching first:** detect by long passive holds before movement -> recover by switching to dynamic movement and saving static holds for after training.
- **F2 Warmup too hard:** detect by fatigue before the workout begins -> recover by lowering pace and shortening drills.
- **F3 Pain during warmup:** detect by sharp or worsening symptoms -> recover by stopping and consulting a professional if symptoms persist or are severe.

## Verification

You complete 8 to 12 minutes of gradual movement, dynamic mobility, and activity-specific practice, and begin the workout without sharp pain or sudden intensity shock.

## Variations

- Short session: do 3 minutes easy movement plus two movement-specific drills.
- Cold weather: extend easy movement until hands and joints feel warm.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when movements stay controlled. Warmups do not diagnose injuries; recurring pain, dizziness, chest symptoms, or instability should be discussed with a clinician or qualified trainer.
