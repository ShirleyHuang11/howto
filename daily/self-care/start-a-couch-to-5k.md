## Goal

Build from little or no running to finishing a comfortable 5 km using gradual run-walk sessions.

## Preconditions

- Shoes that feel stable, weather-appropriate clothing, and a safe route or treadmill.
- Thirty minutes available three days per week with at least one rest day between runs.
- No unexplained chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or injury that changes walking; consult a clinician before starting if those apply or if you have a medical condition affected by exercise.

## Steps

1. **Pick three weekly run days.** Put them on the calendar with rest or easy walking days between them. → *Expect:* three specific sessions are scheduled before the week starts.
2. **Start each session with walking.** Walk briskly for 5 minutes before any jogging. → *Expect:* breathing is warmer and joints feel less stiff.
3. **Use short run-walk intervals.** Begin with 60 seconds easy jogging followed by 90 seconds walking, repeated for 20 minutes. → *Expect:* you finish feeling worked but able to speak in short sentences.
4. **Progress only one notch per week.** Add a little jogging time or reduce walking time, keeping total session time near 25 to 35 minutes. → *Expect:* the next week feels challenging but not like a test.
5. **Keep the pace easy.** Jog slowly enough that you could hold a conversation, even if that feels almost like walking. → *Expect:* breathing stays controlled and form does not collapse.
6. **Stop for sharp pain.** If pain is sharp, one-sided, or changes your stride, stop running and walk home. → *Expect:* discomfort is not forced into a limp.
7. **Finish with a cooldown.** Walk 5 minutes, drink water, and note distance or time completed. → *Expect:* breathing settles and the session is logged.
8. **Attempt 5 km when continuous running feels manageable.** Choose a flat route and treat the first 5 km as completion, not racing. → *Expect:* the distance is completed without sprinting or limping.

## Decision points

- You cannot finish the first interval set -> repeat the same week until it feels controlled.
- You miss a week -> resume the last completed comfortable week, not the hardest planned week.
- Pain persists more than a few days or affects walking -> pause the plan and consult a health professional.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Starting too fast:** detect by gasping in the first 5 minutes -> recover by slowing to a shuffle and repeating the current week.
- **F2 Shin or knee pain:** detect by sharp or worsening pain during impact -> recover by stopping, resting, checking shoes and surface, and seeking professional advice if it persists.
- **F3 Skipped sessions:** detect by fewer than two sessions in a week -> recover by restarting the previous completed week.

## Verification

You complete 5 km at an easy effort without sharp pain, with a warmup, cooldown, and at least eight weeks of mostly consistent gradual sessions recorded.

## Variations

- Treadmill: set a slight incline if comfortable and use time-based intervals instead of distance.
- Low-impact start: replace one weekly run with cycling, swimming, or brisk walking while joints adapt.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from overuse, traffic, heat, and underlying medical issues. Use visible routes, hydrate, stop for sharp pain, and consult a clinician for chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness, pregnancy concerns, or chronic conditions.
