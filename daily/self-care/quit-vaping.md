## Goal

Stop vaping nicotine with a clear quit plan, withdrawal supports, and relapse recovery steps.

## Preconditions

- A chosen quit date within the next two weeks.
- Awareness of current vaping patterns, nicotine strength, and triggers.
- Professional support is available if you are pregnant, under 18, have significant mental-health symptoms, or use other substances.

## Steps

1. **Set the quit date.** Pick a specific date and remove the idea of waiting for a perfect week. → *Expect:* one date is written down.
2. **Map triggers.** List times, places, emotions, and people linked to vaping. → *Expect:* the top three triggers are visible.
3. **Choose support.** Decide whether to use counseling, a quitline, a clinician, nicotine replacement, or a pharmacist-guided plan. → *Expect:* at least one support is ready before the quit date.
4. **Remove supplies.** Dispose of devices, pods, chargers, and backups on quit day. → *Expect:* vaping requires leaving to obtain supplies, not reaching into a drawer.
5. **Prepare craving actions.** Use delay, deep breathing, water, walking, gum, or texting support for 5 to 10 minutes. → *Expect:* each craving has a replacement behavior.
6. **Handle withdrawal as temporary.** Expect irritability, cravings, sleep changes, or concentration problems during the first days. → *Expect:* symptoms are treated as withdrawal, not proof you need to vape.
7. **Review daily.** Each evening, note cravings survived, triggers, and tomorrow's highest-risk moment. → *Expect:* the next day has one concrete prevention move.

## Decision points

- Cravings feel unmanageable -> contact a clinician, pharmacist, quitline, or cessation program about medication and counseling options.
- A slip happens -> discard the device or pod and resume immediately instead of declaring failure.
- Mood becomes severe, unsafe, or unstable -> seek urgent mental-health support or medical care.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Keeping a backup vape:** detect by saved devices or pods -> recover by removing them from home, car, bag, and desk.
- **F2 Trigger without replacement:** detect by craving at a usual time with no plan -> recover by doing the prepared 5-minute action and changing location.
- **F3 All-or-nothing relapse:** detect by one slip turning into a full restart -> recover by logging the trigger, removing access, and continuing the quit plan the same day.

## Verification

You reach seven consecutive vape-free days, have no devices or pods accessible, and can name your top triggers plus the support used when cravings peaked.

## Variations

- Taper plan: reduce nicotine strength or frequency with clinician or pharmacist guidance if abrupt quitting has failed.
- Social vaping: avoid high-risk gatherings for the first two weeks or attend with a support person.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from nicotine dependence, withdrawal, and relapse. Medical help improves success, especially for pregnancy, teens, heart or lung disease, and mental-health concerns. Quit apps or groups may expose health behavior data.
