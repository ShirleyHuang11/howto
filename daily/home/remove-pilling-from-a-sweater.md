## Goal

Loose pills are removed from a sweater without cutting the underlying yarns.

## Preconditions

- Sweater is clean and dry.
- Fabric shaver, sweater comb, pumice sweater stone, or small scissors are available.
- Flat work surface and good light are available.

## Steps

1. **Lay sweater flat.** Smooth one small section without stretching the knit. → *Expect:* pills sit above the surface and are easy to see.
2. **Choose the tool.** Use [BRANCH: fabric shaver | sweater comb | scissors] based on knit delicacy. → *Expect:* tool matches the fabric thickness.
3. **Test a hidden area.** Make several light passes on an inside hem or underside. → *Expect:* pills lift without fuzzing or cutting yarn.
4. **Remove pills lightly.** Work in short strokes over one section, keeping the fabric supported. → *Expect:* raised pills detach while intact stitches remain.
5. **Empty or clean the tool.** Clear lint from the shaver head or comb teeth as it fills. → *Expect:* the tool cuts or catches cleanly again.
6. **Brush away debris.** Use hands or a lint brush to remove loose fibers. → *Expect:* the cleaned section looks smoother.
7. **Store folded.** Fold the sweater instead of hanging it by the shoulders. → *Expect:* the knit keeps shape after depilling.

## Decision points

- Knit is loose or hand-knit → use scissors on individual pills instead of a shaver.
- Pills are near embroidery or seams → slow down and trim manually.
- Fabric starts fuzzing → stop and switch to a gentler tool.
- Pills return quickly → reduce abrasion from bags, seatbelts, and laundering.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Yarn gets nicked:** a strand is cut or pulled → stop, pull the loop to the inside, and avoid that tool.
- **F2 Hole forms:** shaver caught fabric → mend the hole before wearing.
- **F3 Surface looks fuzzy:** too many aggressive passes lifted fibers → brush lightly and stop depilling.
- **F4 Uneven appearance:** only one panel was cleaned → depill adjacent panels lightly for consistency.

## Verification

The sweater surface is visibly smoother, no intact yarn strands are cut, and seams, cuffs, and high-friction areas have been checked.

## Variations

- Cashmere: use a cashmere comb with very light pressure.
- Thick wool: a sweater stone can work, followed by lint brushing.
- Synthetic knits: an electric fabric shaver works if the guard is set high.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Keep blades away from fingers and avoid aggressive shaving because cut yarns can become permanent holes.
