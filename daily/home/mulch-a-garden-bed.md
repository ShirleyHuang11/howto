## Goal

Apply mulch to a garden bed to conserve moisture, moderate soil temperature, suppress weeds, and protect soil without smothering plant crowns or stems.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: suitable mulch such as shredded leaves, straw, bark, compost, wood chips, or clean grass clippings, plus gloves, rake, and water.
- The bed is weeded and plants are visible.
- Soil is moist or can be watered before mulching.

## Steps

1. **Choose mulch for the bed.** Use fine organic mulch for vegetables and perennials, and coarser wood mulch for paths or ornamental beds. → *Expect:* mulch suits the plants and use area.
2. **Weed first.** Pull weeds by the roots before covering the surface. → *Expect:* the bed starts with low weed pressure.
3. **Water dry soil.** Irrigate before mulching if the root zone is dry. → *Expect:* moisture is sealed in rather than blocked out.
4. **Spread an even layer.** Apply 1-3 inches depending on mulch texture and plant size. → *Expect:* soil is covered without forming a thick mat.
5. **Keep crowns and stems clear.** Pull mulch back a few inches from stems, trunks, and perennial crowns. → *Expect:* plant bases remain visible and dryable.
6. **Feather edges.** Thin mulch near seedlings, self-sowers, and places where bulbs need to emerge. → *Expect:* small plants are not buried.
7. **Water lightly to settle.** Spray or water gently if mulch is dusty or loose. → *Expect:* mulch settles and is less likely to blow away.
8. **Refresh as needed.** Check monthly and add small amounts where soil becomes exposed. → *Expect:* coverage remains consistent through the season.

## Decision points

- Slugs are a problem → keep mulch thinner and away from tender stems.
- Soil is cold in spring → wait until it warms before mulching heat-loving crops.
- Using grass clippings → apply thin dry layers only if untreated by herbicides.
- Tree or shrub bed → avoid mulch volcanoes against trunks.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Plants rot at base:** detect soft stems or crowns under mulch → pull mulch back and improve airflow.
- **F2 Weeds grow through:** detect weeds emerging quickly → add a thin top-up after hand weeding.
- **F3 Mulch repels water:** detect dry soil beneath crusted mulch → break up the layer and water slowly.
- **F4 Seedlings are buried:** detect missing or bent small plants → uncover immediately and use a thinner layer.

## Verification

The bed is weeded, soil is moist, mulch covers exposed soil evenly, and every plant crown, stem, or trunk has a clear gap around it.

## Variations

- `vegetable-bed`: use straw, shredded leaves, compost, or untreated grass clippings in thin layers.
- `ornamental-bed`: use bark or wood chips while keeping stems clear.
- `winter-protection`: apply after soil cools to reduce freeze-thaw heaving.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Wear gloves, avoid moldy mulch dust, do not use herbicide-treated clippings around food crops, and keep bagged mulch from blocking paths.
