## Goal

A small tear or hole in a window screen is patched so insects stay out and the screen remains usable.

## Preconditions

- You have screen patch material matching fiberglass or aluminum mesh, scissors, rubbing alcohol, cloth, and adhesive if the patch is not self-adhesive.
- The hole is small enough to patch without replacing the entire screen.
- Replace the screen spline and mesh instead if the fabric is brittle, sagging, or torn along the frame.

## Steps

1. **Remove loose strands.** Trim frayed mesh around the hole without enlarging it. → *Expect:* the damaged area has a neat edge.
2. **Clean the screen.** Wipe both sides around the tear with a lightly damp cloth and let dry. → *Expect:* dust and grease are gone.
3. **Cut the patch.** Cut patch mesh at least 1 inch larger than the hole on all sides. → *Expect:* the patch overlaps sound screen evenly.
4. **Apply the patch.** Press a self-adhesive patch in place or weave aluminum patch wires through the mesh. → *Expect:* the patch lies flat and covers the opening.
5. **Secure the edges.** Add a thin bead of clear outdoor adhesive if needed and smooth it around the patch edge. → *Expect:* the patch edges do not lift.
6. **Let it set.** Keep the screen flat or untouched until adhesive cures. → *Expect:* the patch stays bonded when lightly touched.
7. **Reinstall and inspect.** Put the screen back and check from indoors and outdoors. → *Expect:* no open gap remains around the patch.

## Decision points

- Tear is longer than several inches → replace the screen mesh instead of patching.
- Mesh is metal → use aluminum mesh patch or woven wire tabs.
- Screen frame is bent → straighten or replace the frame before patching.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Patch peels:** edge lifts after curing → clean again and use outdoor-rated adhesive or a larger patch.
- **F2 Hole spreads:** old mesh tears while trimming → rescreen the frame.
- **F3 Patch blocks sash:** raised patch catches the window → move patch to the outside face or rescreen.

## Verification

The patched screen has no visible open hole, the patch edge remains attached, and the window opens and closes without catching it.

## Variations

- Fiberglass mesh: adhesive patches usually blend best.
- Aluminum mesh: woven patch tabs hold better than glue alone.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: cut metal mesh can be sharp. Wear gloves for aluminum screen and avoid leaning on window screens, which are not fall protection.
