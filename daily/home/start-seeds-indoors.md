## Goal

Start vegetable or flower seeds indoors so they germinate evenly and grow into sturdy transplants ready for hardening off.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: fresh seeds, seed-starting mix, clean trays or cells with drainage, labels, water, light source, and optional heat mat.
- You know the average last frost date or intended transplant date for the crop.
- A bright window or grow light area is available where trays can be checked daily.

## Steps

1. **Check the sowing date.** Count back from the transplant date using the seed packet timing, usually 4-10 weeks depending on crop. → *Expect:* each crop has a realistic indoor start date.
2. **Moisten the mix.** Add water to seed-starting mix until it feels damp like a wrung-out sponge. → *Expect:* the mix holds together lightly without dripping.
3. **Fill and label containers.** Pack cells gently, level the surface, and label variety and sowing date before seeds are covered. → *Expect:* each row or pot is identifiable.
4. **Sow at the correct depth.** Plant seeds at the packet depth, or press light-needed seeds onto the surface. → *Expect:* seeds are covered or exposed according to their germination need.
5. **Water without washing seeds out.** Mist or bottom-water until the surface is evenly moist. → *Expect:* seeds remain in place and mix is damp.
6. **Provide warmth and humidity.** Cover with a dome or plastic wrap and keep near the crop's germination temperature. → *Expect:* the tray stays humid but not soggy.
7. **Move under strong light.** Remove covers when sprouts appear and place lights 2-3 inches above seedlings for 14-16 hours per day. → *Expect:* seedlings grow upright instead of stretching.
8. **Thin and maintain.** Keep the strongest seedling per cell, water from below when possible, and add airflow. → *Expect:* seedlings have space, firm stems, and no fuzzy soil growth.

## Decision points

- Seeds require cold stratification → chill them as directed before sowing.
- Seedlings appear leggy → move lights closer or increase light duration.
- Roots fill cells before outdoor conditions are ready → pot up once into a larger container.
- Outdoor weather is still cold → delay transplanting and begin hardening off later.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Seeds do not germinate:** detect empty cells after the packet germination window → check temperature and seed age, then resow.
- **F2 Damping off:** detect seedlings pinching at the soil line → improve airflow, reduce moisture, and start clean replacement cells.
- **F3 Seedlings stretch:** detect long pale stems leaning toward light → lower the light and rotate trays.
- **F4 Labels are lost:** detect unknown seedlings → compare leaf shapes if possible and relabel future trays before watering.

## Verification

Seedlings are labeled, evenly moist, under strong light after emergence, thinned to suitable spacing, and have compact green growth with no stem collapse.

## Variations

- `tomatoes-peppers`: use bottom heat for faster germination and start 6-8 weeks before transplanting.
- `cool-season`: start brassicas and greens earlier and harden off in cool weather.
- `direct-sow-crop`: skip indoor starting for roots, beans, corn, and crops that resent transplanting.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Use clean containers to reduce disease, avoid overloading electrical outlets with grow lights, and store seeds away from pets and children.
