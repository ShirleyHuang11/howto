## Goal

Grow edible microgreens indoors in a shallow tray and harvest them cleanly at the first true leaf stage.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: food-grade microgreen seeds, shallow tray with drainage, second tray or cover, seed-starting mix or grow mat, spray bottle, light source, scissors, and clean water.
- Seeds are intended for sprouting or microgreens and have not been treated with garden chemicals.
- The growing area can be kept clean and well lit.

## Steps

1. **Sanitize the tray.** Wash reusable trays and tools before planting. → *Expect:* surfaces are clean before seeds touch them.
2. **Prepare the medium.** Add 1-2 inches of moist seed-starting mix or place a moistened grow mat in the tray. → *Expect:* the surface is evenly damp and level.
3. **Sow densely but evenly.** Scatter seeds across the surface without thick piles, then press them gently for contact. → *Expect:* seeds cover the tray in a single even layer.
4. **Cover for germination.** Use a lid, second tray, or towel according to the crop for the first few days. → *Expect:* humidity stays high and seeds begin sprouting.
5. **Move into light.** Remove the cover when seedlings lift it or most seeds germinate, then place under bright light. → *Expect:* shoots turn green and stand upright.
6. **Water from below.** Add water to the bottom tray and pour off excess after the medium absorbs moisture. → *Expect:* stems stay cleaner and roots stay moist.
7. **Harvest at the right stage.** Cut just above the medium when cotyledons are open and first true leaves begin. → *Expect:* greens are tender and flavorful.
8. **Store or use promptly.** Keep harvested greens dry in a covered refrigerator container or eat immediately. → *Expect:* microgreens remain crisp and clean.

## Decision points

- Seeds are mucilaginous such as basil or chia → do not soak heavily and follow crop-specific directions.
- Mold appears → improve airflow, reduce seed density, and water less from above.
- Crop tastes too strong → harvest younger or choose milder seeds.
- No grow light is available → use the brightest window and rotate trays often.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Seeds mold:** detect fuzzy growth or sour smell → discard the tray, sanitize equipment, and resow less densely.
- **F2 Seedlings stretch:** detect pale long stems leaning hard → move closer to light and uncover sooner.
- **F3 Tray dries out:** detect wilting or dry medium edges → bottom-water promptly and cover edges less tightly next time.
- **F4 Seeds fail to sprout:** detect little germination after expected days → check seed age, temperature, and moisture before resowing.

## Verification

Microgreens are grown from food-safe seed in a clean tray, have green upright shoots, show no mold or sour odor, and are harvested with clean scissors above the medium.

## Variations

- `soil-free-mat`: use hemp, coir, or fiber mats and monitor drying more closely.
- `pea-shoots`: soak seeds first and grow slightly longer before harvest.
- `spicy-mix`: include radish or mustard and harvest young for milder flavor.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Use food-grade seed, clean water, sanitized trays, and discard any batch with mold, slime, or rotten odor.
