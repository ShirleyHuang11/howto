## Goal

A down jacket is washed and dried until the down clusters are clean, separated, and fully lofted.

## Preconditions

- Jacket care label allows washing.
- Down-specific wash, front-loading washer or large basin, dryer, and clean dryer balls are available.
- Tears are repaired before washing so down cannot escape.

## Steps

1. **Inspect and empty.** Close zippers, empty pockets, fasten tabs, and check seams for holes. → *Expect:* jacket is secured and no items remain inside.
2. **Clean the washer.** Remove detergent and softener residue from the dispenser. → *Expect:* only down wash will contact the jacket.
3. **Wash gently.** Use down wash on a gentle cycle with cold or warm water as the label allows. → *Expect:* jacket is wet through and cleaned without regular detergent.
4. **Rinse again.** Run an extra rinse to remove all soap. → *Expect:* rinse water has no visible suds.
5. **Support while moving.** Lift the wet jacket from underneath instead of pulling one sleeve. → *Expect:* heavy wet down does not strain seams.
6. **Dry low and slow.** Tumble dry low with dryer balls, removing and shaking every 30 minutes. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* high heat can melt shell fabric or damage down; confirm low heat before starting. → *Expect:* clumps gradually break apart and loft returns.
7. **Break clumps by hand.** Pinch and separate damp down clusters between dryer checks. → *Expect:* baffles feel less lumpy each cycle.
8. **Confirm full dryness.** Continue drying until cuffs, seams, and thick baffles feel dry and airy. → *Expect:* no cool damp spots remain.

## Decision points

- Top-loading washer has a central agitator → hand wash or use a front loader to avoid tearing.
- Jacket leaks feathers heavily → stop and repair seams before continuing.
- DWR finish no longer beads after washing → reproof the jacket after it is clean.
- Label forbids tumble drying → follow manufacturer drying instructions and expect much longer drying.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Down stays clumped:** jacket is still damp → keep drying low with balls and manual separation.
- **F2 Soap remains:** fabric feels slick or foamy → rinse again before drying fully.
- **F3 Bad odor appears:** down dried too slowly or incompletely → wash again if needed and dry fully with airflow.
- **F4 Shell damage:** heat marks or delamination appear → stop heat and avoid further machine drying.

## Verification

The jacket has no soap feel, no damp or cool baffles, and the down is evenly lofted across sleeves, body, and collar.

## Variations

- Hand wash: press water through the jacket in a tub without wringing, then rinse repeatedly.
- Hydrophobic down: still use down wash, not regular detergent.
- Small dryer: dry one jacket at a time so it can tumble freely.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from appliance heat and permanent garment damage. Avoid bleach, fabric softener, wringing, and high heat.
