## Goal

Dry surface dirt and light marks are removed from suede while preserving the nap.

## Preconditions

- Item is suede or nubuck, not smooth leather.
- Suede brush, suede eraser or clean pencil eraser, white cloth, and paper stuffing are available.
- Item is dry before cleaning.

## Steps

1. **Dry completely.** Let wet suede air dry away from heat before brushing. → *Expect:* the nap feels dry and no damp dark patches remain.
2. **Stuff for support.** Place paper inside shoes or under bags to support the surface. → *Expect:* suede does not collapse under brushing.
3. **Brush with the nap.** Use a suede brush in one direction to lift loose dirt. → *Expect:* dust and flattened fibers loosen.
4. **Erase small marks.** Rub a suede eraser gently over scuffs or dry stains. → *Expect:* marks lighten and eraser crumbs appear.
5. **Brush crumbs away.** Brush again to remove eraser debris and reset nap direction. → *Expect:* surface texture looks even.
6. **Treat edges carefully.** Use light strokes around seams, welts, and trim. → *Expect:* dirt lifts without fraying stitching.
7. **Let rest and reassess.** Leave the item for 10 minutes, then inspect in natural light. → *Expect:* cleaned and uncleaned areas blend.

## Decision points

- Stain is oil or grease → apply suede-safe absorbent powder first and avoid water.
- Salt stains are present → use a suede-specific salt remover or professional cleaner.
- Dye transfers to cloth → stop and avoid wet cleaning.
- Item is expensive or color-critical → test hidden areas or use professional cleaning.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Nap flattens:** brushing was too hard or wet → let dry and brush gently in multiple directions.
- **F2 Water ring appears:** moisture spread unevenly → dry fully and brush; persistent rings need suede cleaner.
- **F3 Color lightens:** eraser removed dye or finish → stop and blend lightly with brushing only.
- **F4 Stain spreads:** liquid treatment moved soil → blot dry and use a professional suede cleaner.

## Verification

The suede is dry, the nap is raised evenly, loose dirt is gone, and no new water rings or pale rubbed patches are visible.

## Variations

- Nubuck: use a nubuck cloth or very soft brush.
- Suede shoes: brush soles and welts separately so grit does not scratch uppers.
- Protective spray: apply only after cleaning, drying, and spot testing.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because water, heat, and harsh cleaners can permanently stain suede. Avoid soaking, direct heat, and untested household solvents.
