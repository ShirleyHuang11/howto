## Goal

A door lock turns smoothly after the keyway, latch, and strike alignment are cleaned and adjusted.

## Preconditions

- You have graphite or lock-specific dry lubricant, screwdriver, cloth, flashlight, and the correct key.
- The door can be opened before starting.
- Call a locksmith if the key is bent, broken inside the lock, or the lock protects an exterior door that cannot be secured.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the key.** Compare it with a spare and wipe off dirt or burrs. → *Expect:* the key is clean and not visibly bent.
2. **Test with the door open.** Turn the key and thumb turn while the latch is not in the strike. → *Expect:* you know whether the cylinder or door alignment is sticking.
3. **Lubricate the keyway.** Apply a small amount of dry lock lubricant and work the key in and out. → *Expect:* the key enters farther and turns more smoothly.
4. **Tighten hardware.** Snug loose screws on the lock body, latch plate, hinges, and strike plate. → *Expect:* the lock and door no longer shift by hand.
5. **Check latch alignment.** Close the door slowly and watch whether the latch rubs the strike. → *Expect:* rub marks or smooth entry identify the next action.
6. **Adjust the strike.** File a small rub point or loosen and shift the strike plate slightly. → *Expect:* the latch enters the strike without lifting or pushing the door.
7. **Retest locked and unlocked.** Operate the key several times from both sides if accessible. → *Expect:* the bolt extends and retracts without binding.

## Decision points

- Lock works open but sticks closed → adjust hinges or strike alignment.
- Lock sticks even with the door open → the cylinder may be worn or contaminated.
- Exterior lock cannot be made reliable → replace it or call a locksmith the same day.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Key will not turn:** cylinder still binds → stop forcing it, try a spare key, and call a locksmith if unchanged.
- **F2 Latch rubs high or low:** door has sagged → tighten hinge screws or use longer hinge screws into framing.
- **F3 Oily buildup:** previous oil attracts grime → clean accessible residue and switch to dry lubricant.

## Verification

With the door closed, the key locks and unlocks the door five times without lifting, pulling, or forcing the door.

## Variations

- Deadbolts: verify the bolt fully enters the strike, not just partway.
- Interior knobs: latch replacement may be faster than cylinder service.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: do not leave an exterior door unsecured. Avoid sharing key photos or lock codes, and call a locksmith if security is compromised.
