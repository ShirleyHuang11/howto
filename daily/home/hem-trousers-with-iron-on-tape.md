## Goal

Trouser hems are shortened evenly with fusible hem tape and a crisp fold.

## Preconditions

- Trousers are washable or pressable according to the care label.
- Iron, ironing board, fusible hem tape, ruler, pins or clips, damp pressing cloth, and scissors are available.
- Shoes normally worn with the trousers are available for fitting.

## Steps

1. **Try on and mark length.** Wear the trousers with shoes and mark the desired finished hem on both legs. → *Expect:* both legs have visible length marks.
2. **Measure both legs.** Lay trousers flat and compare inseam lengths from crotch seam to marks. → *Expect:* marks are even or intentionally adjusted for posture.
3. **Set the fold.** Turn each leg inside out and fold the hem to the marked finished length. → *Expect:* the fold line matches the chosen length around each leg.
4. **Press the crease.** Use label-safe heat and a pressing cloth to crease the fold. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* high heat can scorch or shine fabric; confirm the fiber setting first. → *Expect:* the new hem crease stays in place.
5. **Trim excess if needed.** Leave at least 1-1/2 inches of allowance before cutting bulky extra fabric. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* cut fabric cannot be restored; confirm both legs twice before trimming. → *Expect:* each leg has a manageable, even allowance.
6. **Insert hem tape.** Place fusible tape inside the fold, set back from the visible edge. → *Expect:* tape is hidden and does not protrude.
7. **Fuse in sections.** Press with the damp cloth for the tape label time without sliding the iron. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* melted adhesive can mark fabric; test a hidden area first. → *Expect:* the folded allowance bonds to the trouser leg.
8. **Cool flat.** Leave the hem untouched until fully cool. → *Expect:* adhesive sets without lifting.
9. **Check the outside.** Turn trousers right side out and inspect the hem line. → *Expect:* both hems are even with no adhesive bleed-through.

## Decision points

- Fabric cannot be ironed → use sewing or tailoring instead of fusible tape.
- Hem has a cuff → preserve the cuff structure or use a tailor.
- Adhesive shows through thin fabric → stop and sew by hand instead.
- Length is uncertain → baste or pin and wear briefly before fusing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hem lifts:** heat, time, or pressure was insufficient → re-press with a damp cloth according to tape instructions.
- **F2 Legs are uneven:** marks shifted before fusing → soften adhesive with heat, peel carefully, and reset if fabric allows.
- **F3 Shiny fabric mark:** iron was too hot or unprotected → use a pressing cloth and lower heat; shine may be permanent.
- **F4 Tape leaks out:** tape was too wide or near the edge → trim exposed adhesive after cooling or redo with narrower tape.

## Verification

Both trouser legs measure the same finished inseam, the hems stay bonded after cooling, and no adhesive or scorch marks are visible outside.

## Variations

- Heavy denim: use stronger tape and longer pressing time, or sew for durability.
- Dress trousers: use lightweight tape and a pressing cloth to reduce shine.
- Temporary hem: use removable fashion tape instead of fusible tape.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from hot iron burns and permanent fabric damage. Keep fingers clear of steam and confirm length, heat setting, and tape position before fusing or cutting.
