## Goal

Prepare garden beds, tools, containers, and tender plants for freezing weather while preserving soil health and habitat.

## Preconditions

- The main harvest is finished or frost is forecast.
- You have gloves, pruners, mulch or leaves, labels, and a place to store hoses and tools.
- You know which plants are annuals, perennials, bulbs, or tender containers.

## Steps

1. **Harvest and label.** Pick remaining vegetables, herbs, and seeds worth saving, and label perennials before foliage disappears. → *Expect:* edible crops and plant locations are accounted for.
2. **Remove diseased plants.** Pull blighted tomatoes, mildewed squash, and pest-infested debris for trash, not compost. → *Expect:* obvious disease sources are out of the bed.
3. **Leave useful stems and leaves.** Keep healthy perennial stems or a thin leaf layer where wildlife habitat and local practice allow. → *Expect:* beds are tidy but not stripped bare.
4. **Mulch exposed soil.** Add straw, shredded leaves, compost, or cover crop seed to protect soil from erosion and freeze-thaw cycles. → *Expect:* bare soil is covered without burying plant crowns.
5. **Protect tender plants.** [BRANCH: bring indoors | mulch outdoors] move tender pots inside after checking for pests, or mulch hardy crowns and bulbs. → *Expect:* plants have an appropriate cold plan.
6. **Drain water gear.** Disconnect hoses, drain watering cans, empty ceramic pots that may crack, and shut off outdoor spigots if needed. → *Expect:* no trapped water remains in freeze-prone equipment.
7. **Clean and store tools.** Brush soil from tools, dry metal parts, and oil pruner blades lightly. → *Expect:* tools are dry, sharp, and stored under cover.

## Decision points

- Plant debris is diseased → remove it from the property waste stream if local rules allow.
- Winters are mild → focus on mulch, drainage, and cool-season crops rather than full bed shutdown.
- Wildlife habitat is a priority → leave some stems and leaves away from foundations and paths.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hose freezes:** detect a stiff or split hose → thaw, drain, and replace damaged sections before spring.
- **F2 Mulch smothers crowns:** detect mulch piled against plant centers → pull it back to prevent rot.
- **F3 Pests come indoors:** detect insects on overwintered pots → isolate, wash foliage, and treat before placing near houseplants.
- **F4 Disease returns:** detect the same bed infected next season → rotate crops and avoid composting suspect debris.

## Verification

Beds are covered, diseased material is removed, tender plants have a cold plan, hoses are drained, and tools are clean and stored.

## Variations

- `vegetable-bed`: add compost and cover crops after removing spent annuals.
- `pollinator-garden`: leave hollow stems and seed heads until spring cleanup.
- `cold-climate`: add deeper mulch after the ground begins to freeze.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk from sharp tools, moldy debris, lifting heavy pots, and frozen walkways. Wear gloves, lift with help, and keep fertilizers, bulbs, and pest treatments away from children and pets.
