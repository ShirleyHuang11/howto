## Goal

Help a kitten build positive experiences with handling, people, normal home sounds, carriers, and gentle play.

## Preconditions

- The kitten is healthy enough for interaction and has food, water, litter, rest, and safe hiding.
- Interactions are short, calm, and supervised.
- Vaccination and parasite prevention timing has been discussed with a vet before outside exposure or unknown animals.

## Steps

1. **Create a safe base.** Keep the kitten in a kitten-proofed room with hiding, scratching, toys, litter, food, and water. → *Expect:* the kitten can retreat and reappear voluntarily.
2. **Pair people with good things.** Offer treats, meals, play, and soft voices from different calm people. → *Expect:* the kitten approaches or watches with relaxed curiosity.
3. **Handle briefly.** Touch paws, ears, mouth area, tail base, and body for one or two seconds, then reward. → *Expect:* handling predicts food or play instead of restraint.
4. **Introduce normal sounds gently.** Play household sounds at low volume or run appliances at a distance while feeding or playing. → *Expect:* the kitten notices the sound and recovers quickly.
5. **Teach carrier comfort.** Leave the carrier open with bedding and treats inside. → *Expect:* the kitten enters or rests near the carrier without being forced.
6. **Redirect biting and scratching.** Use toys for play and stop hand play calmly when teeth or claws touch skin. → *Expect:* the kitten attacks toys instead of hands.

## Decision points

- If the kitten hides constantly, will not eat, or seems sick → call a vet.
- If the kitten is feral or undersocialized → use slower steps and consider rescue or behavior support.
- If visitors include children → teach sitting, quiet hands, and no chasing before meeting the kitten.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Overwhelmed kitten:** flattened ears, hiding, hissing, or freezing appears → increase distance, reduce duration, and let the kitten retreat.
- **F2 Rough play escalates:** hands become targets → end play briefly and restart with wand toys.
- **F3 Unsafe exposure:** unvaccinated kitten contacts unknown animals → call a vet about disease and parasite risk.

## Verification

The kitten voluntarily approaches familiar people, tolerates brief gentle handling, uses the carrier area calmly, and recovers from normal household sounds.

## Variations

- `single-person-home`: invite calm visitors after vaccinations and with the kitten's escape route preserved.
- `multi-pet`: keep introductions separate from basic socialization until scent and health checks are complete.
- `older-kitten`: expect slower progress and use the same reward-based structure.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk when the kitten controls distance. See a vet for not eating, diarrhea, eye or nose discharge, lethargy, parasites, or bite wounds.
