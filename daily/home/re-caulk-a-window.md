## Goal

Failed window caulk is removed and replaced with exterior-rated sealant so drafts and water intrusion are reduced.

## Preconditions

- You have exterior window caulk, caulk gun, utility knife, scraper, rubbing alcohol or appropriate cleaner, painter's tape, and rags.
- The surface is dry and the forecast allows the sealant to cure.
- Call a pro if trim is rotten, flashing is missing, glass is loose, or water damage extends into the wall.

## Steps

1. **Inspect the joint.** Identify cracked, missing, or separated caulk around the window trim. → *Expect:* the repair area is clear and limited to sealant failure.
2. **Remove old caulk.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* cut and scrape failed caulk without gouging trim or siding. → *Expect:* the joint is open and loose material is gone.
3. **Clean and dry.** Brush dust away, wipe bonding surfaces, and let them dry fully. → *Expect:* the joint is clean, dry, and ready for sealant.
4. **Tape neat edges.** Apply painter's tape along both sides of the joint if a crisp bead is needed. → *Expect:* the intended bead width is defined.
5. **Cut the nozzle.** Cut the caulk tip to the joint width and puncture the inner seal. → *Expect:* caulk flows in a controlled bead.
6. **Apply the bead.** Run a continuous bead over the joint with steady pressure. → *Expect:* the gap is filled without skips.
7. **Tool the bead.** Smooth the bead with a wet finger or caulk tool, then pull tape before skin forms. → *Expect:* the sealant bridges both sides with a concave surface.
8. **Let it cure.** Keep the window dry and undisturbed for the product cure time. → *Expect:* the bead firms without cracks or bubbles.

## Decision points

- Gap is wider than the caulk label allows → install backer rod before caulking.
- Old caulk is silicone → remove it completely or use a compatible silicone product.
- Water damage or rot appears → stop cosmetic caulking and repair the assembly.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Bead separates:** caulk pulls from one side → remove it after curing failure and recaulk on clean, dry surfaces.
- **F2 Messy line:** excess caulk smears trim → wipe immediately with the recommended solvent or let cure and trim carefully.
- **F3 Leak persists:** water still enters → inspect flashing, siding, and sill; call a window or exterior repair pro.

## Verification

After curing, the caulk bead is continuous, adhered to both sides, and no draft or water path is visible around the repaired joint.

## Variations

- Interior window trim: use paintable interior acrylic latex caulk for non-wet locations.
- Masonry openings: use a sealant rated for masonry and wider movement.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk: ladders, blades, and water intrusion create hazards. Work from stable footing, cut away from hands, ventilate solvent use, and do not seal over hidden rot.
