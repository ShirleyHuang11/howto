## Goal

Set up a stable aquarium with conditioned water, filtration, heat if needed, and a cycling plan before adding fish.

## Preconditions

- You have an aquarium, stand rated for its filled weight, filter, dechlorinator, thermometer, test kit, substrate, and species-appropriate heater if needed.
- The tank location is away from direct sun, drafts, speakers, and busy child or pet traffic.
- You are prepared to cycle the tank before stocking fish.

## Steps

1. **Place the tank on a proper stand.** Set it level on a surface designed for the filled weight, leaving room for cords and filter access. → *Expect:* the tank does not rock and all edges are supported.
2. **Rinse substrate and decor.** Rinse gravel, sand, rocks, and decorations with plain water, not soap. → *Expect:* rinse water runs mostly clear and no chemical residue is present.
3. **Install equipment dry.** Add substrate, decor, filter, thermometer, and heater while the tank is empty or partly filled, following equipment instructions. → *Expect:* equipment is secure and reachable.
4. **Add conditioned water.** Fill slowly onto a plate or bag to avoid disturbing substrate, then dose dechlorinator for the full water volume. → *Expect:* the tank is filled, treated, and not cloudy from a hard pour.
5. **Start filter and heater.** Make drip loops in all cords before plugging in equipment. → *Expect:* water circulates, temperature begins stabilizing, and cords dip below outlet height before rising.
6. **Begin cycling.** Add an ammonia source or starter bacteria according to the method chosen and test ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate over time. → *Expect:* the tank has a written cycling log rather than fish added immediately.
7. **Stock only after water tests pass.** [BRANCH: cycled | not cycled] add a small first group only when ammonia and nitrite read zero after processing ammonia, or keep cycling. → *Expect:* fish are not exposed to toxic startup spikes.

## Decision points

- Tank is for goldfish, bettas, tropical community fish, or shrimp → adjust size, heater, flow, and stocking plan to species.
- Tap water has chloramine or unusual pH → use conditioner and test before choosing sensitive species.
- Children help with setup → assign rinsing and measuring tasks, but adults handle plugs, glass, and water weight.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Tank leaks:** detect drips or wet stand → unplug equipment, move water safely, and replace or reseal before use.
- **F2 Cloudy water:** detect milkiness after filling → wait, filter, and avoid overfeeding or early stocking.
- **F3 Ammonia spike:** detect ammonia above zero with fish present → do partial water changes and pause stocking.
- **F4 Heater unsafe:** detect heater exposed to air or wrong temperature → unplug, let cool, reposition, and verify thermometer reading.

## Verification

The aquarium is level, filtered, dechlorinated, heated if required, electrically safe with drip loops, and has a cycling test log before fish are added.

## Variations

- `betta`: use gentle filtration, warm stable water, and a covered tank.
- `coldwater`: verify the species truly fits the tank volume and room temperature.
- `planted-tank`: add plant substrate or root tabs and a timed light.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from heavy water, glass breakage, electrical equipment near water, and animal welfare. Use drip loops, never use soap inside the tank, and cycle before stocking.
