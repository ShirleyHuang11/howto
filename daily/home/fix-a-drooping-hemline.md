## Goal

A loose or drooping hem is temporarily or permanently secured so the garment hangs evenly again.

## Preconditions

- Garment is clean and dry.
- Needle and thread, fusible tape, safety pins, or fabric tape are available.
- The original hem fold is still visible.

## Steps

1. **Find the loose area.** Turn the garment inside out and trace where stitches or adhesive failed. → *Expect:* the drooping section and secure section are clearly identified.
2. **Realign the fold.** Fold the hem back to the original crease and clip or pin it in place. → *Expect:* the outside hemline looks even.
3. **Choose the repair.** Use [BRANCH: hand stitch | fusible tape | temporary tape or pins] based on time and fabric. → *Expect:* the repair method fits the garment and event.
4. **Secure the hem.** Stitch tiny catch stitches, fuse tape with a pressing cloth, or place temporary tape inside the fold. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* iron heat and adhesive can mark fabric; test before fusing. → *Expect:* the loose fold stays against the inside of the garment.
5. **Remove visible pins.** Take out alignment pins or replace emergency pins with hidden ones. → *Expect:* no sharp pins show on the outside.
6. **Press or smooth lightly.** Use label-safe pressing or hand smoothing to settle the hem. → *Expect:* the hem edge hangs flat.
7. **Inspect while hanging.** Hang or wear the garment and check the hem all around. → *Expect:* the repaired section matches the rest of the hemline.

## Decision points

- Fabric cannot be ironed → hand stitch or use temporary fabric tape.
- Hem fabric is fraying → fold raw edges under or use seam binding.
- Formal garment has lining → stitch only the hem allowance, not through the outer face.
- Repair is for one wearing only → temporary tape or hidden safety pins may be enough.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Stitches show outside:** needle caught too much fabric → remove visible stitches and resew smaller.
- **F2 Tape fails:** fabric is dusty, textured, or damp → clean, dry, and use stitching.
- **F3 Hem is uneven:** fold was misaligned → unpin or unstick before final pressing and reset the crease.
- **F4 Fabric shines:** iron was too hot → stop pressing and use a cloth at lower heat next time.

## Verification

With the garment hanging naturally, the repaired hem stays level, does not gape, and has no visible stitches, pins, or adhesive on the outside.

## Variations

- Skirt or dress: check the hem while worn because body shape affects the line.
- Trousers: match both leg inseams before securing.
- Emergency event repair: use double-sided fashion tape and inspect after sitting.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Needles and pins can puncture skin, and fusible products can permanently mark fabric if overheated.
