## Goal

Start a small vegetable garden with suitable crops, prepared soil, and a watering plan that can be maintained through the season.

## Preconditions

- You have a sunny spot, container, or raised bed with access to water.
- You know the approximate last frost date or current growing season.
- You have seeds or seedlings, compost, mulch, labels, and basic hand tools.

## Steps

1. **Pick a small first plot.** Choose a bed or containers you can reach and water easily, such as a 4-by-4-foot bed or a few pots near the kitchen. → *Expect:* the garden size feels maintainable.
2. **Check sunlight and drainage.** Observe whether the site gets 6 or more hours of sun and whether water drains within a few hours. → *Expect:* vegetables will not sit in shade or soggy soil.
3. **Choose seasonal crops.** [BRANCH: cool season | warm season] plant peas, lettuce, radishes, and kale in cool weather; tomatoes, peppers, beans, basil, and squash after frost. → *Expect:* selected crops match the current season.
4. **Prepare the soil.** Loosen compacted soil, remove weeds, and mix in compost without overworking wet clay. → *Expect:* soil is crumbly and roots can enter.
5. **Plant at proper spacing.** Follow seed packet or tag depth and spacing, label rows, and keep tall crops where they will not shade short ones. → *Expect:* each crop has room and a readable label.
6. **Water gently.** Soak seeds or transplants at soil level, avoiding a hard spray that uncovers seeds. → *Expect:* soil is evenly moist but not flooded.
7. **Mulch and protect.** Add straw, shredded leaves, or clean mulch after seedlings emerge, and install cages or netting if pets, kids, rabbits, or birds are likely. → *Expect:* soil stays moist and plants are protected from trampling or browsing.
8. **Schedule care.** Put watering, weeding, and harvest checks on a calendar two or three times per week. → *Expect:* maintenance has a visible routine.

## Decision points

- Soil may contain lead or contaminants → use raised beds or containers with clean soil.
- Water access is far away → reduce garden size or install a hose plan before planting.
- Pests are common → choose fencing, row cover, or resistant crops early.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Seeds do not sprout:** detect bare rows after the packet's germination window → check moisture and temperature, then reseed.
- **F2 Seedlings flop:** detect stretched pale stems → move to more light or thin crowded plants.
- **F3 Plants wilt daily:** detect drooping despite watering → mulch, water deeply, and check container drainage.
- **F4 Weeds take over:** detect weeds larger than seedlings → weed after rain and add mulch once crops are established.

## Verification

The garden has season-appropriate crops planted at labeled spacing, soil is moist, water access is practical, and a recurring care schedule exists.

## Variations

- `container-garden`: use large pots with drainage holes and potting mix, not garden soil.
- `family-garden`: include fast crops such as radishes, snap peas, or cherry tomatoes for children.
- `hot-summer`: use mulch, morning watering, and shade cloth for tender greens.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, mostly soil contamination, tool cuts, sun exposure, and trip hazards from hoses. Test or avoid questionable soil, wash produce, and store fertilizers away from children and pets.
