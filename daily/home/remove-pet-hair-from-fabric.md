## Goal

Pet hair is removed from clothing, upholstery, or bedding without grinding it deeper into the fabric.

## Preconditions

- Fabric is dry.
- Lint roller, rubber glove, squeegee, tape, or vacuum upholstery tool is available.
- Delicate fabric care limits are known.

## Steps

1. **Dry the fabric fully.** Let damp fabric air dry before hair removal. → *Expect:* hair sits on the surface instead of smearing.
2. **Shake outdoors if possible.** Snap loose washable items away from your face. → *Expect:* loose hair falls off before detailed cleaning.
3. **Use a directional pass.** Drag a lint roller, damp rubber glove, or squeegee in one direction. → *Expect:* hair gathers into lines or clumps.
4. **Collect clumps.** Pick up gathered hair and discard it. → *Expect:* removed hair does not redeposit on the fabric.
5. **Vacuum creases.** Use an upholstery tool along seams, cushions, pockets, and folds. → *Expect:* embedded hair lifts from recessed areas.
6. **Repeat with fresh adhesive or rinsed rubber.** Replace lint sheets or rinse the glove as pickup drops. → *Expect:* each pass continues collecting hair.
7. **Finish before laundering.** Remove visible hair before putting washable fabric in the machine. → *Expect:* less hair transfers to washer and dryer surfaces.

## Decision points

- Fabric is velvet, suede, or delicate knit → use a soft brush or low-tack roller first.
- Hair is woven into upholstery → use a rubber squeegee followed by vacuuming.
- Static keeps hair attached → lightly mist the glove, not the fabric, and try again.
- Person has allergies → wear a mask and clean outdoors or near ventilation.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Hair smears:** fabric or glove is too wet → let fabric dry and use a barely damp rubber surface.
- **F2 Fabric pills:** tool is too abrasive → switch to a lint roller or vacuum brush.
- **F3 Hair returns after washing:** too much hair entered the washer → clean washer gasket and dryer lint screen, then pre-remove next time.
- **F4 Adhesive residue remains:** tape or roller was too sticky → dab residue with a damp cloth and use lower-tack tools.

## Verification

In bright light, the fabric surface, seams, and folds show no obvious pet-hair clumps after brushing your hand across the grain.

## Variations

- Car seats: use a rubber squeegee in short strokes before vacuuming.
- Laundry: tumble dry no-heat for 5 minutes first, then clean the lint screen and wash.
- Bedding: brush both sides before laundering to reduce washer buildup.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Avoid abrasive tools on delicate fabric and keep loose hair away from drains, washer filters, and people with pet allergies.
