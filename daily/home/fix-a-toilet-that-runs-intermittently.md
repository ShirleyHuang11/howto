## Goal

A toilet that refills intermittently is adjusted or fitted with a new flapper so tank water no longer leaks into the bowl.

## Preconditions

- You have a towel, flashlight, replacement flapper if needed, and food coloring for leak testing.
- You know the toilet shutoff valve location.
- Call a plumber if the shutoff valve leaks, tank bolts leak, porcelain is cracked, or the flush valve seat is damaged.

## Steps

1. **Remove the tank lid.** Place it flat on a towel away from the toilet. → *Expect:* the flapper, chain, fill valve, and overflow tube are visible.
2. **Check water height.** Let the tank fill and compare water level to the overflow tube. → *Expect:* water stops below the overflow tube rim.
3. **Adjust a high float.** Lower the float if water enters the overflow tube. → *Expect:* the fill valve shuts off below the tube.
4. **Test for flapper leak.** Add food coloring to the tank and wait ten minutes without flushing. → *Expect:* bowl water stays clear, or color reveals leakage.
5. **Shut off water.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* turn the toilet supply valve clockwise before removing the flapper. → *Expect:* the tank does not refill after a flush.
6. **Replace or clean flapper.** Unhook the old flapper, clean the flush valve seat, and attach a matching flapper. → *Expect:* the flapper sits flat over the valve opening.
7. **Set chain slack.** Adjust the chain so it has slight slack when the flapper is closed. → *Expect:* the handle lifts the flapper without holding it open.
8. **Restore water and test.** Open the shutoff, flush twice, and listen after refills. → *Expect:* the tank refills, stops, and remains silent.

## Decision points

- Water is flowing into overflow → adjust or replace the fill valve.
- Dye appears in bowl → replace the flapper or repair the flush valve seat.
- Shutoff valve will not close fully → call a plumber before replacing tank parts.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Chain too tight:** flapper stays slightly open → lengthen the chain by one or two links.
- **F2 Flapper floats:** flapper closes too late or not at all → use the correct model and adjust float settings on adjustable flappers.
- **F3 Seat roughness:** new flapper still leaks → clean mineral buildup or replace the flush valve assembly.

## Verification

After two flushes and a ten-minute dye test, no color enters the bowl and the toilet does not refill on its own.

## Variations

- Dual-flush toilets: use manufacturer-specific seals instead of a standard flapper.
- Canister flush valves: replace the seal ring rather than a hinged flapper.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk: water leaks can damage flooring. Shut off water before removing parts, do not force brittle valves, and call a plumber for leaks outside the tank.
