## Goal

A flattened pillow is fluffed, dried, and checked for support so it can be used comfortably or identified for replacement.

## Preconditions

- Pillow care label is readable.
- Dryer, clean tennis balls or dryer balls, sunlight or airflow, and pillow protector are available.
- Pillow has no mold, pest contamination, or torn casing.

## Steps

1. **Check the label and fill.** Identify [BRANCH: down | polyester | foam] and drying limits. → *Expect:* the pillow type and safe method are known.
2. **Inspect for failure.** Look for clumps, odor, stains, torn seams, or foam crumbling. → *Expect:* pillow is suitable for restoration or marked for replacement.
3. **Hand-fluff corners.** Push fill from edges toward the center and shake the pillow on all sides. → *Expect:* fill redistributes more evenly.
4. **Air it out.** Place the pillow in sun or strong indoor airflow for 30-60 minutes. → *Expect:* moisture and stale odor reduce.
5. **Tumble if allowed.** Dry on no-heat or low heat with dryer balls, checking every 15 minutes. → *Expect:* fill lifts and separates without overheating.
6. **Cool and reshape.** Lay the pillow flat and pat it into an even rectangle. → *Expect:* the pillow holds a fuller shape.
7. **Add protection.** Put on a clean pillow protector and pillowcase. → *Expect:* restored loft is shielded from sweat and oils.

## Decision points

- Foam pillow is involved → do not tumble unless the label explicitly allows it.
- Pillow smells musty after airing → wash if label allows or replace it.
- Fill stays hard and lumpy → replacement may be more effective than repeated drying.
- Allergies worsen near the pillow → wash, encase, or replace.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Pillow overheats:** casing feels hot or smells scorched → stop dryer, cool flat, and inspect damage.
- **F2 Fill clumps:** damp fill sticks together → continue low/no-heat drying with balls until fully dry.
- **F3 Loft collapses quickly:** fill is worn out → replace the pillow.
- **F4 Odor remains:** moisture or body oils are embedded → wash according to label or discard.

## Verification

The pillow is fully dry, evenly filled, free of musty odor, and rebounds after being compressed by hand for 10 seconds.

## Variations

- Down pillow: use low heat and longer drying so the fill dries completely.
- Polyester pillow: no-heat tumble often restores loft temporarily.
- Memory foam: air only and spot clean; replace if foam no longer rebounds.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Avoid overheating foam or synthetic fill, and do not keep pillows with mold, pest residue, or persistent damp odor.
