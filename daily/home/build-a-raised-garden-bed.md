## Goal

Build a stable raised bed in a sunny, accessible spot and fill it with safe growing soil.

## Preconditions

- You have lumber or bed panels safe for edible gardens, exterior screws or brackets, cardboard, soil blend, and basic tools.
- The site receives enough sun for the intended plants and is reachable by hose.
- Underground lines are not at risk from shallow leveling or anchoring.

## Steps

1. **Choose and measure the site.** Pick a level area with 6 or more hours of sun for vegetables and leave paths wide enough for a wheelbarrow or child helper. → *Expect:* the bed footprint is marked and accessible.
2. **Prepare the ground.** Remove rocks and aggressive weeds, then lay overlapping plain cardboard if building over lawn. → *Expect:* the base is flat and weed-suppressed.
3. **Cut or arrange materials.** Use rot-resistant boards, kits, blocks, or untreated cedar; avoid railroad ties or unknown treated wood for food beds. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* saws and drills can injure hands and permanently cut materials, so clamp boards and confirm measurements before cutting. → *Expect:* pieces match the planned bed size.
4. **Assemble the frame.** Fasten corners squarely with exterior screws or brackets, checking diagonals for alignment. → *Expect:* the frame sits solidly without wobbling.
5. **Set the bed level.** Place the frame, shim low spots with soil or gravel, and confirm it does not rock. → *Expect:* water will not pool heavily at one end.
6. **Fill in layers.** Add quality raised-bed mix or compost blended with mineral soil; avoid burying fresh manure or uncomposted woody chunks in the root zone. → *Expect:* soil is loose, moist, and a few inches below the rim.
7. **Water and settle.** Soak the bed, top up low spots, and label the bed if multiple crops or kids will use it. → *Expect:* soil settles evenly and the bed is ready for planting.

## Decision points

- Soil below may be contaminated → use a bottom barrier and imported clean soil, or choose containers.
- Bed is for small children → keep width narrow enough to reach the center without stepping inside.
- Drainage is poor → raise the bed higher and avoid a sealed plastic bottom.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Frame racks out of square:** detect uneven diagonals → loosen screws, square the frame, and refasten.
- **F2 Soil sinks:** detect a large drop after watering → add more mix and water again before planting.
- **F3 Bed dries fast:** detect dry soil below the surface → add compost, mulch, and a consistent watering plan.
- **F4 Unsafe materials used:** detect treated, creosote, or unknown salvaged wood near edibles → replace or line appropriately before planting.

## Verification

The bed is level, stable, filled with appropriate soil below the rim, drains after watering, and can be reached without stepping into the planting area.

## Variations

- `container-bed`: use fabric or metal beds on patios with saucers or drainage protection.
- `mobility-access`: build taller and narrower with firm paths around it.
- `cool-season`: cover with hoops or row cover after assembly.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from saws, drills, splinters, heavy soil bags, and contaminated materials. Wear eye protection and gloves, lift with help, and use materials appropriate for food growing.
