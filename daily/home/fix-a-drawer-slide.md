## Goal

A drawer slide is cleaned, realigned, or reattached so the drawer opens and closes smoothly without falling off track.

## Preconditions

- You have screwdriver, flashlight, vacuum, cloth, replacement screws, and a matching slide if damaged.
- The drawer can be removed from the cabinet.
- Call a cabinet repair pro if the cabinet side is split, the drawer box is broken, or heavy file drawers are involved.

## Steps

1. **Empty the drawer.** Remove contents and set them aside. → *Expect:* the drawer is light enough to handle safely.
2. **Remove the drawer.** Pull out fully and release tabs or lift the front depending on slide type. → *Expect:* the drawer separates from the cabinet tracks.
3. **Clean the slides.** Vacuum debris and wipe tracks, rollers, and drawer sides. → *Expect:* dirt and loose objects are gone.
4. **Inspect hardware.** Check for loose screws, bent rails, broken rollers, or missing stops. → *Expect:* the cause of binding or sagging is identified.
5. **Tighten or replace screws.** Drive loose screws snugly and replace missing screws with matching length screws. → *Expect:* slides are fixed firmly to wood.
6. **Realign the slide.** Loosen screws slightly, square the rail, and retighten. → *Expect:* both slides sit parallel and level.
7. **Reinstall the drawer.** Align rails and push until release tabs or rollers engage. → *Expect:* the drawer travels on both slides.
8. **Test under load.** Reload lightly and open and close several times. → *Expect:* the drawer moves smoothly and stays level.

## Decision points

- Rail is bent or roller broken → replace the slide pair.
- Screws will not hold → repair stripped holes with wood glue and dowels.
- Drawer box is out of square → repair the box before adjusting slides.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Drawer falls off track:** one slide misses engagement → remove and reinstall with both sides aligned.
- **F2 Binding at one point:** rail is misaligned → loosen screws and adjust the high or tight side.
- **F3 Screws poke through:** replacement screws are too long → remove them and use shorter screws immediately.

## Verification

The drawer opens fully, closes flush, stays on both slides, and does not bind after being loaded with normal contents.

## Variations

- Side-mount ball-bearing slides: use release levers to remove the drawer.
- Center wooden slides: clean wax buildup and apply paste wax, not wet oil.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: drawers can drop on feet or pinch fingers. Empty heavy contents first and use screws short enough not to pierce finished surfaces.
