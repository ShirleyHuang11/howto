## Goal

A narrow loose grout line between tiles is cleaned out, refilled, and cured so water and dirt cannot enter the joint.

## Preconditions

- You have matching sanded or unsanded grout, grout saw, vacuum, sponge, margin float or gloved finger, bucket, and clean cloth.
- The tile is firmly bonded and the loose area is limited to grout, not moving tile.
- Call a tile pro if tiles flex, hollow sounds spread, or the loose grout is in a shower with signs of wall damage.

## Steps

1. **Identify the loose line.** Press nearby tiles lightly and mark only the crumbling grout section. → *Expect:* tiles stay fixed and only grout flakes move.
2. **Remove weak grout.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* saw out loose grout to at least half the tile thickness without scratching tile edges. → *Expect:* the joint contains firm edges and no powdery grout.
3. **Clean the joint.** Vacuum dust and wipe with a barely damp sponge. → *Expect:* the joint is open, dust-free, and not wet.
4. **Mix grout.** Mix a small batch to peanut-butter consistency and let it slake if directed. → *Expect:* grout holds on the float without running.
5. **Pack the joint.** Force grout diagonally across the line until the gap is filled. → *Expect:* the joint is full with no visible voids.
6. **Shape the surface.** Wipe diagonally with a damp sponge after the grout firms slightly. → *Expect:* grout is level with neighboring joints and tile faces are mostly clean.
7. **Buff haze.** After haze forms, polish tile faces with a dry cloth. → *Expect:* tiles look clean while grout remains undisturbed.
8. **Cure and protect.** Keep the area dry and unused for the grout maker's cure time. → *Expect:* the repaired line hardens without cracking.

## Decision points

- Joint is wider than the grout type allows → use sanded grout for wider joints or follow the product label.
- Tile moves when pressed → stop; grout will fail until the tile is reset.
- Color match matters in a visible area → test a small cured sample before grouting the full line.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Grout cracks again:** repair opens after curing → check for tile movement and reset the tile before regrouting.
- **F2 Haze remains:** tile looks cloudy → clean with a grout-haze remover approved for the tile.
- **F3 Joint washes out:** sponge removes too much grout → let grout firm longer and repack the low section.

## Verification

After curing, the grout line is hard, flush with adjacent grout, free of cracks, and does not crumble when rubbed with a dry cloth.

## Variations

- Shower joints: use cement grout only where grout belongs; use silicone caulk at plane changes and corners.
- Natural stone: confirm cleaner and grout will not stain or etch the stone.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: grout dust irritates eyes and lungs. Wear eye protection, avoid breathing dust, and keep wet grout off skin.
