## Goal

Establish a biological nitrogen cycle in a new aquarium before adding fish, so ammonia and nitrite can be processed safely.

## Preconditions

- Aquarium, filter, heater if needed, dechlorinator, thermometer, and liquid tests for ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and pH are available.
- The tank is filled with dechlorinated water and the filter can run continuously.
- No fish are in the tank unless this is an emergency fish-in cycle.

## Steps

1. **Set up equipment.** Install substrate, filter media, heater, thermometer, and decor, then fill with dechlorinated water. → *Expect:* the tank runs without leaks and the temperature is stable for the intended species.
2. **Run the filter continuously.** Start the filter and keep media wet and powered. → *Expect:* water circulates through the filter at all times.
3. **Add an ammonia source.** Use a measured aquarium ammonia product or another controlled fishless source; ⚠️ *Irreversible:* do not add live fish to test unsafe water. → *Expect:* the ammonia test shows a readable starting level.
4. **Test every few days.** Record ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate. → *Expect:* ammonia rises first, then nitrite appears, then nitrate appears.
5. **Wait for conversion.** Keep feeding the cycle until ammonia and nitrite can return to zero after an ammonia dose. → *Expect:* tests show zero ammonia, zero nitrite, and some nitrate.
6. **Change water before stocking.** Do a partial water change to lower nitrate and match temperature with dechlorinated water. → *Expect:* nitrate is in a safe range for the species and temperature remains stable.

## Decision points

- If ammonia or nitrite is present → do not add fish yet.
- If pH crashes or readings stall for weeks → check water hardness, expired tests, and ammonia dose.
- If fish are already present → reduce feeding, test daily, use water changes, and seek species-specific aquarium advice.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Chlorine killed bacteria:** cycle stalls after untreated tap water → dechlorinate all new water and reseed with established filter media if available.
- **F2 Overdosed ammonia:** readings are off-scale → perform partial water changes until tests are readable.
- **F3 Filter stopped:** media dried or power was off long enough to harm bacteria → restart testing and treat the tank as partly uncycled.

## Verification

After a controlled ammonia dose, ammonia and nitrite test at zero within the expected interval, nitrate is present but controlled, and the filter has run continuously.

## Variations

- `planted-tank`: fast-growing plants can reduce nitrate but do not replace testing.
- `saltwater`: cycling principles are similar, but salinity and live rock handling add species-specific requirements.
- `fish-in`: use daily tests and water changes because animal welfare is already at risk.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk is animal welfare harm from toxic ammonia or nitrite. Add fish slowly after the cycle, quarantine when possible, and ask an aquatic vet or experienced aquarist about sick fish.
