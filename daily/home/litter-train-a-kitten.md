## Goal

Teach a kitten to use a clean, accessible litter box consistently while preventing accidents and stress.

## Preconditions

- You have a low-sided litter box, unscented kitten-safe litter, scoop, trash bags, and enzyme cleaner.
- The kitten is eating, drinking, and active enough for normal training.
- The box can be placed in a quiet, reachable area away from food and water.

## Steps

1. **Prepare the box.** Fill a low-sided box with 1-2 inches of unscented litter and place it where the kitten can reach it quickly. → *Expect:* the kitten can step in without climbing or slipping.
2. **Limit the starting area.** Keep the kitten in one kitten-proof room with food, water, bed, toys, and the box. → *Expect:* the box is easy to find from anywhere in the room.
3. **Place the kitten after key moments.** Gently set the kitten in the box after waking, eating, playing, and any sniffing or crouching. → *Expect:* the kitten paws or sniffs the litter without fear.
4. **Reward quiet success.** Praise softly or offer a tiny treat after use, then leave the kitten alone to exit. → *Expect:* the kitten associates the box with relief and calm.
5. **Scoop daily.** Remove waste at least once daily and fully refresh litter as needed. → *Expect:* the box smells mild and has clean digging space.
6. **Clean accidents correctly.** Use enzyme cleaner and block access to repeated accident spots until dry. → *Expect:* odor cues are removed rather than masked.
7. **Expand territory gradually.** [BRANCH: reliable use | repeated accidents] add access to more rooms after reliable box use, or return to a smaller area and add another box. → *Expect:* the kitten can still find a nearby box.

## Decision points

- Kitten is very young or tiny → use an extra-low tray with non-clumping litter if recommended by a vet.
- Kitten strains, cries, has diarrhea, or urinates frequently → call a veterinarian promptly.
- Other cats live in the home → provide multiple boxes and slow introductions.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Box avoidance:** detect accidents near but not in the box → lower the entry, change litter texture, and move the box to a quieter spot.
- **F2 Dirty box refusal:** detect sniffing then leaving → scoop more often and avoid strong fragrances.
- **F3 Location confusion:** detect accidents in distant rooms → add temporary boxes and expand space more slowly.
- **F4 Medical issue:** detect blood, straining, or repeated tiny urinations → seek veterinary care.

## Verification

The kitten uses a clean, accessible litter box for urine and stool for at least a full day in its current area, with no repeated accidents in the same spot.

## Variations

- `multi-cat`: use at least one box per cat plus one extra where space allows.
- `small-apartment`: use two small boxes during training, then reduce only after reliability.
- `long-haired-kitten`: keep fur around the rear clean so litter does not stick painfully.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, mostly hygiene and missed illness. Wash hands after scooping, keep waste away from children and dogs, and treat urinary straining as urgent.
