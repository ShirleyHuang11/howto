## Goal

Plant garlic cloves in fall or the proper local season, grow them through dormancy and spring growth, and harvest cured bulbs for storage.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: disease-free seed garlic, compost, balanced organic fertilizer if needed, mulch, trowel, labels, and curing space.
- The bed has full sun, loose well-drained soil, and is not freshly planted after onions, leeks, or garlic if rotation is possible.
- Planting occurs several weeks before the ground freezes in cold climates, or during the local cool season in mild climates.

## Steps

1. **Choose seed garlic.** Buy seed garlic or locally adapted bulbs and keep cloves intact until planting day. → *Expect:* cloves are firm, large, and disease-free.
2. **Prepare the bed.** Loosen soil, remove weeds, and mix in compost without leaving soggy pockets. → *Expect:* the planting area is loose and drains well.
3. **Separate cloves.** Break bulbs into individual cloves and keep the papery skins on. → *Expect:* each clove has an intact base plate.
4. **Plant point up.** Set cloves 2-3 inches deep and 4-6 inches apart, with the pointed end upward. → *Expect:* rows or clusters are evenly spaced.
5. **Mulch after planting.** Cover with straw, shredded leaves, or similar mulch once soil cools. → *Expect:* cloves are insulated and weeds are suppressed.
6. **Water during active growth.** Keep soil evenly moist in spring, then reduce watering as lower leaves begin yellowing. → *Expect:* plants grow strong leaves before bulbing.
7. **Remove hardneck scapes.** Cut curling scapes when they form and use them in the kitchen. → *Expect:* hardneck plants direct more energy to bulbs.
8. **Harvest and cure.** Lift bulbs when several lower leaves brown, then cure in a dry airy shaded place. → *Expect:* wrappers dry and bulbs store without rotting.

## Decision points

- Climate has cold winters → plant in fall after heat passes and before soil freezes.
- Climate is mild → plant during the coolest season using locally adapted varieties.
- Garlic sends scapes → remove them while tender unless saving bulbils.
- Soil stays wet → grow in raised beds or improve drainage before planting.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Cloves rot:** detect missing shoots or mushy cloves → improve drainage and plant disease-free cloves next season.
- **F2 Bulbs stay small:** detect thin stems or early stress → plant larger cloves, feed earlier, and water during spring growth.
- **F3 Weeds overtake bed:** detect weed competition around garlic → hand weed shallowly and maintain mulch.
- **F4 Bulbs split wrappers:** detect overmature bulbs with opened skins → harvest earlier next time and cure promptly.

## Verification

Garlic cloves are planted point-up at proper spacing in well-drained soil, mulched, watered during spring growth, and harvested when lower leaves brown with bulbs cured dry.

## Variations

- `hardneck`: expect scapes and better cold tolerance.
- `softneck`: often stores longer and suits milder climates.
- `container`: use a deep draining pot and keep winter moisture from becoming soggy.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Use disease-free planting stock, avoid imported grocery garlic treated to inhibit sprouting, and keep allium plants away from pets that may chew them.
