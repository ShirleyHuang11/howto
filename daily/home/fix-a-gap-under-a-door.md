## Goal

A gap under a door is reduced with an adjusted threshold, door sweep, or draft seal so light, air, and insects are limited.

## Preconditions

- You have a tape measure, screwdriver, hacksaw or scissors, pencil, drill if needed, and a door sweep or seal.
- The door opens and closes normally before the repair.
- Call a door installer if the slab is warped, the threshold is rotten, or the door frame is out of square.

## Steps

1. **Measure the gap.** Close the door and measure the largest space between door bottom and threshold or floor. → *Expect:* the needed seal height is known.
2. **Check door swing.** Open and close the door while watching the floor clearance. → *Expect:* you know whether a sweep will drag.
3. **Adjust the threshold.** If adjustable, turn threshold screws upward evenly until the gap narrows. → *Expect:* the door closes without rubbing hard.
4. **Size the sweep.** Mark the sweep to door width and trim it to fit. → *Expect:* the sweep spans the door without hitting jambs.
5. **Position the sweep.** Hold it against the closed door so the flexible fin touches the threshold lightly. → *Expect:* no daylight shows under the fin.
6. **Fasten the sweep.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* drill pilot holes only after confirming height and door swing. → *Expect:* screws hold the sweep straight and snug.
7. **Test operation.** Open, close, and latch the door several times. → *Expect:* the sweep seals without blocking the latch.
8. **Check from outside.** Look for daylight or air movement along the bottom edge. → *Expect:* the visible gap is closed.

## Decision points

- Gap is uneven side to side → check hinge sag before adding a thick sweep.
- Door will not latch after sealing → lower the sweep or threshold slightly.
- Threshold is rotten or loose → replace the threshold or call a pro.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Sweep drags:** door becomes hard to move → loosen screws and raise the sweep slightly.
- **F2 Seal leaks at corners:** light remains near jambs → add corner pads or adjust weatherstripping.
- **F3 Screws strip:** sweep loosens → use slightly larger screws or fill holes before reinstalling.

## Verification

With the door latched, no continuous daylight is visible under the door and the door opens and closes without scraping hard.

## Variations

- Exterior doors: use weather-rated sweeps with drip caps where rain exposure is high.
- Interior doors: use a simple draft stopper or brush sweep for sound and air control.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: drilling into metal or fiberglass doors can be permanent. Confirm placement before pilots, and keep fingers clear of the closing door.
