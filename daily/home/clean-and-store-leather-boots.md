## Goal

Leather boots are cleaned, conditioned if appropriate, dried, and stored so the leather keeps its shape.

## Preconditions

- Boots are smooth leather, not suede or nubuck.
- Soft brush, damp cloth, leather cleaner or saddle soap, conditioner, newspaper or boot trees, and breathable storage bag are available.
- Boots are away from direct heat.

## Steps

1. **Remove laces and loose dirt.** Take out laces and brush mud from seams, welt, and sole edges. → *Expect:* dry debris is gone before moisture is added.
2. **Wipe with a damp cloth.** Clean the leather surface using light pressure. → *Expect:* surface grime transfers to the cloth.
3. **Use leather cleaner sparingly.** Apply a small amount to the cloth and work on one panel at a time. → *Expect:* leather looks clean without becoming soaked.
4. **Wipe residue away.** Use a clean damp cloth to remove cleaner from stitching and creases. → *Expect:* no soap film remains.
5. **Dry slowly.** Stuff boots with paper or insert boot trees and air dry at room temperature. → *Expect:* boots hold shape while moisture leaves.
6. **Condition lightly.** Rub a thin layer of leather conditioner into dry leather if it feels dry or stiff. → *Expect:* leather looks even and supple, not greasy.
7. **Buff the surface.** Use a clean cloth or horsehair brush after conditioner absorbs. → *Expect:* excess product is removed and the finish is even.
8. **Store upright.** Keep boots in a cool dry place with trees or paper support. → *Expect:* shafts and toe boxes do not collapse.

## Decision points

- Boots are suede or nubuck → use a suede-specific method instead.
- Salt stains are present → dissolve with a leather-safe vinegar solution before conditioning.
- Leather darkens during spot test → decide whether the color change is acceptable before treating all panels.
- Boots are wet through → dry fully over 24-48 hours before conditioning.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Leather feels sticky:** too much conditioner was applied → buff repeatedly and let absorb overnight.
- **F2 Water spots appear:** cleaning was uneven → wipe the full panel evenly with a barely damp cloth.
- **F3 Cracks remain dry:** leather is neglected → apply small amounts of conditioner over several sessions.
- **F4 Mold smell develops:** boots were stored damp → dry completely, clean, and store with airflow.

## Verification

Boots are dry to the touch, clean at seams and soles, lightly conditioned without residue, and standing in shape in breathable storage.

## Variations

- Waxed leather: use wax-compatible dressing instead of regular conditioner.
- Dress boots: use polish after conditioning if a shine is desired.
- Tall boots: use boot shapers or rolled paper to prevent shaft creases.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Test products in a hidden area, avoid heaters and direct sun, and do not seal damp leather in plastic.
