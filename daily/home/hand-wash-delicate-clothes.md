## Goal

Delicate clothing is cleaned gently, rinsed fully, and dried flat without stretching, felting, or color transfer.

## Preconditions

- Garment care label allows hand washing.
- Clean sink or basin, mild detergent, towels, and a flat drying surface.
- Similar colors are washed separately or colorfastness has been tested.

## Steps

1. **Read the care label.** Check fiber, water temperature, and drying instructions before wetting the garment. → *Expect:* the label does not say dry clean only or do not wash.
2. **Test dye stability.** Dab an inside seam with a wet white cloth and mild detergent. → *Expect:* no visible dye transfers to the cloth.
3. **Fill cool water.** Add cool water to a clean basin and mix in a small amount of mild detergent. → *Expect:* the water feels slippery but has little foam.
4. **Submerge and soak.** Press the garment under water for 5-10 minutes without twisting. → *Expect:* fibers are fully wet and soil begins to loosen.
5. **Agitate gently.** Swish the garment and squeeze sudsy water through stained areas with open hands. → *Expect:* cloudy water or loosened soil appears without fabric distortion.
6. **Drain and support.** Lift the garment from underneath while draining the basin. → *Expect:* the wet fabric is not hanging from one stretched point.
7. **Rinse repeatedly.** Refill with cool clean water and press out suds until the rinse water stays clear. → *Expect:* no slippery detergent remains in the fabric.
8. **Remove water by rolling.** Lay the garment on a towel, roll it up, and press the roll. → *Expect:* the garment is damp rather than dripping.
9. **Dry flat to shape.** Lay it on a dry towel or mesh rack and reshape seams, cuffs, and hem. → *Expect:* the garment dries in its normal dimensions.

## Decision points

- Label says dry clean only → stop and use professional cleaning unless you accept shrinkage or finish damage.
- Dye transfers during testing → wash alone in cold water or use a professional cleaner.
- Wool or cashmere is involved → use wool wash and avoid temperature changes.
- Structured garment has lining, padding, or tailoring → do not soak; spot clean or send out.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Suds remain:** water feels slippery after rinsing → rinse in fresh cool water until clear.
- **F2 Garment stretches:** wet fabric lengthens while lifted → support the full garment and reshape flat.
- **F3 Color bleeds:** rinse water turns strongly colored → drain, rinse cold, and keep away from other items.
- **F4 Fabric feels harsh:** too much detergent remains → rinse again and avoid fabric softener on delicate fibers.

## Verification

The garment is clean-smelling, free of visible suds, reshaped to its original outline, and drying flat without dripping.

## Variations

- Silk: use very brief soaking and a detergent labeled safe for silk.
- Bras and lingerie: fasten hooks and press cups gently without wringing.
- Knitwear: measure body and sleeve length before washing, then reshape to those measurements.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Avoid hot water, wringing, bleach, and mixed-color soaking because they can permanently shrink, distort, or stain delicate clothing.
