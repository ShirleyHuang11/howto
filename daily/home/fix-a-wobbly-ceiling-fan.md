## Goal

A ceiling fan runs with minimal wobble after loose hardware, blade alignment, and balance are corrected.

## Preconditions

- You have a sturdy ladder, screwdriver, tape measure, fan balancing kit, and helper if the fan is high.
- The fan is mounted to a fan-rated ceiling box.
- Call an electrician or installer if the ceiling box moves, wiring is exposed, or the fan mount is not fan-rated.

## Steps

1. **Turn off the fan.** Let the blades stop completely and switch wall power off if tightening near wiring or the canopy. → *Expect:* the fan is still and controls are inactive.
2. **Check the mount.** Gently hold the downrod or motor housing and look for ceiling-box movement. → *Expect:* the mount is solid, or unsafe movement is found.
3. **Tighten visible screws.** Tighten blade bracket screws, blade screws, downrod set screws, and canopy screws. → *Expect:* no hardware turns loosely.
4. **Clean the blades.** Wipe dust from the tops and edges of all blades. → *Expect:* blade weight is not affected by uneven dust.
5. **Measure blade height.** Measure from ceiling to each blade tip at the same point. → *Expect:* blade tips are within about 1/8 inch of each other.
6. **Correct bent brackets.** Gently adjust only a visibly bent bracket or replace a damaged blade. → *Expect:* blade tips sit at similar height.
7. **Use a balance clip.** Run the fan on low, move the kit clip along the wobbliest blade, and find the quietest position. → *Expect:* wobble reduces at one clip location.
8. **Add balance weight.** Stick the matching weight to the blade top at the tested position. → *Expect:* the fan runs smoother without the temporary clip.

## Decision points

- Ceiling box or mounting bracket moves → stop and call a pro; the fan could fall.
- Wobble appears only on high speed → balance blades and confirm the fan is rated for that speed.
- A blade is warped or cracked → replace the blade set rather than bending it.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wobble remains:** fan still rocks after balancing → repeat balance on another blade or replace mismatched blades.
- **F2 Clicking noise:** sound continues after tightening → check pull chains, light shades, and blade brackets.
- **F3 Loose mount:** canopy or box shifts → leave fan off and have the mount repaired professionally.

## Verification

At low and medium speed, the fan runs for five minutes with no loose hardware noise and no visible mount movement at the ceiling.

## Variations

- Flush-mount fans: check housing screws instead of downrod set screws.
- Fans with lights: tighten glass shades and bulbs after power is off and bulbs are cool.

## Safety & privacy

High risk: a poorly mounted fan can fall, and fan wiring can shock. Use a stable ladder, shut off power before canopy work, and call a pro for any moving electrical box.
