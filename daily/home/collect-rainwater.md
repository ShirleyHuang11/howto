## Goal

Collect roof runoff in a rain barrel or cistern for garden watering while managing overflow, mosquito prevention, and local rules.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: rain barrel with lid, screened inlet, spigot, overflow hose, stable blocks or stand, downspout diverter or elbow, and basic hand tools.
- Local regulations and homeowner or rental rules allow rainwater collection.
- The collection site has a firm level base near a downspout and away from electrical hazards.

## Steps

1. **Confirm rules and use.** Check that rainwater collection is allowed and plan to use it for ornamental or soil-level garden watering. → *Expect:* collection is permitted for the intended use.
2. **Choose a safe location.** Place the barrel near a downspout on compact level ground that can hold heavy water weight. → *Expect:* the barrel will not tip or sink.
3. **Build a stable base.** Set concrete blocks, pavers, or a stand level under the barrel. → *Expect:* the spigot is accessible and the barrel sits solidly.
4. **Fit the inlet screen.** Keep all openings covered with fine mesh or a sealed lid. → *Expect:* leaves and mosquitoes cannot enter easily.
5. **Connect the downspout.** Cut or redirect the downspout into the barrel or diverter following the kit instructions. → *Expect:* roof runoff enters the screened inlet.
6. **Set overflow away from the foundation.** Attach an overflow hose that drains to a safe area or existing storm path. → *Expect:* excess water will not pool near the building.
7. **Test with hose water or rain.** Run water into the inlet and watch the spigot, seams, base, and overflow. → *Expect:* water flows in and out without leaks or tipping.
8. **Use and maintain the barrel.** Water at soil level, clean screens, drain before freezing, and empty stagnant water if unused. → *Expect:* stored water remains contained and usable for the garden.

## Decision points

- Roof has treated wood shingles, fresh chemicals, or contamination risk → do not use water on edible plant surfaces.
- Freezing winters occur → drain and disconnect the barrel before hard freezes.
- Mosquitoes appear → repair screens and empty or treat standing water with a product labeled for rain barrels.
- Overflow cannot drain safely → choose another downspout or do not install the barrel there.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Barrel tips or shifts:** detect leaning, sinking, or wobbling → empty it and rebuild a level stronger base.
- **F2 Mosquito larvae appear:** detect wrigglers in water → seal openings and use labeled mosquito dunks if appropriate.
- **F3 Foundation gets wet:** detect pooling near the wall → extend overflow farther away or reconnect downspout.
- **F4 Spigot clogs:** detect slow flow → clean sediment from the barrel and flush the spigot.

## Verification

The rain barrel is legal for the location, sits level on a stable base, has screened openings, directs overflow away from the foundation, and releases water through the spigot.

## Variations

- `apartment-balcony`: use only small containers allowed by building rules and avoid roof runoff modifications.
- `cold-climate`: disconnect and store parts before freezing weather.
- `vegetable-garden`: apply collected water to soil, not directly onto edible leaves or fruit.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from heavy stored water, ladder or cutting work, and contaminated runoff. Keep barrels lidded, protect children from drowning hazards, and follow local collection rules.
