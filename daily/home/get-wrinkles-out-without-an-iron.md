## Goal

Wrinkles are relaxed from clothing using steam, moisture, and tension without using an iron.

## Preconditions

- Garment care label allows moisture or steam.
- Hanger, spray bottle, shower steam or dryer, and clean towel are available.
- Garment is clean enough that heat or moisture will not set stains or odor.

## Steps

1. **Check the fabric.** Read the label and inspect for stains before adding heat or moisture. → *Expect:* fabric can tolerate the selected method.
2. **Choose a method.** Use [BRANCH: shower steam | dryer tumble | spray-and-hang] based on fabric and available time. → *Expect:* one wrinkle-release path is selected.
3. **Hang with tension.** Place the garment on a broad hanger and smooth seams, collar, and placket by hand. → *Expect:* the garment hangs straight without folded sections.
4. **Add controlled moisture.** Steam in a bathroom, tumble with a damp towel, or mist lightly from 8-12 inches away. → *Expect:* wrinkled areas become slightly damp or warm, not soaked.
5. **Smooth by hand.** Pull fabric gently along grain lines and flatten collars, cuffs, and hems. → *Expect:* wrinkles visibly soften while the fabric is warm or damp.
6. **Let it dry undisturbed.** Keep the garment hanging with airflow until fully dry. → *Expect:* fabric holds the smoother shape.
7. **Repeat targeted areas.** Re-mist stubborn wrinkles and smooth again rather than soaking the whole garment. → *Expect:* remaining creases are reduced without water spots.

## Decision points

- Fabric is silk, leather, suede, or structured wool → avoid dryer tumbling and use gentle steam at a distance.
- Garment has a stain → clean the stain first so moisture and heat do not set it.
- Wrinkle is a sharp crease → steaming may reduce it but may not replace pressing.
- Water spots appear → stop misting and let the garment dry, then reassess.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Garment gets too wet:** fabric sags or drips → blot with a towel and dry flat or on a shaped hanger.
- **F2 Wrinkles return:** garment was worn while damp → hang until fully dry before wearing.
- **F3 Dryer shrinks fabric:** heat was too high → stop the cycle and reshape while damp.
- **F4 Water marks form:** minerals or droplets spot the fabric → use distilled water next time and test a hidden area.

## Verification

The garment is fully dry, hangs smoothly from shoulders to hem, and has no visible water spots or heat damage.

## Variations

- Travel: hang the garment in a steamy bathroom while showering, away from direct spray.
- Cotton shirt: tumble on low with a damp towel for 5-10 minutes, then hang immediately.
- Polyester blouse: light misting and hand smoothing often works without dryer heat.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Avoid steam burns, keep garments away from wet shower spray, and do not use high dryer heat on fabrics prone to shrinking.
