## Goal

A dishwasher with standing water is cleared of common filter, sump, air-gap, or drain-hose blockages.

## Preconditions

- You have towels, shallow cup, bucket, gloves, sponge, screwdriver, and the dishwasher manual if available.
- You can access the sink drain, garbage disposal, or air gap connected to the dishwasher.
- Call appliance service or a plumber if wiring is exposed, the pump hums without moving water, or water leaks from under the machine.

## Steps

1. **Stop power at the control.** Cancel the cycle and turn the dishwasher off before reaching inside. → *Expect:* spray arms and pump are inactive.
2. **Remove standing water.** Scoop water into a bucket and sponge the bottom. → *Expect:* the filter and sump area are visible.
3. **Clean the filter.** Remove the lower rack and filter assembly, then rinse debris from the screen. → *Expect:* water can pass through the filter mesh.
4. **Check the sump.** Remove food, glass, labels, or bones from the sump without forcing pump parts. → *Expect:* the sump is clear of visible debris.
5. **Inspect the air gap.** If present, remove the cap and clear buildup from the air-gap body. → *Expect:* the air gap is open and not packed with debris.
6. **Check the disposal inlet.** Confirm the garbage disposal knockout plug is removed if the dishwasher hose was recently connected. → *Expect:* the hose path into the disposal is open.
7. **Run a drain test.** Reassemble the filter, restore normal control power, and start a drain or cancel cycle. → *Expect:* water pumps out through the sink drain path.
8. **Watch for leaks.** Check under the sink and around the dishwasher toe area during the drain. → *Expect:* hoses and connections stay dry.

## Decision points

- Disposal was recently installed → the knockout plug is a likely cause.
- Air gap overflows onto the sink → clear the hose from air gap to disposal or sink tailpiece.
- Pump runs but no water moves → suspect a kinked hose, blocked check valve, or failed pump.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Water returns:** basin refills after draining → clear the sink tailpiece or check valve.
- **F2 Glass in sump:** sharp pieces are visible → wear gloves and remove pieces with pliers.
- **F3 Leak appears:** water drips under sink or machine → stop the cycle and tighten or replace the leaking connection.

## Verification

After a drain cycle, the dishwasher tub has no standing water above the sump screen and all visible drain connections remain dry.

## Variations

- High-loop systems: make sure the drain hose rises under the counter before entering the sink drain.
- Air-gap systems: clean both the cap and the hose leaving the air gap.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk: dirty water, sharp debris, and leaks can cause injury or damage. Keep hands away from pump openings, avoid exposed wiring, and call a pro for leaks under the appliance.
