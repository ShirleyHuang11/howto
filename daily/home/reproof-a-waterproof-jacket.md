## Goal

A waterproof jacket's durable water repellent finish is renewed so water beads on the outer fabric again.

## Preconditions

- Jacket care label allows washing and reproofing.
- Technical wash and spray-on or wash-in DWR treatment are available.
- Washing machine, dryer or iron if label allows heat activation, and ventilation are available.

## Steps

1. **Clean detergent residue.** Rinse the washer detergent drawer or choose a clean basin. → *Expect:* no regular detergent or softener residue remains.
2. **Close hardware.** Zip zippers, close hook-and-loop tabs, and loosen drawcords. → *Expect:* trims are secured and fabric can wash evenly.
3. **Wash with technical cleaner.** Use the product dose and label-safe cycle. → *Expect:* dirt and body oils are removed without fabric softener.
4. **Rinse thoroughly.** Run an extra rinse if the cleaner label or jacket label recommends it. → *Expect:* no suds remain in the jacket.
5. **Apply DWR.** Use [BRANCH: spray-on | wash-in] according to the product type, coating the outer face evenly. → *Expect:* treatment reaches the shell fabric without puddles.
6. **Remove excess.** Wipe drips, especially on dark panels and inside linings. → *Expect:* the jacket surface is evenly damp, not streaked.
7. **Heat activate if allowed.** Tumble dry low or press with a towel barrier as directed. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* excess heat can delaminate seams or melt trim; confirm the care label first. → *Expect:* the finish cures without scorching or peeling.
8. **Cool and test beading.** Sprinkle water on shoulders and sleeves. → *Expect:* water beads and rolls instead of soaking in immediately.

## Decision points

- Jacket has down insulation → use down-safe wash guidance and dry fully with low heat.
- Membrane is peeling or seam tape lifts → reproofing will not repair waterproof structure.
- Label forbids tumble drying → air dry or use only the treatment's no-heat method.
- Spray-on treatment is used → protect floors and avoid inhaling mist.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Water still wets out:** oils or detergent remain → wash again with technical cleaner and reapply DWR.
- **F2 Streaky finish:** excess product dried unevenly → wipe damp, redistribute, and dry again.
- **F3 Breathability drops:** wash-in coated a wicking liner → wash once with technical cleaner and use spray-on next time.
- **F4 Seam tape lifts:** heat or age damaged adhesive → stop heat and repair or replace the jacket.

## Verification

After drying, water sprinkled on high-wear outer panels beads for at least 30 seconds without darkening the fabric.

## Variations

- Softshell: use a softshell-specific treatment and lower heat.
- Rain shell with absorbent liner: prefer spray-on DWR to avoid coating the inside.
- Hand wash: clean and rinse in a tub, then apply spray-on treatment while damp.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from hot appliances, chemical sprays, and permanent jacket damage. Ventilate spray products and follow garment heat limits exactly.
