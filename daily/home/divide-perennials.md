## Goal

Divide overcrowded perennial plants into healthy sections that can be replanted at the right depth with reduced transplant stress.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: sharp spade or garden fork, knife or pruning saw for tough crowns, compost, water, mulch, gloves, and labels.
- The plant is suitable for division and is not in full bloom unless rescue is necessary.
- Soil is moist enough to dig without crumbling roots dry or compacting wet clay.

## Steps

1. **Choose the right season.** Divide spring bloomers after flowering or in fall, and divide late bloomers in spring when possible. → *Expect:* the plant is not stressed by peak bloom or heat.
2. **Water the plant.** Water the day before if soil is dry. → *Expect:* roots are hydrated and soil releases more cleanly.
3. **Cut back excess foliage.** Trim tall stems by about one-third if they will wilt or get in the way. → *Expect:* the plant is easier to handle.
4. **Lift the clump.** Dig around the drip line and pry from several sides to keep roots intact. → *Expect:* the clump comes up with a usable root mass.
5. **Separate healthy divisions.** Pull, slice, or saw the clump into sections with roots and several growing points. → *Expect:* each division can grow independently.
6. **Discard weak centers.** Remove dead, woody, diseased, or hollow center material. → *Expect:* only vigorous outer growth is replanted.
7. **Replant promptly.** Set divisions at the original soil depth with compost-enriched backfill and firm gently. → *Expect:* crowns are level with the surrounding soil.
8. **Water and mulch.** Soak the new plantings and mulch around, not over, crowns. → *Expect:* soil settles around roots and moisture is conserved.

## Decision points

- Plant has a taproot or dislikes division → propagate by seed or cuttings instead.
- Weather is hot or windy → delay or provide temporary shade and extra watering.
- Clump is too tough by hand → use two forks back-to-back or a clean saw.
- You have extra divisions → pot them temporarily or give them away before roots dry.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Division wilts severely:** detect limp foliage after replanting → shade temporarily and keep soil evenly moist.
- **F2 Crown planted too deep:** detect crown buried under soil or mulch → lift and reset at original depth.
- **F3 Roots dry out:** detect brittle exposed roots during work → soak briefly and replant immediately.
- **F4 Plant does not regrow:** detect no new growth after the normal recovery period → check crown viability and replace with a healthy division.

## Verification

Each replanted division has roots, growing points, original planting depth, firm moist soil contact, and mulch that does not cover the crown.

## Variations

- `ornamental-grass`: divide in spring and use a saw or sharp spade for dense crowns.
- `hosta`: divide in spring or fall, keeping several eyes per piece.
- `iris`: divide rhizomes shallowly after bloom and discard soft rhizomes.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Wear gloves, use sharp tools away from hands and feet, and lift large wet clumps with help to avoid strain.
