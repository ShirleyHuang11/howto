## Goal

Cut grass to an even, healthy height while keeping people, pets, windows, and the mower safe.

## Preconditions

- Grass is dry enough that your shoes do not leave wet tracks.
- You have a mower with fuel or charge, hearing/eye protection, closed-toe shoes, and a clear yard.
- Children and pets can stay indoors or behind a closed gate during mowing.

## Steps

1. **Walk the yard first.** Pick up sticks, toys, hoses, rocks, dog waste, and fallen fruit that could become projectiles. → *Expect:* the mower path is clear from driveway edge to fence line.
2. **Check the mower.** Set the deck to remove no more than one-third of the blade height, confirm the bag or chute is attached, and inspect the blade area with the mower off. → *Expect:* the mower is intact, stable, and set for the season's grass height.
3. **Protect the work area.** Close windows near the lawn, move cars if gravel is nearby, and keep kids and pets inside. → *Expect:* no bystanders or breakable targets are beside the cutting path.
4. **Start on the perimeter.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* power mower blades can amputate or throw debris, so keep hands and feet away from the deck and stop the blade before clearing jams. → *Expect:* one safe border pass gives room to turn.
5. **Mow in straight overlapping lanes.** Walk at a steady pace, overlap each pass by a few inches, and alternate direction from the last mow when possible. → *Expect:* grass is cut evenly without scalped stripes.
6. **Handle slopes across, not up and down.** [BRANCH: push mower | riding mower] push across gentle slopes; ride up and down only if the manual allows. → *Expect:* wheels stay planted and you do not fight the mower's weight.
7. **Finish edges and cleanup.** Empty clippings into compost or yard waste, blow or sweep clippings off sidewalks, and store the mower cool and dry. → *Expect:* hard surfaces are clear and the lawn has no heavy clumps.

## Decision points

- Grass is wet or thunderstorms are near → postpone; wet mowing ruts soil and increases slipping.
- Grass is very tall → raise the deck, cut once, then cut lower a few days later.
- You hit a rock, root, or metal object → stop, disconnect power or spark plug, and inspect before restarting.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Mower stalls:** detect sudden stop or bogging → release the handle, disconnect power, clear clumped grass with a stick or scraper, then restart at a higher deck setting.
- **F2 Scalped patches:** detect pale or shaved soil spots → raise the deck and avoid tight turns over uneven ground.
- **F3 Clipping clumps:** detect wet piles behind the mower → slow down, mow when drier, or rake piles so they do not smother grass.
- **F4 Debris strike:** detect a loud hit or thrown object → stop mowing, check for damage or injury, and remove remaining debris before continuing.

## Verification

The lawn is uniformly cut, hard surfaces are swept, the mower is off and stored, and no toys, stones, or clippings remain in walkways.

## Variations

- `mulching-mower`: leave fine dry clippings on the lawn, but bag heavy spring growth.
- `hot-weather`: mow higher and later in the day to reduce turf stress.
- `new-lawn`: wait until grass is established and tall enough for the first light cut.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from blades, projectiles, noise, heat, and fuel. Wear eye and hearing protection, never mow with children nearby, and do not reach under a mower until power is disconnected and the blade is stopped.
