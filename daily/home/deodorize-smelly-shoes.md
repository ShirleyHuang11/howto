## Goal

Shoes are dried, aired, and treated so odor is reduced without damaging the uppers or insoles.

## Preconditions

- Shoes are not wet with hazardous substances.
- Baking soda, newspaper, clean socks or sachets, mild disinfecting spray safe for shoes, and airflow are available.
- Removable insoles can be taken out if present.

## Steps

1. **Remove insoles and laces.** Open the shoes fully so air can reach the inside. → *Expect:* the interior is exposed.
2. **Dry the shoes.** Stuff with newspaper or place near airflow away from heat. → *Expect:* interiors feel dry rather than clammy.
3. **Brush out debris.** Shake out grit, lint, and loose sock fibers. → *Expect:* no loose material remains under the toes or heel.
4. **Apply odor absorber.** Put baking soda in sachets or thin socks and place one inside each shoe overnight. → *Expect:* powder stays contained while absorbing odor.
5. **Air out.** Remove sachets and let shoes ventilate for several hours. → *Expect:* stale smell is noticeably reduced.
6. **Treat insoles.** Wipe washable insoles with mild soap or spray with shoe-safe disinfectant and dry fully. → *Expect:* insoles smell cleaner and are dry before reinsertion.
7. **Restart with dry socks.** Wear clean moisture-wicking socks and rotate shoes between wears. → *Expect:* odor does not immediately return after one use.

## Decision points

- Shoes are machine washable → wash only if the label permits it.
- Leather or suede shoes smell → avoid soaking and use removable sachets plus airflow.
- Odor is sour and persistent → replace insoles and consider foot hygiene or medical causes.
- Mold is visible → isolate the shoes and clean or discard based on material and severity.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Powder residue remains:** loose baking soda spilled inside → vacuum thoroughly before wearing.
- **F2 Odor returns quickly:** shoes are not drying between wears → rotate pairs and use breathable storage.
- **F3 Material stains:** spray or powder reacted with lining → stop product use and clean with a damp cloth if safe.
- **F4 Insoles stay damp:** drying was incomplete → remove them again and dry longer before wearing.

## Verification

After overnight treatment and airing, the shoes and insoles are dry, have no loose powder inside, and have no strong odor when smelled at the opening.

## Variations

- Athletic shoes: wash removable insoles separately if allowed.
- Boots: use longer newspaper stuffing and replace it when damp.
- Travel: use cedar sachets or activated charcoal bags instead of loose powder.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Do not mix cleaning chemicals, do not apply high heat, and keep powders contained so they do not irritate skin or lungs.
