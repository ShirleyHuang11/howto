## Goal

Remove leaves, grit, and blockages from gutters and downspouts so roof water drains away from the house.

## Preconditions

- You have a stable ladder, gloves, eye protection, bucket or tarp, hose, and a spotter if possible.
- Weather is dry, calm, and daylight.
- You can avoid overhead electrical lines and unsafe roof edges.

## Steps

1. **Inspect from the ground.** Look for sagging gutters, wasp nests, power lines, and downspout outlets before setting the ladder. → *Expect:* hazards and work zones are identified.
2. **Set the ladder safely.** Place it on firm level ground at a proper angle, extending above the gutter if using an extension ladder. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* ladder falls can cause serious injury, so move the ladder often instead of overreaching. → *Expect:* the ladder does not shift when weighted.
3. **Scoop debris by hand or tool.** Wear gloves and remove leaves, seed pods, roof grit, and twigs into a bucket or tarp. → *Expect:* the gutter channel is visible for several feet at a time.
4. **Flush toward the downspout.** Use a hose to rinse remaining dirt and watch water flow. → *Expect:* water moves freely toward the outlet.
5. **Clear downspout clogs.** [BRANCH: water drains | water backs up] if water backs up, flush from the bottom or use a plumber's snake gently. → *Expect:* water exits at the downspout base.
6. **Check drainage away from the foundation.** Reattach elbows or extensions so water discharges several feet away from the house. → *Expect:* runoff points away from basement walls, paths, and garden beds.
7. **Clean the work area.** Bag debris, rinse siding splashes, and store the ladder flat or secured. → *Expect:* walkways are not slippery and tools are put away.

## Decision points

- Roof is steep, high, wet, or near power lines → hire a professional.
- Gutter is pulling away or leaking at seams → clean first, then schedule repair.
- Birds, squirrels, or stinging insects are nesting → stop and handle wildlife or pest issues appropriately.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Ladder feels unstable:** detect wobble or sinking feet → climb down, reset on firm level ground, and use a stabilizer or helper.
- **F2 Downspout remains blocked:** detect standing water after flushing → disconnect the lower elbow and clear the clog from below.
- **F3 Gutter overflows after cleaning:** detect water spilling during hose test → check slope, hidden debris, or undersized downspouts.
- **F4 Debris stains siding:** detect muddy streaks → rinse promptly before it dries.

## Verification

Water poured or hosed into each gutter flows to the downspout and away from the foundation, with no standing debris, visible overflow, or unsafe debris left on walkways.

## Variations

- `one-story`: a gutter scoop and short ladder may be sufficient if footing is stable.
- `gutter-guards`: lift or brush guards according to the product rather than forcing them.
- `autumn-cleanup`: clean after most leaves fall and again after heavy seed drop if trees overhang the roof.

## Safety & privacy

High risk from ladders, roof edges, electrical lines, sharp metal, moldy debris, and insects. Do not work alone on high ladders if avoidable, keep three points of contact, and hire help for unsafe height or access.
