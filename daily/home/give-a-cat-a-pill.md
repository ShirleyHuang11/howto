## Goal

Give a cat one prescribed pill safely and confirm it was swallowed, with minimal fear and no dosing mistake.

## Preconditions

- The medication, dose, timing, and whether food is allowed have been confirmed from the label or vet.
- A pill pocket, small food portion, pill giver, towel, and water or wet food are available.
- The cat is alert enough to swallow and is not vomiting repeatedly, choking, or struggling to breathe.

## Steps

1. **Verify the dose.** Read the label, cat name, tablet count, and timing before opening the medication. → *Expect:* the pill in your hand matches the prescription.
2. **Try the food method first if allowed.** Hide the pill in a tiny treat or wet food bite before the full meal. → *Expect:* the cat eats the medicated bite completely or refuses it clearly.
3. **Set up gentle restraint.** If needed, place the cat on a stable surface or wrap the body in a towel with the head free. → *Expect:* the cat is contained without chest compression or panic.
4. **Place the pill behind the tongue.** Tilt the head slightly up, open the mouth from the corners, place the pill far back, and close the mouth. → *Expect:* the cat licks the nose or swallows.
5. **Follow with food or water.** Offer wet food, a treat, or the vet-recommended water chaser. → *Expect:* the pill moves down and the cat does not spit it out.
6. **Check the area.** Look on the floor, fur, towel, and food bowl for the pill. → *Expect:* no pill is found and the dose can be recorded.

## Decision points

- If the medication cannot be crushed, split, or given with food → follow the label exactly or ask the vet for alternatives.
- If the cat bites, foams, vomits, or repeatedly spits the pill → ask the vet about liquid, transdermal, compounded, or injectable options.
- If a dose may have been missed or doubled → call the prescribing vet before guessing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Pill spit out:** the pill appears on the floor or in fur → give the same pill again only if it is intact and the dose is not duplicated.
- **F2 Cat aspirates or chokes:** coughing, gagging, blue gums, or trouble breathing appears → seek emergency veterinary care.
- **F3 Wrong dose:** the wrong pill or amount was given → call the vet or poison hotline with the drug name, strength, time, and cat weight.

## Verification

The prescribed dose is no longer visible, the cat has swallowed and is breathing normally, and the dose time is recorded.

## Variations

- `pill-pocket`: give an empty treat, then the medicated treat, then another empty treat.
- `multi-cat`: separate cats until the medicated bite is consumed by the intended cat.
- `bitter-pill`: ask the vet whether a capsule or compounded form is safer.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk includes wrong dosing, choking, and bites. Store medications away from pets and children, and contact a vet for dosing uncertainty, severe side effects, or any breathing problem.
