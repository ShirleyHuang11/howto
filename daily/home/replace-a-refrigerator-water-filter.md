## Goal

The refrigerator water filter is replaced with a compatible cartridge, flushed, and reset so dispenser water tastes normal.

## Preconditions

- You have the exact replacement filter model, towel, cup or pitcher, and the refrigerator manual if available.
- The refrigerator is powered and the water dispenser or ice maker is accessible.
- Call appliance service if the filter housing is cracked, the cartridge will not lock, or water leaks behind the refrigerator.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the filter model.** Match the cartridge number to the refrigerator label or manual. → *Expect:* the new cartridge shape and part number match the old one.
2. **Find the filter housing.** Open the grille, upper compartment, or interior filter door. → *Expect:* the old cartridge and release mechanism are visible.
3. **Protect the area.** Place a towel below the housing and remove nearby food if needed. → *Expect:* minor drips will land on the towel.
4. **Remove the old filter.** Press, twist, or pull according to the housing design. → *Expect:* the cartridge releases without forcing the plastic.
5. **Prepare the new filter.** Remove caps, keep seals clean, and align arrows or tabs. → *Expect:* the cartridge is ready to insert without exposed packaging.
6. **Install the new filter.** Push or twist until it seats and locks. → *Expect:* the filter sits flush and cannot be pulled out lightly.
7. **Flush the filter.** Dispense the manual's recommended amount of water, usually several quarts, discarding cloudy first water. → *Expect:* flow becomes steady and water runs clear.
8. **Reset the indicator.** Hold the filter-reset button or menu command until the light changes. → *Expect:* the filter status returns to normal or full.

## Decision points

- New filter will not insert → recheck model number and tab alignment before applying force.
- Flow is weak after replacement → flush longer and check that the cartridge is fully locked.
- Water leaks from the housing → remove and reseat the filter once; call service if leaking continues.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong cartridge:** filter stops halfway or will not latch → exchange it for the exact model.
- **F2 Air spurts:** dispenser spits after installation → continue flushing in short bursts until air clears.
- **F3 Indicator stays red:** reset command fails → repeat the model-specific reset sequence from the manual.

## Verification

The filter is locked in place, no water drips from the housing, dispenser flow is steady, and the filter indicator is reset.

## Variations

- Push-button filters: press the release button while supporting the cartridge.
- Twist filters: turn only to the marked stop to avoid cracking the housing.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: spilled water can damage flooring or food. Keep packaging off the filter seals, wipe drips promptly, and use only a compatible cartridge.
