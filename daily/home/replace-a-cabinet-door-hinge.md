## Goal

A damaged cabinet door hinge is replaced and the door hangs square, closes cleanly, and matches adjacent doors.

## Preconditions

- You have a matching hinge, screwdriver or drill driver, pencil, tape, and helper or support block for heavy doors.
- The cabinet frame and door screw holes are not split beyond repair.
- Call a cabinet installer if the door is warped, the frame is cracked, or concealed hinge boring is damaged.

## Steps

1. **Match the hinge.** Compare overlay, opening angle, cup size, and screw pattern before removal. → *Expect:* the replacement matches the old hinge geometry.
2. **Support the door.** Tape the door closed lightly or hold it with a helper while removing screws. → *Expect:* the door cannot fall when the hinge releases.
3. **Remove the bad hinge.** Unscrew the hinge from the frame and door. → *Expect:* the old hinge comes off without tearing the wood.
4. **Inspect holes.** Check whether screw holes are firm or stripped. → *Expect:* screws can bite, or stripped holes are identified.
5. **Repair stripped holes.** Fill loose holes with wood glue and toothpicks or dowels, then trim flush after set. → *Expect:* the screw holes accept screws tightly.
6. **Install the new hinge.** Fasten the hinge to the door first, then to the cabinet frame or plate. → *Expect:* the hinge sits flat and screws are snug.
7. **Adjust alignment.** Use hinge adjustment screws or loosen and shift the hinge until gaps are even. → *Expect:* the door lines up with neighboring doors.
8. **Test swing and close.** Open and close the door fully several times. → *Expect:* the door clears the frame and stays closed.

## Decision points

- Replacement hinge does not match overlay → stop and get the correct hinge.
- Screw holes are badly blown out → use a wood plug repair or a hinge repair plate.
- Door still sags after hinge replacement → check the other hinge and frame attachment.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Door binds:** edge rubs cabinet → adjust hinge depth or side screw until the reveal is even.
- **F2 Screws spin:** wood is stripped → plug the hole and redrive after glue sets.
- **F3 Soft-close fails:** damper does not engage → confirm hinge type and activation switch.

## Verification

The cabinet door opens fully, closes without rubbing, maintains an even reveal, and the hinge screws remain tight after ten cycles.

## Variations

- Face-frame cabinets: hinge plates may mount to the frame edge.
- Frameless cabinets: concealed hinges usually adjust in three directions with small screws.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk: cabinet doors can fall and chip counters or fingers. Support the door during removal and keep drill torque low to avoid stripping screws.
