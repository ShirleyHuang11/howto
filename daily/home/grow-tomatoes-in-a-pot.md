## Goal

Grow a tomato plant in a draining container with enough soil, support, sun, water, and feeding to produce healthy fruit.

## Preconditions

- Supplies: tomato seedling, 5 gallon or larger pot with drainage, potting mix, tomato cage or stake, slow-release or liquid vegetable fertilizer, mulch, and water.
- The site gets 6-8 hours of direct sun during the growing season.
- Frost danger has passed or the pot can be moved under protection.

## Steps

1. **Choose the right tomato.** Select a healthy determinate or patio tomato for compact spaces, or a larger indeterminate only if you can support it well. → *Expect:* the plant has sturdy green growth and no obvious pests.
2. **Prepare the container.** Use a clean pot at least 12-18 inches wide with open drainage holes and fill partway with fresh potting mix. → *Expect:* water can leave the pot and the mix is loose.
3. **Plant deeply.** Remove the lowest leaves and bury the stem so only the top leaf clusters remain above the mix. → *Expect:* the seedling sits upright with buried stem ready to root.
4. **Add support early.** Install a cage, stake, or small trellis before roots spread. → *Expect:* the support is firm and does not wobble in the pot.
5. **Water thoroughly.** Soak the mix until water drains from the bottom, then let the top inch dry before watering again. → *Expect:* the whole root zone is moist without standing water.
6. **Mulch the surface.** Add straw, shredded leaves, or compost mulch while keeping it slightly away from the stem. → *Expect:* soil surface is shaded and the stem base remains clear.
7. **Feed on schedule.** Apply tomato fertilizer after planting according to the label, reducing nitrogen if the plant grows leaves but few flowers. → *Expect:* growth is steady and flower clusters form.
8. **Monitor weekly.** Tie loose stems, remove yellow lower leaves, check leaf undersides, and harvest ripe fruit promptly. → *Expect:* the plant stays supported, ventilated, and productive.

## Decision points

- Nights are below 50 F → delay planting outdoors or move the pot to a protected warm spot.
- Plant is a determinate type → prune lightly and keep most branches for one main harvest.
- Plant is indeterminate → provide taller support and remove some suckers for airflow.
- Container dries out daily → use a larger pot, add mulch, or water once in the morning and again during heat.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Blossom end rot:** detect dark sunken patches on fruit bottoms → keep moisture even and avoid damaging roots; new fruit should improve.
- **F2 Leaves yellow from the bottom:** detect lower leaves fading or spotting → remove affected leaves and water at soil level.
- **F3 Plant topples:** detect leaning cage or loose root ball → add a heavier pot, deeper stake, or tie support to a railing.
- **F4 Few flowers set fruit:** detect flowers dropping in heat or cold → wait for moderate weather and keep watering consistent.

## Verification

The tomato is in a draining pot of adequate size, receives direct sun, is supported before heavy growth, has evenly moist soil, and produces flowers or fruit without collapse.

## Variations

- `balcony`: secure the pot against wind and use saucers that do not hold standing water.
- `hot-summer`: provide afternoon shade during heat waves above 90 F.
- `short-season`: choose early determinate varieties and start with a larger transplant.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Lift large pots with help, wash hands after handling soil or fertilizer, and keep fertilizers away from children, pets, and edible leaves.
