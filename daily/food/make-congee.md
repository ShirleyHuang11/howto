## Goal

Rice is simmered with plenty of liquid until it breaks down into a soft, spoonable porridge.

## Preconditions

- Rice, water or stock, heavy pot, spoon, salt, and optional toppings.
- If using meat, seafood, or egg, have a thermometer or clear doneness cue.

## Steps

1. **Rinse rice.** Rinse 1 cup rice until water is less cloudy. → *Expect:* loose surface starch is washed off but grains remain intact.
2. **Add liquid.** Combine rice with 8-10 cups water or stock in a heavy pot. → *Expect:* rice is submerged under a large volume of liquid.
3. **Bring to a boil.** Heat uncovered, stirring so grains do not stick. → *Expect:* steady bubbles rise and foam may collect on top.
4. **Lower to a simmer.** Reduce heat and partially cover the pot. → *Expect:* gentle bubbling without boilover.
5. **Stir occasionally.** Cook 60-90 minutes, scraping the bottom every 10-15 minutes. → *Expect:* grains split and porridge thickens from watery to creamy.
6. **Adjust texture.** [BRANCH: too thick: add hot water | too thin: simmer uncovered] → *Expect:* congee flows slowly from a spoon.
7. **Cook additions safely.** Add cooked toppings at the end, or simmer raw chicken pieces to 74 °C and shrimp until opaque. → *Expect:* proteins are fully cooked with no translucent raw centers.
8. **Season and serve.** Add salt, soy sauce, ginger, scallion, or sesame oil to taste. → *Expect:* porridge tastes savory and smooth, with toppings hot.

## Decision points

- Want silky texture faster → use broken rice or cooked leftover rice.
- Using stock → taste before salting because stock may already be salty.
- Serving later → cool in shallow containers and reheat with extra water.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Scorched bottom:** detect burnt smell or brown stuck layer → stop stirring the bottom, transfer clean congee to another pot, and thin with hot water.
- **F2 Boilover:** detect foam climbing the pot → uncover, lower heat, and use a larger pot next time.
- **F3 Gluey paste:** detect texture too stiff to spoon → whisk in hot water or stock until loose.
- **F4 Undercooked protein:** detect pink chicken or translucent seafood → continue simmering until safe internal temperature or opacity is reached.

## Verification

Congee is creamy, rice grains are mostly broken, no burnt flavor is present, and any meat or seafood is fully cooked.

## Variations

- `leftover-rice`: use 2 cups cooked rice with 5-6 cups liquid and simmer 25-35 minutes.
- `century-egg-pork`: add cooked shredded pork and diced century egg near the end.
- `plain-sick-day`: use water, ginger, and salt only.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from hot porridge burns and cooked rice storage. Stir carefully, keep children away from bubbling pots, cook proteins fully, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
