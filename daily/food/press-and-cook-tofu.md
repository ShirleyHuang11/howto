## Goal

Firm tofu is pressed, seasoned, and cooked until browned outside and tender inside.

## Preconditions

- Firm or extra-firm tofu, towels, plate, weight, knife, skillet, oil, seasoning or sauce, and spatula.
- Clean surface for handling ready-to-eat soy product.

## Steps

1. **Drain tofu.** Open package and pour off liquid. → *Expect:* tofu block is intact and no longer sitting in packing water.
2. **Press tofu.** Wrap in towels, place between plates, and add a moderate weight for 15-30 minutes. → *Expect:* towels become wet and tofu feels firmer.
3. **Cut evenly.** Slice into cubes, slabs, or triangles of similar thickness. → *Expect:* pieces will cook at the same rate.
4. **Season or coat.** Toss with salt and cornstarch, or marinate after pressing and pat dry. → *Expect:* surfaces are seasoned and not dripping.
5. **Heat the pan.** Add oil to a skillet over medium-high heat. → *Expect:* oil shimmers before tofu goes in.
6. **Brown without crowding.** Add tofu in one layer and cook 2-4 minutes per side. → *Expect:* golden crust forms before pieces release from the pan.
7. **Sauce at the end.** Add sauce after browning and toss briefly. [BRANCH: dry-crisp | glazed] → *Expect:* sauce clings without washing off the crust.
8. **Serve hot.** Move tofu to the dish before crust steams soft. → *Expect:* exterior is crisp or glazed and center is tender.

## Decision points

- Tofu is silken → do not press hard; use gentle steaming, soup, or cold preparation.
- Pan sticks badly → wait longer before turning and add a little more oil.
- Want stronger flavor → press first, marinate, then dry before searing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Crumbling tofu:** detect pieces breaking while turning → use firmer tofu and a thinner spatula, or convert to scramble.
- **F2 Pale tofu:** detect no browning after several minutes → increase heat, reduce crowding, and pat pieces drier.
- **F3 Burnt sauce:** detect sticky black glaze → add sauce later next time and wipe pan before continuing.
- **F4 Bland center:** detect seasoned crust only → marinate after pressing or cut smaller pieces.

## Verification

Tofu pieces are evenly browned or glazed, hot through the center, not watery on the plate, and seasoned to taste.

## Variations

- `baked`: bake oiled, coated tofu at 220 °C until browned.
- `air-fryer`: cook coated tofu in a single layer until crisp.
- `scramble`: crumble pressed tofu and cook with spices instead of cubing.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk from hot oil splatter and soy allergens. Pat tofu dry before frying, disclose soy to diners, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
