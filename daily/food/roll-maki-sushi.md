## Goal

Seasoned sushi rice, nori, and fillings are rolled into tight maki and sliced cleanly.

## Preconditions

- Prepared sushi rice, nori sheets, fillings cut into strips, bamboo mat, plastic wrap if desired, sharp knife, and water for hands.
- Fish for raw use must be purchased and handled for raw consumption; otherwise use cooked fillings.

## Steps

1. **Set up the mat.** Place a nori sheet shiny side down on the bamboo mat. → *Expect:* nori lines run horizontally and the rough side faces up.
2. **Wet hands lightly.** Dip fingertips in water and shake off excess. → *Expect:* rice sticks less to hands without making nori soggy.
3. **Spread rice.** Press a thin even layer over nori, leaving 2 cm bare at the far edge. → *Expect:* rice covers corners but individual grains remain visible.
4. **Add fillings.** Lay filling strips in a narrow line across the lower third. → *Expect:* filling is centered and not piled taller than about 2 cm.
5. **Roll the first tuck.** Lift the near edge of the mat over the filling and tuck nori around it. → *Expect:* filling is enclosed in a tight cylinder.
6. **Finish the roll.** Pull the mat forward and roll to the bare edge, moistening that edge if needed. → *Expect:* seam seals and roll feels firm, not loose.
7. **Slice cleanly.** Wet a sharp knife, trim ends, and cut into 6-8 pieces. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* a dull knife crushes the roll; sharpen or switch knives before cutting. → *Expect:* pieces hold round shape with visible filling centers.
8. **Serve promptly.** Arrange cut sides up with soy sauce, wasabi, or pickled ginger. → *Expect:* nori is still crisp-tender and rice is not dried out.

## Decision points

- Roll is overfilled → remove filling before the first tuck; tight rolls need less filling than expected.
- Making inside-out rolls → cover mat in plastic, spread rice first, flip, then fill the nori side.
- Serving vulnerable guests → use cooked vegetables, egg, tofu, or cooked seafood instead of raw fish.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Loose roll:** detect gaps or falling fillings → compress gently with the mat and use less filling next roll.
- **F2 Soggy nori:** detect tearing or limp sheets → use drier hands and serve sooner.
- **F3 Rice mashed flat:** detect paste-like rice layer → spread with damp fingertips using light pressure.
- **F4 Food-safety lapse:** detect raw fish left warm or touched by dirty board → discard affected fish and sanitize surfaces.

## Verification

Each maki piece holds together when lifted, has an even rice layer, centered filling, sealed seam, and safe fillings served promptly.

## Variations

- `hosomaki`: use half sheets and one filling for thin rolls.
- `futomaki`: use a full sheet and multiple fillings, keeping the filling line compact.
- `vegetarian`: use cucumber, avocado, pickled radish, carrot, tofu, or cooked mushroom.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from raw seafood and rice holding. Keep seafood cold, separate raw fish tools from ready-to-eat items, wash hands often, and limit room-temperature rice holding.
