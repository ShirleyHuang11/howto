## Goal

A balanced stir-fry sauce is mixed with salt, sweetness, acidity, aromatics, and starch so it coats food without becoming watery.

## Preconditions

- Soy sauce or other salty base, sugar, vinegar or wine, stock or water, cornstarch, aromatics, bowl, and spoon.
- Ingredients to stir-fry are cut and ready before cooking.

## Steps

1. **Choose a salty base.** Add 3 tbsp soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, or tamari to a bowl. → *Expect:* sauce base tastes clearly salty.
2. **Add liquid.** Stir in 3 tbsp water or stock. → *Expect:* saltiness is diluted enough to coat a full pan of food.
3. **Balance with sugar and acid.** Add 1-2 tsp sugar and 1 tbsp rice vinegar, Shaoxing wine, or citrus juice. → *Expect:* sauce tastes salty first, then slightly sweet and bright.
4. **Add aromatics.** Stir in minced garlic, ginger, chile, sesame oil, or white pepper as desired. → *Expect:* aroma is vivid but not muddy.
5. **Make a slurry.** Mix 1-2 tsp cornstarch into the cold sauce until smooth. → *Expect:* no dry starch lumps remain.
6. **Use at the end.** Stir-fry ingredients first, then pour sauce around the hot pan edge and toss. → *Expect:* sauce bubbles immediately and coats food.
7. **Adjust final texture.** [BRANCH: too thick: splash water | too thin: simmer 30-60 seconds] → *Expect:* sauce lightly glazes the food with little pooling.

## Decision points

- Protein is already marinated salty → reduce soy sauce in the final sauce.
- Vegetables release lots of water → use the higher cornstarch amount.
- Sauce includes sugar → keep heat moving to prevent scorching.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Lumpy sauce:** detect white starch clumps → strain or mash lumps and whisk slurry cold next time.
- **F2 Too salty:** detect harsh salt after cooking → add unsalted vegetables, water, or a little sugar and acid.
- **F3 Watery stir-fry:** detect sauce pooling in the pan → simmer briefly and use less crowded, hotter cooking next time.
- **F4 Burnt aromatics:** detect bitter garlic flecks → discard burned bits and add fresh aromatics later in the cook.

## Verification

Finished stir-fry has a glossy coating, balanced salty-sweet-acid flavor, no starch lumps, and little loose liquid in the pan.

## Variations

- `brown-sauce`: soy sauce, oyster sauce, stock, sugar, white pepper, and cornstarch.
- `sweet-sour`: soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, ketchup or fruit juice, and cornstarch.
- `gluten-free`: use tamari and verify all bottled sauces are gluten-free.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, with heat and allergen concerns. Watch for soy, shellfish, gluten, and fish sauce allergens, and add sugary sauces carefully to avoid scorching.
