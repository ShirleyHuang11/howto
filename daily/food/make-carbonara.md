## Goal

Pasta is coated in a glossy sauce made from eggs, hard cheese, pork fat, and pasta water without scrambling.

## Preconditions

- Spaghetti or rigatoni, eggs, Pecorino Romano or Parmesan, guanciale or pancetta, black pepper, pot, skillet, bowl, and tongs.
- Use fresh, clean eggs; vulnerable guests may prefer a fully cooked sauce alternative.

## Steps

1. **Prepare the sauce base.** Whisk 2 whole eggs plus 2 yolks with 80 g finely grated cheese and black pepper. → *Expect:* thick yellow paste with no dry cheese pockets.
2. **Render pork.** Cook diced guanciale or pancetta slowly in a skillet until crisp and fat renders. → *Expect:* golden pieces and clear fat in the pan.
3. **Boil pasta.** Cook pasta in salted water until just shy of al dente. → *Expect:* pasta bends but has a firm center.
4. **Reserve pasta water.** Scoop at least 1 cup starchy water before draining. → *Expect:* cloudy hot water is ready beside the skillet.
5. **Combine off heat.** Add hot pasta to the pork pan, turn off heat, and toss 30 seconds. → *Expect:* pasta is glossy with rendered fat and pan is no longer sizzling hard.
6. **Emulsify egg sauce.** Add egg-cheese paste and splash in pasta water while tossing constantly. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* high heat scrambles eggs; keep the pan off direct heat while adding eggs. → *Expect:* sauce turns creamy and coats strands without curds.
7. **Adjust thickness.** [BRANCH: too thick: add pasta water | too loose: toss over very low residual heat] → *Expect:* sauce clings in a shiny layer.
8. **Serve immediately.** Plate with extra cheese and pepper. → *Expect:* pasta is hot, glossy, and not oily or grainy.

## Decision points

- Pan is very hot after frying pork → cool it 1 minute before adding egg mixture.
- No guanciale or pancetta → use bacon, but expect smokier flavor.
- Avoiding undercooked egg → use a pasteurized egg product or choose another sauce.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Scrambled sauce:** detect visible egg curds → remove from heat, add hot pasta water, toss hard, and accept a less smooth sauce.
- **F2 Dry pasta:** detect clumps and dull surface → add pasta water 1 tbsp at a time while tossing.
- **F3 Greasy sauce:** detect oil pooling → add a little pasta water and cheese, then toss to emulsify.
- **F4 Oversalted dish:** detect harsh salt from pork and cheese → add unsalted pasta or serve smaller portions with salad.

## Verification

Pasta is al dente, sauce is glossy and creamy with no scrambled curds, pork is crisp, and the dish is served hot immediately.

## Variations

- `romano-heavy`: use only Pecorino Romano for a sharper, saltier sauce.
- `parmesan-milder`: replace half the cheese with Parmesan.
- `vegetarian-inspired`: omit pork and bloom pepper in butter or olive oil, noting it is not traditional carbonara.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from hot water, pork fat, and lightly cooked eggs. Use clean eggs, keep vulnerable diners in mind, and prevent grease splatter while rendering pork.
