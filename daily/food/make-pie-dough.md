## Goal

Flour, fat, salt, and water are mixed into a flaky pie dough that rolls without cracking or greasing.

## Preconditions

- Flour, cold butter or shortening, salt, ice water, bowl, bench scraper or fork, plastic wrap, and refrigerator.
- Counter space for rolling later.

## Steps

1. **Measure ingredients.** Use 300 g flour, 225 g cold butter, 5 g salt, and 90-120 ml ice water for a double crust. → *Expect:* fat is cold and ingredients are measured before mixing.
2. **Cut in fat.** Rub or cut butter into flour until pieces range from pea-size to flat flakes. → *Expect:* mixture has visible butter pieces, not a uniform paste.
3. **Add water gradually.** Sprinkle in ice water while tossing with a fork or fingers. → *Expect:* dough clumps when squeezed but still looks shaggy.
4. **Gather the dough.** Press crumbs together on the counter with a bench scraper. → *Expect:* dough holds together with dry streaks but no loose flour pile.
5. **Divide and shape.** Split into disks, wrap tightly, and flatten to even thickness. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* overworking now reduces flakiness; stop once the disk holds. → *Expect:* wrapped disks have smooth edges with butter flecks visible.
6. **Chill before rolling.** Refrigerate at least 30 minutes and up to 2 days. → *Expect:* dough feels firm but not rock hard.
7. **Roll as needed.** Let stand 5-10 minutes if too hard, then roll on a lightly floured surface. → *Expect:* dough bends without cracking and does not smear butter.

## Decision points

- Dough crumbles after resting → mist with a little cold water and fold once.
- Dough feels greasy → chill longer before rolling.
- Warm kitchen → freeze butter briefly and work in shorter intervals.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Tough crust:** detect elastic dough that snaps back → rest longer and mix less next batch.
- **F2 Cracking disk:** detect splits while rolling → let warm slightly and patch cracks with overlapping dough.
- **F3 Butter smearing:** detect shiny greasy streaks → chill dough and rolling pin before continuing.
- **F4 Too wet:** detect sticky paste on counter → dust lightly with flour and fold once, then chill.

## Verification

Chilled dough rolls into a sheet without sticking heavily, cracking apart, or losing visible cold fat pieces.

## Variations

- `all-butter`: best flavor and flake, but needs careful chilling.
- `butter-shortening`: replace up to half the butter for easier handling.
- `savory`: omit sugar if present and add herbs or pepper.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk from knives, cold fat, and raw flour. Wash hands after handling raw flour and keep dough cold to limit bacterial growth during long rests.
