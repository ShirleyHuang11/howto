## Goal

Short-grain rice is cooked, seasoned, cooled, and handled so it is glossy, sticky, and safe for sushi.

## Preconditions

- Japanese short-grain rice, rice vinegar, sugar, salt, pot or rice cooker, paddle, wide bowl, and clean towel.
- If serving with raw fish, use fish sold for raw consumption and keep it cold.

## Steps

1. **Measure rice.** Use 2 cups short-grain rice for about 4 rolls or bowls. → *Expect:* rice quantity is known and matched to cooker capacity.
2. **Rinse thoroughly.** Wash in cold water, drain, and repeat until water is mostly clear. → *Expect:* water changes from milky to faintly cloudy.
3. **Soak and drain.** Soak rice 20-30 minutes, then drain 10 minutes. → *Expect:* grains look opaque white and slightly swollen.
4. **Cook with measured water.** Add water near a 1:1 rice-to-water ratio by volume, or use the cooker sushi line. → *Expect:* pot or cooker is loaded with level rice and correct water.
5. **Rest after cooking.** Let cooked rice sit covered 10 minutes. → *Expect:* steam equalizes and grains are tender to the center.
6. **Mix seasoning.** Dissolve 60 ml rice vinegar, 25 g sugar, and 8 g salt for 2 cups raw rice. → *Expect:* seasoning is clear with no sugar crystals.
7. **Season and fan.** Spread rice in a wide bowl, drizzle seasoning, and fold while fanning. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* crushing hot rice makes paste; cut and fold instead of mashing. → *Expect:* rice becomes glossy and cools to body temperature.
8. **Cover for use.** Cover with a damp towel and use within a few hours. → *Expect:* rice stays moist, sticky, and not refrigerated-hard.

## Decision points

- Rice is for rolls → cool to body temperature before touching nori.
- Rice is for bowls → it can be slightly warmer, but raw fish must stay cold.
- Holding longer than 4 hours → refrigerate for safety, knowing texture will firm.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Mushy rice:** detect broken grains and paste → use for bowls; rinse and reduce water next batch.
- **F2 Crunchy center:** detect hard cores after rest → sprinkle hot water, cover, and steam 5 more minutes.
- **F3 Harsh seasoning:** detect sharp vinegar pockets → fold more thoroughly and let rice stand 5 minutes.
- **F4 Unsafe raw topping:** detect fish warming above 4 °C or unknown sourcing → cook the topping or discard it.

## Verification

Rice is glossy, lightly tangy, sticky enough to clump, tender through the center, and held covered at room temperature for no more than 4 hours.

## Variations

- `brown-sushi-rice`: cook longer with more water and expect less stickiness.
- `less-sweet`: reduce sugar by one-third while keeping salt and vinegar.
- `rice-cooker`: use the cooker cup and sushi water mark if provided.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from rice holding and raw seafood. Keep raw fish below 4 °C, wash hands, avoid cross-contamination, and do not leave cooked rice at room temperature all day.
