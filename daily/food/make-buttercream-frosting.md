## Goal

Butter and sugar are beaten into a smooth frosting that spreads, pipes, and holds shape.

## Preconditions

- Softened unsalted butter, powdered sugar, salt, vanilla, milk or cream, bowl, and mixer or sturdy spoon.
- Butter is cool-room-temperature, not melted.

## Steps

1. **Soften butter.** Let 225 g butter sit until it dents with gentle pressure. → *Expect:* butter bends but does not look shiny or oily.
2. **Beat butter.** Mix 2-3 minutes until lighter and smooth. → *Expect:* butter looks pale and creamy.
3. **Add sugar gradually.** Beat in 450 g powdered sugar in batches. → *Expect:* frosting thickens without a sugar cloud after each addition.
4. **Add flavor and salt.** Mix in vanilla and a pinch of salt. → *Expect:* sweetness tastes rounded rather than flat.
5. **Adjust consistency.** Add milk or cream 1 tsp at a time. [BRANCH: spreading | piping] → *Expect:* frosting is soft for spreading or firmer with clear ridges for piping.
6. **Beat smooth.** Mix 1-2 minutes, then stir by hand to knock out large air pockets. → *Expect:* frosting is fluffy but not full of large holes.
7. **Use or store.** Use immediately, or cover and refrigerate up to several days. → *Expect:* surface is protected from drying and odors.

## Decision points

- Frosting is for detailed piping → keep it slightly firm and cool.
- Frosting is too sweet → add salt, acid, or a small amount of unsweetened cocoa rather than lots of liquid.
- Using food color → gel color changes texture less than liquid color.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Split frosting:** detect greasy curds → chill 10 minutes if warm, then beat again.
- **F2 Gritty texture:** detect sugar crunch → beat longer and use powdered sugar, not granulated sugar.
- **F3 Too thin:** detect sagging ridges → add powdered sugar or chill briefly.
- **F4 Too stiff:** detect tearing cake while spreading → beat in cream 1 tsp at a time.

## Verification

Buttercream is smooth, spreadable or pipeable for its purpose, holds ridges for several minutes, and tastes balanced with no greasy separation.

## Variations

- `chocolate`: replace 60 g powdered sugar with cocoa and add extra cream as needed.
- `cream-cheese`: beat in softened cream cheese and refrigerate the finished cake.
- `salted-caramel`: mix in thick cooled caramel and reduce added liquid.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk from dairy storage and mixer use. Keep fingers and spatulas clear of beaters, refrigerate cream-cheese versions, and discard frosting contaminated by cake crumbs after extended room-temperature service.
