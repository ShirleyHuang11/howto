## Goal

Draw a simple front-facing face with balanced placement of eyes, nose, mouth, ears, and hair.

## Preconditions

- Paper, pencil, eraser, and a flat drawing surface.
- Optional mirror or reference photo for observation.
- Willingness to use light construction lines before dark final lines.

## Steps

1. **Draw an oval head.** Sketch lightly and leave room around it for hair and ears. → *Expect:* a soft head shape sits centered on the page.
2. **Place the eye line.** Draw a faint horizontal guide halfway down the head. → *Expect:* the face has a clear center line for features.
3. **Add two simple eyes.** Place almond or oval eyes on the guide with one eye-width between them. → *Expect:* the eyes look evenly spaced.
4. **Mark nose and mouth guides.** Place the nose halfway between eye line and chin, then the mouth halfway between nose and chin. → *Expect:* the lower face has believable proportions.
5. **Draw ears and brows.** Put ears between the eye line and nose line, then add eyebrows above the eyes. → *Expect:* side features align with the central features.
6. **Add hair as a shape.** Draw the hairline and outer hair mass before individual strands. → *Expect:* the hairstyle reads as one clear silhouette.
7. **Darken selected lines.** Strengthen the eyes, nostrils, mouth line, jaw, and hair edges while leaving construction lines light. → *Expect:* the face reads clearly without every guide becoming permanent.

## Decision points

- Face looks too childish → raise or define the jaw and reduce oversized eyes.
- Face looks stiff → tilt eyebrows or vary mouth corners slightly.
- Symmetry is off → check distances from the center line instead of redrawing everything.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 High forehead error:** detect by eyes placed too high in the oval → redraw the eye guide halfway down.
- **F2 Crossed eyes:** detect by pupils pointing different directions → align both pupils to the same gaze point.
- **F3 Sticker hair:** detect by individual strands floating on the skull → block in the hair mass first.
- **F4 Heavy guidelines:** detect by construction marks competing with features → erase guides lightly before final darkening.

## Verification

The drawing shows a recognizable front-facing face with eyes near the halfway line, nose and mouth placed below, ears aligned, and hair attached as a coherent shape.

## Variations

- Cartoon face: exaggerate one feature while keeping the feature guides.
- Profile practice: switch to a side-view reference and rebuild the guides.
- Ink finish: pencil lightly first, ink only the chosen final lines.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Use references respectfully, and avoid publishing recognizable drawings of private people without permission.
