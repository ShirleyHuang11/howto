## Goal

Tune a standard six-string guitar to E A D G B E so chords and melodies sound in pitch.

## Preconditions

- A guitar with strings installed and no visibly damaged tuning machines.
- A clip-on tuner, pedal tuner, phone tuner app, or reference pitch.
- A quiet enough place for the tuner to hear the instrument.

## Steps

1. **Identify the strings.** From thickest to thinnest, name them E, A, D, G, B, and high E. → *Expect:* you know which tuner reading belongs to each string.
2. **Attach or open the tuner.** Clip it to the headstock or place the app near the sound hole or pickup. → *Expect:* the tuner reacts when a string is plucked.
3. **Tune the low E.** Pluck the thickest string and turn its peg slowly toward E. → *Expect:* the tuner shows E centered or very close.
4. **Tune A, D, and G.** Repeat for the next three strings, always turning gently and re-plucking. → *Expect:* each string's note name and center indicator match the target.
5. **Tune B and high E carefully.** Use smaller peg turns on the two thinnest strings. → *Expect:* the thin strings reach pitch without sudden jumps.
6. **Stretch and recheck.** Gently tug each string upward once, then tune all six again. → *Expect:* the readings settle closer to center after the second pass.
7. **Strum a familiar chord.** Play an open G, C, D, or E minor chord. → *Expect:* the chord sounds stable and not sour.

## Decision points

- Tuner shows the wrong note name → keep turning slowly toward the target, but stop if string tension feels extreme.
- String is new → expect multiple retuning passes as it stretches.
- Tuner app jumps around → mute the other strings and move to a quieter spot.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Overshot pitch:** detect by the tuner moving sharp past center → loosen below pitch and tune upward again.
- **F2 Wrong string tuned:** detect by a different string changing than expected → trace the string from bridge to peg before turning.
- **F3 String break risk:** detect by high tension and a reading far above target → stop loosening, protect your eyes, and retune from below.
- **F4 No tuner response:** detect by a blank or frozen display → replace battery, check microphone permission, or use another tuner.

## Verification

All six open strings read E, A, D, G, B, and E on the tuner within its centered or in-tune range after a full strum and recheck.

## Variations

- Electric guitar: plug into a pedal or interface tuner for a cleaner signal.
- Alternate tuning: confirm the target tuning before changing string tension.
- No tuner: use a reliable reference pitch, then compare fretted fifths carefully.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Turn pegs slowly, keep your face away from old strings under tension, and avoid granting microphone access to unfamiliar apps unless needed.
