## Goal

Edit phone video clips into a short finished sequence with clean trimming, logical order, readable sound, and an exported file.

## Preconditions

- A phone video editor and source clips you are allowed to use.
- Enough battery and storage for an exported copy.
- A rough idea of the beginning, middle, and end.

## Steps

1. **Create a new project.** Import only the clips that support the short video. → *Expect:* the timeline contains usable source clips in one project.
2. **Order the story.** Place context first, action next, and result or reaction last. → *Expect:* the sequence makes sense even before fine trimming.
3. **Trim dead time.** Cut shaky starts, long pauses, and accidental endings from each clip. → *Expect:* each shot begins with purpose and ends before it drags.
4. **Use cutaways.** Place close-up details over jumps or repeated action. → *Expect:* edits feel smoother and the viewer sees useful details.
5. **Balance sound.** Lower music under speech and remove clips with distracting noise if possible. → *Expect:* speech or main sound is understandable.
6. **Add minimal text if needed.** Use short title or label text only where it clarifies the video. → *Expect:* text is readable and does not cover important action.
7. **Watch full-screen once.** Play the whole edit without stopping and note only obvious problems. → *Expect:* pacing, orientation, sound, and text can be judged as a viewer.
8. **Export the final video.** Save at the same orientation and a standard quality setting. → *Expect:* a playable finished video appears in the photo library or files app.

## Decision points

- Video feels slow → cut earlier and remove repeated actions.
- Video feels confusing → add one wider context shot near the beginning.
- Speech competes with music → lower music first instead of raising speech into distortion.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Jump cut confusion:** detect by action teleporting awkwardly → cover the cut with a close-up or trim to a cleaner moment.
- **F2 Unreadable text:** detect by text disappearing too fast or blending into the image → lengthen duration or change placement and contrast.
- **F3 Export failure:** detect by an error or missing final file → free storage, restart the app, and export a shorter test.
- **F4 Audio clipping:** detect by harsh distorted speech → lower the clip volume or use a cleaner take.

## Verification

The exported video plays from start to finish, follows a clear order, has trimmed clips without obvious accidents, and keeps important sound understandable.

## Variations

- Social reel: keep it vertical, brief, and front-loaded with the clearest action.
- Family memory: favor complete moments over fast pacing.
- Tutorial clip: add labels only for materials, measurements, or step names.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Review frames for private faces, addresses, documents, and background screens before sharing.
