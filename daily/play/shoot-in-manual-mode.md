## Goal

Take a correctly exposed photograph by setting aperture, shutter speed, and ISO yourself.

## Preconditions

- A camera or camera app with manual exposure controls.
- A still subject in steady light.
- Basic knowledge of where the meter, aperture, shutter speed, and ISO controls appear.

## Steps

1. **Switch to manual exposure.** Turn the mode dial or app control to M or Manual. → *Expect:* aperture, shutter speed, and ISO can be changed independently.
2. **Set the ISO low.** Start at ISO 100 or 200 in bright light, or ISO 800 indoors. → *Expect:* the image preview or meter responds without adding obvious noise.
3. **Choose the aperture for depth.** Use a low f-number for blurrier background or f/5.6 to f/8 for more depth. → *Expect:* the subject area matches the amount of sharpness you want.
4. **Choose a safe shutter speed.** Set at least 1/125 second for hand-held still subjects, faster for motion. → *Expect:* hand shake and subject movement are unlikely to blur the frame.
5. **Balance the meter.** Adjust shutter speed, aperture, or ISO until the exposure meter sits near zero. → *Expect:* the preview or test frame is neither very dark nor blown out.
6. **Take a test frame.** Photograph the subject and inspect the histogram or highlight warning if available. → *Expect:* important tones fit inside the range without clipped highlights.
7. **Make one creative adjustment.** Change one control on purpose, then compensate with another control to keep exposure similar. → *Expect:* the brightness stays controlled while depth, motion, or noise changes.

## Decision points

- Subject is moving → prioritize faster shutter speed and raise ISO if needed.
- Background blur matters most → choose aperture first, then balance shutter and ISO.
- Highlights are important → expose slightly darker and lift shadows later if needed.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Motion blur:** detect by a smeared subject despite correct focus → use a faster shutter speed or ask the subject to hold still.
- **F2 Camera shake:** detect by the whole frame looking smeared → brace the camera, use stabilization, or raise shutter speed.
- **F3 Noisy image:** detect by grainy shadows → lower ISO and add light or use a tripod.
- **F4 Clipped highlights:** detect by blinking highlights or a histogram pressed right → reduce exposure until detail returns.

## Verification

The final image was taken in manual mode and has the intended brightness, sharp subject detail, and a deliberate aperture, shutter speed, and ISO combination.

## Variations

- Tripod practice: use low ISO and slower shutter speeds for static scenes.
- Action practice: set shutter speed first and accept higher ISO.
- Manual phone app: use the same exposure triangle, but aperture may be fixed.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Keep attention on surroundings while adjusting settings, and do not photograph private spaces or people without permission.
