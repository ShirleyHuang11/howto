## Goal

Create a flattering portrait with intentional light, clean background, sharp eyes, and a relaxed expression.

## Preconditions

- A phone or camera with enough battery and storage.
- A willing subject and a place with open shade, window light, or soft indirect light.
- Permission to photograph and share the image if anyone else will see it.

## Steps

1. **Choose soft light.** Place the subject beside a window or in open shade, facing slightly toward the brightest area. → *Expect:* the face is evenly lit without squinting or harsh nose shadows.
2. **Clean the background.** Move clutter, bright objects, and poles away from behind the head. → *Expect:* the subject stands out as the clearest element in the frame.
3. **Set the camera at eye level.** Hold the lens level with the subject's eyes and step back enough to avoid wide-angle distortion. → *Expect:* facial proportions look natural and the horizon does not tilt.
4. **Focus on the near eye.** Tap the eye on a phone screen or use single-point autofocus on a camera. → *Expect:* the eye closest to the camera appears crisp.
5. **Coach a small action.** Ask the subject to breathe out, turn the shoulders slightly, or look just beside the lens. → *Expect:* the pose looks relaxed instead of frozen.
6. **Make three variations.** Take one close crop, one waist-up frame, and one environmental frame. → *Expect:* at least three usable compositions are available for review.
7. **Review before leaving.** Zoom in on the eyes and check the edges of the frame. → *Expect:* the chosen image has sharp eyes, no unwanted background distractions, and comfortable expression.

## Decision points

- Light is too contrasty → move fully into shade or turn the subject away from direct sun.
- Background stays busy → use a wall, doorway, trees, or a wider aperture or portrait mode.
- Subject feels awkward → give a simple task such as looking down then back up.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Soft eyes:** detect by zooming in and seeing eyelashes blur → refocus on the near eye and shoot again.
- **F2 Harsh shadows:** detect by blocked black eye sockets or a bright forehead → move to softer light or add white card fill.
- **F3 Distracting background:** detect objects touching the head → shift the subject or camera angle.
- **F4 Stiff expression:** detect a tense jaw or forced smile → pause, talk normally, and shoot between posed moments.

## Verification

The final portrait has a consenting subject, sharp eyes, controlled light on the face, and no major background distraction touching the subject.

## Variations

- Phone portrait mode: use it only after checking that hair and glasses edges are not blurred badly.
- Black-and-white portrait: prioritize shape and expression over clothing color.
- Self-portrait: use a timer, stable support, and a focus mark where your eyes will be.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Do not photograph people without consent, avoid unsafe posing locations, and remove location tags before sharing sensitive images.
