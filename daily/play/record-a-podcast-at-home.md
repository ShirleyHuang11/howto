## Goal

Record a clear short podcast segment at home with understandable speech, steady levels, and a usable audio file.

## Preconditions

- A phone, computer, or recorder with a microphone.
- Headphones, a quiet room, and a short outline or script.
- Consent from anyone whose voice will be recorded.

## Steps

1. **Pick the quietest room.** Choose a space with soft furnishings and turn off fans, alerts, and noisy appliances. → *Expect:* the room sounds dull and quiet when you pause.
2. **Place the microphone close.** Position the mic 10 to 20 cm from your mouth and slightly off to the side. → *Expect:* speech sounds present without loud breath pops.
3. **Set recording levels.** Speak at your real volume and adjust gain so peaks stay below clipping. → *Expect:* the meter moves strongly but does not hit red.
4. **Record a test.** Capture 20 seconds of intro, silence, and normal speech. → *Expect:* playback is clear through headphones and background noise is acceptable.
5. **Record the segment.** Follow the outline, leave short pauses after mistakes, and keep your mouth distance steady. → *Expect:* the take contains all planned points with visible pauses for edits.
6. **Capture room tone.** Record 10 seconds of silence in the same position. → *Expect:* a clean noise sample is available for smoothing edits.
7. **Save with a clear name.** Export or save the file with topic and date in the filename. → *Expect:* the recording can be found and played outside the recording app.

## Decision points

- Room echo is obvious → move near curtains, bookshelves, bedding, or a closet of clothes.
- Plosives pop loudly → angle the mic off-axis or add a pop filter.
- You stumble often → record in short sections and leave pauses for editing.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Clipped audio:** detect by red meters or crunchy distortion → lower gain and re-record the affected section.
- **F2 Distant voice:** detect by hollow room sound overpowering speech → move closer to the microphone.
- **F3 Background interruption:** detect by doorbells, alerts, or voices in the take → pause, wait for quiet, and restart from the previous sentence.
- **F4 Missing file:** detect by no playable export after recording → check app permissions and save a short test before recording again.

## Verification

The exported audio file plays from start to finish, speech is easy to understand through headphones, and no section clips or disappears.

## Variations

- Interview at home: record each person on separate devices if possible and clap once for sync.
- Phone-only recording: use airplane mode and place the phone on a soft surface.
- Improvised episode: make a three-bullet outline instead of a full script.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Get consent before recording voices, avoid revealing addresses or private schedules, and keep raw recordings private until reviewed.
