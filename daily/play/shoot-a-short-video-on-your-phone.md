## Goal

Shoot a short phone video with a clear subject, stable framing, usable sound, and enough coverage to edit.

## Preconditions

- A phone with charged battery and free storage.
- A subject, activity, or tiny story to film.
- Permission from people and locations that will appear in the video.

## Steps

1. **Choose the video shape.** Decide horizontal for screens or vertical for social stories before recording. → *Expect:* all clips share the same orientation.
2. **Clean the lens and frame.** Wipe the lens, then place the subject away from clutter and strong backlight. → *Expect:* the preview looks clear and the subject is easy to see.
3. **Record a steady wide shot.** Hold the phone with two hands or brace it for 10 seconds. → *Expect:* the scene context is captured without wobble.
4. **Record a medium shot.** Move closer and film the subject or action for another 10 seconds. → *Expect:* the main action is visible and fills more of the frame.
5. **Record close details.** Film hands, tools, textures, or reaction shots. → *Expect:* the video has cutaway clips for editing.
6. **Capture usable sound.** Move close enough for voices or record a few seconds of clean natural sound. → *Expect:* playback is understandable without maxing volume.
7. **Review the clips.** Play the first and last seconds of each clip before leaving. → *Expect:* the files are saved, correctly oriented, and not accidentally blocked by a finger.

## Decision points

- Audio matters most → get closer and choose a quiet location before worrying about visuals.
- Movement is fast → use more light and avoid digital zoom.
- You plan to edit to music → capture extra close-ups and hold each shot longer than feels necessary.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Shaky footage:** detect by distracting wobble on playback → brace the phone, widen the shot, and re-record.
- **F2 Backlit subject:** detect by a dark face against bright background → turn the subject toward light or tap exposure on the face.
- **F3 Bad audio:** detect by muffled or distant voices → move closer, block wind, or record voice separately.
- **F4 Missing coverage:** detect by only one angle available → shoot wide, medium, and close clips before editing.

## Verification

The phone contains at least three usable clips in the same orientation: a wide shot, a medium shot, and a close detail shot with acceptable image and sound.

## Variations

- Mini documentary: film place, person, action, and detail.
- Recipe clip: record every transformation step before cleanup.
- Silent montage: prioritize stable visuals and gather separate ambient sound.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Do not film where attention to the screen creates danger, and get consent before recording identifiable people or private spaces.
