## Goal

Create a coherent photo book from selected images with a sequence, captions if needed, and a final proof ready to print or share.

## Preconditions

- A folder of photos you own or have permission to use.
- A photo book app, print service, or page-layout tool.
- Budget awareness if ordering a printed book.

## Steps

1. **Define the book idea.** Choose an event, trip, year, family story, portfolio, or visual theme. → *Expect:* every photo can be judged against one purpose.
2. **Select a first edit.** Gather 40 to 80 candidate images and remove duplicates, blurred mistakes, and private images. → *Expect:* the working set is strong enough to sequence.
3. **Choose the final set.** Pick fewer images than the maximum pages allow, favoring variety in distance, people, details, and mood. → *Expect:* the book has breathing room instead of crowded pages.
4. **Build a sequence.** Arrange photos with a beginning, middle, and ending, using full-page images for important moments. → *Expect:* flipping pages feels like moving through a story.
5. **Crop and align pages.** Check faces, horizons, margins, and gutter areas before finalizing each spread. → *Expect:* important details are not cut off or buried near the spine.
6. **Add captions sparingly.** Use names, places, dates, or short context where memory may fade. → *Expect:* captions clarify without competing with the photos.
7. **Proof every page.** Preview the whole book at page size and inspect spelling, image quality warnings, and page order. → *Expect:* obvious layout, crop, and typo problems are fixed before ordering.
8. **Export or order.** Save a PDF proof or place the print order only after confirming cost, shipping, and privacy settings. → *Expect:* a final file or order confirmation exists.

## Decision points

- Too many photos → remove near-duplicates and keep the image that advances the story best.
- Print cost is high → reduce page count, choose softcover, or export a digital book.
- Image quality warning appears → replace the photo or shrink it on the page.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Cropped face:** detect by previewing page edges and gutter → reposition the image or choose a different layout.
- **F2 Repetitive sequence:** detect by many similar shots in a row → swap in details, wide scenes, or quiet transitions.
- **F3 Typo in caption:** detect during full-book proofread → correct before export or ordering.
- **F4 Privacy leak:** detect by addresses, documents, school names, or location clues in images → remove or crop those photos.

## Verification

The finished photo book proof has a clear theme, intentional image sequence, checked crops and captions, and either an exported file or confirmed print order within budget.

## Variations

- Portfolio book: organize by project or visual strength rather than chronology.
- Family yearbook: use month dividers and short factual captions.
- Gift book: confirm recipient privacy comfort before including personal images.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because print orders cost money and photos may reveal private information. Confirm price, shipping address, permissions, and sensitive image content before ordering.
