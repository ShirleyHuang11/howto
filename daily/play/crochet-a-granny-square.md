## Goal

Crochet a basic granny square with four corners, even clusters, and a secured yarn tail.

## Preconditions

- Yarn and a crochet hook size recommended on the yarn label.
- Scissors and a yarn needle.
- Basic familiarity with chain stitch, slip stitch, and double crochet.

## Steps

1. **Make a center ring.** Chain four stitches and slip stitch into the first chain to form a ring. → *Expect:* a small loop is ready to receive stitches.
2. **Build the first corner round.** Chain three, work two double crochets into the ring, chain two, then make three more double-crochet clusters separated by chain-two corners. → *Expect:* four clusters sit around the center ring.
3. **Join the round.** Slip stitch into the top of the starting chain three. → *Expect:* the first round closes into a tiny square.
4. **Start the next round.** Chain three and work two double crochets into the nearest corner space. → *Expect:* the hook is positioned to grow the square from a corner.
5. **Fill each corner.** In every corner space, make three double crochets, chain two, and three double crochets. → *Expect:* each corner becomes wider and clearly squared.
6. **Work side spaces.** On later rounds, place one three-double-crochet cluster in each side gap between corners. → *Expect:* sides lengthen while corners remain distinct.
7. **Fasten off and weave tails.** Cut the yarn, pull the tail through the final loop, and weave tails through the back. → *Expect:* the square keeps its shape when handled.

## Decision points

- Square curls inward → corners may be too tight, so add the chain-two spaces consistently.
- Square ripples outward → tension may be loose, so use a smaller hook or tighten slightly.
- You want color rounds → change color after joining a round, before chaining up.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missing corner:** detect by three corners or a rounded shape → count four chain-two corner spaces and redo the round.
- **F2 Wrong stitch height:** detect by squat clusters that do not match the pattern → confirm you are making double crochet, not single crochet.
- **F3 Split yarn:** detect by fuzzy partial loops on the hook → remove the stitch and reinsert through the full loop.
- **F4 Loose tail:** detect by the center opening widening → weave the starting tail around the ring and snug it.

## Verification

The granny square has four clear corners, straight-ish sides, repeated three-stitch clusters, and woven ends that do not pull free.

## Variations

- One-color square: continue rounds without cutting yarn.
- Color-change square: use a different color for each round.
- Blanket practice: make multiple same-size squares and compare gauge before joining.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Rest hands if they cramp, store hooks safely, and keep yarn loops away from small children and pets.
