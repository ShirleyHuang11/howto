## Goal

Play three beginner guitar chords cleanly enough to strum a simple progression.

## Preconditions

- A tuned guitar and a pick or comfortable strumming finger.
- A chair, strap, or posture that lets both hands move freely.
- Short fingernails on the fretting hand.

## Steps

1. **Hold the guitar steady.** Rest the body against you and keep the neck angled slightly upward. → *Expect:* the guitar stays still without the fretting hand gripping it for support.
2. **Form G major.** Place fingers for an open G chord and press just behind the frets. → *Expect:* each string rings or the muted strings match your chord chart.
3. **Check G one string at a time.** Pick from low to high and adjust buzzing or muted notes. → *Expect:* the chord sounds clear across the intended strings.
4. **Form C major.** Move to open C and curve fingertips so they do not touch neighboring strings. → *Expect:* the five intended strings ring with a warm, stable sound.
5. **Form D major.** Move to open D and strum only the top four strings. → *Expect:* the chord sounds bright and avoids the low strings.
6. **Practice slow changes.** Move G to C to D to G, pausing until fingers land together. → *Expect:* each change gets smoother without rushing.
7. **Strum a pattern.** Use four slow downstrokes per chord and count evenly. → *Expect:* the progression stays in time and the chord names match your hand shapes.

## Decision points

- Fingers hurt sharply → stop and rest; mild fingertip pressure is normal but joint pain is not.
- Chords buzz → place fingertips closer to frets and press with curved fingers.
- Changes are too slow → practice silent shape changes before strumming.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Muted strings:** detect by dull thuds during one-string checks → curl fingers higher and move the thumb behind the neck.
- **F2 Wrong bass string:** detect by muddy D or C chords → aim the strum at only the strings shown in the chord chart.
- **F3 Rushed rhythm:** detect by uneven gaps between chords → slow the count until changes fit.
- **F4 Sore wrist:** detect by bent wrist strain → raise the neck and relax shoulder and thumb pressure.

## Verification

You can play G, C, and D in sequence with four steady strums each, and every chord is recognizable without stopping the rhythm.

## Variations

- Easier set: learn Em, G, and D if C is too hard at first.
- Song practice: choose a simple two- or three-chord song using the chords learned.
- Left-handed guitar: mirror chord charts only if the instrument is strung left-handed.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Stop if wrist or fingertip pain becomes sharp, and keep volume considerate for neighbors or housemates.
