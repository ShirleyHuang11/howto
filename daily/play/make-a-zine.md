## Goal

Make a small folded zine with a clear theme, readable pages, and a reproducible master copy.

## Preconditions

- One sheet of letter or A4 paper, plus pencil, pen, scissors, and optional copier or scanner.
- A theme small enough for eight tiny pages.
- A flat work surface and safe cutting setup.

## Steps

1. **Fold the sheet into eighths.** Fold in half lengthwise, unfold, then fold into quarters across the short direction. → *Expect:* the paper has eight equal panels.
2. **Make the center cut.** Fold the sheet in half across the short direction and cut only the middle crease between the two central panels. → *Expect:* a single slit appears in the center of the page.
3. **Fold into booklet form.** Push the slit open into a diamond, then collapse the paper into a small booklet. → *Expect:* the sheet becomes an eight-page zine.
4. **Number a dummy copy.** Write page numbers lightly on each panel, then unfold it flat. → *Expect:* the flat layout shows which panel becomes each page.
5. **Plan one idea per page.** Assign cover, intro, several images or notes, and back page. → *Expect:* the zine has a beginning, middle, and ending.
6. **Draw and letter the master.** Add text and images in the correct panel directions using dark readable marks. → *Expect:* the unfolded master can be copied or refolded.
7. **Copy or refold the zine.** Duplicate the flat master if desired, then fold and cut each copy the same way. → *Expect:* a finished booklet opens in page order.

## Decision points

- You want many copies → make the master high contrast and avoid pale pencil.
- Page order is confusing → keep the numbered dummy beside the master while drawing.
- Scissors are not available → make a no-cut accordion zine instead.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong cut:** detect by the booklet not collapsing → start with a fresh sheet and cut only the center crease segment.
- **F2 Upside-down page:** detect by text inverted after folding → rotate that panel on the flat master and redraw.
- **F3 Tiny unreadable text:** detect by squinting at the folded page → use fewer words and thicker lettering.
- **F4 Weak copies:** detect by gray or broken lines → darken the master before copying again.

## Verification

The zine folds into a booklet with pages in order, readable text or images on every intended page, and a flat master that can be copied.

## Variations

- Photo zine: paste or print small images into the panel layout.
- Poetry zine: give each page one short poem or line.
- Collaborative zine: assign one panel to each person before copying.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Cut away from fingers, supervise children using scissors, and avoid distributing private stories or images without permission.
