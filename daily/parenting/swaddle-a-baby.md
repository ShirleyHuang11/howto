## Goal

You swaddle a young baby securely enough to calm startle reflexes while keeping hips, breathing, temperature, and sleep safety protected.

## Preconditions

- The baby is a newborn or young infant who has not started showing signs of rolling.
- You have a thin receiving blanket or correctly sized swaddle sack.
- The baby is fed, changed, and ready for supervised soothing or back-sleep placement.

## Steps

1. **Confirm swaddling is still appropriate.** Do not swaddle a baby who can roll or is close to rolling; switch to a sleep sack instead. ⚠️ *Hazard:* a swaddled rolling baby can become trapped face-down; confirm no rolling signs first. → *Expect:* swaddling is age- and development-appropriate.
2. **Use a light, breathable layer.** Avoid heavy blankets, loose quilts, hats indoors, or overheating. → *Expect:* the baby will not be overbundled.
3. **Lay the blanket in a diamond and fold the top corner.** Place baby's shoulders along the fold with head and neck above the blanket. → *Expect:* fabric stays below the neck and away from the face.
4. **Wrap the first side across the chest.** Bring one side over the arm and torso, tucking under the opposite side while keeping the chest comfortably snug. → *Expect:* one arm is contained and breathing movement remains easy.
5. **Fold the bottom loosely around legs.** Leave room for hips and knees to bend and move outward. ⚠️ *Hazard:* tight leg wrapping can stress developing hips; confirm legs are not forced straight. → *Expect:* hips are loose, not bound.
6. **Wrap the second side and secure.** Bring the remaining side across and tuck firmly, keeping fabric below the chin. → *Expect:* the swaddle does not unravel and no fabric covers the mouth or nose.
7. **Check fit with two fingers at the chest.** The swaddle should be snug at the upper body but not compress breathing. → *Expect:* two fingers fit between swaddle and chest.
8. **Place baby on their back in an empty crib or bassinet.** Use a firm, flat sleep surface with no loose blankets, pillows, bumpers, or toys. → *Expect:* baby rests on their back with face visible and clear.
9. **Monitor temperature and remove if unsafe.** Feel chest or back for sweating or heat; remove swaddle if baby overheats, fights it strongly, or starts rolling. → *Expect:* baby remains comfortable and safe.

## Decision points

- Baby escapes repeatedly → use a properly sized swaddle sack or stop swaddling if it creates loose fabric.
- Baby prefers hands near face → use arms-up or one-arm-out style if safe and product instructions allow.
- Baby shows rolling signs → stop swaddling immediately and use a wearable sleep sack.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Fabric near face:** detect blanket riding above chin → unwrap and redo lower, or use a fitted swaddle product.
- **F2 Hips wrapped straight:** detect legs pinned down → loosen bottom so hips can flex and abduct.
- **F3 Overheating:** detect sweating, flushed skin, damp hair, or hot chest → remove layers and check room temperature.
- **F4 Swaddle used after rolling:** detect rolling attempts → discontinue swaddling and use safe sleep clothing.

## Verification

The baby is on their back on a firm, flat, empty sleep surface, with fabric below the neck, chest able to expand, hips loose, and no rolling signs.

## Variations

- Swaddle sack: follow the manufacturer's size, closure, and arm-position instructions exactly.
- Hospital or clinician instruction: premature or medically fragile babies may need individualized positioning guidance.
- Warm climate: use a lighter garment or skip swaddling to prevent overheating.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because incorrect swaddling can affect breathing, overheating, hip position, and safe sleep. Confirm no rolling signs, keep fabric away from the face, place baby only on their back, and stop swaddling when development changes.
