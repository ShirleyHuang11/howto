## Goal

You select a preschool that fits your child's development, family logistics, safety expectations, and budget.

## Preconditions

- Child's age, toilet status if relevant, language needs, allergies, and developmental supports.
- Your desired schedule, commute range, budget, and start date.
- A way to compare program notes consistently.

## Steps

1. **Define what fit means.** Rank schedule, cost, location, class size, teaching philosophy, outdoor time, language, and support needs. → *Expect:* you have criteria before tours begin.
2. **Build a shortlist.** Include licensed preschools, public pre-K if available, cooperative programs, and childcare centers with preschool rooms. → *Expect:* you have several options with age eligibility and application dates.
3. **Review safety and licensing.** Check licensing status, inspection history, background-check policy, emergency procedures, and pickup controls. → *Expect:* programs with unresolved safety concerns are removed.
4. **Tour during active hours.** Watch how teachers speak to children, manage conflict, support toileting, handle transitions, and include quiet or sensory-sensitive children. → *Expect:* you have observed the real classroom, not only an empty tour.
5. **Ask about curriculum and play.** Confirm daily rhythm, outdoor play, early literacy, art, social-emotional learning, rest time, and screen use. → *Expect:* the program's approach matches your priorities.
6. **Clarify readiness and support.** Ask about toileting expectations, naps, separation anxiety, speech or developmental services, and behavior policies. → *Expect:* you know whether your child can succeed there now.
7. **Compare costs and contract terms.** Review tuition, fees, deposits, calendar closures, withdrawal notice, and extended care. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* read enrollment and refund terms before signing or paying a deposit. → *Expect:* total cost and commitment are clear.
8. **Check references or parent feedback.** Ask current families about communication, turnover, illness policies, and how concerns are handled. → *Expect:* feedback aligns with what the program told you.
9. **Submit the application and prepare backup options.** Apply before deadlines and keep waitlist alternatives until placement is confirmed. → *Expect:* you have either a confirmed spot or a ranked backup list.

## Decision points

- Child needs special education evaluation or accommodations → ask how the program coordinates with local services before enrolling.
- Program has strict toilet-training rules → decide whether timing fits your child's readiness.
- Choice is between convenience and classroom quality → prioritize safety, warmth, and fit over minor commute differences.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Tour feels polished but classroom feels chaotic:** detect stressed teachers or frequent harsh corrections → revisit during another active period or choose another program.
- **F2 Hidden calendar mismatch:** detect many closures after enrollment → compare the school calendar with work coverage needs before signing.
- **F3 Waitlist uncertainty:** detect no guaranteed start date → keep applications active elsewhere.
- **F4 Child support needs minimized:** detect vague answers about allergies, speech, or behavior → require a written support plan or keep looking.

## Verification

You have a selected preschool with verified safety status, clear schedule and costs, accepted enrollment or application confirmation, and a plan for transition and backups.

## Variations

- `us`: public pre-K eligibility, age cutoffs, and lottery timelines vary by state, city, and school district.
- Montessori, Reggio-inspired, Waldorf, play-based, academic: ask what the philosophy looks like in daily routines, not just the label.
- Cooperative preschool: parents may owe classroom work shifts or committee time.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because preschool affects child supervision, medical privacy, and money. Confirm licensing, emergency procedures, pickup authorization, allergy handling, and nonrefundable fees before committing.
