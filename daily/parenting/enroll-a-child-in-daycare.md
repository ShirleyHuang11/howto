## Goal

You choose an appropriate daycare, complete enrollment accurately, and prepare the child and provider for a safe first day.

## Preconditions

- Child's date of birth, health information, immunization record, allergies, and emergency contacts.
- Your needed schedule, budget, commute limits, and preferred start date.
- Access to local licensing or inspection information where available.

## Steps

1. **Define care requirements.** Write down hours, location, budget, age group, meals, nap needs, and any medical or developmental accommodations. → *Expect:* you have a practical filter before contacting centers.
2. **Check licensing and reputation.** Look up whether the provider is licensed or registered as required locally and review inspection or complaint history if public. → *Expect:* unsafe or noncompliant options are removed.
3. **Tour shortlisted providers.** Observe supervision, cleanliness, sleep spaces, caregiver-child interaction, handwashing, outdoor safety, and pickup security. → *Expect:* you have notes from at least two viable options if available.
4. **Ask operational questions.** Confirm ratios, staff turnover, sick policies, closure calendar, late pickup fees, meals, naps, discipline approach, and communication method. → *Expect:* policies are clear enough to compare.
5. **Verify cost and contract terms.** Review tuition, deposits, notice period, vacation charges, supply fees, and refund rules before paying. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* deposits and signed contracts may be hard to undo, so read all payment and withdrawal terms first. → *Expect:* you know the full monthly cost and exit terms.
6. **Submit enrollment forms.** Provide emergency contacts, authorized pickup names, medical permissions, allergy plans, immunization records, and custody restrictions if any. → *Expect:* the provider confirms the child is enrolled or waitlisted.
7. **Prepare labeled supplies.** Label diapers, wipes, bottles, pacifiers, spare clothes, comfort items, and medication according to provider rules. → *Expect:* the provider has everything required for the first day.
8. **Plan a transition.** [BRANCH: provider allows visits, do a short visit or half day first | no visits, keep drop-off brief and predictable] → *Expect:* the child has a clear goodbye routine.
9. **Confirm first-day logistics.** Recheck start date, arrival time, door code or check-in procedure, payment status, and pickup authorization. → *Expect:* the first day can proceed without missing paperwork or supplies.

## Decision points

- Provider will not share policies or licensing information → choose another provider.
- Child has allergies, medication, or special needs → require a written care plan before the first day.
- No openings match your date → join waitlists and arrange backup care with clear end dates.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Surprise fees:** detect charges not discussed during tour → compare against the signed contract and ask for written clarification.
- **F2 Missing medical documents:** detect enrollment held up → request records from the pediatrician and ask whether provisional attendance is allowed.
- **F3 Difficult separation:** detect prolonged distress at drop-off → keep goodbye consistent, send a comfort item if allowed, and ask staff for a post-drop-off update.
- **F4 Unsafe practice observed:** detect poor supervision, unsafe sleep, or unsecured pickup → document specifics and escalate to director or licensing authority.

## Verification

The provider has accepted enrollment, all forms and medical records are complete, payment terms are understood, supplies are labeled, and the child has an authorized pickup list and first-day plan.

## Variations

- `us`: states regulate childcare licensing differently; use your state childcare licensing lookup and immunization requirements.
- In-home daycare: verify substitute coverage, pets, household members, and emergency exits.
- Infant care: confirm safe sleep practices, bottle labeling, feeding logs, and caregiver ratios.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because daycare involves child safety, medical data, custody information, and recurring payments. Confirm licensing, pickup authorization, medication permissions, and contract terms before leaving the child or paying nonrefundable fees.
