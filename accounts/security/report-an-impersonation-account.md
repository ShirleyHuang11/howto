## Goal

You report an account pretending to be you, your business, or someone you represent, and provide enough evidence for the platform to act.

## Preconditions

- The impersonating profile URL, username, screenshots, or messages.
- Proof of your identity or authority to represent the person or organization.
- Access to the platform's official impersonation or trademark report form.

## Steps

1. **Capture evidence before reporting.** Save profile URL, handle, display name, photos, bio, posts, messages, and timestamps. → *Expect:* you have evidence even if the account changes or disappears.
2. **Confirm it is impersonation, not parody or name similarity.** Look for copied photos, false claims, scams, or attempts to contact your audience. → *Expect:* the report reason is accurate.
3. **Use the official platform report flow.** Open the profile's report menu or the platform's impersonation help form. → *Expect:* the form asks who is being impersonated and what account is responsible.
4. **Provide identity or authority documents if required.** Upload only what the platform requests, such as government ID, business documentation, trademark proof, or authorization. → *Expect:* the form accepts the supporting evidence.
5. **Submit a clear description.** State who is impersonated, why it is harmful, and include profile links. → *Expect:* the platform issues a confirmation screen, email, or case number.
6. **Warn affected contacts if needed.** Post from your real account or notify customers not to send money or information to the fake account. → *Expect:* people have a verified place to check updates.
7. **Monitor and follow up.** Save the report ID and check whether the account is removed, labeled, or still active. → *Expect:* you can escalate if the first report is denied or ignored.

## Decision points

- Account is scamming people for money -> report fraud to the platform and payment provider, and consider law-enforcement reporting.
- Business trademark or brand is copied -> use trademark, business impersonation, or legal report channels if personal impersonation is insufficient.
- The impersonated person is a minor or vulnerable person -> use priority safety channels where available.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Report denied:** platform says no violation -> submit clearer evidence showing copied identity, deception, or scam behavior.
- **F2 Account changes handle:** URL or username changes after reporting -> use saved screenshots and profile IDs if available.
- **F3 Multiple fake accounts appear:** takedown leads to clones -> keep a running evidence log and report each account.
- **F4 Your real account is also targeted:** users are confused -> pin a verified notice and strengthen your own account security.

## Verification

The platform provides a report confirmation or case number, and follow-up shows the impersonation account removed, restricted, labeled, or under review.

## Variations

- Social media: in-app reporting is usually fastest for personal impersonation.
- Marketplaces: include order IDs, payment requests, and buyer messages if the fake account is scamming.
- Businesses: trademark, domain, and brand-abuse channels may require formal documentation.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because reports may require identity documents and impersonators may escalate. Share only required documents through official forms, preserve evidence, and avoid engaging directly with the fake account.
