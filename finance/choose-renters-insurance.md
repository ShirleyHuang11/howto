## Goal

Choose renters insurance with suitable personal property, liability, loss-of-use coverage, deductible, and proof for a landlord if required.

## Preconditions

- You know the rental address, lease start date, landlord requirements, roommates, pets, and approximate value of belongings.
- You can compare quotes from insurers or agents.
- You have payment method and identity information if buying.
- You understand that landlord insurance usually does not cover tenant belongings.

## Steps

1. **Check lease requirements.** Read required liability limit, named interested party, address format, and coverage start date. → *Expect:* landlord requirements are recorded.
2. **Estimate belongings.** Walk through rooms and estimate replacement cost for furniture, electronics, clothing, kitchen items, and valuables. → *Expect:* a personal property limit target is set.
3. **Choose coverage basis.** Compare replacement cost against actual cash value for personal property. → *Expect:* depreciation treatment is understood.
4. **Set liability and loss-of-use limits.** Choose liability, medical payments, and additional living expense coverage that meets lease and household needs. → *Expect:* major limits are selected.
5. **Review exclusions and add-ons.** Check flood, earthquake, business property, jewelry, bikes, pets, water backup, and roommate treatment. → *Expect:* gaps are marked before purchase.
6. **Compare quotes.** Compare premium, deductible, coverage limits, discounts, claims reputation, and bundling without lowering required coverage. → *Expect:* one quote is selected with reasons.
7. **Buy the policy.** Submit the application and payment through the insurer or agent. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* coverage and payment start based on the submitted application, so confirm address, dates, limits, and interested party first. → *Expect:* declarations page and proof of insurance are available.
8. **Send proof and save inventory.** Send required proof to the landlord and store the policy with photos or inventory of belongings. → *Expect:* landlord proof is sent and records are saved.

## Decision points

- Roommates are present → verify whether each person needs a separate policy.
- High-value items exceed sublimits → schedule items or buy separate coverage.
- Landlord requires specific wording → match the certificate or declarations exactly.
- Premium is too high → compare deductibles and discounts without dropping required liability.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong address:** detect unit number or building address mismatch → recover by correcting policy and resending proof.
- **F2 Roommate not covered:** detect policy names only one insured → recover by adding eligible insureds or having each roommate buy coverage.
- **F3 Belongings undervalued:** detect inventory exceeds limit → recover by increasing personal property coverage.
- **F4 Excluded peril:** detect flood or earthquake not covered → recover by getting separate coverage if needed.

## Verification

The active policy declarations show the correct rental address, effective date, required liability limit, chosen property limit, deductible, and landlord proof if required.

## Variations

- `us`: landlord interested-party wording, pet liability exclusions, and state insurance rules vary.
- `college-housing`: some belongings may be covered under a parent's policy, but lease requirements may still require separate proof.
- `roommates`: coverage for unrelated roommates is policy-specific.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because liability and loss-of-use gaps can be costly. Policy documents expose address, belongings, and payment details, so send proof only to verified landlord contacts.
