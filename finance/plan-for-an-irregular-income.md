## Goal

Build a budgeting system for variable income that covers essentials first, saves during high-income periods, and plans for taxes or slow months.

## Preconditions

- You have at least three months of income history, or a conservative estimate if new.
- You know essential monthly expenses, debt minimums, and annual or irregular bills.
- You can keep separate buckets for operating cash, taxes, savings, and spending.
- You know whether income is pre-tax, post-tax, self-employment, commission, seasonal, or gig-based.

## Steps

1. **Find the baseline month.** Total essential bills, minimum debt payments, food, transport, insurance, and required savings. → *Expect:* the minimum monthly survival number is known.
2. **Estimate conservative income.** Use the lowest recent month, average of low months, or contracted minimum rather than the best month. → *Expect:* the budget is built on cautious income.
3. **Create priority buckets.** Order money as taxes, essentials, debt minimums, emergency fund, sinking funds, long-term savings, and flexible spending. → *Expect:* each dollar has a priority before optional spending.
4. **Build a holding account.** Deposit income into one account and pay yourself a steady monthly amount when possible. → *Expect:* high-income months can support low-income months.
5. **Set tax aside.** If income arrives without withholding, reserve a percentage for estimated taxes using current official guidance or a tax professional. → *Expect:* tax money is separated from spending money.
6. **Fund buffers in good months.** Put surplus into emergency fund and upcoming irregular bills before lifestyle upgrades. → *Expect:* slow-month protection grows after above-baseline income.
7. **Use a low-income protocol.** When income falls below baseline, pause lower-priority categories and use the buffer according to the priority list. → *Expect:* essentials remain funded first.
8. **Review monthly and quarterly.** Update income assumptions, tax set-aside, and minimum expenses based on actual results. → *Expect:* the plan adjusts before cash runs out.

## Decision points

- Income is self-employment → prioritize tax set-aside and business expense separation.
- Buffer is under one month → keep optional spending low until the holding account grows.
- Debt payments are variable → keep minimums in the baseline and extra payoff in surplus months.
- Seasonal income is predictable → build the plan around the annual cycle, not a single average month.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Best-month budgeting:** detect normal bills based on unusually high income → recover by rebuilding from conservative income.
- **F2 Tax money spent:** detect tax bucket used for living costs → recover by separating accounts and recalculating estimated payments.
- **F3 Annual bill missed:** detect insurance, dues, or taxes not in baseline → recover by creating sinking funds.
- **F4 Buffer depleted:** detect holding account below target → recover by cutting lower-priority spending and seeking earlier invoices or payment plans.

## Verification

The plan shows a baseline monthly number, conservative income estimate, priority buckets, tax set-aside if needed, buffer balance, and low-income protocol.

## Variations

- `us`: self-employed workers may owe estimated federal, state, and local taxes.
- `commission`: use base pay for essentials and commission for priorities after it clears.
- `seasonal`: set a yearly spending plan and monthly paycheck from savings.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from tax shortfalls, late bills, and unstable cash flow. Keep tax and bill money separate, protect account access, and avoid relying on expected income before it arrives.
