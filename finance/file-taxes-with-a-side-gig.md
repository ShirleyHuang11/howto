## Goal

Prepare and file a tax return that includes side-gig income, deductible business expenses, and any required estimated-payment records.

## Preconditions

- You know the tax year, filing jurisdiction, and whether the side gig is self-employment, platform work, contracting, or hobby income.
- You have payer forms, platform reports, invoices, payment-app records, bank deposits, refunds, and cash income notes.
- You have receipts or logs for business expenses and mileage if claimed.
- You can access official tax-agency guidance or reputable tax software for current forms and thresholds.

## Steps

1. **Separate side-gig records.** Put income, expenses, mileage, fees, refunds, and tax payments for the gig in one folder or spreadsheet. → *Expect:* side-gig records are separated from wage and household records.
2. **Reconcile total income.** Compare payer forms, platform exports, invoices, payment-app records, and bank deposits, including income that has no form. → *Expect:* one gross-income total is documented with sources.
3. **Classify expenses.** Group ordinary and necessary business costs such as supplies, platform fees, advertising, software, payment processing, phone use, or mileage. → *Expect:* each claimed expense has a category and supporting record.
4. **Check vehicle and home-office rules.** Use the current official rules before claiming mileage, actual vehicle costs, or home office expenses. → *Expect:* only eligible deductions remain in the workpapers.
5. **Enter side-gig forms.** In tax software or official forms, add business activity, gross receipts, returns, expense categories, and any self-employment tax screens. → *Expect:* the return shows side-gig profit or loss and related tax.
6. **Apply payments and withholding.** Enter estimated payments, extension payments, backup withholding, and wage withholding from other jobs. → *Expect:* the balance due or refund reflects all payments already made.
7. **Review before filing.** Compare totals against your spreadsheet and investigate large mismatches, negative profit, or unfamiliar forms. → *Expect:* income, expenses, and tax due match your records.
8. **File and pay.** Submit the return and schedule any payment through the official agency, software, or preparer channel. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* accepted returns usually require an amendment to correct, so confirm legal name, tax ID, bank details, income, expenses, and payment amount first. → *Expect:* filing acceptance, mailing proof, or payment confirmation is saved.
9. **Set next-year reminders.** Calendar estimated-tax dates, recordkeeping reviews, and document-download windows. → *Expect:* future side-gig tax tasks are scheduled.

## Decision points

- Net profit is material or recurring → consider estimated tax payments for the next period.
- Expenses exceed income → check hobby-loss, startup, and documentation rules before claiming a business loss.
- You received a form that looks duplicated → verify payer IDs and transaction totals before entering it twice.
- You hired helpers or paid contractors → review payroll, contractor, and information-reporting obligations.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missing gig income:** detect deposits or platform payouts not in the return → recover by adding them before filing or amending after acceptance.
- **F2 Unsupported deduction:** detect no receipt, log, or business purpose → recover by removing it or finding contemporaneous evidence.
- **F3 Estimated payment omitted:** detect payment confirmation not reflected in the return → recover by entering the date, amount, and agency.
- **F4 Wrong business category:** detect software questions that do not match the work → recover by choosing the closest official activity description or getting tax help.
- **F5 Cannot pay balance:** detect balance due above available cash → recover by filing on time and requesting an agency payment plan.

## Verification

The filed return or draft workpaper includes side-gig gross income, categorized expenses, self-employment or equivalent tax treatment when applicable, all prior payments, and a saved filing or payment confirmation.

## Variations

- `us`: Schedule C, self-employment tax, 1099 forms, estimated taxes, and state returns may apply.
- `platform-work`: download annual tax summaries and monthly transaction exports before account access expires.
- `cash-income`: keep a dated income log because no payer form may arrive.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from tax penalties, underreported income, unsupported deductions, and exposure of tax ID, bank, and business records. Use official portals or reputable software and keep records in a secure location.
