## Goal

You lower a cable or internet bill by negotiating a better plan, promotion, or equipment setup without losing required service quality.

## Preconditions

- Recent bill, current plan speed/channels, equipment fees, contract status, and promotional end date.
- Competing provider prices at the service address.
- A target monthly price and minimum acceptable speed or channel package.

## Steps

1. **Audit the current bill.** Separate base service, equipment rental, taxes, fees, add-ons, and expired promotions. → *Expect:* the avoidable charges and true monthly total are visible.
2. **Check actual usage needs.** Review internet speed usage, data cap, TV channels watched, and streaming alternatives. → *Expect:* a minimum service level you can accept.
3. **Gather competitor offers.** Look up available providers at your address and note price, speed, installation cost, contract, and promo length. → *Expect:* at least one credible alternative or proof of limited competition.
4. **Set your target deal.** Choose desired monthly price, maximum contract length, and acceptable equipment changes. → *Expect:* a private walk-away price and service floor.
5. **Call official support and ask for retention.** Verify identity and state that the current bill is too high compared with alternatives. → *Expect:* you reach an agent who can review promotions or cancellation options.
6. **Request specific savings.** Ask about current promotions, loyalty discounts, autopay discounts, owned modem use, plan downgrade, or removal of unused TV/add-ons. → *Expect:* the agent presents concrete options with monthly totals.
7. **Evaluate the full cost.** Include taxes, broadcast/regional fees, equipment, installation, data overage, and any early termination fee. → *Expect:* the new total is lower than the old bill and meets service needs.
8. **Accept the best option or schedule cancellation.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* service changes can remove channels, speed, phone lines, or email access, so confirm all features and fees before approval. → *Expect:* the account shows a new plan, discount, or cancellation date.
9. **Verify the next bill.** Save confirmation and check the first bill after the change. → *Expect:* the monthly charge matches the agreed total or a correction request is opened.

## Decision points

- You are under contract → include early termination fee before threatening to switch.
- Provider offers a bundle → compare only services you will actually use.
- Owned modem is allowed → buy only compatible equipment and return rentals with receipt.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Verbal price excludes fees:** detect next bill higher than quoted → ask for itemized total and escalate with confirmation notes.
- **F2 Speed downgrade hurts work or streaming:** detect outages or slow service → upgrade within any grace period or switch providers.
- **F3 Equipment return not credited:** detect rental fee after return → provide return receipt and serial number.
- **F4 Promo expires unnoticed:** detect discount ending after term → set a reminder 30 days before expiration.

## Verification

The provider confirms a new lower monthly total, plan, effective date, and any contract term, and the next bill reflects the agreed savings or has an open correction case.

## Variations

- `us`: local taxes, broadcast fees, and franchise fees may remain even after negotiation.
- Fiber provider: speed tiers may be simpler, with fewer TV bundle negotiations.
- Apartment or HOA contract: competition may be limited; ask for loyalty credits and equipment savings.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because billing, service continuity, and contract terms are involved. Call official numbers, confirm total price including fees, and do not cancel critical internet service until replacement installation is scheduled.
