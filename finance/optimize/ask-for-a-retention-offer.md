## Goal

You ask a provider or card issuer for a retention offer and decide whether the credit, discount, bonus, or downgrade option is worth keeping the account.

## Preconditions

- Account login, recent bill or annual fee, renewal date, and cancellation policy.
- Competing offers or your planned alternative.
- A clear maximum price or minimum offer needed to stay.

## Steps

1. **Define your reason to leave.** Identify price increase, annual fee, underused service, competitor offer, or missing feature. → *Expect:* a concise and truthful cancellation rationale.
2. **Calculate your walk-away point.** Decide the credit, discount, bonus, or downgrade that would make staying worthwhile. → *Expect:* a private minimum acceptable retention value.
3. **Check existing offers online.** Review account offers, downgrade options, contract terms, and cancellation deadlines before calling. → *Expect:* you know what is already available without negotiation.
4. **Call official customer service.** Verify identity and ask for the cancellation or retention department. → *Expect:* you reach a representative authorized to discuss retention.
5. **State the cancellation intent clearly.** Explain that the account no longer makes sense at the current price and ask whether there are offers to keep it. → *Expect:* the representative checks retention offers.
6. **Compare the offer to your walk-away point.** Include credits, bonus requirements, contract extensions, benefit changes, and taxes or fees. → *Expect:* the offer's net value is clear.
7. **Negotiate or ask for alternatives.** [BRANCH: offer is close, ask if a stronger offer or downgrade exists | offer is poor, proceed with cancellation steps] → *Expect:* you have the best available option or a confirmed refusal.
8. **Accept, downgrade, or cancel.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* cancellations, point forfeiture, and contract changes can be hard to undo, so confirm benefits, fees, renewal date, and unused credits first. → *Expect:* the account status reflects the chosen action.
9. **Save confirmation.** Record offer terms, confirmation number, effective date, and next billing date. → *Expect:* you can verify the account was credited, discounted, downgraded, or canceled.

## Decision points

- Offer requires new spending or a contract → calculate net value after required spend and commitment.
- Points or credits will be forfeited → redeem or transfer them before canceling if allowed.
- Representative offers only temporary relief → set a calendar reminder before the discount expires.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Offer not applied:** detect next bill lacks the promised credit → contact support with confirmation details.
- **F2 Hidden contract extension:** detect early termination fee or new term in disclosure → decline or cancel within any allowed reversal window.
- **F3 Downgrade removes needed benefits:** detect loss of insurance, storage, warranty, or access → compare replacement cost before accepting.
- **F4 Cancellation loses data or rewards:** detect pending rewards, files, or credits → export, redeem, or use them before final cancellation.

## Verification

The account shows the accepted retention credit, discount, bonus, downgrade, or cancellation with confirmation number and effective date, and the net value meets the pre-set walk-away point.

## Variations

- Credit cards: ask about retention bonus, annual-fee credit, or product change.
- Internet/mobile providers: retention may include promotional pricing with a term commitment.
- Software subscriptions: online chat may have the same offers as phone support.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because account access, billing, rewards, and cancellations are involved. Use official support channels, avoid bluffing about facts, and confirm irreversible cancellation or contract changes before approving them.
