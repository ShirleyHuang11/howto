## Goal

You earn a credit card signup bonus responsibly by meeting the minimum spend with purchases you already planned and paying the card in full.

## Preconditions

- Approval for the card and access to the issuer account.
- The exact bonus terms: minimum spend, deadline, excluded transactions, annual fee, and bonus posting rules.
- A realistic budget showing planned expenses before the deadline.
- A way to pay the statement balance in full.

## Steps

1. **Record the bonus contract.** Save the offer page or approval email showing minimum spend, deadline, bonus amount, and exclusions. → *Expect:* a written source for the offer terms.
2. **Calculate the true deadline.** Count from approval, account opening, or card receipt according to the terms, and set reminders two weeks and three days before it. → *Expect:* a calendar deadline with buffer.
3. **Build an eligible-spend plan.** List normal expenses that can move to the card without fees: groceries, utilities, insurance, travel, medical bills, or taxes only if fee-adjusted value is positive. → *Expect:* planned eligible spend meets the target without unnecessary buying.
4. **Avoid excluded or risky transactions.** Do not count cash advances, balance transfers, gift-card abuse, person-to-person payments, returned purchases, or manufactured spend barred by terms. → *Expect:* the spend plan uses ordinary purchases likely to qualify.
5. **Set autopay and alerts.** Enable statement-balance autopay, due-date alerts, and large-transaction alerts. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm the bank account and autopay amount before enabling. → *Expect:* payment safeguards are active.
6. **Track posted purchases, not pending authorizations.** Update a running total only after transactions post and subtract returns or credits. → *Expect:* an accurate remaining amount to spend.
7. **Finish with a buffer before the deadline.** Aim to exceed the minimum by a modest cushion in case a transaction is excluded or refunded. → *Expect:* posted eligible spend is above the threshold several days early.
8. **Verify bonus posting.** Check rewards activity after the statement closes and save the posting record. → *Expect:* bonus points, miles, or cash back appear in the rewards account.

## Decision points

- Planned expenses fall short → do not buy junk; consider prepaying legitimate bills only if there is no fee or the value exceeds the fee.
- Annual fee outweighs bonus value → reconsider keeping the card after the first year, but avoid closing before points or benefits are secure.
- Carrying a balance would be required → stop; interest can erase the bonus.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Deadline misunderstood:** detect issuer counts from approval instead of card arrival → call or message the issuer for the exact account-opening date and adjust spend immediately.
- **F2 Return drops spend below target:** detect a refund before bonus posts → make replacement eligible purchases before the deadline if still possible.
- **F3 Bonus does not post:** detect threshold met but no bonus after the stated cycle → contact issuer with offer proof and posted-spend total.
- **F4 Overspending for points:** detect purchases outside budget → stop using the card for bonus chasing and pay down the balance.

## Verification

Posted eligible purchases exceed the required minimum before the offer deadline, the statement balance is paid in full, and the promised signup bonus has posted to the rewards account.

## Variations

- Travel card: confirm whether taxes, fees, and travel credits affect qualifying spend.
- Business card: keep business and personal expenses separated for bookkeeping and tax records.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from credit, debt, and identity information. Never carry interest-bearing debt just to earn rewards, avoid prohibited spending schemes, and keep screenshots of offer terms without exposing full account numbers.
