## Goal

You apply for a homestead property tax exemption on your primary residence and keep proof of filing before the local deadline.

## Preconditions

- You own and occupy the property as your principal residence under local rules.
- Property parcel number, deed or closing statement if needed, government ID, and proof of residency.
- County assessor, appraisal district, or tax office application instructions.

## Steps

1. **Confirm eligibility and deadline.** Check the local assessor or appraisal district rules for ownership date, occupancy date, filing window, and renewal requirements. → *Expect:* you know whether you can apply for the current tax year.
2. **Find your parcel record.** Search the official property appraiser or assessor site by address or parcel number. → *Expect:* the parcel, owner, and legal description match your home.
3. **Gather proof of primary residence.** Common proofs include driver's license address, voter registration, vehicle registration, utility bill, deed, or closing documents. → *Expect:* your documents support occupancy and ownership.
4. **Select all applicable exemptions.** Homestead may be separate from senior, disability, veteran, surviving spouse, or low-income exemptions. → *Expect:* the application includes every exemption you are eligible to claim.
5. **Complete the application.** Enter owner names, parcel ID, occupancy date, prior exemption status, and required ID numbers. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* false homestead claims can trigger back taxes, penalties, and liens; verify residency and prior exemptions before filing. → *Expect:* the form is complete and truthful.
6. **Submit through the official channel.** File online, by mail, or in person as allowed, with copies of required proof. → *Expect:* you receive a confirmation, timestamp, or filing receipt.
7. **Respond to assessor questions.** Provide missing proof or clarify occupancy if the office requests it. → *Expect:* the application remains under review rather than denied for nonresponse.
8. **Verify on the tax roll.** Check the parcel record or notice of assessment after processing. → *Expect:* the exemption appears for the correct tax year.

## Decision points

- You moved from another homestead property → cancel or disclose the prior exemption if required.
- Property is in a trust, life estate, or co-op → check special ownership documentation.
- Deadline passed → ask whether late filing, good-cause relief, or next-year filing is available.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Address mismatch:** ID shows old address → update ID or provide accepted alternate proof.
- **F2 Application denied:** assessor finds ineligibility or missing documents → appeal or reapply by the stated deadline.
- **F3 Duplicate exemption:** another property still has homestead → remove the old exemption and correct records.
- **F4 Exemption not on tax bill:** approval not reflected → contact the assessor with the receipt before the tax deadline.

## Verification

The assessor or appraisal district record shows the homestead exemption approved or applied to the correct parcel and tax year.

## Variations

- `us-state-county`: eligibility, benefit amount, deadline, portability, and automatic renewal vary by jurisdiction.
- `senior-disabled-veteran`: supplemental exemptions usually require additional proof and may have income or service requirements.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because false claims can create tax penalties and public property records expose owner information. Confirm primary-residence eligibility before signing and upload documents only through official portals.
