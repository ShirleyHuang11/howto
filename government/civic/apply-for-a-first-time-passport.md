## Goal

You submit a first-time U.S. passport application in person and track it until the passport is issued.

## Preconditions

- Form DS-11, unsigned until an acceptance agent tells you to sign.
- Evidence of U.S. citizenship, government photo ID, photocopies, passport photo, and fees.
- Appointment at a passport acceptance facility unless a walk-in facility is available.

## Steps

1. **Confirm DS-11 is required.** First-time adult applicants, children, people with lost or damaged passports, and many long-expired passport holders must use DS-11. → *Expect:* you know you cannot renew by mail instead.
2. **Complete Form DS-11.** Use the State Department form filler or print neatly, but do not sign yet. → *Expect:* the form is complete and unsigned.
3. **Gather citizenship evidence.** Use a prior undamaged passport, certified U.S. birth certificate, Consular Report of Birth Abroad, naturalization certificate, or citizenship certificate. → *Expect:* the evidence matches State Department requirements.
4. **Prepare identity proof and photocopies.** Bring a current government photo ID and photocopies of citizenship and ID documents as instructed. → *Expect:* the acceptance facility can keep required copies.
5. **Get a compliant passport photo.** Use a recent color photo that meets State Department specifications. → *Expect:* the photo is not rejected for size, background, glasses, or image quality.
6. **Calculate fees and payment methods.** Passport application and execution fees may be paid separately and by different methods. → *Expect:* you have acceptable payment for both.
7. **Apply in person.** Bring all materials to the acceptance facility and sign DS-11 only when instructed. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* signing certifies citizenship and identity statements under penalty of law; review all details first. → *Expect:* the agent accepts the packet and gives a receipt.
8. **Track and inspect delivery.** Use the State Department status tool, then check the passport and returned documents when they arrive. → *Expect:* the passport is issued with correct personal data.

## Decision points

- Travel is within the urgent-travel window → use State Department urgent travel appointment guidance instead of routine acceptance.
- Applicant is under 16 → both parents or guardians generally must appear or provide formal consent.
- You need land/sea ID only → a passport card may be enough, but it cannot be used for international air travel.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Signed too early:** facility may reject DS-11 → complete a fresh unsigned form.
- **F2 Birth certificate rejected:** document lacks required seal or parent details → order a certified replacement.
- **F3 Photo rejected:** acceptance agent refuses it → get a new compliant photo before resubmitting.
- **F4 Status stalls near travel:** application remains in process → contact the National Passport Information Center and ask about upgrade or urgent options.

## Verification

The State Department status shows the passport approved or mailed, and the passport arrives with correct name, date of birth, photo, and expiration date.

## Variations

- `us`: adult passports are issued by the U.S. Department of State; most first-time applicants apply at acceptance facilities such as post offices, courts, or libraries.
- `child-under-16`: children must apply in person each time and parental consent rules are stricter.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because citizenship documents and passport applications are identity-sensitive. Use official State Department resources, keep mailing receipts, and store the passport and returned evidence securely.
