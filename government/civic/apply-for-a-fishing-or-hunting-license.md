## Goal

You obtain the correct fishing or hunting license, endorsements, and tags before taking fish or game.

## Preconditions

- State wildlife agency website for the state where you will fish or hunt.
- Proof of residency if claiming resident rates.
- Hunter education certificate, prior license, or exemption if hunting rules require it.

## Steps

1. **Identify the state and activity.** Choose freshwater fishing, saltwater fishing, hunting, trapping, migratory bird, big game, or another specific license type. → *Expect:* you know which agency and license category applies.
2. **Check season, area, and species rules.** Review dates, bag limits, size limits, legal methods, public-land rules, and required tags or stamps. → *Expect:* your planned activity is legal on the chosen date and location.
3. **Confirm education requirements.** For hunting, verify hunter safety, bowhunter education, firearm permits, or apprentice-license rules. → *Expect:* the portal accepts your certificate or exemption.
4. **Create or access the wildlife account.** Use the official agency portal and enter legal name, date of birth, address, and customer ID if you have one. → *Expect:* your customer profile is found or created.
5. **Select licenses and endorsements.** Add only the licenses, stamps, tags, permits, or harvest report cards needed for your activity. → *Expect:* cart contents match your trip plan.
6. **Review legal acknowledgments.** Confirm residency, eligibility, and conservation rules before paying. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* false residency or hunting eligibility claims can lead to fines, license suspension, and criminal penalties; verify before submission. → *Expect:* the checkout page shows accurate information.
7. **Pay and save proof.** Download, print, or store the digital license and tag numbers as the state requires. → *Expect:* you have valid proof available offline.
8. **Record harvests as required.** Carry physical tags if required and report harvests by app, phone, check station, or online system. → *Expect:* harvested animals or fish are tagged and reported on time.

## Decision points

- You are on federal, tribal, or private land → check additional permits and access rules.
- You are a nonresident → expect different fees, quotas, and draw deadlines.
- You are taking migratory birds → confirm federal duck stamp and HIP registration requirements where applicable.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong state or water body:** license does not cover the location → buy the correct jurisdiction license before fishing or hunting.
- **F2 Missing tag or stamp:** officer says license is incomplete → stop activity until the required endorsement is purchased.
- **F3 Hunter education not verified:** portal blocks purchase → upload certificate or contact the state agency.
- **F4 License not printable:** download fails → save receipt, use the agency app if valid, or request reprint from the portal.

## Verification

The wildlife agency portal shows an active license for the correct person, state, dates, activity, and required tags or endorsements.

## Variations

- `us-state`: licenses, seasons, reporting, tagging, and residency definitions vary by state and species.
- `youth-senior-disabled-veteran`: discounted or free licenses may require special proof.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because wildlife violations can carry fines and firearm or outdoor hazards may apply. Confirm legal seasons and safety education, carry required proof, and do not rely on unofficial summaries for regulations.
