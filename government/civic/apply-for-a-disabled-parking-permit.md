## Goal

You apply for a disabled parking placard or license plate through the correct motor vehicle agency, with medical certification when required.

## Preconditions

- Your state DMV or motor vehicle agency's disabled parking application form.
- Medical certification from an authorized clinician if your state requires it.
- Vehicle registration details if applying for disabled parking plates.

## Steps

1. **Find the official state form.** Use your DMV's disabled parking or disability placard page and check whether temporary, permanent, placard, or plate rules apply. → *Expect:* you have the current state-specific application.
2. **Choose the permit type.** Select temporary placard, permanent placard, disabled veteran plate, or disability license plate as appropriate. → *Expect:* the form matches your need and eligibility.
3. **Complete applicant information.** Enter your legal name, address, driver's license or ID number if required, and contact details. → *Expect:* the applicant section is complete and legible.
4. **Obtain medical certification.** Ask the permitted clinician to complete and sign the medical section, including duration if temporary. → *Expect:* the medical certification is signed, dated, and within any state validity window.
5. **Attach vehicle and fee items if needed.** For plates, include vehicle registration, title information, insurance proof, and fees listed by the DMV. → *Expect:* the packet has every required supporting item.
6. **Submit the application.** [BRANCH: online portal | mail | DMV office | county tag office] Use the official method listed by your state. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* false disability certification can carry fines or criminal penalties; confirm the medical information is accurate before submission. → *Expect:* you receive a receipt, confirmation, or accepted application stamp.
7. **Use the permit according to the rules.** Hang a placard only when parked, remove it before driving if required, and use it only when the eligible person is present. → *Expect:* parking use complies with state law.
8. **Calendar renewal or return.** Note expiration dates and return temporary placards if your state requires it. → *Expect:* you avoid expired-permit tickets.

## Decision points

- Disability is temporary → request a temporary placard with the expected end date.
- You drive multiple vehicles → a placard may be more practical than a plate.
- You are applying for someone else → check caregiver, parent, organization, or power-of-attorney rules.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missing clinician signature:** DMV rejects the form → return to the clinician for a complete dated certification.
- **F2 Wrong permit type:** you applied for plates but need a portable placard → amend or refile using the correct form.
- **F3 Expired medical certification:** state requires recent certification → get a new signature before resubmitting.
- **F4 Placard lost or stolen:** permit disappears → file the DMV replacement request and report theft if the state requires it.

## Verification

The DMV or issuing office shows the application approved, and you receive the placard, plate, or temporary permit with the correct name, permit number, and expiration date.

## Variations

- `us-state`: qualifying conditions, clinician types, fees, and renewal periods differ by state.
- `organization`: transportation providers may need organization placards rather than individual placards.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because the application contains medical information and misuse can create legal penalties. Share medical details only with the issuing agency and authorized clinician, and do not lend the permit to anyone when the eligible person is not traveling.
