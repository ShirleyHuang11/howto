## Goal

You file a timely USCIS Form I-90 renewal for an expiring or expired 10-year Permanent Resident Card and keep proof of status while the new card is pending.

## Preconditions

- Current or expired 10-year Permanent Resident Card, or a copy of it.
- USCIS online account, payment method, and current mailing address.
- Understanding that conditional residents generally use a different process to remove conditions.

## Steps

1. **Check the card type and expiration date.** Confirm whether the card is a 10-year permanent resident card or a 2-year conditional resident card. → *Expect:* you know whether Form I-90 renewal is appropriate.
2. **Start at the current USCIS Form I-90 page.** Review the filing options, fees, and instructions before opening the online application. → *Expect:* you are using the latest USCIS renewal process.
3. **Sign in to USCIS and select renewal.** Choose the reason indicating the card has expired or will expire. → *Expect:* the application shows a renewal reason, not a lost-card or replacement reason unless that is also true.
4. **Enter biographic and immigration information.** Use your name, A-Number, date of birth, mailing address, and class of admission exactly as shown in records. → *Expect:* the form has no missing required fields.
5. **Upload the required card image or evidence.** Provide front and back images if available and any additional documents USCIS requests. → *Expect:* uploads are readable and accepted by the portal.
6. **Review the mailing address and fee.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* confirm the address and application reason before payment because USCIS notices and the card will rely on this record. → *Expect:* the final review page is accurate.
7. **Submit and save the receipt notice.** Pay the fee if required and download the Form I-797 receipt notice when issued. → *Expect:* a receipt number appears in your USCIS account.
8. **Use the receipt notice as extension evidence if applicable.** USCIS receipt notices for proper I-90 renewals may extend evidence of permanent resident status for a stated period. → *Expect:* the receipt notice states the extension terms that apply to your card.
9. **Complete biometrics or respond to notices.** Attend biometrics if scheduled and answer any request for evidence by the deadline. → *Expect:* the case continues processing without missed appointments or overdue requests.

## Decision points

- You have a 2-year conditional card → review Form I-751 or Form I-829 instead of filing I-90 renewal.
- You are eligible for naturalization → renewal may still be needed for evidence of status; compare timing with naturalization plans.
- You need international travel soon → confirm acceptable proof for reentry before departing.
- You moved after filing → update your USCIS address immediately and keep confirmation.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Conditional card mistake:** detect a 2-year card or category requiring conditions removal → stop and use the proper petition route.
- **F2 Receipt extension not accepted:** detect an employer, DMV, or airline refusing evidence → present the full I-797 language and request agency-specific review.
- **F3 Biometrics notice missed:** detect a missed appointment → request rescheduling through USCIS as soon as possible.
- **F4 Card mailed to wrong address:** detect USPS forwarding or old address on file → update USCIS and file a non-delivery inquiry if needed.
- **F5 Request for evidence issued:** detect an RFE in the USCIS account → respond with the exact requested documents before the deadline.

## Verification

USCIS has accepted Form I-90, issued a receipt number and Form I-797 notice, and the USCIS case status shows the renewal is pending, approved, produced, or delivered.

## Variations

- `us`: Form I-90 is the standard USCIS renewal application for most 10-year Green Cards.
- `fee waiver`: eligible applicants may request a fee waiver using USCIS instructions, often requiring paper filing.
- `naturalization pending`: USCIS may provide separate evidence options in some cases; follow the current notice and appointment guidance.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because this affects immigration evidence and identity. Use only USCIS.gov, keep every receipt and notice, and confirm conditional resident status before filing.
