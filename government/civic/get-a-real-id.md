## Goal

You upgrade or obtain a state driver's license or ID card that is REAL ID compliant, so it can be used for U.S. domestic flights and certain federal facilities.

## Preconditions

- Proof of identity, lawful status, Social Security number or ineligibility, and residency documents required by your state DMV.
- Current driver's license or state ID if you have one.
- Appointment access or walk-in availability at your state motor vehicle agency.

## Steps

1. **Confirm you actually need REAL ID.** Check whether you can use another accepted document, such as a valid passport, for federal identification. → *Expect:* you know whether a REAL ID card is worth obtaining now.
2. **Open your state DMV's REAL ID checklist.** Use the official state motor vehicle website, because document rules and appointment systems vary. → *Expect:* a checklist tailored to your state and card type.
3. **Gather original or accepted documents.** Typical categories are identity and lawful status, Social Security number, and two proofs of state residence. Name changes usually require certified marriage, divorce, or court records. → *Expect:* every document category is covered.
4. **Check name consistency.** Compare your full legal name across documents and gather linking records for any change. → *Expect:* the DMV can trace your current name from your identity document to your current address.
5. **Complete any pre-application.** Upload documents or fill out the online license/ID application if your state offers it. → *Expect:* the portal gives a confirmation, barcode, or appointment prompt.
6. **Book and attend the DMV visit.** Bring originals, payment, current license/ID, and glasses or contacts if needed for a vision test. → *Expect:* DMV staff accept your documents or explain the missing item.
7. **Review the temporary credential.** Confirm your name, address, date of birth, license class, and REAL ID indicator before leaving. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* correcting an issued card may require another appointment and fee; catch errors immediately. → *Expect:* the temporary document is accurate.
8. **Track delivery of the card.** Keep the temporary credential until the permanent card arrives. → *Expect:* the physical card arrives with the REAL ID star or state-specific REAL ID marking.

## Decision points

- You have a valid passport and rarely fly domestically → you may not need a REAL ID immediately.
- Your name documents do not line up → get certified linking documents before the DMV visit.
- You are not eligible for REAL ID → ask the DMV about a standard license or ID and alternative federal ID documents.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Document rejected:** DMV refuses a photocopy, screenshot, or expired proof → return with originals or certified copies listed on the state checklist.
- **F2 Address proof mismatch:** proofs show old or different addresses → update accounts and bring accepted recent documents.
- **F3 Name mismatch:** current name is not connected to birth or immigration document → bring certified marriage, divorce, adoption, or court-order records.
- **F4 Appointment unavailable:** no slots before travel → use a passport or other TSA-accepted ID, then book the earliest DMV appointment.

## Verification

Your state DMV record shows a REAL ID-compliant license or ID was issued, and the delivered card has the required REAL ID marking with correct personal information.

## Variations

- `us-state`: every state has its own document checklist, fees, renewal timing, and appointment system.
- `license-vs-id`: a driver's license may require vision, testing, or insurance steps; a nondriver ID usually does not.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because identity documents can be misused. Use only the official DMV site, do not upload documents over public Wi-Fi, and keep birth certificates, immigration papers, and Social Security documents secured during travel to the office.
