## Goal

You prepare for a placement test so the result reflects your current skill level and places you into a course that is challenging but not damagingly mismatched.

## Preconditions

- The school, program, or platform's placement test instructions.
- A list of topics covered and whether retakes are allowed.
- Any required account login, ID, calculator, or browser setup.

## Steps

1. **Confirm what the placement result controls.** Find out whether it affects math level, language course, writing support, credit, or remediation. → *Expect:* you know the consequence of the score.
2. **Read retake and preparation rules.** Check whether studying is encouraged, restricted, or limited to review materials. → *Expect:* your prep plan follows the institution's rules.
3. **Review prerequisite skills.** Refresh foundational topics instead of trying to learn an entire course ahead. → *Expect:* old knowledge is reactivated.
4. **Take a sample or diagnostic.** Use official placement examples if available. → *Expect:* you know which topics are rusty.
5. **Practice weak foundations.** Review grammar, algebra, reading, vocabulary, or writing basics as appropriate. → *Expect:* repeated errors from the diagnostic are corrected.
6. **Set up the test environment.** Install required lockdown browser, check ID, calculator policy, scratch paper rules, and internet connection. → *Expect:* the system check passes before test time.
7. **Take the test honestly.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* using outside help can place you into a class you are not ready for and may violate policy; confirm allowed materials before starting. → *Expect:* the score reflects your own ability.
8. **Act on the placement result.** Discuss surprising results with an advisor, especially if retesting or prerequisite review is possible. → *Expect:* your course choice matches the placement policy and your goals.

## Decision points

- Score is much higher than comfort level → talk with an advisor before enrolling in the advanced course.
- Score is lower than transcript history suggests → ask about retake, appeal, or alternate evidence.
- Test is adaptive and untimed → still pace steadily so fatigue does not distort the result.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Overprepping beyond current ability:** detect needing help to solve most practice items → aim for accurate placement, not artificial inflation.
- **F2 Technical failure:** detect browser or login errors → contact testing support and document screenshots if allowed.
- **F3 Misread course consequence:** detect placement into non-credit or remedial coursework unexpectedly → meet with advising before registration deadlines.
- **F4 Outside-help violation:** detect use of forbidden notes, calculators, or another person → stop and report or retake according to policy.

## Verification

The test is completed under allowed conditions, the result is saved, and an advisor or placement chart confirms the next course decision.

## Variations

- College math placement: ALEKS or similar systems may include learning modules before reassessment.
- Language placement: speaking or writing interviews may supplement the online score.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because placement can affect tuition, degree progress, and academic record. Use your own work, protect login credentials, and confirm consequences before submitting.
