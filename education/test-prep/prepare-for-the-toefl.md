## Goal

You prepare for the TOEFL by practicing academic English reading, listening, speaking, and writing in the test format required by your school or immigration process.

## Preconditions

- A target institution or agency with a required TOEFL score and deadline.
- Access to official ETS TOEFL practice tasks or reliable equivalents.
- A microphone or recording app for speaking practice.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the accepted test and score requirement.** Check whether TOEFL iBT, Home Edition, IELTS, Duolingo English Test, or another proof is accepted. → *Expect:* exact minimum total and section scores for each destination.
2. **Take a diagnostic across all skills.** Complete sample reading, listening, speaking, and writing tasks under time. → *Expect:* estimated strengths and weaknesses by section.
3. **Build academic vocabulary in context.** Study words from lectures, articles, and practice passages, including pronunciation. → *Expect:* vocabulary notes include a sentence, not just a translation.
4. **Practice reading for structure.** Identify thesis, paragraph purpose, reference words, and inference questions. → *Expect:* passage notes show main idea and evidence locations.
5. **Practice listening notes.** Listen once, write symbols for examples and contrasts, then answer without replaying. → *Expect:* notes capture lecture organization and speaker attitude.
6. **Record speaking responses.** Use timed prompts, then review pronunciation, completeness, grammar, and pacing. → *Expect:* recordings fit the time limit and answer every part of the prompt.
7. **Write integrated and academic responses.** Summarize source relationships accurately and write clear opinion or discussion paragraphs. → *Expect:* essays have a direct answer, organized support, and source-based detail where required.
8. **Simulate test conditions.** Use a headset, timer, quiet room, and no translation tools. → *Expect:* a realistic practice score range and reduced test-day uncertainty.
9. **Prepare ID and score delivery.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* submitted score recipients may receive the result according to ETS rules; confirm institution names and deadlines before sending. → *Expect:* registration, ID, and recipient choices are correct.

## Decision points

- Institution requires section minimums → target the lowest section even if total score is acceptable.
- Speaking score is weak from fluency, not content → practice short daily recordings with feedback.
- Home testing environment is unreliable → choose a test center if available.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Speaking answers run out of time:** detect unfinished conclusions → use a fixed template with shorter examples.
- **F2 Listening notes are too detailed:** detect missed later information → write structure and key terms, not full sentences.
- **F3 Writing copies source language:** detect repeated phrases from the prompt → paraphrase and cite relationships in your own words.
- **F4 Score sent too late:** detect delivery date after deadline → contact the institution and ask whether unofficial scores are accepted temporarily.

## Verification

You can complete all four TOEFL sections under timed conditions, your practice scores meet or approach the required minimums, and your test registration and score recipients match the institution's instructions.

## Variations

- TOEFL iBT Home Edition: equipment, room, and proctoring checks are stricter than normal practice.
- University conditional admission: some programs accept lower scores with English support courses; confirm directly.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because immigration, admission, and fee deadlines may depend on the score. Protect account credentials, use your legal name exactly as on ID, and never use unauthorized aids or impersonation services.
