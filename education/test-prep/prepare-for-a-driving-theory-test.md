## Goal

You learn the road rules, signs, hazard awareness, and local test format well enough to pass a learner permit or driving theory test without relying on memorized guesses.

## Preconditions

- The official driver handbook or highway code for your licensing jurisdiction.
- A confirmed test type, language options, fee, ID requirement, and passing score.
- Access to official or licensed practice questions.

## Steps

1. **Identify the exact authority and test.** Use the government licensing site for your state, province, or country. → *Expect:* the correct handbook, appointment process, and fee are saved.
2. **Read the rules by category.** Cover signs, right-of-way, speed, parking, impairment, seat belts, school zones, motorcycles, bicycles, and pedestrians. → *Expect:* notes list the rules most likely to appear on the test.
3. **Study signs visually.** Group signs by shape, color, and meaning before memorizing individual examples. → *Expect:* you can infer many sign meanings from design alone.
4. **Practice hazard judgment.** Review stopping distances, following distance, weather adjustments, blind spots, and vulnerable road users. → *Expect:* you can explain the safest action in common scenarios.
5. **Take short practice sets.** Use official-style questions and review every wrong answer in the handbook. → *Expect:* your miss list shrinks by topic.
6. **Run a full timed mock test.** Match the number of questions, time limit, and pass threshold. → *Expect:* a passing score without hints or retries.
7. **Prepare test documents.** Gather legal name ID, residency documents, application forms, parental consent if required, and payment. → *Expect:* documents match the licensing authority checklist.
8. **Book and attend the test.** ⚠️ *Hazard:* passing the theory test does not make unsupervised driving safe or legal everywhere; confirm permit restrictions before driving. → *Expect:* you know the conditions attached to a learner permit.

## Decision points

- You are under the minimum age → wait or complete required driver education first.
- Practice scores vary by topic → return to handbook chapters instead of repeating random quizzes.
- You need accommodations or translated materials → request them before booking, not at the counter.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Studying the wrong jurisdiction:** detect questions about unfamiliar local rules → switch to the official handbook for the licensing authority that will test you.
- **F2 Sign confusion:** detect mixing warning and regulatory signs → drill by color and shape first.
- **F3 Failed mock test from careless reading:** detect mistakes on "except" or "not" questions → slow down and underline the task words.
- **F4 Missing documents:** detect name mismatch or absent residency proof → reschedule if necessary and bring originals requested by the authority.

## Verification

You can pass at least two full official-style practice tests above the local threshold and can state the main learner permit restrictions before attending the real test.

## Variations

- `us`: rules, permit ages, Real ID documents, and graduated licensing restrictions vary by state DMV or RMV.
- UK-style theory tests: hazard perception video scoring may be a separate part; practice that format specifically.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because driving rules affect public safety and licensing records contain identity documents. Use official sources, protect personal documents, and do not drive beyond permit restrictions.
