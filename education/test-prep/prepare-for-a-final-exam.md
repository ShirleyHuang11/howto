## Goal

You prepare for a final exam with a prioritized plan, realistic practice, and a final check of logistics and weak areas.

## Preconditions

- Final exam date, time, location, format, and allowed materials.
- Syllabus, notes, assignments, quizzes, rubrics, and review guide.
- Access to instructor guidance, office hours, tutoring, or classmates if needed.

## Steps

1. **Confirm exam logistics.** Verify date, start time, room or online platform, allowed tools, and identification requirements. → *Expect:* there is no uncertainty about where, when, and how the exam happens.
2. **Map the tested content.** List units, readings, labs, problem types, and skills likely to appear. → *Expect:* the study scope is explicit.
3. **Prioritize by weight and weakness.** Rank topics using exam weighting, instructor emphasis, past quiz errors, and confidence. → *Expect:* high-value weak areas come first.
4. **Build a review calendar.** Schedule content review, active recall, practice, office hours, and buffer time before the exam. → *Expect:* every major topic has a planned slot.
5. **Practice in exam format.** Use timed problems, essay outlines, oral recall, lab scenarios, or past papers matching the final. → *Expect:* practice resembles the real task.
6. **Create and use an error log.** Record missed questions, causes, corrections, and retest dates. → *Expect:* mistakes become study targets.
7. **Do a final logistics and light-review pass.** Pack materials, charge devices, review summaries, and sleep. → *Expect:* the final night supports performance instead of cramming.

## Decision points

- The final is cumulative → schedule older units early and interleave them with recent material.
- The final is project or essay based → prepare outlines, evidence, rubrics, and revision checklists.
- You are failing practice under time → practice smaller timed sections and learn when to move on.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Studying everything equally:** detect equal time spent on low- and high-weight topics → reallocate using the syllabus and past errors.
- **F2 Passive review:** detect hours of rereading with no testing → add closed-book recall and timed practice.
- **F3 Panic from too much backlog:** detect an impossible plan → triage core outcomes and ask the instructor what to emphasize.
- **F4 Missed logistics:** detect uncertainty about location, software, or allowed materials → verify through the official course page or instructor.

## Verification

The exam logistics are confirmed, major topics are reviewed, timed or format-matched practice is completed, errors are logged and retested, and required materials are ready.

## Variations

- `online proctored exam`: test the software, camera, ID process, power, internet, and room rules before exam day.
- `STEM final`: emphasize mixed problem sets, formula use, units, and common mistake patterns.
- `essay final`: prepare thesis statements, evidence banks, and timed outlines.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Follow academic-integrity rules, use only authorized materials, protect login credentials, and avoid unsafe sleep deprivation or stimulant misuse.
