## Goal

You prepare for IELTS Academic or General Training by matching the right module to your purpose and practicing listening, reading, writing, and speaking to the required band score.

## Preconditions

- A target institution, employer, or immigration authority with IELTS requirements.
- The correct IELTS module identified: Academic or General Training.
- Access to official IELTS sample questions and a recording device.

## Steps

1. **Choose the correct IELTS module.** Check whether your destination requires Academic, General Training, UKVI, or another version. → *Expect:* the exact test type, minimum overall band, and section bands are recorded.
2. **Take a timed diagnostic.** Complete sample tasks for listening, reading, writing, and speaking. → *Expect:* estimated band strengths and weak sections.
3. **Study the scoring descriptors.** Read what IELTS rewards for task achievement, coherence, lexical resource, grammar, fluency, and pronunciation. → *Expect:* you know what each section is actually graded on.
4. **Practice listening with answer transfer discipline.** Use one-play audio and check spelling, plurals, and word limits. → *Expect:* answers fit instructions and are spelled correctly.
5. **Practice reading by question type.** Drill headings, true/false/not given, matching, sentence completion, and skimming for location. → *Expect:* you can explain why evidence supports each answer.
6. **Write Task 1 and Task 2 responses.** [BRANCH: Academic, describe charts, maps, or processes accurately | General Training, write letters in the requested tone] Then write essays with a clear position. → *Expect:* timed writing meets the word minimum and stays on task.
7. **Record speaking practice.** Answer Part 1, a Part 2 long turn, and Part 3 discussion questions aloud. → *Expect:* recordings show complete answers without memorized scripts.
8. **Run full practice tests.** Use official time limits and review with answer explanations or a qualified teacher. → *Expect:* a practice band estimate and a focused final study list.
9. **Confirm ID and test logistics.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* some score reports and immigration-linked tests cannot be substituted after registration; confirm module, venue, ID, and recipient rules before paying. → *Expect:* registration matches your required module and deadline.

## Decision points

- Immigration route names UKVI → register for the approved UKVI version, not ordinary IELTS.
- Writing band is below target → get human feedback because self-scoring writing is unreliable.
- Speaking sounds memorized → practice flexible ideas and follow-up answers instead of scripts.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong module booked:** detect Academic booked when General Training is required, or the reverse → contact the test provider immediately and rebook if needed.
- **F2 Writing under word count:** detect Task 1 below 150 words or Task 2 below 250 words → practice outlines and time splits.
- **F3 True/false/not given confusion:** detect answers based on outside knowledge → answer only from passage evidence.
- **F4 Speaking freezes:** detect long silence in recordings → use short answer-expansion patterns: answer, reason, example.

## Verification

Your practice band estimate meets the target or shows a specific remaining gap, and your registration, ID, test module, and score-reporting plan match the destination's requirements.

## Variations

- IELTS on paper vs computer: typing speed, highlighting, and answer entry differ; practice in the format you booked.
- Academic vs General Training: Listening and Speaking are similar, but Reading and Writing tasks differ substantially.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because visa, admissions, and employment deadlines may depend on the score. Use your legal name exactly as on ID, confirm module requirements, and never buy leaked questions or impersonation services.
