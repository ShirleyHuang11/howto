## Goal

You enter an exam with a pacing plan that helps you answer high-value questions, avoid time traps, and leave time to check work.

## Preconditions

- The exam length, sections, question count, and point values.
- A watch or visible clock if permitted.
- Knowledge of whether guessing is penalized.

## Steps

1. **Map the exam before starting.** Read directions, section names, point values, and any choice rules. → *Expect:* you know where the points are.
2. **Calculate rough time per section.** Divide minutes by points or questions and reserve final review time. → *Expect:* pacing checkpoints are written on scratch paper or remembered.
3. **Start with reliable points.** Answer questions you can do cleanly before sinking time into uncertain ones. → *Expect:* easy and medium points are banked early.
4. **Mark and skip time traps.** If a question exceeds its budget and no path is clear, mark it and move on. → *Expect:* one hard item does not steal time from the rest.
5. **Use section checkpoints.** Compare elapsed time to the checkpoint after each page, passage, or problem set. → *Expect:* you know whether to speed up, stay steady, or return to skipped items.
6. **Prioritize high-value unfinished work.** Return to questions with large point values or partial-credit potential first. → *Expect:* remaining time goes to the best score payoff.
7. **Check answer entry and required work.** Confirm bubbles, online selections, units, signs, names, and essay prompts. → *Expect:* correct answers are not lost to transfer errors.
8. **Make final guesses if allowed.** [BRANCH: no guessing penalty, answer every item | guessing penalty, guess only when you can eliminate enough options] → *Expect:* no permitted easy points are left blank.

## Decision points

- Essay has a large point value → outline quickly and leave time for a complete conclusion.
- Calculator or formula work is slow → estimate first and check whether exact calculation is necessary.
- You fall behind halfway through → switch to shorter answers and skip low-confidence questions faster.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Perfecting early answers:** detect spending too long on first questions → set a hard skip point.
- **F2 Misread directions:** detect answers in the wrong format → reread directions and correct before continuing.
- **F3 Bubble sheet mismatch:** detect question numbers and bubbles out of sync → stop and realign immediately.
- **F4 No time to review:** detect finishing at the bell repeatedly in practice → reserve review time as a fixed section, not leftover time.

## Verification

You can state the exam's pacing checkpoints, complete a timed practice section with review time remaining, and avoid leaving high-value questions unseen.

## Variations

- Computer exams: use built-in flags and review screens instead of paper marks.
- Open-book exams: budget lookup time separately; searching too much can be worse than answering from memory.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Follow exam rules about watches, calculators, notes, and devices so your work is not disqualified.
