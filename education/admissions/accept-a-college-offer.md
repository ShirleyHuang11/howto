## Goal

You accept one college offer deliberately, meet enrollment requirements, and avoid losing deposits, aid, or options through an accidental commitment.

## Preconditions

- You have all admission decisions you plan to consider or have reached the decision deadline.
- You can compare net cost, academic fit, housing, aid conditions, and family constraints.
- You can access the admitted-student portal and payment method if a deposit is required.

## Steps

1. **Compare offers using net cost and fit.** Use official aid letters, program details, location, support services, and graduation outcomes. → *Expect:* one school is your preferred committed choice.
2. **Read acceptance conditions.** Check deposit amount, refund rules, housing deadline, aid requirements, placement tests, and final transcript obligations. → *Expect:* you know what accepting legally and financially triggers.
3. **Resolve unanswered questions.** Contact admissions, financial aid, disability services, housing, or the department before committing. → *Expect:* no critical question remains open.
4. **Confirm you are not double-depositing improperly.** Most colleges expect one active enrollment commitment unless a waitlist changes later. → *Expect:* you are ready to commit to this school.
5. **Accept in the official portal.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* accepting and paying a deposit may be nonrefundable and can require withdrawing elsewhere, so confirm school, term, major, aid, and cost first. → *Expect:* the portal shows accepted or intent to enroll submitted.
6. **Pay the enrollment deposit if required.** Use the official payment page and save the receipt. → *Expect:* the deposit posts to your admitted-student account.
7. **Activate next-step accounts.** Set up student email, multi-factor authentication, housing, orientation, advising, placement tests, and final transcript tasks. → *Expect:* the admitted-student checklist updates with new enrollment tasks.
8. **Decline other offers politely.** After your chosen school confirms acceptance, decline schools you will not attend. → *Expect:* other portals no longer show pending enrollment decisions.

## Decision points

- Aid appeal is unresolved -> wait if the deadline allows, or ask for a deposit extension before accepting.
- You are on a waitlist elsewhere -> accept one current offer by deadline, then follow the waitlist school's instructions if admitted later.
- Deposit is unaffordable -> ask admissions about a fee waiver or extension before the deadline.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Accepted wrong term or campus:** detect a mismatch in the portal confirmation -> contact admissions immediately before downstream accounts are created.
- **F2 Aid changed after acceptance:** detect a revised award or missing requirement -> contact financial aid and ask whether documents, verification, or enrollment assumptions changed.
- **F3 Missed final transcript:** detect a hold on registration -> order the final official transcript from your school.
- **F4 Double deposit conflict:** detect commitments at multiple schools -> choose one promptly and withdraw from the other according to policy.

## Verification

The chosen college portal shows your intent to enroll accepted, required deposit paid or waived, and next enrollment checklist items available under your student account.

## Variations

- `us`: May 1 is a common first-year reply deadline, but early decision, transfer, graduate, and rolling offers can use different dates.
- Need-based aid: accepting admission is separate from accepting loans; review loan terms before borrowing.

## Safety & privacy

High risk because this commits money and enrollment choices. Confirm the exact school, term, program, net cost, refund rules, and aid conditions before clicking submit or paying a deposit.
