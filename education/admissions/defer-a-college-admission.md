## Goal

You request permission to delay enrollment while preserving admission, scholarship, visa, housing, and program requirements as much as the institution allows.

## Preconditions

- You have an admission offer and know the enrollment deposit deadline.
- You have a specific reason and proposed new entry term.
- You can access the admitted-student portal and admissions contact information.

## Steps

1. **Read the college's deferral policy.** Check eligibility, allowed reasons, maximum length, deposit rules, and scholarship treatment. → *Expect:* you know whether deferral is possible and what it affects.
2. **Confirm deadlines before depositing.** Identify whether you must accept admission first, pay a deposit, or submit the deferral request before accepting. → *Expect:* you know the required sequence.
3. **Plan the gap period.** Describe work, service, military duty, health recovery, family need, religious mission, or other structured plan. → *Expect:* your request explains how you will use the time responsibly.
4. **Check financial-aid and housing effects.** Ask whether aid, merit scholarships, honors admission, housing priority, and program seats carry over. → *Expect:* you have written notes or emails on what continues.
5. **Submit the deferral request.** Use the official form or email with applicant ID, admitted term, requested term, reason, and plan. → *Expect:* the college acknowledges receipt.
6. **Wait for written approval before relying on it.** Do not assume admission is deferred from a phone conversation alone. → *Expect:* you receive a written decision with conditions.
7. **Complete required follow-up.** [BRANCH: approved | denied] If approved, accept conditions and calendar reactivation steps; if denied, decide whether to enroll now, reapply later, or choose another school. → *Expect:* your enrollment status is clear.
8. **Preserve documentation.** Save approvals, scholarship notes, deposit receipts, and contacts. → *Expect:* you can resolve future portal or aid discrepancies.

## Decision points

- Deferral cancels scholarship review -> compare the cost of deferral against enrolling now.
- You plan to take college courses elsewhere -> ask first; some colleges prohibit coursework during a deferral.
- International student visa timing changes -> contact the international office before travel or I-20/visa decisions.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missed deposit deadline:** detect that the offer lapsed before deferral approval -> contact admissions immediately and ask whether reinstatement is possible.
- **F2 Scholarship not deferred:** detect aid disappears for the new term -> appeal with the written policy or prior confirmation.
- **F3 Unauthorized coursework:** detect planned credits could violate policy -> get written permission or change the plan.
- **F4 Verbal-only approval:** detect no written record -> email a summary to admissions and ask for confirmation.

## Verification

You have written deferral approval showing the new entry term, conditions, deposit status, and any financial-aid or scholarship consequences.

## Variations

- `us`: admitted-student portals often separate admission deferral, housing cancellation, orientation, and financial-aid steps.
- Mandatory service or military duty: schools may have separate leave or deferral protections; provide official documentation if requested.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because deferral can affect money, immigration, housing, and enrollment rights. Confirm terms in writing before canceling housing, declining other offers, or making travel plans.
