## Goal

You submit Common App applications accurately, on time, and with proof that each college received the required application components.

## Preconditions

- A Common App account with colleges added.
- Completed profile, family, education, testing, activities, essay, college-specific questions, and fee waiver or payment method.
- Counselor and recommender sections requested when required.

## Steps

1. **Check each college's requirements and deadline.** Review application plan, deadline time zone, writing supplements, recommendations, test policy, portfolio, and fee. → *Expect:* every college has a complete requirement checklist.
2. **Complete the shared Common App sections.** Fill profile, family, education, testing, activities, writing, and courses if required. → *Expect:* dashboard progress shows shared sections complete.
3. **Complete college-specific questions.** Answer academics, residency, major, scholarship, disciplinary, honors, and writing supplement questions for each school. → *Expect:* each college tab shows no unanswered required questions.
4. **Review recommender and counselor status.** Confirm invitations, school forms, teacher recommendations, and optional reports are requested or submitted as required. → *Expect:* missing school materials are visible before you submit.
5. **Preview the application PDF.** Read the generated preview carefully for formatting, truncated text, wrong test entries, activity errors, and essay display. → *Expect:* the application preview matches your intended submission.
6. **Resolve fee or fee-waiver steps.** Complete payment, fee waiver request, or school-specific payment instruction. → *Expect:* the platform is ready for final submission.
7. **Submit each college application deliberately.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* after submission, edits usually do not update that college's application, so confirm college name, deadline, plan, essays, and PDF preview first. → *Expect:* Common App shows submitted status for that college.
8. **Submit writing supplements separately if required.** Some colleges require an additional supplement submission after the main application. → *Expect:* both application and supplement show submitted when required.
9. **Save confirmation records.** Download PDFs or screenshots showing submitted status, date, college, payment or waiver, and confirmation emails. → *Expect:* you have proof for every submitted college.
10. **Check applicant portals later.** Once colleges send portal credentials, confirm received materials and resolve missing checklist items. → *Expect:* each college portal eventually shows application components received or identifies what is missing.

## Decision points

- Deadline is close → submit earlier than the last hour and save screenshots of any technical failure.
- You discover an error after submission → email the college admissions office with a concise correction if the error is material.
- Recommender is pending → submit your application anyway if allowed; recommendations often can arrive separately by the school's policy.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Supplement not submitted:** detect only the main application shows submitted → return to the college tab and submit the supplement.
- **F2 Wrong application plan:** detect Early Decision, Early Action, Regular, or major choice is wrong → contact admissions immediately; some plan changes require official forms.
- **F3 Payment failure:** detect no receipt or submitted status → verify payment method, retry, and contact Common App or the college before the deadline.
- **F4 Truncated essay:** detect preview cuts text due to character limits or formatting → shorten and re-preview before submitting.

## Verification

Common App shows submitted status for each intended college, required supplements are submitted, confirmation records are saved, and each college portal later shows received materials or clear missing-item instructions.

## Variations

- `common-app-us`: deadline times can depend on the college's local time or Common App rules; verify each college's stated deadline.
- `early-decision`: submitting may require a binding agreement signed by student, parent or guardian, and counselor.
- `portfolio-program`: arts, architecture, music, and film programs may require a separate portfolio platform and deadline.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because submission sends personal, educational, disciplinary, and family information to colleges and may be difficult to change. Confirm every preview, plan, supplement, fee, and consent before submitting.
