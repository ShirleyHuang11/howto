## Goal

You submit a complete scholarship application that matches the eligibility rules and presents strong evidence for selection.

## Preconditions

- Scholarship eligibility requirements, deadline, award terms, and application instructions.
- Transcript, resume, recommendation contacts, essays, financial documents, or proof of enrollment if required.
- A calendar with enough time for recommendations and official documents.

## Steps

1. **Verify legitimacy and eligibility.** Confirm the scholarship sponsor, official website, award terms, residency, grade level, major, identity, need, or merit criteria. → *Expect:* you are applying only to real awards for which you qualify.
2. **Build a requirement checklist.** List essays, forms, transcripts, recommendations, FAFSA or aid data, portfolio items, and submission format. → *Expect:* every required item has an owner and due date.
3. **Request documents early.** Order transcripts, ask recommenders, and gather proof of eligibility before drafting essays. → *Expect:* outside dependencies are in motion.
4. **Write essays to the scholarship mission.** Connect your experience, goals, service, leadership, or need to the award's stated criteria. → *Expect:* the application fits this scholarship specifically.
5. **Complete forms accurately.** Enter names, contact information, school, intended major, financial figures, and consent fields carefully. → *Expect:* the application has no avoidable administrative errors.
6. **Review privacy and award obligations.** Check publicity consent, renewal rules, GPA requirements, service requirements, tax reporting, and whether funds go to you or the school. → *Expect:* you understand what accepting the award would require.
7. **Submit through the official channel.** ⚠️ *Irreversible:* once submitted, some applications cannot be edited, so confirm files, essays, and documents before final submission. → *Expect:* you receive a submitted status, receipt, or confirmation email.
8. **Track the result and next steps.** Record notification date, interview requirements, thank-you notes, and acceptance instructions. → *Expect:* you will not miss follow-up actions if selected.

## Decision points

- Application asks for payment to apply → treat as suspicious and verify with the sponsor before proceeding.
- Scholarship requires sensitive identity or financial documents → submit only through a secure official portal.
- Essay can be reused → adapt it to the mission and prompt; do not submit a generic version unchanged.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Ineligible application:** detect a missed requirement after drafting → stop and redirect effort to scholarships you qualify for.
- **F2 Missing recommendation:** detect pending letter near deadline → send a concise reminder and have a backup recommender if rules allow.
- **F3 Scam scholarship:** detect fees, guaranteed awards, or requests for banking credentials → do not submit; report or ignore the offer.
- **F4 File upload problem:** detect wrong file type or failed confirmation → convert to required format and resubmit before the deadline.

## Verification

The scholarship portal or sponsor confirms submission, every required item is received, and you have recorded award terms, notification date, and proof of submission.

## Variations

- `us`: some scholarships affect financial aid packages; report outside scholarships to the college as required.
- `local-community`: paper applications may require mailed transcripts or signatures; use tracked delivery if the deadline is strict.
- `renewable-award`: verify GPA, enrollment, major, service, and annual reapplication requirements.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because scholarships can involve money, identity documents, and financial data. Avoid fee-based applications, protect tax and banking information, and read consent and renewal obligations before submitting.
