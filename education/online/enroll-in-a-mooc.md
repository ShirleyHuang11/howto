## Goal

You enroll in a massive open online course using the right session, access type, and payment choice for your goal.

## Preconditions

- You have chosen a course or platform.
- You know whether you need free audit access, graded assignments, academic credit, or a certificate.
- You have an email account and payment method if paying.

## Steps

1. **Open the official course page.** Use the platform or university link and check instructor, syllabus, start date, workload, and language. → *Expect:* you are on the correct course and session.
2. **Review access options.** Compare audit, free access, paid certificate, subscription, financial aid, and credit-bearing tracks. → *Expect:* you know what each option unlocks.
3. **Check deadlines and pacing.** Look for self-paced, cohort, assignment due dates, and access expiration. → *Expect:* the timeline fits your schedule.
4. **Create or sign in to the account.** Use an email you will keep and set strong authentication if available. → *Expect:* your account profile is active.
5. **Choose the enrollment track.** [BRANCH: audit | certificate | credit] Select the track that matches your need and budget. → *Expect:* the enrollment summary shows the intended access level.
6. **Submit payment or aid request if needed.** Confirm price, taxes, renewal terms, refund period, or financial-aid review time. → *Expect:* the platform shows enrolled or aid request submitted.
7. **Enter the course and inspect module one.** Check videos, readings, assignments, forums, and grading policy. → *Expect:* course materials are accessible according to your track.
8. **Schedule the first study block.** Put the first lesson and next deadline on your calendar. → *Expect:* enrollment turns into an actual learning plan.

## Decision points

- Certificate is optional -> audit first if you are unsure the course fits.
- Course is part of a subscription -> set a cancellation reminder before renewal.
- Academic credit is advertised -> verify with your institution before assuming transfer.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong paid track:** detect you paid for a certificate you do not need -> request a refund within the platform's policy window.
- **F2 Session mismatch:** detect deadlines already passed or wrong language -> switch session if available.
- **F3 Locked assignments:** detect audit access excludes graded work -> upgrade only if grading matters.
- **F4 Forgotten enrollment:** detect no progress after sign-up -> schedule fixed study blocks or unenroll before renewal.

## Verification

The platform account shows enrolled status for the correct MOOC session and track, and you can access the first required learning activity.

## Variations

- University credit MOOC: may require identity verification, proctored exams, and separate tuition rules.
- Audit track: certificates and graded assignments may be unavailable even when videos are free.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, with possible payment exposure. Verify renewal terms, refund deadlines, identity-verification requirements, and what personal data appears on certificates before paying.
