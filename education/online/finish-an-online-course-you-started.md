## Goal

You restart an unfinished online course, reduce the remaining work to a concrete plan, and complete the parts that prove learning.

## Preconditions

- You still have access to the course or can renew it.
- You know why you stopped or can identify the blocker.
- You have calendar time available before any expiration date.

## Steps

1. **Reopen the course dashboard.** Check progress, remaining modules, deadlines, expiration, and certificate requirements. → *Expect:* you know exactly what is left.
2. **Decide whether finishing still matters.** Compare the course to your current goal before spending more time. → *Expect:* you either recommit or intentionally abandon it.
3. **Identify the stopping point.** Find whether the blocker was difficulty, boredom, time, missing prerequisites, or platform friction. → *Expect:* you know what must change this time.
4. **Shrink the completion plan.** Schedule only the remaining lessons, assignments, quizzes, and project work, with buffers. → *Expect:* a dated plan fits your calendar.
5. **Restart with a quick review.** Skim notes, redo one key exercise, or retake a checkpoint instead of rewatching everything. → *Expect:* you regain enough context to continue.
6. **Complete active work first.** Prioritize assignments, labs, quizzes, and projects over passive videos when time is tight. → *Expect:* course progress and skill evidence increase each session.
7. **Use support when stuck.** Search course forums, documentation, office hours, or peer groups for the exact issue. → *Expect:* blockers turn into specific fixes or questions.
8. **Submit final requirements and archive proof.** Finish assessments, download certificate if earned, and save projects or notes. → *Expect:* the course shows complete and your output is accessible.

## Decision points

- Certificate requires paid upgrade -> pay only if the credential has value for your goal.
- Course content is outdated -> finish only transferable foundations or switch to a newer resource.
- You are stuck on a prerequisite -> pause the course and fill the prerequisite with a smaller lesson.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Restart spiral:** detect rewatching old lessons without progress -> set a one-session review limit and continue.
- **F2 Expired access:** detect locked content -> ask support about extension or download available materials.
- **F3 Assignment bottleneck:** detect one project blocking completion -> split it into submission requirements and optional polish.
- **F4 Motivation drop:** detect skipped study blocks -> add accountability, reduce session length, or change completion criteria.

## Verification

The course dashboard shows complete or all personally required modules done, and you have saved the certificate, project, notes, or other evidence you needed.

## Variations

- Subscription platforms: finish before billing renewal or cancel immediately after completing.
- Audit learners: define your own finish line, such as completing core modules and one project.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Watch for recurring payments, avoid uploading private work files to public forums, and do not keep paying for a course you no longer need.
