## Goal

You join a study community that helps you stay accountable, ask better questions, and learn without compromising privacy or academic integrity.

## Preconditions

- A subject, course, exam, or skill you want support with.
- The class or institution rules for collaboration.
- A separate account or profile if you do not want your main identity public.

## Steps

1. **Define what help you need.** Choose whether you need accountability, explanations, practice partners, feedback, or resource recommendations. → *Expect:* you can evaluate communities by fit.
2. **Find candidate communities.** Search official course forums, Discord or Slack groups, subreddit communities, professional associations, or platform discussion boards. → *Expect:* you have at least three options to compare.
3. **Check legitimacy and norms.** Read rules, moderation history, pinned resources, and recent discussions. → *Expect:* the group appears active, respectful, and aligned with learning.
4. **Screen for academic-integrity risk.** Avoid groups focused on answer sharing, paid cheating, leaked exams, or assignment completion. → *Expect:* the community supports learning rather than misconduct.
5. **Join with minimal personal data.** Use only the profile information needed to participate. → *Expect:* your account does not expose unnecessary school, location, or contact details.
6. **Introduce yourself briefly.** State your topic, level, goal, and how you plan to contribute. → *Expect:* members understand what kind of study partner you are.
7. **Ask a specific first question.** Include what you tried, where you got stuck, and what kind of help you want. → *Expect:* replies can target the actual problem.
8. **Contribute back.** Answer questions you can, share clean notes, or organize a study session. → *Expect:* you become a useful member rather than a passive requester.

## Decision points

- Community allows answer dumps → leave and use official or moderated spaces.
- Discussions are inactive → keep the account but choose a more active group.
- You need private tutoring, not group support → use the community only for referrals and vet tutors separately.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Low-quality advice:** detect confident answers without sources or reasoning → verify with official materials before relying on them.
- **F2 Notification overload:** detect study time lost to chat → mute channels and schedule fixed check-in windows.
- **F3 Privacy exposure:** detect requests for school ID, phone number, or private documents → decline and report if needed.
- **F4 Collaboration violation:** detect members sharing graded answers → leave the thread and follow your course policy.

## Verification

You are in an active, rule-abiding community, have asked or answered at least one substantive question, and know how to manage notifications and privacy settings.

## Variations

- University course: start with the official LMS discussion board or instructor-approved platform.
- Professional certification: prefer vendor forums, professional associations, or moderated study groups.
- Language learning: choose groups with correction norms, target-language practice, and clear harassment rules.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but protect your identity, school records, and graded work. Do not share assignment answers, exam content, private documents, or contact details in public channels.
