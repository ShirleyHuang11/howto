## Goal

You complete an online certificate program and obtain a credential that is legitimate, useful, and verifiable for your purpose.

## Preconditions

- You know why you need the certificate: employment, compliance, school, portfolio, or personal learning.
- You have confirmed the provider, cost, timeline, and assessment requirements.
- You can meet identity, attendance, project, or exam rules.

## Steps

1. **Verify credential value before enrolling.** Check whether the employer, school, regulator, or client recognizes the certificate. → *Expect:* the certificate is worth pursuing for your goal.
2. **Review the full requirements.** Identify modules, graded assignments, projects, exams, minimum score, deadlines, identity checks, and fees. → *Expect:* there are no hidden completion requirements.
3. **Enroll in the correct track.** Choose the certificate or verified track, not a free audit track that cannot issue credentials. → *Expect:* the dashboard shows certificate eligibility.
4. **Create a completion schedule.** Work backward from the deadline and include assessment retakes or project revision time. → *Expect:* all certificate tasks fit your calendar.
5. **Complete graded work honestly.** Submit assignments, labs, quizzes, peer reviews, or exams according to the honor code. → *Expect:* each module records passing progress.
6. **Resolve failed or missing items quickly.** Use feedback, retake policies, support tickets, or office hours before access expires. → *Expect:* every requirement returns to passing or pending review.
7. **Claim the certificate.** Confirm name spelling, issue date, credential ID, and public verification link. → *Expect:* the certificate downloads or appears in your profile.
8. **Store and share appropriately.** Save the PDF, verification URL, and transcript; add it to a resume or profile only if relevant. → *Expect:* others can verify the credential without seeing private account data.

## Decision points

- Certificate is not recognized by the target audience -> choose a different provider before paying.
- Program uses recurring billing -> schedule completion before renewal or budget for the full term.
- Identity verification is required -> decide whether you are comfortable submitting ID before enrolling.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Nonverifiable certificate:** detect no credential ID or verification link -> ask support or avoid using it as formal evidence.
- **F2 Name mismatch:** detect wrong legal or display name -> correct profile before issuance or request reissue.
- **F3 Missed deadline:** detect expired assignments -> ask about extension, retake, or re-enrollment costs.
- **F4 Academic-integrity violation:** detect flagged work -> respond honestly with evidence and follow the appeal process if applicable.

## Verification

You have a certificate with the correct name, issue date, provider, credential ID or verification link, and all dashboard requirements marked complete.

## Variations

- Professional compliance certificates: verify expiration date, continuing education rules, and regulator recognition.
- Portfolio certificates: pair the credential with a public project because the certificate alone may not prove skill.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because certificates can involve fees, identity checks, and employment claims. Confirm recognition before paying, use accurate identity information, and do not post certificate URLs that expose private profile details.
