## Goal

You identify why you are avoiding study work and start with a small, specific action that can build momentum without relying on panic.

## Preconditions

- A study task, assignment, exam, or project you are avoiding.
- The actual deadline or exam date.
- A place to write a short plan and track completion.

## Steps

1. **Name the avoided task precisely.** Write the assignment, chapter, problem set, or exam topic you have been postponing. → *Expect:* the vague feeling becomes one named task.
2. **Identify the avoidance reason.** Choose the main cause: unclear instructions, boredom, fear of failure, too much work, missing materials, or distraction. → *Expect:* the response matches the real barrier.
3. **Define the next physical action.** Pick the smallest visible action, such as opening the rubric, doing one problem, or writing three bullet points. → *Expect:* the action can be done in under 10 minutes.
4. **Remove one friction point.** Gather materials, close distracting tabs, ask for the missing instruction, or move to a better location. → *Expect:* starting requires fewer decisions.
5. **Use a short commitment.** Set a 5-15 minute timer and work only until it ends. → *Expect:* the task has begun even if motivation is low.
6. **Continue or stop deliberately.** When the timer ends, choose another short block, schedule the next block, or stop after recording progress. → *Expect:* stopping is planned, not drifting away.
7. **Make progress visible.** Cross off the first action and write the next one. → *Expect:* the task now has a trail of completed work.

## Decision points

- The barrier is confusion → contact the teacher, tutor, classmate, or office hours before spending hours guessing.
- The barrier is fear of a bad grade → make a rough draft or imperfect attempt first, then improve it.
- The deadline is very near → triage the highest-value requirements and submit the best honest work possible.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Planning instead of studying:** detect repeated schedule making with no task attempt → require one 5-minute work block before more planning.
- **F2 Perfection paralysis:** detect refusal to start until the work can be excellent → create an intentionally rough first pass.
- **F3 Distraction loop:** detect app switching during the first minute → use site blocking, a different room, or a phone-free setup.
- **F4 Hidden missing information:** detect stalling because instructions are unclear → write the exact question and ask for clarification.

## Verification

The avoided task has a named barrier, one completed starter action, one recorded next action, and a scheduled follow-up block if the task is not finished.

## Variations

- `group accountability`: tell a study partner the exact next action and send proof when it is done.
- `long project`: create a daily minimum action, such as one source note or one paragraph.
- `test prep`: begin with five practice questions to expose what needs review.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. If procrastination is tied to severe anxiety, depression, sleep loss, or inability to function, contact a counselor, clinician, trusted adult, or campus support service; do not share private grades or mental-health details in public study apps.
