## Goal

You maintain a vocabulary notebook that helps you recall and use words in context, not just recognize translations.

## Preconditions

- A target language and regular source of new words.
- A notebook, spreadsheet, notes app, or spaced-repetition tool.
- A plan to review entries repeatedly.

## Steps

1. **Choose a capture format.** Decide on paper, digital notes, spreadsheet, or flashcard app. → *Expect:* new words have one consistent home.
2. **Record words in context.** Write the sentence or phrase where you found the word, not only a dictionary meaning. → *Expect:* each entry shows real usage.
3. **Add pronunciation and grammar.** Include audio link, stress, gender, plural, verb form, particles, or register where relevant. → *Expect:* you know how the word behaves.
4. **Write your own sentence.** Create a sentence that is true or useful to you. → *Expect:* the word is connected to personal meaning.
5. **Group by theme and function.** Tag words by topic, situation, part of speech, or exam unit. → *Expect:* related words are easier to review together.
6. **Review with active recall.** Cover the answer, translate both directions, or explain the word from context. → *Expect:* you retrieve words without simply rereading.
7. **Use spaced repetition.** Review difficult words more often and mature words less often. → *Expect:* review time goes where memory is weakest.
8. **Prune low-value entries.** Remove rare words you do not need and merge duplicates. → *Expect:* the notebook stays useful rather than bloated.

## Decision points

- You need speaking fluency → prioritize phrases and collocations over isolated nouns.
- You need reading exams → include synonyms, roots, and example sentences from texts.
- The notebook is too slow to maintain → simplify fields to word, context, meaning, and review date.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Translation-only entries:** detect words you recognize but cannot use → add context and your own sentence.
- **F2 Too many new words:** detect review backlog growing daily → cap new entries and review old ones first.
- **F3 No pronunciation memory:** detect reading-only knowledge → add audio and say entries aloud.
- **F4 Forgotten notebook:** detect weeks without review → schedule short recurring review sessions.

## Verification

Your notebook contains contextual entries, pronunciation or grammar notes where useful, and a review routine that lets you actively recall older words.

## Variations

- Spaced-repetition deck: use cloze cards for phrases and one fact per card.
- Paper notebook: leave space for later examples, corrections, and review marks.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Do not include private messages, student records, or sensitive personal details as example sentences if others may see the notebook.
