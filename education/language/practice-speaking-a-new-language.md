## Goal

You build speaking ability through repeated, feedback-informed practice that improves fluency, pronunciation, and confidence in real situations.

## Preconditions

- A target language and approximate level.
- Speaking prompts, conversation partner, tutor, class, or recording tool.
- A small list of situations you want to handle.

## Steps

1. **Pick one speaking situation.** Choose a concrete task such as ordering food, introducing yourself, explaining your job, or discussing an article. → *Expect:* practice has a real communicative purpose.
2. **Prepare useful phrases.** Collect sentence starters, connectors, repair phrases, and key vocabulary. → *Expect:* you have language scaffolding before speaking.
3. **Record a first attempt.** Speak for 1-3 minutes without stopping to look up every word. → *Expect:* a baseline recording shows fluency and gaps.
4. **Notice communication blockers.** Mark missing words, grammar breakdowns, pronunciation issues, and pauses. → *Expect:* practice targets the few issues that blocked meaning.
5. **Repeat with small improvements.** Re-record the same prompt using corrected phrases and smoother pacing. → *Expect:* the second attempt is clearer than the first.
6. **Practice with a person.** Use a tutor, teacher, classmate, or language exchange partner and ask for specific feedback. → *Expect:* you receive corrections a recording alone cannot provide.
7. **Use repair strategies.** Practice asking for repetition, rephrasing, slowing down, and checking understanding. → *Expect:* conversation can continue even when you miss a word.
8. **Rotate topics and review.** Revisit old topics after a week while adding new ones. → *Expect:* speaking gains transfer beyond one memorized script.

## Decision points

- Pronunciation blocks understanding → spend time on sounds, stress, rhythm, and shadowing.
- You know grammar but cannot speak quickly → prioritize timed speaking over more written exercises.
- You feel embarrassed speaking with people → start with private recordings, then low-stakes partners.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Script dependence:** detect fluent recitation but failure in follow-up questions → practice variations and unscripted prompts.
- **F2 Correction overload:** detect stopping after every mistake → ask for only the top recurring errors after you finish.
- **F3 Avoiding hard sounds:** detect replacing difficult words → practice minimal pairs and short phrases aloud.
- **F4 No review loop:** detect repeated mistakes across recordings → keep an error list and rehearse corrected versions.

## Verification

You have recordings or partner sessions showing that you can complete target speaking tasks more clearly, with fewer pauses and corrected recurring errors.

## Variations

- Exam speaking: practice exact timing, prompt types, and scoring rubric.
- Workplace speaking: rehearse meetings, presentations, phone calls, and polite disagreement.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Ask permission before recording other people, avoid sharing private voice recordings publicly, and keep personal details limited with new conversation partners.
